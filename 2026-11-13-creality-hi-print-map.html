On the printer

The files sit on the machine under plain names — Acc 1 - scraper and tools.gcode through Acc 5 - purge chute.gcode. All single-colour T0 from lane A. Each one carries its own 6 mm brim; peel it after the bed cools or it will take chunks of sheet with it.

Layouts and times generated from the same profile the toys use (process-hi-quality-brim.json). Bed 260 × 260 × 300 mm, 0.4 mm nozzle, 210 °C, 80 mm/s outer walls.
