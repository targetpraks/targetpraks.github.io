What you're actually asking for

Espetada (Portuguese) and souvla (Greek/Cypriot) are the same physics: chunks of pork, lamb or chicken on a skewer, turned slowly over coals, nothing touching a grid. So the unit you want is not a bigger grid — it's a braai with a motorised skewer frame. Get that right and both of your family's traditions come off the same fire, plus a flat grill for everything else.

  • Buy: souvlabraai.co.za — Built In Souvla Braai · full range · 076 463 5306

Measure these three numbers before you order

  1. Opening width — the clear width between the cheeks of your braai recess, in mm.
  2. Depth — front lip to back wall. The drop-in frame needs ≥ 550 mm; the Souvla built-in needs 500–700 mm depending on the model you pick.
  3. Where the motor hangs — it sits outside the frame on the left (right on request). Leave ~150 mm clear on that side and a plug point or battery access.

Both makers custom-size. Send those three numbers and a photo of the opening and they'll confirm the variant.

If it were mine

Step 1 — 10 minutes with a tape Opening width, depth, and which side the motor can hang. Photo of the opening.
Step 2 — order the frame Brilliant Braai at R14 455 if a built-in braai already exists there; Souvla Braai Built In at R14 999 if the area is still bare.
Step 3 — the surround Both makers assume a non-combustible surround: brick, heat-resistant board or a proper braai cowl. If the area is timber or plasterboard, that's the real project — get the unit first, then size the opening to its drawing.

Two honest caveats

1. The gate's pick assumes you already have a braai to slide into. It scored 0.54 for the Brilliant Braai against 0.28 for the Souvla built-in. If that assumption is wrong, the order flips — the Souvla built-in is the one that includes the firebox, the grill and the spit fork for the same money.

2. One outlier price I do not trust. Raines lists a Brilliant Braai knockdown stand at R2 777, while the maker's own site prices the same family at R33 750. That is an 11× gap — most likely a bad feed price. Treat it as "worth one phone call", not as a bargain.
