The occupational therapist, who cost the most per hour of anyone here, wrote nothing at all for fifteen months after promising twice. The one who charged the most reported the least.
What the spend was actually for — and where the baton wentYou funded all of this mainly for his speech, and to assist him. Judged on that purpose the record is coherent: speech ran 26 months and closed on 17 September 2026 with two goals achieved — ‘th’ articulation at 100% in conversation, and listening & comprehension — plus diphthong reading moving from 60% to 100% with sounding-out.

Her exit note names what comes next: “focus next: spelling + reading transfer to everyday”. That is precisely what Tina Cowley and Reddam learning support are for — the reading and learning-support money is the baton handed over when speech finished, not a separate expense. So the spend was never misplaced. What is missing is the measuring: those two streams will not tell you whether the spelling and reading transfer she asked for is actually happening, and they are the only two left running.

That gives you one number to hold them to. Ask both for progress against spelling and reading transfer to everyday text, against her September baseline. If they cannot answer in those terms, the R380–R950 a month is buying attendance, not outcomes.
