Dummy 13 no.2 — white inner arms, red-yellow silk body

Donatello's second kit, split across three plates so each one runs on a single spool — no purge tower, no colour mixing, and the white job is short enough to prove the mapping before the long silk plates go on.

White — slot 2 · T1

Inner arms only: upper-arm frames, forearm frames, elbow joints.

Red-yellow silk — slot 4 · T3

Everything else: armour runner, six hands, stand adapter, and the rest of the skeleton.

Plate 1 — white inner arms

CFS slot 2 · tool T115 m 47 s1.27 g

Upper-arm frames, forearm frames and the elbow joints. The knee/elbow file is a shared 4-up, so two knees print white as well — both sit under armour and never show.

Plate 2 — armour, hands, stand adapter

CFS slot 4 · tool T33 h 53 m 31 s20.32 g

Soozafone's own armour runner, used exactly as shipped: his spacing, no supports, no brim (an 8 mm brim would weld the runner's parts together).

Plate 3 — the rest of the skeleton

CFS slot 4 · tool T359 m 45 s5.75 g

Everything the frame needs that is not an arm: chest, abdomen, hips, waist, neck, head, clavicles, shoulders, thighs, shins, ankles. Packed here with an 8 mm gap and a 5 mm brim.
