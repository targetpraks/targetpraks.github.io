Donatello's second kit: the skeleton prints white, the armour prints in the red-yellow silk. Three plates so each runs on a single spool — no purge tower, no mixed waste, and the arms plate is short enough to prove the slot mapping before the long armour run.

The entire skeleton — both arms and the whole frame.

Plate 1 — white arms

CFS slot 2 · tool T133 m 28 s2.79 g

8 pieces: two upper-arm bones, two forearm bones, and the shared 4-up that carries two elbows and two knees. Every frame file named 2x or 4x holds ONE solid — the suffix is how many copies to place, not how many are inside.

18 pieces: hips, chest, abdomen, waist, neck, head, plus 4 hip-and-shoulder, 2 clavicles, 2 thighs, 2 shins, 2 ankles. The whole skeleton is white, so only the armour carries colour.

Soozafone's own armour runner used exactly as shipped — his spacing, no supports, no brim (a brim would weld the runner's parts together). Plus all six hands and the stand adapter.
