Where it lives

The 72 applicable documents are loaded into Zoho Tables as Brand = DID rows on the Brand Register, so every one of them carries the group's owner, subject and source row. The filtered view is Divorced Dads (DID) on the Brand Register tab. The master repository is Franchise Document Repository.

One caveat: the Audience and Sub-Department columns on these rows are inherited from the source documents and still say things like "Franchisor" or "Franchisee". That is the document's original audience in the group repository, not a description of Divorced Dads. Say the word and I will relabel that column for the DID rows.
