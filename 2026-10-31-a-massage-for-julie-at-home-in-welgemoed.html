01

The short answer

one call, one appointment, in that order

Two different jobs are hiding in "the best home massage". The first is comfort: someone qualified to come to the house and work gently on a body that is still healing. The second is clinical: an assessment of what the surgery left behind, which decides what a massage may safely do. The local decision lane ran both as separate questions, and it did not pick the same provider for each.

If you want one phone call today: book Senses Mobile Spa for a 90-minute lymphatic drainage at home (R1,050), and tell them on the call what was operated on and when. If you want the answer that actually protects her recovery: book Lymph Draining Plus first for the R1,500 assessment, then use Senses for the at-home sessions afterwards if she wants them.

The call script

six questions, five minutes

Ask her own surgeon or midwife for the go-ahead first, and tell whoever you book exactly what was operated on and when. Neither of the two picks replaces that, and none of the mobile operators publishes a post-operative credential — so the therapist's own training is the thing to check on the call.

  1. Do you travel to Welgemoed, and is there a travel or after-hours surcharge?
  2. Which therapist would come, and what lymph-drainage or post-operative training does she hold — with which body?
  3. Can you work around a healing operation site, and will you stay off the abdomen and the incision?
  4. How much pressure do you use, and can the session be shortened if she has had enough?
  5. What is the earliest slot, and what is your last booking time?
  6. How do you want payment — and do you need anything from us beforehand (bed, space, parking)?

For Lymph Draining Plus, the questions are shorter: whether the R1,500 initial consultation is the right starting point, whether they bill medical aid directly, and what the earliest opening is. 067 396 8136 or their contact page.

What I could not verify

  • No mobile operator publishes a lymph-drainage or lymphoedema certification. Senses lists the treatment and says it takes post-operative clients; it does not say who is qualified to give it.
  • Review counts. Google shows 5.0 for the three mobile operators without a review count in the list view, so the sample size behind those ratings is unknown. Lymph Draining Plus publishes 34 reviews at 5.0.
  • Last-booking times. None of the mobile operators publishes one; only opening and closing hours are on their sites.
  • Melissa Saw's and Lomé Prinsloo's fees. Not published anywhere on their sites.
  • Nothing was telephoned. Every availability answer in this brief is a published claim, not a confirmed slot.
