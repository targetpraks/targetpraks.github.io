What the two codes actually were

Three names for one thing, and only one of them is the workspace's. BND1 is the sheet's name for BU 07 — ACDC Express. BND2 is its name for BU 08 — The Local Farmer. The sheet inherited the words from V3, which labels those two groups BU 7 ZOR BO 1 and BU 8 ZOR BO 2 — brand operations — and numbers its own records BND1-01 to BND2-07. PRD-A v1.1 §3.2 now records all three names against each other.

Staff, linked to how the teams are set up

39 people, 14 teams. Every person sits in at least one space and 38 of 39 in at least one team. The teams are flat — members and no parent — so ClickUp stores no reporting line; the organogram it draws is the team list plus the space each person sits in. Every handle already states the unit, the tier and the role, which is what makes ownership readable from the roster instead of from a spreadsheet.

  • expgm — EXP, GM. finhofmanager — FIN, HOF, manager. tlfzorsupport2 — TLF, ZOR, support.
  • 31 of the 46 unit-team memberships match the unit in the handle. The 15 that do not are listed one by one.
  • Owner must be a member, never a typed name. A row with no unit-matched owner stays Draft and appears on a list.

The one decision this needs

PRD §10.5 still rebuilds the field to seven functions, and makes OPS conditional on the 48 retired-code rows needing it. Measured: 0 of 48 rows name a function. So on a seven-function field all 48 arrive with no unit — exactly as they arrived with BND1/BND2 before the direction.

Field setRows strandedWhat it costs
AKeep the workspace set: BUs 01 to 12 plus NVP, 13 options.0the field is not functions-only. A venture and a function share one list, which is how the workspace is already built.
BSeven functions plus `OPS` as an eighth.48`OPS` is a new unit that exists in no ClickUp space, and it names a venture's operating arm - the exact thing `BND` was, retired one day earlier in a different costume.
CSeven functions only, as §10.5 is written.4848 rows wait on a human to name a discipline nobody recorded. The import cannot close without it.

My recommendation: A. It strands nothing, it matches the workspace the reporting has to come from, and it is the shortest reading of "like the ClickUp setup". Option B is BND under a new name; option C leaves 48 rows waiting on a human to name a discipline nobody recorded.
