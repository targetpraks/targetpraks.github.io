03 Before you use them

  • The two sets do not cover the same floors. 2006 draws a basement the 2021 set ignores; 2021 draws a second floor the 2006 set stops short of. If someone quotes "the first floor", ask which drawing they read.
  • The space plans are not as-built. Rev D is stamped "issued for comment" and every sheet carries ACDC's standing note: drawings must not be scaled, figured dimensions beat scaling, and all dimensions and levels must be checked on site.
  • The team labels are marker pen. Sales, HRM, Graphic Marketing, CTO, Design, SMM, Copy Writing, Digital Copy / Video — the desk allocations were still moving when the sheets were printed, so treat them as intent, not record.
  • Nothing newer is on file. The last plans filed in the head-office folder are dated 3 November 2021. If the layout changed after that, we hold no drawing of it.
  • Nothing here is drawn for print at this size. Open the PDF for the sheet at full resolution — the on-screen previews are for finding the right drawing.
