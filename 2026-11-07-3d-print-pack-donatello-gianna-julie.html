Before you press print

  • Printer assumed: 220 × 220 × 250 mm bed, 0.4 mm nozzle, PLA — sliced at conservative speeds (40 mm/s walls, 60 mm/s infill). If yours is faster or bigger, tell me and I re-slice in a minute; the layout is generated, not hand-placed.
  • Settings: 0.20 mm layers, 2 walls, 15% gyroid infill, automatic brim where a part needs it, no supports. Nozzle 210 °C, bed 60 °C.
  • Vase mode is already applied to the three spiral plates (Rose vase, pen cup, Greek column) — single wall, no infill, which is what those shapes are designed for and cuts them to a third of the filament.
  • Files: every plate is one merged STL with the arrangement baked in. Open the G-code and print, or open the STL to re-arrange. Individual parts live in stl/<person>/. Profiles used are in profiles/.
  • Bed fill is the share of the 208 mm usable square covered by plastic — 58% on the busy plates, lower on the vase plates because a 240 mm vase needs the height, not the floor.

Models: Printables. Downloaded 15 files, measured each mesh, packed by hand-checked bin packing.
