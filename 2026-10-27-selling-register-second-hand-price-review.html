How the prices were checked. Each line was compared to (a) the current new retail price in South Africa where the item is still sold, and (b) live used listings where it is not. The working rule for used goods is 45–65% of new retail for tools with a name, 50–70% for Apple and business IT, and a straight comparison to used listings for board games. "Can't judge" means the sheet does not state the model, size, length or whether the price is per unit or for the lot.

Two things worth knowing before anything sells. The generator and the two Titan gazebos are already committed to Pamela Fourie — the generator has no kVA on the sheet, and if it is a 6.5 kVA set then R7 500 is a third of what it is worth. And several lines price a multi-unit quantity at one price ("6 boxes @ R400", "2 boxes @ R450"): the buyer will read the lowest number they can defend.
