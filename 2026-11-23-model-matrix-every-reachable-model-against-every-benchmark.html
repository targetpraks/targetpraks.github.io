2 · The benchmark sites and my probes do not agree

AA index vs my puzzles — rank correlation ρ = 0.58 (moderate)

Moderately correlated across the 14 models where I have both numbers — the rank order roughly agrees, then misses badly model by model: It points the same way on average, then misses badly model by model: Kimi K3.1 Pro is the highest-scoring open model on the board (46) and got 3/8 on my puzzles; GLM-5.3 scores 32 there and 8/8 here; MiMo-V2.6-Pro scores 30 and got 1/8.

Neither number is "the truth". AA's index is nine evaluations weighted across knowledge work, agentic coding, science and hallucination — broad. Mine is four arithmetic-and-logic puzzles — narrow, and my own bias is baked in. Where they disagree, the task shape decides.

What that means for picking a seat

  • For your workload use AA's Terminal-Bench and FrontierCode rows, not the aggregate — that is agentic tool use, which is the job.
  • For a hard puzzle use mine: Opus, Neon, Argon and GLM-5.3 clear 8/8.
  • For a cheap bulk job neither matters; price and latency decide, and MiMo-Flash wins at $0.13 per AA task.
  • Two models scored well on both — Opus 5.5 (59 / 8/8) and Gemini 4 Neon (61 / 8/8). That is why they are the escalation seat.
