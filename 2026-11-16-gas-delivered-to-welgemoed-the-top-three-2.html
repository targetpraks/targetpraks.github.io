Gas delivered to your house — the top three

Welgemoed, Cape Town · checked Monday 16 November 2026 · refill or exchange of a cylinder you already own, VAT in

The benchmark. LPG for homes is price-capped by government, per kilogram, per zone. Cape Town is Zone 1A at 3 639 c/kg for 4 November – 1 December 2026, so the gas inside a 9 kg is capped at R327.51 and a 19 kg at R691.41. Anything above that on a delivered bill is delivery and service — which is exactly what separates the three below.

Third choice

Dial4Gas

One-man Northern Suburbs run — “you ring, we bring”

  • No delivery cost inside 15 km, which covers Welgemoed from the northern suburbs.
  • Same-day or next-day in most areas, and the driver connects the bottle and does a safety check.
  • Prices are quoted on WhatsApp, and payment is EFT before delivery — so it is a message, not a checkout.

Catch: no published price list and no card payment. Best used when you want the cheapest local total and don’t mind asking.

WhatsApp 072 344 4637

If none of those answer

Gas Partners has the cheapest gas in Cape Town — R330 for a 9 kg and R670 for a 19 kg with delivery included, both under the cap — but only inside 5 km of its Lansdowne branch, and Welgemoed is further, so it will quote an extra fee (021 692 7866 · WhatsApp 065 594 3573). Help U Gas prints Welgemoed on its delivery map, charges R40–R100, connects the bottle free, but takes 1–3 business days and publishes no refill price (072 870 5712). Gas2Home is R379 for a 9 kg and R849 for a 19 kg plus R49 delivery, with same-day time slots.
