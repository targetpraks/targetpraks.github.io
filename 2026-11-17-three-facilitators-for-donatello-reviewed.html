03 — Panashe, in full

Read from her CV and the attached letter, and corroborated independently.

The qualification is real, it is the right one, and the university confirms it

Bachelor of Education in Foundation Phase from Rhodes University, 2022, with distinction. That is the four-year, phase-specific degree for Grades R–3 — the phase the boy's reading sits in while he is placed in Grade 4. It is not a general BA plus a PGCE. And it is no longer only her word: Rhodes' own 2022 notice lists her among the six Education students nominated by the Dean for its Top 100 Academic Achievement Award.

She has already taught the classroom this job describes

At Hillview Academy, March 2023 to September 2025, she was the support teacher inside a Grade 4 assisted class of eight learners with ADD, ADHD, dyslexia and autism spectrum disorder — small-group literacy and maths interventions, plus one-on-one scaffolding across the academic day. She substituted for absent teachers at short notice, and co-ran the aftercare for 25 learners from 07:30 to 18:00.

The reference from Catherine Fourie, Head of Primary, corroborates all of it independently and in writing, and ends: “I would recommend Panashe for any support position for which she applies within a school setting.” Her number is on the letter. One detail worth knowing: Kara-Anne taught drama at the same school from January 2022 to November 2023, so the two candidates overlapped there — a useful cross-check if either referee is called.

The two certificates map onto the two things Reddam asked for

Reddam's letter names a reader and a scribe; the plan runs Barton as a daily scripted programme delivered by the facilitator. She holds a Readers and Scribers certificate from Oakley House (2024) and Search and Teach structured literacy from NILD South Africa (2024) — a paid five-day course listed at R7 850. No other candidate holds either, and only she has ever delivered a structured intervention to a group of children.

She produces a number, not an adjective

Since February 2026 she has tutored a Grade 7 learner at Curro Uitzicht one-to-one in English and maths. Her claim is specific: after the learner failed English and repeated the grade, targeted work on reading strategies, comprehension and written expression took the result to 75%.

What she does not have
  • No one-to-one in-school facilitation. Her record is group and class support plus seven months of private tutoring. Allana's is the reverse, and this is the real risk in her file.
  • No police clearance — about 15 working days, which is why 1 December is the appointment line.
  • No driver's licence or transport stated, and no named referees — the letter supplies one verifier.
  • SACE number not stated. A B.Ed makes her registrable; confirm it, because Reddam may ask.
