Three defects that block it as a working tool

It is not machine-readable

Body text is set in fonts with broken Unicode mapping. Copying a rule out of the PDF returns only page numbers and headers — verified: extracting all 155 pages yields 5,032 lines, of which every body paragraph is absent. It cannot be searched, quoted, fed to an AI, or made accessible. The page images are complete; the text layer is not.

Five copies, three editions, no owner

The manual exists as five PDFs in four folders: v2.0 twice (72.7 MB each, identical), v1.0 twice (325.5 MB each, identical) and an older June v1.0 (67.2 MB). Nothing marks which is canonical at the file level, and the largest copy — 325 MB of superseded deck — is the one sitting loose in Brand Assets.

It describes a different brand to the rest of the stack

The manual's visual system (black and white core, lime #C9F034, six logo marks, neon only on dark) contradicts both the concept wiki (navy, gold, cream, Playfair Display) and the Brand GM knowledge base (deep navy #0A0E27, Space Grotesk, glassmorphism). The manual is the newest and says it supersedes v1.0 — but no downstream document has been updated to agree.

05

The record contradicts itself

Five conflicts that will cost money or credibility if they reach a printer, a franchisee or a lender. Both sides are shown with their source; nothing is averaged or resolved here.

C1The tagline was replaced — and nobody downstream was toldHighest
Says this

Brand Manual v2.0 §1.10: “The most consequential change in this manual: Fresh. Seasonal. Quality. is no longer the primary tagline … It has been demoted to what it is genuinely excellent at: a quality seal.” The new primary tagline is LEVEL UP YOUR HUNGER., which appears with the logo lockup, on the primary fascia strapline, at the end of every film and in every social bio.

Still says that

Every logo master still carries FRESH · SEASONAL · QUALITY inside the crest. The Brand GM knowledge base lists it as a “fixed tagline”. The franchise wiki's front page headlines Fresh. Seasonal. Quality. as the brand line.

So the current logo, the current wiki and the current internal brief all carry a line the current manual has just retired. Any fascia, bag or menu printed this month will be wrong.

C2Three incompatible visual identities are all marked currentHighest
Brand Manual v2.0 (Aug 2026)

Black and white are the brand; the crest outline never changes; six logo marks stepped down by size; lime #C9F034; neon only on black; maximum three treatments per image.

Franchise Build wiki (front page)

“Primary colours: Navy #1a2b4a, Gold #d4af37, Cream #f5f5dc. Typography: Playfair Display.”

Brand GM knowledge base & Brand DNA note

“Design tokens: deep navy #0A0E27 cosmic base · Space Grotesk / Inter · glassmorphism.” The Brand DNA note also still records the palette as “proposed — verify with Creative Director”.

Three palettes, three type systems, three logo philosophies. The manual is the newest and explicitly supersedes v1.0 — but it is a PDF, and nobody has rewritten the wiki or the agent knowledge base to match it. Anyone briefing a designer from the wrong file gets a different brand.

C3The manual specifies a kiosk channel that was cancelledMedium

Part 4.8 specifies “App, web & kiosk UI” and Part 2 writes brand voice for “menus and ordering kiosks”. The franchisee bank model records that the kiosk was excluded as no longer viable on 3 September 2026, and separately notes the logo carries EST 2025 while the financial models start in January 2027 and no store has yet traded.

The manual is less than three months old and already contains a channel you have killed and a founding year you may want to reconsider before the first fascia is cut.

C4The portion standard disagrees by 37% with the recipe manualHighest
Recipe manual

A 10 kg dough batch yields 70 servings — 190 g each.

Menu and costing model

140 g standard portion, confirmed by the CFO on 3 September 2026 — about 94 servings from the same batch. The costing workbook states the 70-serving figure is “arithmetically impossible… it would need 143 g of blend per portion”.

That is a 37% swing in the pasta cost of every plate. The opening stock model was built on 120 g and is short 105.6 kg of pasta against a 140 g standard. Ricardo ruled 10.20 kg as the Farmhouse batch on 30 October — that settles the blend, not the portion.

C5Three commercial models and a withdrawn price list, all still publishedHighest

Three incompatible fee models sit in the documentation as current: zone licensing at 3%+3% (Franchise Agreement, FDD, Prospectus), per-unit at R350,000 plus 6%+2% (concept wiki Section 15), and R250,000 for five years at 4%+3% (April 2026 Prospectus). The operating models run 5.0% royalty and 1.0% levy swept monthly on a CFO instruction of 2 September 2026, while the draft agreement template still says 3.0% and 3.0% weekly.

The zone fee was restructured on 4 September 2026 from R1.5 m to R80,000 per zone, withdrawing five published tiers from R800,000 to R4,500,000 and a R142,200,000 network value. Both the Prospectus and the draft FDD still print the withdrawn schedule — a price 10 to 56 times what will actually be charged.

This is a legal and disclosure matter, not a brand one. Reported only; nothing here is changed, filed or sent.

06

Ten moves, in order

Cheapest first. The first four cost nothing but a decision and unblock everything downstream.

  1. Rule the tagline
    One line: LEVEL UP YOUR HUNGER as primary, or keep FRESH · SEASONAL · QUALITY. Until this is ruled, no fascia, bag, menu or logo file can be signed off — and every one of them is currently wrong on one side or the other.
    Ricardo
  2. Declare the canonical identity file
    Mark Brand Manual v2.0 as the single source of truth and retire the other four copies; then bring the wiki front page and the Brand GM knowledge base into line with it. Two documents to edit, and the three-identity problem ends.
    Adrianna → Brand GM
  3. Commission the six logo marks
    The manual defines six marks — Primary Crest, Simplified Crest, Field Mark, Avatar, Living Crest, Arena. The asset library has none of them: it holds a 645-variant multicolour catalogue from the previous identity whose own QA gate still has all five acceptance boxes unchecked.
    Creative Director
  4. Re-export the manual as searchable text
    Same layout, correct text layer. It makes the document quotable, searchable, accessible, and usable as the knowledge base every agent and franchisee should be reading instead of re-inventing.
    Design
  5. Build the brand presentation
    A 12–16 slide deck drawn from Parts 1–3: who we are, the manifesto, the identity system, the store, the model. This is the artefact you asked for and the one that does not exist in any format. It also becomes the landlord and franchise enquiry opener.
    Scribe / CMO
  6. Extend the concept boards to the missing surfaces
    Seven boards exist, all posters and stationery. Add boards for packaging, uniform, signage, social grid and menu boards — the five surfaces a franchisee sees before they sign.
    Creative Director
  7. Clear the price and menu decisions
    Menu boards, packaging labels and the deck's unit economics all wait on final prices (R92–R125 band), the full SKU and pack-size list, and the collectibles-versus-grab-and-go sightline call. Four decisions, six blocked deliverables.
    Ricardo
  8. One asset library, not four
    5.0 GB across Work/Papa Pasta, the vault, a 302-page wiki export and 689 loose Magnific generations. Part 6.2 defines file naming and the manual's own §1.05 asset library page is empty. Pick the home, file per 6.2, delete the duplicates.
    Beast / Adrianna
  9. Shoot real food
    All 757 images in the work tree are generated or rendered. Manual §3.20 sets photography direction and §3.21 bans untreated stock — but there is no photograph of a real plate, a real extrusion or a real crew anywhere in the brand.
    CMO
  10. Register the marks
    Name collisions are already confirmed in the US and Singapore, and the wiki's trademark and IP pages are empty placeholders. The manual has a full §6.6 trademark usage section governing a register that does not exist.
    Ricardo + Legal
