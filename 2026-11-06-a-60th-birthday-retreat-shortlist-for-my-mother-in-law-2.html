Breathe & Rewild

Struisbaai and Cape Agulhas, Western Cape · 7–13 February 2027

Seven days on the wild southern tip of Africa with Susan White and Irma Smegen: conscious breathwork, mindfulness, singing bowls, gentle movement, creativity, a symbolic stone journey on the coast and an unhurried lot of laughing. Six nights, a small group of women, a house called Driftwood by the sea.

Why it suits a 60th

  • The most genuinely spiritual of the women-only options — sound, silence, ritual and nature.

  • Booking is by personal enquiry, so you can ask exactly how gentle it is before paying.
The catch: at €2,800–3,950 it is the priciest per night of the women-only options, and accommodation is in nearby guesthouses rather than one house — ask about walking distance and stairs.

05 — Giving it well Six practical steps before anyone pays

Check the three things that decide it

Can she take 7–10 days away, and when? Will she fly, or does it need to be a drive from home? And does she want silence and ritual, or company and conversation? Those three answers narrow this to one option.

Ask the host four questions in writing

Is a single room guaranteed with no single supplement? How many women are in the group, and what is the age range? What is the walking like, and are there stairs? What exactly does the price include — meals, treatments, transfers?

Book the room, not a voucher

Most of these are small and sell out. A deposit against a named date is a better gift than a voucher she has to redeem, and it removes the admin from her. Then give her a printed card with the dates, the address and the packing list.

Protect her body

A cleanse or a detox is a dietary intervention — mention it to her doctor, especially with any medication. For the yoga options, ask whether chairs, bolsters and a gentler track are available; all of these hosts are used to beginners.

Sort the travel in the same week

Plettenberg Bay means a flight to George and a host transfer; Emoyeni and Bodhi Khaya are drives; Ixopo means Durban plus two hours. Book the travel with the retreat or it becomes her homework.

Say what it is for

The gift lands better with one sentence in the card — “for the year you put yourself first” beats “happy birthday, here is a retreat”. Every host on this list will happily write a welcome note if you ask.
