What a conveyancer needs, and what we have

Four gates. A house is sale-ready only when all four are green. Today none of the three houses clears even three.

Gate A - Legal title and council record

A deeds-search result naming the registered owner, plus the approved plan set that matches the erf. Samuel: neither is proven. Frances: plans recovered but unverified. Bushwillow: wrong erf on file.

Gate B - Compliance certificates

Electrical COC, electric fence COC and solar PV COC per house, all valid and filed. Samuel and Bushwillow have quotes approved but no certificate. Frances has no live instruction at all.

Gate C - Physical condition

The house must present and function: pools clean and running, the Frances garage working, power on at Samuel. A broken pool or garage is a price argument in the buyer's favour.

Gate D - Transfer pack

One pack per house: title deed, plans, occupancy certificate, all COCs, HOA compliance and levy clearance where applicable, rates clearance, FICA. Handed to the conveyancer with a written acknowledgement.

The one control failure worth naming. The deeds-search task for 11 Samuel Street (B11-T1586) was marked Completed on 10 September 2026 with zero comments and no recorded outcome. Two months later the ownership question is still open in writing. A card was closed without the answer it existed to produce. That pattern — closed, not evidenced — is what this plan is built to stop.
