1 · How the review was run

Three reference models — glm-5.3, kimi-k3 and minimax-m3.1-flash — were each given the real route list of this build, the live item counts for every section, and the company's constraints. Nothing was recommended from imagination: each action had to be executable against a real endpoint, and anything needing a human decision was flagged as such.

5 · Left empty on purpose

These sections are not gaps; filling them without a real source would be inventing the company's operations.

  • Workflows, triggers, apps — no event source until Zoho is connected, and no app is needed while chat, channels and tables cover the ask.
  • Remote agents, sandboxes, shared links — one company org, retrieval-only bots, and no external sharing of company knowledge.
  • Memory, shortcuts, desktop, mobile — personal settings with sane defaults; nothing company-specific to impose.
  • Inbox, work, outcomes — they fill with real staff activity and with targets you set, not with placeholder rows.

One thing worth naming

Month-to-date spend reads $0.00 across 50 recorded runs, and every run is marked partial. The runs are real — the bots answer — but the cost and completion telemetry is not being closed out. That is a platform defect to fix before the spend cap means anything, not a setup step.
