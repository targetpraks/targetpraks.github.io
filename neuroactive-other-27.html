Why the other 27 lines stayed out of the basket

Four of NeuroActive's 31 products made the cart. Here is the same treatment for the rest: what it is, what it costs, and the single reason it did not earn a place — a duplicate, a sub-trial dose, no human outcome data, or a question a doctor should answer.

Summary

  • Duplication is the biggest reason. Nine of the twenty repeat a molecule you already take or already have in this basket — magnesium, theanine, NAC, black seed oil, and lion's mane.
  • Second is the dose. Two blends and one capsule carry a third to a sixth of the amount that was actually tested, at a price that looks cheap per bottle.
  • Third is the evidence. Noopept, NMN, apigenin and shilajit have no human outcome data worth the money — one of them has no English trial at all.
  • Fourth is a test, not a bottle. TMG, TUDCA and tryptophan are only worth buying after a blood test or a doctor's steer.

02 The seven devices

The red-light range is a different purchase with a different evidence base and a different price band: a wand at R486, a 75 W portable at R2,599, the face and neck mask at R3,899, the 300 W panel at R3,899, the 1,200 W full-body panel at R9,425, plus a movable stand at R4,549 and a grounding wrist strap at R161. I left them out because none of them belongs in a supplement basket — they are a separate decision about irradiance, treatment distance and dose in joules, and they deserve their own week rather than a corner of this one.

03 If you want to add to the basket anyway

Three lines are defensible, and only one of them changes the total much:

  • Alpha GPC, R148 — correct dose, R4.93 a day, the only clean cholinergic single on the shelf. It would take the NeuroActive cart from R679 to R827.
  • L-Theanine, R122 — only as a restock if your current bottle is nearly out.
  • Ashwagandha, R149 — only if the thing you actually want help with this month is stress rather than focus.

The rule behind all twenty-seven: a supplement earns a place when it adds a molecule you do not already take, at the dose that was tested, with an outcome someone measured. Anything that fails one of those three tests is a subscription, not a purchase. The callout on this page is that rule, not a judgement about the brand — NeuroActive's labels are more honest than most in South Africa, which is exactly why the numbers can be checked this closely.

Evidence

  • NMN meta-analysis, 2026 — 15 RCTs, no effect on the metabolic endpoints.
  • Magnesium L-threonate trial, 2025 — 2 g a day, funded by the ingredient's patent holder.
  • Bacopa network meta-analysis, 2026 — the 600 mg threshold.
  • Noopept in post-stroke impairment — one of seven records in total.
  • Apigenin and sedation — the mouse study that is the entire human-adjacent record.
