How to use it

Five doors, then specialists

The programme is not sixteen equal choices. It is a spine of five doors and a set of specialists you reach for by crop and by season.

The spine. Cape Town Market for volume and price — it sets the price the rest of the city follows. Bellville Market when you cannot do 05:00. C&M for a delivered box at wholesale-adjacent prices. Citi Produce if you are feeding a kitchen and need chef-grade selection at 05:00. Wild Organics when the produce has to be certified organic.

Then by crop: Polkadraai, Mooiberge and Klondyke for the two short fruit windows (strawberries to January, cherries to late December); Southern Fruit Growers for lychees, mangoes and imported lines; Peregrine when you are on the N2 and want a farm stall that actually sells farm produce; Philippi Agri Hub for the cheapest odd lots in the city.

Honesty

What is still not verified

  • Prices behind the wholesale trade. Bellville Market's own product page returns a price of 0.00; Raw Nutrition, Gypsyco, Citi and C&M quote on order. Only the market floor and the organic shops publish numbers, which is why the Top 8 doors list carries three priced entries, not eight.
  • Gypsyco's street address is not published anywhere on its site — phone and email only.
  • Philippi Agri Hub publishes no market days or contact number; PEDI runs it and has to be called.
  • Mangoes have no ripe line on the floor as at 18 November: green atchar mango only, R10/kg in a 10 kg bag. The first Tommy Atkins should land within a fortnight; until then any mango price is a retail price, not a market one.
  • Ethical Co-op's order cut-off is quoted from an older published account of their cycle; confirm when you order.
