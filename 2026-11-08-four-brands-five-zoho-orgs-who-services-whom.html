What follows from the picture Three consequences to plan around

Channels, not orgs

One Cliq org means brand separation is expressed as channels and private groups. A person in two brands sees both sets of channels — which is the point, and the thing to police.

App cards stay home

A Projects or Desk bot speaks inside the org that owns the app. Anything from a brand into the group chat arrives by webhook into a channel, not as a native card.

Two doors to close

Offboarding is a People Plus action plus a brand-org action. Leave the second one undone and the person keeps their ACDC or Papa Pasta login after payroll has let them go.
