Section 1 — Identification

The man, and what he is charged with

Angelos Giannakopoulos (Άγγελος Γιαννακόπουλος), 57, built and ran the “Το Χωριάτικο” (Horiatiko) bakery chain. He was arrested on Sunday 25 October 2026 at Ioannina, in north-west Greece, as he drove towards the Albanian border — reportedly trying to get back to Dubai, where he had spent the preceding months. He was remanded in custody in Athens and denies the fraud charge, while accepting that tax and social-security contributions went unpaid.

  • The charge sheet: 59 people arrested and 149 defendants in total, in a file of roughly 850 pages built by the Organised Crime Directorate (ΔΑΟΕ, the “Greek FBI”) with the tax authority AADE and the anti-money-laundering authority. ProtoThema (Greek), 27 Oct bot-walled · in.gr, 30 Oct bot-walled
  • The alleged loss: €170m of suspected irregularities, and €95.56m of documented overdue debt once surcharges are added — €45.27m to the tax office and €50.28m to the social-security fund e-EFKA. ProtoThema English, 27 Oct bot-walled
  • The freezes: on 27 October the anti-money-laundering authority issued order 31/2026 — 60 pages covering 130 legal entities and dozens of named natural persons, freezing bank accounts, securities, investment products and safe-deposit boxes. ProtoThema, 27 Oct
  • Seized so far: 18 vehicles, luxury watches, jewellery and handbags; €949,658.38 in cash from a Rhodes property-management office in late August; three bank accounts in the names of three women totalling about €6m. in.gr, 30 Oct bot-walled · Ethnos bot-walled
  • Prior dealings with the law: arrested in 2008 as the alleged head of an international trafficking ring; convicted at first instance in 2015 (10 years 6 months, appeal pending); acquitted in 2018 in a separate trafficking file; fined for driving a Lamborghini at 216 km/h in November 2025. newsit.gr

The one sentence that matters for a buyer

His lawyer has said publicly that once AADE and e-EFKA issue final corrected assessments, Giannakopoulos intends to liquidate movable and immovable property to pay the State in full. That statement — not the newspaper headlines — is the door through which an outsider could realistically buy something, and it opens in years, not weeks. Ethnos bot-walled

Section 2 — The mechanism

One shop, many companies, and a debt that never follows the business

The police description is unusually mechanical, and worth reading as an operating manual rather than a scandal. The organisation — allegedly active since at least 2016 — ran 430 interlinked corporate schemes, of which 340 were used inside the mechanism, plus 32 addresses in Attica serving as shared fictitious head offices. in.gr quoting the Hellenic Police, 30 Oct bot-walled

  • The sectors were chosen for cash and volume: nightclubs, restaurants, beach bars, hotels, travel agencies, bakeries, patisseries and adult-entertainment venues. in.gr bot-walled
  • Shadow partners took 20–25% of results without appearing on any register; the “central operational core” incorporated companies, recruited the nominees and moved the money. in.gr bot-walled
  • The money did not stay in the companies: investigators describe large cash withdrawals from company accounts, interest-free intercompany loans, and a €1.2m severance payment to a member who had notionally worked a short period. in.gr bot-walled
  • Proceeds went into property and cars — real estate in Greece and abroad, luxury vehicles, financial products — and, per the 2008 trafficking file, allegedly through a Cypriot offshore vehicle into the bakery chain itself. in.gr bot-walled · newsit.gr
  • Card terminals routed abroad — Denmark, Iceland and the Cayman Islands — and funds came back as transfers, then into real estate. eKathimerini bot-walled
  • He held no directorships in the operating companies: employment records list him as a security guard, bakery worker, cashier, restaurant cook and senior executive between 2017 and 2026, and he used six phone lines registered to third parties. ProtoThema English bot-walled

Why this matters when you buy

Because the debts sit in entities that were deliberately emptied and abandoned, the trading business and the legal person are not the same thing. Buying “the bakery on the corner” buys a shell with a tax history or a lease with a landlord — the goodwill, the staff and the tills were never in the company you are offered. Every route in section 7 has to answer that problem.

Section 4 — Rhodes

Twenty-eight beaches, thirty-five bars, and a town hall that was fought over

Rhodes is the asset concentration that would interest a buyer, and the part of the case with the ugliest detail. The network is alleged to have controlled 28 beach positions and 35 beach bars and nightclubs on the island with a local partner, generating an estimated €8.7m over 2020–2025, while running up about €5.5m of overdue debt to the Greek state locally. MEGA / Live News, 29 Oct

  • Tsambika was the flagship — two companies took 33 concession contracts there in total, with unpaid fees above €2.1m. MEGA
  • The bidding method was to offer wildly above the starting price — up to €200,000, in some reports €1m — then pay only the first instalment. When the municipality chased the balance, the company went into a nominal liquidation and a new entity bid at the next tender. MEGA · eKathimerini bot-walled
  • Intimidation at the auctions: the file records teams of enforcers at the Rhodes town hall during the 29 June and 17 July 2023 tenders for 95 beach positions, with fights in the corridors and injuries treated by ambulance crews; one competitor’s site at Tsambika was vandalised. MEGA
  • The tell that broke it: every Rhodes beach company declared the same telephone numbers and the same email address — they were not independent businesses at all but branches of one office in Peristeri. MEGA
  • 14 of the 59 arrests were made on Rhodes, including a businessman described in local and national press as a very well-known local figure. He is identified publicly only by the initials Κ.Α., aged 53, and was remanded in pre-trial custody on 30 October. No outlet has named him; treat any name you see online as unverified. Skyrodos, 30 Oct bot-walled · iefimerida, 26 Oct bot-walled
  • Municipal property is entangled too. The municipal company ΔΕΡΜΑΕ — the landlord, not a suspect — asked the Rhodes prosecutor on 26 October whether the Aktaeon restaurant, the Kalithea Springs site and premises in the Valley of the Butterflies should be sealed and its leases terminated. Separately, the state property company ETAD cancelled ΔΕΡΜΑΕ’s own Kalithea concession in May 2026 after transparency-authority findings. dimokratiki.gr bot-walled · ot.gr, 26 May bot-walled

Section 7 — The buying routes

Six ways in, and what each one actually hands you

There is no route in which you buy “the business”. There are six, they deliver different things, and only one of them — the last — can plausibly close within a year.

1

Purchase of shares or assets directly from a group company whose accounts/assets are frozen

Private negotiated SPA/asset purchase, but ONLY after the specific frozen asset (or the company's shares, where the entity itself is covered by order 31/2026) is released or the freeze restricted. Any disposition of a frozen asset without lifting is void against the Greek State (art. 42(3) L.4557/2018: every transaction registered after the freeze note is 'void against the State'). Release is sought by application to the competent judicial council (Συμβούλιο Ακολουθιακών / investigator) under art. 42(4)-(6) L.4557/2018; art. 42(5) expressly permits partial lifting to enable a sale whose proceeds satisfy the injured party (here, primarily AADE and e-EFKA). Buying shares does NOT escape the problem: the buyer acquires the entity with its tax/EFKA debts, the freeze follows the entity's bank accounts, and the criminal exposure of controllers attaches. A clean asset deal with court-authorised release is the viable variant, with escrow paid to the State.

What you actually get
Either (a) shares - the whole entity including EUR-level tax/EFKA liabilities and frozen liquidity (strongly discouraged), or (b) individual movable/immovable assets with a court-authorised release, free of the freeze but still subject to due diligence on pre-existing sequestration/mortgages; the brand and beach concessions do NOT come along (see routes 4-5).
Who controls it
The investigating judge / judicial council of the Athens courts (for the criminal freeze), and the Hellenic FIU Authority (Αρχή Καταπολέμησης Νομιμοποίησης, chaired by Honorary Deputy Prosecutor) whose President issued order 31/2026; seller is technically the entity's management, but they lack free disposal power.
Legal basis
Law 4557/2018 (FEK A' 139/30.7.2018), art. 42 (seizure/freezing of assets during investigation), art. 42(4)-(6) (release/restriction by judicial council), art. 42(7) (ordering by the Authority's President, subject to CPC art. 36(2) time limits) — statute
Timeline
Application for release/restriction to the judicial council: weeks-to-months for a decision; the underlying freeze lasts at least through the investigation and is renewed until referral to trial (criminal cases of this scale: 2-5+ years). A clean deal inside the first year is possible only if the Authorities cooperate because the sale pays down State debt.
Foreign buyer
Yes - no nationality restriction on buying Greek company shares or (non-border) assets; but for real estate on Rhodes (a designated border region) non-EU buyers additionally need the Law 1892/1990 permit. AML/KYC scrutiny on source of funds will be enhanced given the case profile.
Registration needed
Greek tax number (ΑΦΜ) for the buyer; share transfer recorded (ΕΠΕ/ΙΚΕ via GEMI; ΑΕ via GEMI + securities rules); real estate transfers by notarial deed and registration at Land Registry/Cadastre; L.1892/1990 border-zone permit from the competent Decentralised Administration for non-EU buyers on Rhodes.
Risks
Void transaction against the State if done without lifting (art. 42(3)); claw-back/reversal exposure; successor-liability risk on share deals (AADE tax liability certification, EFKA clearance); criminal-taint/reputational risk; possibility of later confiscation (δήμευση, art. 40 L.4557/2018) reaching assets held at judgment; Greek criminal counsel mandatory.
2

Purchase from the insolvency trustee (σύνδικος) or liquidator (εκκαθαριστής) of a group entity in πτώχευση or εκκαθάριση

Some Bread Factory entities are already reported in liquidation (εκκαθάριση); bankruptcy (πτώχευση) petitions under the Insolvency Code L.4738/2020 are a realistic next step for over-indebted entities. The trustee/liquidator sells assets individually (real estate, equipment, movables, bundles) or the business as a going concern; sales follow adapted CPC auction rules, with auctions 30-45 days after publication of the sale notice in the Insolvency Register, starting price = average of two certified appraisals, automatic price drops to 3/4 and then 1/2 on failed auctions. Buyer deals with the trustee, not with Giannakopoulos. Interaction with the AML freeze: if the entity's assets are frozen under art. 42 L.4557/2018, the trustee cannot dispose of them without a lift/restriction from the judicial council - how the criminal freeze ranks against the bankruptcy collective is a question Greek public commentary does not settle; insolvency-to-freeze coordination is where Greek counsel is mandatory.

What you actually get
Assets free of pre-bankruptcy encumbrances to the extent the insolvency sale clears them - individual plant, ovens, fleet, shops, or whole production units; going-concern sales can carry contracts; no automatic transfer of beach concessions (personal contracts - see route 4); trademarks only if expressly included in the lot (route 5).
Who controls it
The bankruptcy trustee (σύνδικος) under the supervision of the rapporteur judge of the competent court; for voluntary liquidations (εκκαθάριση), the liquidator under shareholder/creditor oversight; secured creditors may self-liquidate collateral within 9 months.
Legal basis
Law 4738/2020 (Insolvency Code), art. 92 (bankruptcy estate incl. intangibles/trademarks), arts. 161-163 (liquidation and sale procedure, adapted e-auction rules); CPC arts. 959A ff. (e-auctions) apply analogously per art. 163 — statute
Timeline
From bankruptcy declaration to first asset auctions: typically 6-18 months; individual auctions conclude roughly 30-45 days after notice publication; failed auctions recycle at reduced prices over subsequent months.
Foreign buyer
Yes - insolvency auctions/sales are open to foreign bidders; real-estate lots on Rhodes face the L.1892/1990 border-zone permit for non-EU buyers.
Registration needed
Registration/filing of bid through the trustee as per the sale notice; eauction.gr registration where the trustee routes the sale electronically; ΑΦΜ; notarial transfer deed + Land Registry for real estate; border-zone permit for non-EU buyers on Rhodes.
Risks
Criminal freeze may block or delay the sale until lifted; preferential claims of the State (AADE/EFKA) absorb proceeds first, so the trustee has little incentive to sell cheap; employee and lease issues in going-concern sales; limited/no warranties in insolvency sales; uncertainty flagged: Greek sources are thin on L.4557/2018 freeze vs L.4738/2020 estate interaction.
3

E-auctions (πλειστηριασμοί) of seized real estate run for AADE, e-EFKA or banks

Once AADE issues final corrected assessments (the defendant's lawyer says they intend to settle once issued) and e-EFKA confirms its claims, enforced collection follows the ΚΕΔΕ (Code for Collection of Public Revenue, L.356/1974): seizure (κατάσχεση) of identified properties, then compulsory auction. Since 2017/2024 all first-instance real-estate auctions in Greece are conducted exclusively electronically on the official State platform eauction.gr, with a notary public as auction officer; AADE listings also appear on auctions.aade.gr. Bidders register on eauction.gr (Greek-AFM holders via TaxisNet; others via assisted registration, typically through a notary/lawyer), lodge a participation application and a 30% guarantee deposit on the starting price, and bid electronically. Creditor rank: State and social-security claims enjoy general privileges, so these auctions are effectively run 'for' AADE/e-EFKA even where a bank nominally seizes.

What you actually get
Full ownership of the auctioned property by award (κατακύρωση), generally free of junior encumbrances (senior mortgages survive only if prior); this is the cleanest title route in the whole case, but it delivers ONLY real estate - no shares, no brand, no operating business, no beach rights.
Who controls it
The enforcing creditor (AADE via its competent ΔΟΥ/ΚΕΒΕΙΣ, e-EFKA, or a bank/funds servicer) instructs; the auction is officiated electronically by the appointed notary auction officer on eauction.gr under CPC arts. 959A ff.; for State debts the ΚΕΔΕ procedure applies.
Legal basis
L.356/1974 ΚΕΔΕ (seizure and compulsory sale for tax/social-security debts); CPC arts. 959A-959B (electronic auctions, introduced by L.4335/2015, all-electronic since 2024); AADE public listings at auctions.aade.gr — statute
Timeline
Final assessments (corrected) -> debt certification -> seizure -> auction listing: typically 6-18 months of procedure before the first auction date; from listing to auction day commonly 2-4 months; award, price payment (within set deadlines) and transfer transcription add 2-6 months. With debts of EUR 95m+ the pipeline will run for years.
Foreign buyer
Yes - eauction.gr is open to foreign natural and legal persons (platform registration supports foreign TINs/passport-based verification, commonly completed via a Greek notary who serves as auction officer or a mandated lawyer). Rhodes is a l.1892/1990 border region: non-EU buyers need the prior permit before the transfer can complete; EU/EEA buyers are exempt.
Registration needed
eauction.gr bidder registration + per-auction participation application; Greek ΑΦΜ (issued to non-residents via the relevant tax office); 30% guarantee to the designated account; post-award: notarial/award documentation, property transfer tax, Land Registry/Hellenic Cadastre registration; L.1892/1990 permit (non-EU, Rhodes).
Risks
Auctions are repeatedly suspended/postponed in high-profile cases; debtor third-party challenges (ανακοπή) delay; properties may be occupied or tied to ongoing operations (bakeries); possible tension with the art. 42 criminal freeze (the criminal freeze generally prevails procedurally until lifted - coordinate); 30% deposit at risk during litigation over the auction; Greek enforcement counsel mandatory.
4

Beach-concession positions on Rhodes: re-tendering after the operator collapses (NOT a purchase from the group)

The ~28 beach positions and 35 beach bars/nightclubs rest on concessions of simple use of the shore/beach. These are NOT property and (as a rule) NOT transferable: L.5092/2024 art. 8(1) makes concession contracts personal (προσωποπαγείς) unless specifically provided otherwise, caps duration at 1-3 years, and art. 12 makes unauthorised transfer of use to a third party a ground for termination (καταγγελία αζημίως). Note the competence change: since L.5092/2024 the granting bodies are the State Property Services (Κτηματικές Υπηρεσίες, here the one competent for Rhodes/Dodecanese) and ETAD S.A. - municipalities were REMOVED as grantors (they now just receive 60% of the revenue). Every award must go through a mandatory electronic auction on the new Register of Simple-Use Contracts platform, and applications/awards must be completed by 31 March each year. So the realistic route: let the group's contracts lapse or be terminated (bankruptcy/dissolution of the concessionaire, or serious breach, e.g. the criminal situation), then bid in the next e-tender run by the Rhodes Property Service or ETAD. An outside buyer CANNOT buy the 28 positions from Giannakopoulos or his companies.

What you actually get
A time-limited (max 3-year) simple-use right over a delineated shore/beach section (max 500 m2 per plot, umbrella/lounger coverage capped at 60%/30% for protected sites) - a permit, not a leasehold, not ownership; the beach BAR/nightclub structures, if lawful, sit on adjacent private plots and are acquired separately (routes 1-3).
Who controls it
The competent State Property Service (Κτηματική Υπηρεσία) of the Dodecanese/Rhodes (Ministry of National Economy & Finance) or ETAD S.A. for plots under its management; the e-auction runs on the State's concession platform (art. 15 Register of Simple-Use Contracts). Municipalities of Rhodes are NOT the grantor any more.
Legal basis
Law 5092/2024 (FEK A' 33/4.3.2024), arts. 7 (granting bodies: State Property Services & ETAD), 8 (contract, 1-3 years, personal), 9 (procedures/timing), 10 (mandatory electronic auction), 12 (termination grounds incl. unauthorised transfer); L.2971/2001 survives only residually (works/port-zone grants) — statute
Timeline
Annual cycle: tenders published and awards/contracts to be completed by 31 March of each year for the season; if the incumbent's contracts are terminated mid-year due to collapse, re-tender typically follows in the next cycle (i.e. up to ~12 months wait, unless an ad-hoc re-run is proclaimed).
Foreign buyer
Generally yes - tender terms (διακήρυξη) define eligible bidders; Greek and EU nationals and companies participate routinely; a non-EU bidder should expect to bid via a Greek company. The border-zone rule (L.1892/1990) concerns real-estate OWNERSHIP, not a use-concession, but adjacent-property acquisitions on Rhodes by non-EU buyers still need the permit.
Registration needed
Registration on the concession e-auction platform per the tender notice; Greek ΑΦΜ; for a legal-person bidder, incorporation of a Greek entity (e.g. ΙΚΕ) with GEMI registration (businessportal.gr); operating licences for the beach-bar activity from the competent authorities.
Risks
No automatic succession - a buyer of the group company does NOT get the concessions (personal contracts; art. 12(1)(c) voids unauthorised transfer); concession terms can tighten (protected/untrodden beach designations under arts. 4, 22); termination for breach; winning bids in Rhodes' prime spots are competitive; criminal notoriety of the specific sites may prompt stricter grant terms.
5

Acquisition of the brand/IP ('Χωριάτικο', Bread Factory marks) out of an insolvent chain

Trademarks, trade names, logos, recipes-as-trade-secrets, domain names and franchise know-how are intangible assets that fall into the bankruptcy estate of whichever group entity actually owns them (L.4738/2020 art. 92(1) expressly includes trademarks), or are sold by the liquidator in a voluntary εκκαθάριση. First step is a register search at OBI (Οργανισμός Βιομηχανικής Ιδιοκτησίας, obi.gr) and EUIPO TMview/eSearch to identify the actual registrant of each 'Χωριάτικο'/'Bread Factory' mark - it may be an entity, or Giannakopoulos personally (in which case the criminal freeze and any later confiscation control it). Deal structures: (a) buy the marks from the trustee/liquidator as a defined lot; (b) buy the marks at an AADE/bank e-auction if seized and auctioned; (c) wait for lapse/non-renewal and re-file (slow, uncertain). Assignment must be recorded with OBI to be effective against third parties. The reported Switzgroup interest in Bread Factory shows this brand-without-entity logic is already the market's approach.

What you actually get
The registered marks, domains and goodwill only - no shops, no leases, no concessions, no staff, no recipes unless documented and assigned; brand value here is real (the bakeries keep trading per press) but must be re-paired with sites obtained via routes 3-4.
Who controls it
The owner entity's trustee/liquidator under L.4738/2020 (or, while solvent-and-frozen, the entity subject only to a court-authorised release under art. 42(5) L.4557/2018); recordal authority: OBI for national Greek marks, EUIPO for EUTMs.
Legal basis
L.4738/2020 art. 92(1) (trademarks in the estate) and arts. 161-163 (sale procedure); Greek trademark law L.4679/2020 (assignment and recordal at OBI); if owner is the natural person and assets stay frozen: L.4557/2018 art. 42 and art. 40 (confiscation risk) — statute
Timeline
6-18 months once an insolvency/liquidation is open and the trustee markets the IP; faster (weeks) if a frozen-but-solvent owner obtains a court-authorised carve-out sale to pay the State.
Foreign buyer
Yes - no restriction on foreign ownership of Greek/EU trademarks; professional representation before OBI requires an authorised Greek trademark attorney for non-domestic applicants.
Registration needed
Greek ΑΦΜ for the transaction; assignment agreement; recordal of the transfer at OBI (and EUIPO for EUTMs); franchise-contract novations where applicable.
Risks
Registrant uncertainty (entity vs personal ownership) changes the controlling regime; confiscation (art. 40) on conviction can reach personally-held marks; marks may be encumbered/licensed to franchisees (Χωριάτικο franchise agreements may contain change-of-ownership clauses); reputational contamination of the brand post-criminal-trial; verify renewals/use to avoid cancellation.
6

Voluntary sale by the defendant/his companies to settle State debts (the lawyer's declared plan)

The defence strategy already announced publicly: accept responsibility for the tax/contribution evasion, and liquidate movable and immovable property once AADE and e-EFKA issue FINAL CORRECTED assessments, to pay the State in full. Because the assets are frozen, this can only happen with the Authorities' cooperation - but the law builds exactly that bridge: art. 42(5) L.4557/2018 permits lifting or restricting the freeze 'in order that the injured party be satisfied' («προκειμένου να ικανοποιηθεί ο ζημιωθείς»), and the State (AADE/e-EFKA) is the injured party here. The realistic deal: an outside buyer negotiates with the group (or the trustee where insolvency has opened), the seller applies to the judicial council/investigator for asset-specific release against payment of proceeds directly to AADE/e-EFKA escrow accounts, and the sale completes. This overlaps with route 1 but is State-friendly: the Authorities get cash without running auctions, so cooperation is plausible - though never guaranteed, and a later confiscation judgment can still reach unsold assets.

What you actually get
Negotiated cherry-picked assets (often better quality than auction residue) with a court-stamped release - movable fleets, bakery units, private plots, possibly going-concern bakery branches; again NOT the beach concessions (route 4) and NOT automatically the marks (route 5).
Who controls it
Formally the seller (the group entity or Giannakopoulos personally); functionally gated by the investigating judge/judicial council and the FIU Authority (release under art. 42(4)-(7)), and by AADE/e-EFKA issuing the final assessments that define the payoff figure.
Legal basis
L.4557/2018 art. 42(5) (release to satisfy the injured party), art. 42(2)-(3) (voidness without lift); collection side: AADE assessments under the Code of Tax Procedure L.4987/2022 and ΚΕΔΕ L.356/1974; e-EFKA collection under its ΚΕΔΕ-equivalent powers — statute
Timeline
Entirely contingent: corrected assessments of EUR-scale across 130 entities realistically take 1-3 years; individual asset releases + sales can interleave once an assessment for that entity is final. Do not underwrite any closing inside 12 months.
Foreign buyer
Yes - same rules as route 1; enhanced source-of-funds documentation will be demanded by all sides given the AML context; non-EU buyers need the L.1892/1990 permit for Rhodes real estate.
Registration needed
ΑΦΜ; notarial deeds + Land Registry for real estate; GEMI filings for any entity share transfers; per-asset judicial council release orders appended to the deeds; border-zone permit for non-EU buyers on Rhodes.
Risks
Political/judicial willingness to release assets for a defendant who may still face confiscation is unpredictable; price discipline (courts will resist undervalue sales); the gap between EUR 95.56m-with-surcharges and >=EUR 120m estimates means the asset pool may be fully absorbed by the State, leaving nothing negotiable; any payment routing must avoid money-laundering characterisation - funds to State-designated accounts only; Greek criminal + tax counsel mandatory, non-optional.

The single most important sentence on this page

The 28 Rhodes beach positions cannot be bought. Under law 5092/2024 a beach concession is a personal, time-limited right (maximum three years), granted only through a mandatory electronic auction, and transferring it to a third party is itself a ground for termination. You cannot inherit them by buying a company, and you cannot buy them from Giannakopoulos. You can only win them in the next tender, after his contracts lapse or are terminated — from the state property service or ETAD, not from the municipality, which lost that competence in 2024. Section 8 — The call

What I would do, and what I would refuse to touch

Read straight: this is not a distressed-acquisition opportunity in the ordinary sense. It is a criminal asset pool, and the useful work in the next twelve months is watching and qualifying, not negotiating. Concretely:

Position

  1. Do not approach any party. Any contact with the defendant, his family, the local partner or the operating companies before the freeze is lifted is a reputational and legal liability, and in a file already alleging bribery of officials, a foreign buyer appearing at the door is exactly the story nobody wants written about them.
  2. Treat Rhodes as a tender play, not a purchase. The asset you can actually get is a 3-year concession won at open e-auction in 2027 or after. Watch the state property service and ETAD tender notices, budget for the fact that the prime spots will be contested by locals who have been waiting years for precisely this, and price the licence — not the site.
  3. The bakery brands are the only near-term asset with a market. The reported interest by the Switzgroup/Koulourades side in Bread Factory shows how this is done: buy the mark and the network logic, leave the entities. Before anything else, find out who actually owns the “Χωριάτικο” and “Bread Factory” trademarks — OBI and TMview will say. If they sit with Giannakopoulos personally rather than a company, they are inside the freeze and the confiscation risk, and the answer is no.
  4. Real estate comes through the auction platform, later. AADE and e-EFKA enforced sales run on eauction.gr with a notary as auction officer and a 30% deposit; they are the cleanest title route in the whole case and they deliver nothing but the property. The pipeline realistically starts once the corrected assessments are final — one to three years out.
  5. Your nationality is a live constraint on Rhodes. The Dodecanese is a border region under law 1892/1990: a non-EU buyer needs a prior permit before acquiring real estate there. That applies to the person, not the concession — but it is the first question Greek counsel will ask.
  6. Engage counsel before engaging anyone else. Greek criminal counsel for the freeze interplay, insolvency counsel for anything out of liquidation, and a notary. Three mandated entry points, and there is no DIY path through any of them.

Red flags that would end a deal on the spot

  • A share purchase of any frozen entity. You inherit the tax and EFKA debt, the frozen accounts and the criminal exposure. There is no version of this that is cheap enough.
  • Any disposal attempted without a release. Every transaction registered after the freeze note is void against the Greek State — the money leaves and the asset stays his. L.4557/2018 art. 42
  • The “Horiatiko” name itself. It carries trafficking allegations from the 2008 file, a remand, and eleven years of press about shell companies. A bakery brand is worth what a customer thinks when they read it on a bag.
  • Anything promised verbally by an intermediary. In a case alleging €949,658 in cash found in an office and monthly bribes to a police officer, the intermediary is the risk.

Due-diligence tools to run yourself, all public: GEMI company search for status and filings · eauction.gr for every state and bank property auction · OBI for the trademarks · AADE tax clearance and EFKA insurance clearance certificates demanded in the SPA, since there is no public register of a third party’s tax debt.
