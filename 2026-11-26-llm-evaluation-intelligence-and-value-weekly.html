Why this replaced the flat-lane assumption. Ollama Cloud bills per token against a monthly credit pool, so a model that writes three times the tokens for the same answer costs three times as much to run — a gap the per-million rate card hides. The spread between the cheapest and dearest model on the identical three tasks is over 200×, and no two of them are doing 200× different work.
