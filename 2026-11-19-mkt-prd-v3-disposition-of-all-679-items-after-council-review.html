The date rule, including items with no date

A space automation rewrites the due date of every new task to the following Monday within five seconds. So: create without dates, then apply the real start and due in a second pass, then explicitly clear the due date on the 244 placeable items that have none in Zoho - otherwise they arrive wearing an invented deadline. A post-pass audit compares every item's date to its Zoho value and reports mismatches by name.

9 Concurrency, rollback, ownership

  • One writer. A second thread worked this space concurrently today and deleted seven lanes mid-run. This plan requires a declared freeze on folder, list and field changes for the duration, and one named operator.
