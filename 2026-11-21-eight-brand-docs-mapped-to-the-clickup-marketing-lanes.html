Three questions, and I am not guessing on them again.
1. FND - the ACDC Express, Local Farmer and Papa Pasta foundation docs have no FND lane. Do they hang off the brand's existing ZOR lane, or is FND a fifth tier that the space has not got yet?
2. CLI / King Protea - KPD work sits in MKT COR - DID today because that is the lane you approved. Is the CLI doc meant to point there too, or does KPD belong somewhere else?
3. Divorced Dads - the COR doc has no lane of its own either; the only candidate is the same MKT COR - DID. Is DID the Divorced Dads lane, the King Protea lane, or neither?

4 How the link can be made real

A matrix on paper is not a link. Once you answer the three questions above, the link itself can be made without a single new folder or list, in one of two ways - your call:

  • As a doc task in each lane's existing Briefed-In list: one task per doc, carrying the URL, so it appears in the lane's own list and can be opened from ClickUp.
  • As a lane bookmark: the URL attached to the lane's folder description on the Overview page, visible before anyone opens a list.
