2. The shape of the three weeks

Twenty-one days is long enough that it needs rhythm, not a bucket list. Three weeks, three jobs: land, go big, then slow down into Christmas.
The daily rhythm that makes this work with a newborn in the house: up 07:00 · breakfast 08:00 · out of the house 08:30, home by 11:30 · lunch 12:00 · quiet hour 12:30–14:00 (baby sleeps, screens off, books) · pool or garden 15:00 · supper 18:00 · bath 19:00 · story 19:30. For Donnie: hard physical play before 10:00 and again at 16:00, and the day drawn on the wall each morning — he does far better when he can see what comes next. Screens only after 16:30.
Week Dates Job of the week Load
Week 1 Thu 3 – Sun 6 Dec Land. Sleep, swim, one easy outing a day, the holiday wall-planner up by Friday night. Light — the baby is still 10 weeks old
Week 2 Mon 7 – Sun 13 Dec The big Cape Town week: aquarium, cableway, penguins, treetops. Early starts, home by mid-afternoon. Heavy — two big days back to back, then a home day
Week 3 Mon 14 – Sun 20 Dec Slow and festive: ice rink, Christmas crafts, the tree, the family lunch. No long drives. Light
Finish Mon 21 – Wed 23 Dec Present shopping with the kids, your own Christmas morning on Tue 22, then pack and hand over. Medium

5. The bench — for wind, rain and 38 °C

Keep this list on the fridge. Any day can be swapped for one of these without losing the week.
  • Cape Town Science Centre — Observatory, indoors, R95 pp, closed Sundays.
  • The Ice Station, GrandWest — 20 minutes away, indoor ice, public sessions all day.
  • Ster-Kinekor Tygervalley — the 10:00 school-holiday showing, then lunch in the centre.
  • Iziko South African Museum — the whale skeleton and the dinosaur hall; a proper rainy-day hour.
  • Durbanville library — free, cool, and both children read when there is nothing else to do.
  • Home cinema afternoon — blanket, popcorn, one film, phones in a drawer. Budget one of these a week and stop feeling guilty about it.

8. Four things only you can answer

I have planned on the obvious assumptions. Any of these changes the shape, not the dates.
  • Is Julie in the whole three weeks, or is this dad-and-kids time with her joining some days? The plan assumes she and Sofia are at home and come on the easy outings — which is why nothing is more than 75 minutes away.
  • What time is the handover on 23 Dec, and who drives? The calendar records the day only.
  • Do you want the one away break? Three nights in the Winelands is the only version worth doing with a 10-week-old. The gate said no overnight at all (0.78) — you can override that in one line.
  • Is about R10,600 for the three weeks the right number? Say a lower figure and I will re-cut the outings to fit it.
