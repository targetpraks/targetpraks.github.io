Cliq and Connect for everyone — Infinity's Zoho One, or the People Plus org?

Group identity · where the one room sits
The call

Put everyone in the People Plus organisation. Infinity's Zoho One keeps the apps; it cannot hold the people.

Five brands, one payroll of ~40, and five separate Zoho One organisations — ACDC Express now building its own simpler one. Prices from Zoho's own pages, read 8 Nov 2026, ZAR ex VAT.

Why the obvious answer fails

Three rules Zoho enforces, and what they mean here

One email, one Cliq organisation A Zoho account already registered to a business Cliq org cannot join or create a second one. Networks are the only bridge, and they carry no company directory, no guest chat and no Zoho app cards. Zoho Cliq docs
One Zoho account, one Zoho One org The same account cannot be a user under two Zoho One accounts. So a shared person can be a full citizen of exactly one brand's One org — the rest see them as an outsider. Zoho One thread
Which means the comms home decides the licences If Cliq and Connect live inside Infinity's One org, every shared person needs a second One seat just to chat. That is the per-brand One split showing its true cost.

The call

Three moves, in this order

1 · Make People Plus the company

One People Plus Enterprise org holds all five brands: People as multi-entity for HR, Cliq as the company chat, Connect as the intranet everyone reads. Everyone gets one seat there — 40 seats, R5 040 a month.

2 · Strip Cliq and Connect out of the brand orgs

Each brand's Zoho One keeps Books, CRM, Projects and its own data — ACDC Express keeps a small, single-brand instance with nothing in it but ACDC work. Cliq and Connect stop being provisioned in any of them, so nobody is half-present in five chats.

3 · One identity per person

Give every person a single group address for People Plus — name@infinitybrands.co.za — and let brand addresses stay for brand systems. One directory, one invite, one offboarding.

If the group ever consolidates to a single Zoho One org — which the August architecture paper recommended, and which removes the duplication entirely — this flips: Cliq Enterprise then comes free inside the one seat, and People Plus is no longer needed for comms. Until that migration happens, People Plus is the only org that can hold all forty at a sane price.

Before signing

Two things to get in writing from Zoho

Does People Plus Cliq scale past 25?People Plus bundles Cliq Standard, and the standalone Standard SKU is sold as a flat block for up to 25 users. Confirm in writing that the suite version follows your People Plus user count, not that 25 cap.
Franchisees on ConnectPeople Plus Enterprise ships Connect's internal network. Franchisees and outside partners need the external-user add-on, sold in blocks of five. Price that before the ACDC franchisees are pointed at Connect.
One person, two subscriptionsConfirm a Zoho account holding a brand Zoho One seat can also hold a People Plus seat. The rule Zoho publishes bans two One orgs on one account — not a One seat plus a non-One subscription. Get it on paper, because if they refuse, each person needs a second address.
