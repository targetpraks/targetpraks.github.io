Self-contained, in a three-storey building with a lift, basement and front reception. Measured off the architect's 1:75 plans and accurate to about 2%. 03 How to send it

  • Send from ricardom@infinitybrands.co.za so replies land on the owner's desk, not an agent's.
  • Seven go by email — Anvil, Galetti, Broll, Spire, Firzt, Rawson and Corporate Real Estate. Renprop publishes no address, so it is a phone call to 011 463 6161.
  • Follow up once, after a week. Brokers move on requirement, not on reminder — one chase is enough.
  • Log the replies against the suite so the tenant pipeline is visible: firm, tenant profile, area required, next step.
