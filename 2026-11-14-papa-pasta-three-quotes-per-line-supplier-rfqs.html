Where the drafts are

All 33 sit in the Drafts folder of ricardom@infinitybrands.co.za in Zoho Mail. Nothing has been sent. Open Drafts, read one or two, then send them in a batch.

What each email asks for. Unit price excluding VAT, the pack size that price is based on, minimum order quantity, lead time, and price validity. Delivery is stated as Cape Town, and halaal certification is requested where it applies. Every line carries its SKU code so replies map straight back into the model.

The 21 lines that will still be one short

Beverages — 16 lines. Post-mix syrup, CO2 and the water lines. Coca-Cola Beverages and PepsiCo run post-mix in South Africa through account managers, not public inboxes, and Afrox prices CO2 by phone. Clearworld holds S.Pellegrino and Acqua Panna but publishes no address. These need three phone calls, not three emails.

Mushroom powders — 4 lines. Edulis and Mush Love are in. The third powder supplier needs finding: most SA growers sell fresh only, and the freeze-dried powder is an import line.

Prawns — 1 line. Lumar Foods is halaal-certified but has no published address. One call.
