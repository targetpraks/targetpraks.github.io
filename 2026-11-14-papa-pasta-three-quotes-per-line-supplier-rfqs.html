How the gap was closed

Beverages were the whole problem — sixteen lines on post-mix syrup, CO2 and water. One category hid three different supply chains, so I split it and solved each one:

Syrups. Boom South Africa, VDP Beverage Solutions and Sucromix in the Republic of South Africa — VDP carries the DaVinci and Beverage Chefs ranges, Sucromix supplies the liquid sugar and invert base.
CO2. Igesi Gas are an Afrox agent with a published address, plus Supragas and Sino-Africa Gases.
Water. AquaZen, Letgo Water and Caterlink SA. Caterlink is the useful accident: a broadline foodservice house that also carries premium imported water, so it quotes sixteen lines across water and dry goods.

Mushroom powders. Dolce Superfoods sells bulk medicinal mushroom powder off the shelf in South Africa, which is the third quote the line was missing.

Prawns and halaal meat. Southern Cross Seafood and Forsmay Muslim Butchery — both publish addresses, both already halaal-positioned.

How the three-per-category split works

Every SKU was assigned to one category and every category got three suppliers, so each line reaches three inboxes and each supplier gets one tidy list rather than a scatter of emails.
