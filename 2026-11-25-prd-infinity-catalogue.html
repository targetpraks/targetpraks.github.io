1. Problem and vision

1.1 The request, in the owner's words

"Goal is to build out a PRD for this catalogue. It needs to include absolutely everything for ACDC Express, Local Farmer, Papa Pasta, Infinity Brands. It needs to be able to toggle what is or needs to see what is always… always a product that has to be in stock, and it should show the minimums and maximums of that product. And then also, it should allow a manager to sign off on, maybe loading new products. So, when a new product comes in, it's got a section says 'Newly added', so that we can then sign off on the artwork, and that can be done in each kind of section. I need all of Infinity Brands team members to have access to this. So it needs to be hosted on a link that everyone will have access to… ClickUp login or Zoho login to the application so that you can sort out any form of authentication."

1.3 Vision

One link. Every product the group sells or gives away, with its photo, its price, its stock and its limits. Every new product lands in a queue that a named approver must clear before staff see it. Zoho Inventory stays the single source of the item, and this application is where people decide about it.

4. Source of truth and data model

For the business: Zoho stays the one place a product is described. The app stores only decisions (approvals, who may approve, the audit) and a fast copy of Zoho for search.

4.1 The rule

Zoho Inventory owns the item: name, SKU, brand, category, price, photo, stock, the minimum (native reorder_level) and, by the MoA's ruling, the stock policy, maximum and lead time as item custom fields. The app owns approvals, brand approvers, roles and the audit, plus a read mirror. Zoho always wins, except over an app write still in flight (§4.5). Everything is keyed on Zoho item_id, never on SKU, so a rename never breaks history.

4.3 The snapshot hash

The static catalogue already proves the pattern (b3d648ba…): a SHA-256 over a canonical payload, stored per approval, so what was approved stays provable after the item changes.

  • Payload: sorted-key, whitespace-free UTF-8 JSON of item_id, sku, name, description, brand, category_code, unit, rate (2-decimal string) and image_sha256.
  • Artwork is hashed by its bytes, not by image id and length as in 1.0, so the hash proves which picture was approved, as the locked rule requires.
  • Excluded: stock, min, max, policy, lead time, purchase price and status. They change daily or are decided separately. The print edition keeps its own hash.

4.4 State machine

Zoho item appears ──► NEW (Newly added; hidden from staff)
             ┌──────────┴──────────┐
             ▼                     ▼
         APPROVED               REJECTED (hidden, reason kept)
      (visible to staff)           └─ item edited in Zoho (hash changes) ──► NEW
             └─ hash changes in Zoho ──► stays APPROVED, "changed since approval", re-queued
any state ──► RETIRED when Zoho status is inactive (hidden from staff)
RETIRED ──► APPROVED on reactivation if hash = last_approved_hash, else NEW

Rules: (1) No artwork, no approval (R5). (2) An item with no stock policy may be approved but stays flagged "Policy not set"; 0 of 729 carry one today, and blocking would stall the catalogue. (3) Reject is the send-back: the fix is made in Zoho, and the new hash returns the item to NEW. (4) A changed approved item stays live with current Zoho values, so a price change never blanks the catalogue (Q14 covers artwork). (5) Retiring never deletes history.

4.5 Concurrency rules

For the business: when two people act on one item at once, exactly one action counts, and the other person is told who was first.

Every mutable row has a version. Writes are conditional, and a stale write returns 409 with who and when.

Case Rule
Two approvers decide one entry First commit wins; the second gets 409 "Approved by ‹name› at ‹time›"
Item changed in Zoho after the drawer opened Approve carries the hash shown; a mismatch returns 409 stale_snapshot and reloads
Policy flipped during an approval Independent (policy is outside the hash); both succeed, both audited
Two flips of one item, or a bulk job meeting an edited item Version check: the loser gets 409; a bulk job skips and lists the item
Approver revoked mid-session Authority is checked at commit, so the next action returns 403
Sync meets a pending app write Sync leaves the field alone unless Zoho's last_modified_time is newer; then Zoho wins, audited superseded_by_zoho
Policy edited directly in Zoho Applied by the sync, audited as zoho-external (Zoho does not say who)

4.6 Audit immutability

For the business: nobody, including the app itself, can quietly alter a past approval, and tampering is detected overnight.

approval and audit_event are insert-only: the app's role has INSERT and SELECT only, and a trigger rejects UPDATE and DELETE. Rows form a chain, row_hash = SHA-256(prev_hash ‖ row), which a nightly verifier walks, alerting on any break and storing the head hash in SyncRun and the dump. Mistakes are corrected with a new row, never an edit.

5. Features

5.1 P0 — the app that ships first

Every criterion is a test (§12) that can fail. "Approver" means an approver for the item's brand, or Ricardo.

F1 — Single sign-on through Zoho.

  • AC1.1 A first-time team member with a Zoho login reaches Home in ≤ 3 clicks, with no registration.
  • AC1.2 With no session, any page, image or /api/* route returns a sign-in redirect (pages) or 401 (API), with no item data.
  • AC1.3 Sign-out clears the app cookie and the Access session; the next request asks for sign-in.
  • AC1.4 A new identity becomes Staff and sees approved items only.
  • AC1.5 By Stage 1 exit, the audit holds sign_in events from 39 distinct identities.

F2 — Brand and section browsing.

  • AC2.1 After a sync, the mirrored count equals Zoho's live in-scope count (729 at baseline), and each item sits in exactly one section (holding counted and flagged).
  • AC2.2 Home shows four brand cards counting approved, active items, plus a pending count for approvers.
  • AC2.3 Each card shows photo (or "No photo"), name, SKU, price, policy pill, min/max or lead time, and stock.

F3 — Search and filter chips. Chips: All · Must be in stock · Made to order · Policy not set, plus Stock health and Status (approvers).

  • AC3.1 A full SKU (for example INF-STA-DESKPAD-A3) returns that item first; server p95 < 300 ms over 100 runs.
  • AC3.2 Search is a case-insensitive substring match on name and SKU, limited to what the user may see.
  • AC3.3 A reload, or the same URL opened by another user, gives the same filter, within that user's permissions.
  • AC3.4 Made-to-order and not-set items never appear under a Stock health chip.

F4 — Item detail. Photo, description, SKU, brand, section, variant, unit, price with VAT label (§11.4), pill, stock, min and max (or lead time), approval history.

  • AC4.1 For Staff, purchase price is absent from both the HTML and the API response.
  • AC4.2 Missing limits read "Not set", never 0. AC4.3 A made-to-order item shows its lead time and no minimum field.

F5 — Newly added queue per brand and section.

  • AC5.1 After the next sync, a new in-scope Zoho item is NEW in its section's queue, the brand's pending count rises, and staff see nothing.
  • AC5.2 Ricardo's /new lists all four brands; any other approver's only their brands.
  • AC5.3 Each row shows the thumbnail, the date it appeared, the fields to check (name, category, price, unit) and the brand default policy.

F6 — Approve / reject with artwork: one click, per brand.

  • AC6.1 With the image loaded and the hash unchanged, one click sets APPROVED, writes an Approval row (actor, role, time, snapshot_hash), and staff see the item within ≤ 2 s. No confirm dialog, no e-signature.
  • AC6.2 With no image, Approve is disabled with the reason, and the API returns 422 artwork_missing.
  • AC6.3 A rejection comment under 10 characters returns 422; with 10 or more, the entry becomes REJECTED, hidden from staff, reason kept.
  • AC6.4 A TLF-only approver approving an ACDC item gets 403, and no row is written.
  • AC6.5 Two concurrent approvals leave exactly one Approval row; the other approver gets 409. AC6.6 An item changed in Zoho since the drawer opened returns 409 stale_snapshot.

F7 — Roles, brand authority and scoping.

  • AC7.1 The §2.1 matrix runs as an API test: every ✗ returns 403 (404 for staff reads of unapproved items) and writes nothing.
  • AC7.2 A grant from Settings writes a BrandApprover row and an AuditEvent, and the grantee can act on their next request.
  • AC7.3 A revocation applies on the next request, and revoking Ricardo is refused. AC7.4 A brand with only Ricardo shows a warning.

F8 — Sync with visible health.

  • AC8.1 The nightly run (02:00 SAST) and each admin refresh write a SyncRun row with counts, calls and errors.
  • AC8.2 On a Zoho 429 or 5xx, the last good data keeps serving under "Last synced ‹time›", the error is recorded, and the admin is alerted.
  • AC8.3 A sync with no image changes makes 0 image calls. AC8.4 The call log shows ≤ 1 Zoho request/s overall and ≤ 0.5 writes/s.

F9 — Audit trail.

  • AC9.1 Every approval, rejection, role change, policy or limit change, brand-default change and bulk job writes one AuditEvent per affected item, with before and after.
  • AC9.2 UPDATE or DELETE on approval or audit_event through the app's role fails. AC9.3 A hand-edited row in a scratch copy is reported by the verifier's next run.
  • AC9.4 The admin CSV export for a date range matches the table's row count for that range.

F10 — Stock policy: the on/off switch.

  • AC10.1 ON with min ≥ 1 and max ≥ min writes, and reads back from Zoho, Stock Policy = Must be in stock, reorder_level = min and Maximum Level = max; the pill turns green, with one AuditEvent.
  • AC10.2 ON without a min or a max is refused in a plain sentence, and nothing reaches Zoho. AC10.3 A max below the min is refused: "Maximum must be at least the minimum".
  • AC10.4 OFF requires a lead time of ≥ 1 whole day, clears min and max in Zoho (old values kept in the audit to pre-fill a later ON), and turns the pill amber.
  • AC10.5 A failed Zoho write shows "Pending Zoho", is retried (§10.1), is never shown as confirmed, and alerts the admin on final failure.
  • AC10.6 A bulk set on N items runs as a background job with progress: N Zoho calls, N audit rows, failures listed, undo available.
  • AC10.7 Every card and drawer shows exactly one of three pills (§8), each with an icon and a word. A policy cannot go back to "Not set".

7. Flows

7.1 Sign-in

Zoho login → Cloudflare Access → app. The app maps the verified email to a UserRole. A new identity lands as Staff (read-only) and appears in Settings for an admin to grant a brand. No one is locked out by a missing role.

7.2 Browse and find

Home → brand → section → card. Or type a SKU anywhere and press Enter. Filters live in the URL, so an approver can send "ACDC Uniform, must be in stock, below min" as a link.

7.3 A new product arrives

  1. Someone creates an item in Zoho with a photo, as products are loaded today.
  2. The next sync creates a NEW CatalogueEntry in Newly added for its section.
  3. An approver for that brand, or Ricardo, opens it: artwork large, fields beside it, a "check these" list (name, category, price, unit) and the brand default policy.
  4. Approve, one click: APPROVED, hash recorded, visible to staff. An item with no policy gets the brand default written to Zoho, audited as brand-default. Reject needs a comment and sets REJECTED.
  5. A later Zoho edit follows §4.4 rule 4 and never silently replaces what was signed off.

7.4 Setting the stock policy — the switch

Switch Meaning Required on save Written to Zoho
ON — Must be in stock Forced stocked line; green pill; stock health applies Min ≥ 1, max ≥ min, whole numbers (ASSUMPTION: 727 of 729 in-scope items count in pcs or box) Stock Policy = Must be in stock, reorder_level = min, Maximum Level = max
OFF — Made to order Made when ordered; amber pill; no minimum Lead time, whole days ≥ 1 Stock Policy = Made to order, Lead Time Days; min and max cleared

Saving is one click, audited and written to Zoho in the same request ("Pending Zoho" if Zoho is slow). Bulk: select a section or brand, choose the state, apply. Bulk ON needs min and max for every item (per row or one pair for all) and refuses while any is missing; each item still gets its own audit row and paced Zoho call. Brand defaults carry the mode plus the values it requires, and apply when a new item is approved (§7.3).

7.5 Rejection and re-submission

A rejected item stays in its brand's queue with the reason. Once it is fixed in Zoho (new artwork or fields), the changed hash returns it to NEW, and the approver sees the new image beside the rejected one.

7.6 Deciding who approves a brand

Settings → Brand approvers → pick a brand → search by name or email → grant. Revoking uses the same row. Ricardo holds every brand and cannot be revoked. A brand with no approver but Ricardo shows a warning, so "nobody else can approve this" is visible rather than discovered on a Friday.

The people list comes from Zoho sign-in: with no Directory scope (§9.1), the picker offers every identity that has signed in once, which is all 39 after Stage 1 (AC1.5; Q13).

Credential: a dedicated server-side Zoho OAuth client with item read, item update and settings read scopes (names confirmed in the contract test).

Measured throttle: Zoho rate-limits the whole organisation at roughly 100 requests a minute (429 code 43). A 618-PUT burst on 2026-11-25 cost about half an hour, and the image endpoint blocks for 15+ minutes after a burst.

  • Pacing: one global limiter at ≤ 1 request/s (about 60 a minute, leaving headroom for other integrations) and ≤ 0.5 writes/s, the rate the item-master writer settled on after the lockout.
  • Backoff: on a 429, all calls pause 30 s × attempt (as scripts/rename_skus.py does) for up to 5 attempts (ASSUMPTION), then the write is failed and the admin is alerted. An image 429 suspends image fetches for 15 minutes. Writes are idempotent and confirmed by read-back.
  • Derived load: first import 729 detail reads (about 12 min) plus 637 images (about 11 min), overnight. A full bulk pass is 729 writes at 0.5/s (about 24 min), hence background jobs. ASSUMPTION: the daily API allowance covers a normal night.

14.1 What could still kill this

The four decisions removed the design risk. What remains is execution, most dangerous first:

  1. Nobody does the backfill. The app is only as useful as 729 policy decisions and the limits on every must-be-in-stock item, none of which exists today, and the 60-day window covers the December–January shutdown. Without that work, staff see grey "Not set" pills, and "force my team to have some items in stock" is not met.
  2. Three brands look empty to staff. None of the 33 TLF/PPP/INF lines has a photo, so none can be approved until someone photographs and loads them.
  3. Zoho sign-in through Cloudflare is unproven. Coverage rests on Ricardo's word (the directory endpoints answer 404). If Zoho will not act as an OIDC provider for Access, launch slips onto the ClickUp fallback.
  4. The rate limit is shared. A burst from any other integration, like the 618-PUT run, freezes the app's writes for tens of minutes, and "Pending Zoho" erodes trust.
  5. Production on a staging host. Approvals are the one record Zoho cannot rebuild, and a host loss costs up to a day of them.
  6. Variants turn approval into busywork. 560 of 729 items are variants. One click each, without P1 bulk approve, becomes noise approvers learn to ignore.
