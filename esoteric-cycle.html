The esoteric seat's ruling — jean, 2026-11-24

Plant at the new moon; seal and guard at the full. A lunation is a container that fills and empties. An intention put in at the full moon starts at the top of the container, so its first fortnight is emptying; it goes dark at the new moon and only then begins to grow, by which time the ritual is twenty-nine days old. Planted at the dark it fills through the waxing, is whole at the full, and is released through the waning. The full moon is still load-bearing — it is the day the channel is widest, which is exactly why it is the day to seal and guard.
