PRD-B — The Staff KPI Layer

One operating specification for individual scorecards, evidence, scoring, close and HR exceptions.

This is PRD-B of two. Its companion is PRD-A — the commitment layer.

1. Purpose and authority

This document specifies the staff KPI layer. It covers department heads, individual contributors and the monthly capture beneath them. It defines the KPI object, score composition, evidence gate, null propagation, close authority, performance bands, PIP/PGP review triggers, Zoho People synchronisation and build controls.

The system adopts Luke's V3 workbook as the scoring engine. Its L0/L1 design remains unchanged. Existing weights, named exceptions, score bands and the formal cap remain in force unless MD + EXCO + HR approve a versioned change. V3 is in Design / Prototype mode. Its scores are not live decisions. [E1][E3][E4]

This is a requirements document, not approval to score staff. Nothing is live until the owner signs the decisions at the end and the build stages pass.

1.1 Scope

Layer This document owns Boundary
L1 unit result Only its use as an inherited HOD input The unit result is calculated from its own measured business outcomes. Its definition sits outside this staff specification.
L2 department head HOD/EXCO scorecards, inherited unit share, direct outcomes, leadership and named exceptions A parent link does not define arithmetic.
L3 individual Staff-tier outcomes, actions, drivers, quality and controls Existing legitimate duties remain visible. Activity does not become an outcome by relabelling.
L4 capture Actual, evidence, verification, override, approval and monthly disposition Capture supplies evidence. It never rewrites a target or computes a second score.

The shared vocabulary is fixed: Enterprise → Unit → Department → Individual → Evidence → Score → Review → Action. These arrows describe alignment. They do not instruct a builder to add child scores into a parent. [E12]

The shared month carries a monthly plan, commitments, evidence and the close log. Quarterly groupings and target re-baselining do not replace monthly capture. Work surfaces hold no Actual, Progress % or Score % field. Targets and baselines are never copied onto monthly capture rows. [E1]

1.3 Decision rights

HODs own KPI statements, evidence review and staff results. MD / EXCO approves HOD/EXCO results. MD + EXCO + HR approve versioned weight, band and formula changes. The independent close manager verifies or marks unscored but cannot invent attainment. HOD + HR activate PIP/PGP cases through the applicable process. HR + FXS map People against its live configuration. [E3][E4][E10][E11]

3. KPI object and scorecard design

3.1 Required KPI fields

Every L2 or L3 KPI record carries the following fields.

Field group Required content Control
Identity Stable KPI ID, role ID, employee or approved vacancy, tier, brand, unit IDs do not change when a person or display name changes.
Alignment Parent KPI ID — required, one per record, no skipped level (PRD-A §4.1) — plus pillar and outcome statement Alignment explains why the KPI exists. It does not sum children. A parentless L2 or L3 record is not admissible.
Measure KPI name, metric definition, type, direction, unit Direction has three values only — Higher, Lower, Binary (PRD-A §6.1). No fourth direction exists, and none is added here.
Scale Baseline, target, approved tolerance or standard where applicable Blank means unresolved. It never means zero.
Composition Weight, component bucket, mandatory-control flag Active weights sum to 1.0 for the applicable role model. Zero-weight controls remain mandatory where flagged.
Capture Cadence, evidence source, automation status, actual, evidence reference Actual is entered once in the capture/engine path.
Accountability Owner, HOD, verifier, approver Verifier is independent of the measured owner.
Governance Effective version, status, change reason, approval history Retire by status. Do not delete scored history.

V3 contains compact types such as Action and Outcome, plus compound labels such as Action / Control and Outcome / Leadership. The builder must preserve the approved type and component mapping. It must not guess a new weight from a compound label. The source audit records 51 distinct HOD KPI types; that variety is a mapping requirement, not permission to invent 51 formulas. [E1][E2]

3.2 Action and outcome separation

The four words are defined once, in PRD-A §5.1. This table is the scoring view of the same four values; it adds no fifth type and no new definition. If the two ever disagree, A wins and this table is wrong.

Type Question answered Scoring treatment
Action Was the required work delivered by the agreed deadline or SLA? Weighted only where the approved role model assigns weight. Otherwise it remains evidence or a driver.
Outcome What measurable result changed? Carries the outcome share defined by the role model.
Leadership / Improvement Did leadership, team delivery, process improvement, AI or automation create a measurable result? Uses the approved leadership or exception bucket. Meeting attendance alone is not improvement.
Quality / Control Was the work accurate, compliant, controlled and supportable? Carries its approved weight; a mandatory control can also gate release.

Every material Action remains separately visible and traces to a measurable Outcome where a real result exists. Completing the Action does not prove the Outcome. Original legitimate responsibilities are not deleted merely to make the scorecard shorter. [E3][E5][E6]

3.3 Alignment is not computation

Relationship Required interpretation Forbidden interpretation
Unit outcome → HOD The HOD inherits the stated share of the unit result when the role model includes one. The HOD score is not an average of staff scores.
HOD → individual The individual sees the outcome to which the role contributes. The individual does not automatically inherit the HOD total.
Action → outcome The action supplies delivery evidence and may explain the result. Action completion does not force outcome attainment.
KPI → parent KPI The parent supplies context and line of sight. A parent ID is not a rollup formula.
Employee scores → unit No computation arrow exists. A unit score is never the average, sum or weighted average of employee scores.

A unit result remains an operational business metric. Enterprise KPIs are measured from the business. They are never summed from employee scores. [E1][E3][E8]

3.4 Mandatory outcomes and zero-weight duties

Every BU HOD or executive leader with budget or direct-report responsibility must have a measurable Department Budget/P&L Control outcome and a measurable Team Performance/Delivery outcome or leadership result. Meetings, reviews and 1:1s remain Actions or drivers. They do not substitute for those outcomes. [E4]

A zero-weight row can still be mandatory. V3 uses such rows for duties and controls including financial governance, statutory compliance, reporting and close dependencies. The following rules apply:

  • The row remains visible on the scorecard.
  • Its evidence is required when the row is marked mandatory.
  • It adds no weighted points.
  • Missing evidence blocks the affected composite.
  • A failed mandatory control blocks release of the affected composite and is named in the close disposition.
  • A builder must not drop the row during import because its weight is zero.

4. Score composition

4.1 Individual staff default

The V3 staff-tier default is 70% Outcomes + 20% Actions / Drivers + 10% Quality / Controls. Actions are a maximum 20% in the technical configuration. Activities otherwise remain evidence unless they are critical controls. [E4][E6]

The model measures the result of work without hiding the work. A role with a strong action load must use an approved exception or retain those duties as evidence; a builder must not silently rebalance the role to make the default fit.

4.2 Named role exceptions

These exceptions are stated in V3 Config. They remain explicit configuration, not informal spreadsheet edits. [E4]

Role Outcomes Actions / delivery Quality / controls Leadership / improvement BU inheritance

Lerato Khanyi — Corporate Trainer 70% 20% 10% — None
Anne Fourie — ADM Team Leader 50% 45% — 5% Leadership None
Lea — Videographer / Video Content Creator 40% 60% — — 0%

Original responsibilities remain separate rows. An exception changes composition; it does not remove evidence, null propagation or independent close. [E4][E5]

4.3 HOD/EXCO conflict that must not be flattened

V3 does not express one consistent HOD default. The contradiction is an open design decision.

V3 location Stated model Evidence
Config 50% BU result + 35% direct outcomes + 10% leadership + 5% EXCO accountability [E4]
HOD KPI Master 50% BU result + 35% direct outcomes + 15% leadership drivers [E5]
HOD Composite Scoring 50% BU result + 35% HOD direct outcome score + 15% leadership driver score [E8]

The builder must not choose between these formulations, average them or treat the 5% EXCO row as optional arithmetic. MD + EXCO + HR must decide the governing default and each role's exception map before live scoring.

Where applicable, the executive scorecard includes a 5% Evidence-Based Cross-BU Accountability & Survey Integrity KPI at individual executive level only. It is not a Business or BU KPI. It is not universal to every Department Tier / HOD role. Operational HOD roles such as Bronwyn and Juan do not receive it unless formally designated EXCO/executive. [E4]

Two further facts decide this properly, and neither existed in the earlier editions of this programme:

  • Nine HOD roles carry personal rows that total 0.50. They inherit the other 0.50 from the BU composite. Filling each of those rows to 1.00 would take those nine executives to 1.50, not 1.00. Weight repair therefore applies to the final composite, never to the personal rows.
  • L0's approved vector is 0.20 / 0.20 / 0.05 / 0.05 / 0.18 / 0.12 / 0.05 / 0.05 / 0.10, under a 40 / 30 / 30 pillar split. A statement that "nine roles balance at 1.00" does not preserve those values, and a builder who replaces them has silently changed the company's scoring.

The ITC and MKT banners corroborate the 50 / 35 / 10 + 5 reading. FXS uses the same numbers with a different label on the 35%. All four sites are recorded here so the person deciding decision 3 sees the whole conflict and not one page of it. [E4][E5][E8]

4.4 Score calculation and cap

The engine separates attainment, evidence and approval:

Step Input Output
Attainment Direction, baseline, target and actual Calculated Score %
Evidence Verification state and mandatory controls Permission to score, or null
Override HOD Override % and mandatory comment where used Proposed Final Score % after the evidence gate
Weighting Approved role weight and component map Weighted Score
Approval HOD or MD / EXCO decision Approved monthly score, or unscored disposition
Band Approved final score Performance Band, only for a scored result

Formal scores are capped at 100%. Stretch is tracked separately. The cap applies after the approved attainment and override logic; it must not turn an unscored result into 100%. [E1][E3][E4]

5. Null-propagation contract

No evidence, no score. Null is never zero. The null propagates through every required composite.

This section overrides any earlier spreadsheet behaviour that halves a score for partial evidence, treats a missing cell as zero, or totals only the components that arrived.

5.1 Meaning of the states

State Meaning Numeric result
Verified actual of zero Evidence proves that performance was zero 0 is a valid measured result
Target met / target missed Evidence supports the attainment calculation Example: target met = 100; target missed = 0 in the supplied gate example [E1]
Missing Actual or required evidence was not supplied Null
Rejected Evidence was reviewed and refused Null until the authorised process produces acceptable evidence
Unverified Evidence exists but the required verifier has not accepted it Null
Partial Some, but not all, required evidence exists Unscored; null, not a discount
Overridden without evidence A person entered a score or comment without passing the gate Null; the entry is an annotation only

An empty cell and a measured zero answer different questions. Zero claims performance. Null claims that the evidence process cannot support a performance finding.

5.2 Required-input set

For each person and month, the engine freezes a required-input set before computing a composite. It includes:

  • every active KPI with positive weight in that person's approved role model;
  • every active zero-weight mandatory control attached to that role;
  • every inherited component required by that role model;
  • every direct component needed by the applicable composite;
  • the evidence and approval required for each of those inputs.

A role with no BU inheritance does not acquire a BU requirement. Chiseke, Mahlatsi, Lerato Khanyi and Anne Fourie therefore use their stated exception models without adding a unit component. Lea has 0% BU inheritance. Their own required KPI and control inputs still propagate nulls normally. [E4]

The required-input set is versioned with the role model. A later target or weight change must not retroactively change an already closed month.

5.3 Propagation rules

Rule Builder requirement
Any required null makes the composite null Do not sum only the populated rows.
Inherited inputs are required If the unit result is unscored, a HOD using BU inheritance is unscored even when direct outcomes and leadership are complete.
Partial evidence is not a discount The result is verified-complete or unscored. There is no scored-partial state.
No renormalisation Do not redistribute a missing KPI's weight across available KPIs.
Mandatory controls still block Zero weight does not remove the evidence or release gate.
Overrides annotate only before the gate No HOD, MD, EXCO or HR override can convert null to a number.
Close does not cure null WD4 is the disposition deadline. It is not permission to publish an unsupported score.

For a weighted illustration, a composite with weights 0.60 / 0.40 and component results 100 / null is null. It is not 60. The 60 produced by summing only what arrived would be a performance finding created by missing data. [E1]

5.4 Downstream consequences

An unscored person-month produces:

  • no Final Score %;
  • no Performance Band;
  • no ranking;
  • no inclusion in a scored average or rolling score trend;
  • no increment to a consecutive-low-score count;
  • no PIP/PGP trigger based on that month;
  • no numeric Zoho People rating export;
  • no HR consequence caused by missing evidence.

The operational result is still recorded. If the business missed a target, that miss goes to the results ledger. If evidence or approval failed, the close log records the control debt. Neither is converted into an employee score without evidence.

5.5 Overrides and corrections

An HOD Override % is evaluated only after every required input passes the evidence gate. An override requires a comment and an auditable author. It cannot repair a missing actual, missing evidence, rejected evidence, failed mandatory control or unscored inherited component.

A correction after close requires an authorised versioned reopening. The original disposition, evidence and approvals remain in history. A builder must not overwrite the closed row or silently refresh the People rating.

Missing actual One positive-weight KPI has no actual KPI and person composite are null
Missing evidence Actual exists; required evidence does not Null; no calculated final score

Inherited null HOD has BU inheritance; unit result is null HOD composite is null

6. Evidence and monthly scoring

6.1 Evidence gate

System-generated evidence is preferred. Employees submit manual proof only when the integrated system cannot supply it. Hermes or another agent may append an evidence event, match it to a KPI and flag sufficiency. It does not approve the score or decide an HR outcome. [E3][E9]

Evidence state Capture rule Scoring rule
Approved Named source and evidence reference are present; verifier accepts completeness Attainment may proceed to approval
Missing Required evidence is absent Null
Rejected Verifier records rejection and reason Null
Unverified Evidence exists but verification is incomplete Null

The old technical Config contains evidence multipliers of 1.0 for Approved, 0.5 for Partial and 0.0 for Missing/Rejected. Its demo turns a calculated 90 into 45 for Partial evidence, with weighted score 20.25 at weight 0.45. That behaviour is superseded by this PRD. Partial evidence is unscored; it is not half performance. [E7][E11]

6.2 Monthly scoring record

The engine monthly record retains the V3 fields and adds the controls needed by this contract. [E7]

Field group Fields
Period and person Month, employee, business unit, role, stable role ID
KPI KPI ID, KPI, KPI Type, Weight %, Direction, Baseline, Target, unit, mandatory flag
Measurement Actual, Evidence Status, Evidence Link, Data Source, capture ID
Calculation Calculated Score %, Final Score %, Weighted Score, formula version
Human decision HOD Override %, HOD Approval, HOD Comment, verifier, approval identity
Close Close ID, disposition, close manager, second signature, exception reference
Result Performance Band only when scored; People sync status and version

No employee self-score is required. The formula proposes attainment; the HOD reviews evidence and performance. MD / EXCO approves the applicable HOD/EXCO result. HR manages exceptions and formal processes rather than recalculating every line. [E3][E7]

6.3 Evidence inbox

The evidence event retains the V3 identity, KPI, source, capture, agent-assessment, HOD-decision and exception fields. [E9] Agent confidence is not verification. The verifier decides whether evidence supports the actual and is complete for close.

6.4 Period lock and the close archive

A month locks by value-paste snapshot into a non-formula archive workbook, one per month. A versioned workbook per month is the accepted alternative; the snapshot is preferred because an archived formula can recalculate after the fact and quietly change a closed month.

The archive carries nine parts per line. The month is not locked if any one of them is missing.

# Part
1 The KPI and its ID
2 The period bounds
3 The captured value
4 The evidence reference and the capture version
5 The verifier's timestamp
6 The close authority's timestamp
7 Any override, with its reason
8 Every composite input and its weight
9 The score, or the null reason, with the formula version

Reopening a locked month creates a new version. It never edits the archived one. Without that rule, "locked" means "nobody happens to be looking at it" — which is what V3 has today: 17 hidden tabs and no period lock at all.

7. Close authority and monthly disposition

Every person-month and required unit input reaches a disposition. One independent close manager is accountable for that outcome.

The close is not left to a self-signing chain. V3 gives the HOD monthly approval authority, while this PRD requires an independent verifier and an independent close. The close manager is named by MD + EXCO before live scoring. A deputy is named under the same authority so absence cannot deadlock the month.

7.1 Authority model

Actor Responsibility Cannot do
Employee or system Supply actual and evidence Self-approve the score
HOD Review evidence, attainment, context and staff score Approve alone when verifier or close independence fails
MD / EXCO Approve HOD/EXCO results and resolve escalated conflicts Convert null to a score by instruction
Verifier Confirm evidence completeness and source Verify their own measured object
Close manager Confirm the required set, signatures and final disposition Invent attainment, change weights or activate an HR case
HR Review exceptions and run formal processes Treat unscored as poor performance

The close manager is independent of every measured owner in the close being discharged. If the close manager is also an owner, HOD, verifier or approver for an object, that object escalates to MD / EXCO for an independent disposition. An organisational title alone does not prove independence; the close record identifies the conflict check.

7.2 Deadline and two signatures

The close manager owes a disposition by Working Day 4 (WD4) of the following month. WD4 is read from the close calendar. It is not inferred from a spreadsheet row position or a typed month label. [E1]

A valid close carries two signatures:

  1. the operational approval — HOD for staff, or MD / EXCO for the applicable head; and
  2. the independent close manager's disposition.

The verifier identity is separately recorded. A verifier may not silently substitute for either signature where that creates self-approval.

After WD4, an object without a valid disposition is Undismissed. It remains visible as close debt. It produces no score, band, ranking or HR export. A reminder, meeting or dashboard refresh does not dismiss it.

7.3 Dispositions

Disposition Entry condition Required record Result
Verified Every required input is evidenced and approved; mandatory controls pass; both signatures are present Close ID, required-set version, evidence references, approvals, score version Release the approved score and band
Unscored — disposition A required input is missing, rejected, unverified or blocked; or a mandatory control fails Name each exception, evidence owner, business-miss reference and close decision Null; no band, ranking or HR export
Undismissed WD4 has passed without a valid Verified or Unscored — disposition record Open close debt and escalation Null; visible debt; no HR export

Verified describes the close, not high performance. Unscored — disposition is a completed governance action, not a forgotten blank. Undismissed is a failed close control.

7.4 Close packet

The close manager cannot discharge a month from a single total. The close packet contains:

8. Bands, PIP and PGP

The approved V3 bands remain unchanged. [E1][E3][E4]

Final score Band Required management response
≤65 PIP trigger Formal performance support/review; not an automatic employment decision
66–69 At Risk — HOD + HR review Immediate manager and HR review with clear recovery actions
70–79 PGP Structured performance growth / improvement plan review
80–94 On Track Performance meets the expected standard
≥95 High Performance Recognition territory; formal score remains capped at 100%

A PIP review trigger is a score of ≤65, or <80 for two consecutive months. A PGP review trigger is a score of 70–79. Only scored months count toward the consecutive-month condition. An unscored month neither advances nor substitutes for a low score. [E1]

The trigger creates a review case. HOD and HR confirm context, fairness, support, plan, duration and documentation before activation. The case register keeps Case ID, case type, employee/BU, role/BU, month, score, trigger, plan type, owner, HR reviewer, start/end fields, status, root cause, success measures, document link and next check-in. [E10]

An agent may draft the case. It may not activate it. HR and the HOD own the applicable employment process.

9. Zoho People synchronisation

11.1 The live pilot — the release gate

Nothing scores until the pilot closes. No composite, no band, no ranking, no PIP, no People record. B5's synthetic month proves the configuration; it does not prove the operating system, and a configuration that passes a rehearsal can still fail a person.

Element Rule
Scope One BU — SAL or MKT, one only — for one month
Live files Read-only; the pilot writes to a copy
Entry Staff type their own numbers into the shared copy, or Bruce types and each person confirms
Extent One full close cycle, from capture to the WD4 disposition

Four falsification tests. Pass on at least three, and none may be skipped:

  1. Open three composite figures at random and trace each to its evidence, source and capture record.
  2. Leave one key result deliberately unscored. It must produce null, an empty band and an Unscored — disposition record — not a discount.
  3. Raise one exception, escalate it, and close it inside two working days.
  4. A second person reproduces one composite from the archive alone, with no help.

The pilot also fails if the same three people do everything and none of them can explain the rule to a colleague. That is a transfer failure, it is the most common one, and no amount of passing tests covers it.

Appendix — corrections and vocabulary

A. Corrections that govern this document

Earlier editions said The truth is
Missing evidence could be scored as zero. Missing, rejected or unverified evidence is null. It is unscored, never zero, and cannot trigger an HR consequence.
Partial evidence could multiply a calculated score by 0.5; the demo reduced 90 to 45 and produced 20.25 at weight 0.45. Partial evidence is unscored. A result is verified-complete or null; there is no half-performance state. [E7][E11]
Writing null into the KPI cell was enough. Null propagates through every required composite, including inherited unit inputs. A 0.60 / 0.40 composite with 100 / null is null, not 60. [E1]
Available weights could be redistributed when one result was absent. Required weights are never renormalised around missing evidence.
Partial evidence was a discounted score. Partial evidence is a close exception, not a discount.
A parent KPI link implied arithmetic. Parent links show alignment. Each composite uses only its explicitly approved formula.
A unit score could be an average of employee scores. A unit score comes from its own measured business outcomes. Employee scores never create it.
A zero-weight control could be ignored. A mandatory zero-weight control still requires evidence and can block the composite.
V3 had resolved the HOD default. Config says 50/35/10/5; HOD Master and Composite say 50/35/15. The owner must decide; the builder must not choose. [E4][E5][E8]
The earlier audit wording supported 52 distinct KPI types. The corrected source audit states 51 distinct HOD KPI types. [E1]
HOD approval alone could close a month. An independent close manager owes the disposition, with operational approval and a second close signature.
WD4 was the date by which a score had to be published. WD4 is the close disposition deadline. The lawful outcome may be Verified or Unscored — disposition; an unsupported score is not required.
An executive or HOD override could supply the missing number. An override applies only after the evidence gate and can never lift null.
An unscored month could be exported as zero or omitted without a visible debt. It has no numeric People export and no HR consequence; its close exception or Undismissed debt remains visible.
A configured workbook could be treated as live. V3 remains Design / Prototype until decisions, acceptance tests and owner release are complete. [E3][E4]
