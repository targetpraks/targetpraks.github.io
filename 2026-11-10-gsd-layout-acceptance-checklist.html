The layout Ricardo asked for, written as rows a build can be held against. One row, one pass or fail, one piece of evidence. Source of record: the INF GSD Limited Edition dashboard.

The layout is the requirement, not a preference. A dashboard that reports the same numbers in a different shape is a different product. Every row below is scored on what a person sees on the screen, never on what the data layer intends.

03 KPI tile anatomy

Every number on the board is one of these tiles. Same five parts, everywhere — it is what makes scanning safe.

1. Value and unit — right-aligned, the unit attached (R, %, ratio, months).
2. Delta on prior month — arrow and colour, no delta invented where there is no prior reading.
3. RAG chip — GREEN AMBER RED NO TARGET.
4. Bar to target — thin, shows distance travelled, not decoration.
5. One-line human note — “Months of cash left.”

The scoring standard:

  • GREEN 100% of target or better
  • AMBER 90–99% of target
  • RED below 90% of target
  • NO TARGET a visible state, never a zero and never green

A score without a published target is not a score.

07 Data and trust

The board may be wrong about a number. It may not be vague about where the number came from.

✓RequirementWhat good looks like
Source per metricNamed system (Zoho Books, CRM, Desk, People, Inventory, app database, survey, manual capture) — on the metric itself.
Sync cadence shownDaily, weekly or monthly per metric, visible to the reader.
Staleness warningA reading older than its cadence is flagged, not quietly carried forward.
Month close separated from liveLive trading numbers never presented as final month figures.
Simulated data labelledDummy or partial figures carry the banner; they are never shown as actuals.
