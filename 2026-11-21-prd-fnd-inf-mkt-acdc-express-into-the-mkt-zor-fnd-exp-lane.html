The document is a five-section brand operating manual for ACDC Express: 56 pages, 78 tables, 44 formulas, 6 control buttons and 9 quick links. It is not a copy deck and not a campaign plan — it is the brand's strategy, its operating rules, and the scaffolding into which campaigns and content get written.   1. The campaigns are real; their topics are not. 26 campaign rows carry purpose, pain point, UVP and cadence.
