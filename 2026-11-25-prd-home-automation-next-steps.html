Decision

The gate is open, so stop repairing and start using.

Three things, in this order: pair Apple Home to the HomeKit bridge that is now advertising, settle the Google Home endpoint question — a Nabu Casa subscription or a hostname of our own — then put the house on behaviour, the first automations, before adding any more devices.

Summary

Home Assistant 2026.10.4 runs natively on the Mac mini under launchd, holds 69 entities across the house, takes a verified daily backup, answers on the LAN, and now actually sees its devices: five Sonos players, a JBL bar, an LG soundbar, a TCL television and the Brother printer with live ink levels.

What is still missing is not capability but connection and purpose. The HomeKit bridge advertises Maio Home with zero paired clients — nobody has scanned the code yet. Google Home has no route to Home Assistant because this Mac has no public HTTPS endpoint. And the house has zero automations: everything is controllable, nothing happens by itself.

Problem

The hub works. The house does not yet react.

  • Apple Home is unpaired. _hap._tcp advertises Maio Home, and paired_clients is 0. Pairing is a two-minute phone step nobody has done.
  • Google Home cannot reach HA. Google's cloud needs a public HTTPS URL; Tailscale Funnel's three permitted ports are all occupied, there is no Cloudflare zone, and cloudflared is not installed.
  • The house has no behaviour. automations.yaml is [], scripts and scenes are empty, and the only dashboard is the default Overview.
  • Two dead entities clutter the picture. A stale DLNA entry for the Kitchen Play:1 that left the network (192.168.0.191), and a Cast player for a TV that is off.
