3. What is broken, counted

Duplicated documents

  • 124 groups, 258 files, 0.71 GB of same-name same-size copies. 46 of those groups straddle two different TLF trees.
  • 71 files carry a (1), (2), (3) copy suffix in the name — the drive is keeping version history in the filename.
  • Example: four identical Berry Smoothie artwork files, Options 1–4, each stored twice under two "Work in Progress" branches.

Empty structure

  • 80 of 373 folders are empty — including TLF_HR, all three Health & Safety files (A, B, C), TLF_Luke's assignment, 04_Digital Marketing, 05_Analytics Reports. The reporting corner alone is 49 empty folders: MANCO monthly has 24 empty folders and no files at all, quarterly has 25.
  • 19 folders in Marketing hold at most one file and nothing below them. Empty folders read as "we have this covered"; they usually mean the opposite.

Nested repeats

  • TLF Marketing/Work in Progress/Work in Progress — and 50 paths under Marketing repeat their own name one level down.
  • Same disease in the engineering files: DXF's Lvl 1/DXF's Lvl 1, Engineering Drawings Lvl 1/Engineering Drawings Lvl 1.

Loose files at the top of a tree

  • 12 payment PDFs and completion certificates sitting unfiled at the root of the IT folder.
  • 5 unfiled SOW/proposal files at the root of INFX Solutions, 2 at HR, 5 personal recordings, 2 marketing working docs.

4. The structure I propose — one TLF home, ten drawers

Numbered, so the order survives every file manager and every person. Brand-asset drawer follows the house 00.Brand-Folder-Template that already lives in Shared with Me.

EVERYONE › ZOR BU 3: THE LOCAL FARMER ← the canonical home, built to this shape ├── 00_Index & Register master document register: what lives where, owner, review date ├── 01_Legal & Company CIPC reg docs, NDA, franchise agreement, rental, T&Cs, IP ├── 02_Finance financials, management accounts, debtors, price lists ├── 03_People job profiles, KPI & organogram, training, health & safety ├── 04_Operations SOPs, production logs, grow room & machine, packaging, stock take ├── 05_Sales & Franchising prospectus, application form, stage paperwork, leads, approved quotes ├── 06_Marketing & Brand CI · Content · Campaigns · Digital · Website · Proposals · Reports · Workings ├── 07_Reports & Analytics monthly and quarterly brand + ops reporting, KPI scorecards ├── 08_Meetings & Decisions meeting notes, recordings, approvals └── 09_Archive OLD files and superseded versions — kept, quarantined, out of the way

Two rules that make it hold:

  • One TLF home. If a TLF document is not a working file owned by a BU function, it belongs in this tree. A person looking for anything TLF starts here and nowhere else.
  • BU working folders stay put, but match. Finance keeps its TLF financials; Marketing keeps the 18 GB creative library; Sales keeps its leads. Each of those folders gets the same numbered naming so the drive reads as one system. Nothing gets ripped out of a BU just to be tidy.

5. The work, staged by dependency

No dates, no Gantt charts. Each stage unlocks the next; inside a stage everything runs in parallel.

Stage A — build the skeleton zero moves · zero risk

Unlocks everything else. Nothing is moved, nothing is deleted — folders are created.
  • Create 00–09 in EVERYONE › ZOR BU 3: THE LOCAL FARMER.
  • Write the index/register: which drawer holds what, who owns it, when it gets reviewed.
  • Add a one-page "start here" note at the top of the tree.

Stage B — clear the dead weight trash only · reversible 30 days

Needs your approval. This is the only stage that removes anything, and it removes only empties and confirmed duplicates.
  • Trash the 80 empty folders — the empty scaffold is worse than no scaffold.
  • Hash-confirm the 124 duplicate groups, then trash the redundant copy of each — 258 files, 0.71 GB.
  • Rename out the (1)/(2) suffixes on the 71 surviving copies.

Stage C — fold the small satellites into the home ≈198 files

Depends on A. Small payload, big clarity win — this is where a person currently gives up looking.
  • Move the TLF folders out of Finance, Sales, IT, INFX Solutions, HR, MANCO and EXCO into the matching drawers, or leave them in place and link them from the index — your call, one line each.
  • File the loose root documents (IT payment packs, SOWs, job profiles) into their drawers.
  • Collapse the 50 nested repeats in Marketing — one level each, no content loss.

Stage D — retire the second spine 494 files · the careful one

Depends on A–C. ZOR BU 2 › ZOR TLF stops being a parallel TLF universe and becomes the operations working area.
  • Move the settled documents (legal, SOP-approved versions, training, franchise stages) into 01–09.
  • Leave live working material — machine drawings, production logs in progress — where the people working on it can find it.
  • Leave the 18 GB creative library in BU 7 Marketing. Moving it buys nothing and costs hours; it just gets the numbered names and a link in 06_Marketing & Brand.
Nothing has been moved, renamed, or trashed. This audit was read-only — names, sizes and structure read from the live drive, folder IDs resolved through the WorkDrive API. No write has been made to any business system.
