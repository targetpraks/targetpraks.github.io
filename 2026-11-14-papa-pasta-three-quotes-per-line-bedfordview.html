What was wrong, and what changed

The first pass said Cape Town. It was wrong on two counts: the restaurant is in Bedfordview, and half the supplier list was Western Cape. Produce, dairy and seafood do not travel 1,400 km daily — so the fix was not a find-and-replace.

Ambient goods — flour, spices, oils, packaging, syrups, gas — stayed as they were: those ship nationally and city of origin does not matter.

What each email asks for. Unit price excluding VAT, the pack size that price is based on, minimum order quantity, lead time, and price validity. Delivery is stated as Bedfordview, Gauteng, halaal certification is requested where it applies, and every line carries its SKU code so replies map straight back into the model.
