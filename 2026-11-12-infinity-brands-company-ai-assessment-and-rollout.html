Infinity Brands · AgenticOS · assessment

Company AI is running. It is not yet the company's brain.

What the platform actually does today, what stands between it and every department, and the order I would build it in.

Prepared by Bruce Wayne · 12 November 2026 · pilot platform on macmini-inf

The one-line verdict. The engine works and it is now fed with real company content — ten documents off the 2026 Business Unit Functions & Roles master sheet, answering in four seconds with citations. What it lacks is not intelligence; it is plumbing: identity for staff, a knowledge base per business unit and brand, and a repeatable pipe from Zoho. Those three are the whole job.

3 · The five structural gaps

3.1 · Everything is in one knowledge base

One collection serves all eight assistants. It works at twelve documents; it degrades as the corpus grows, because Finance and Marketing compete for the same top-five search results. Brand knowledge — the part you actually asked for — does not exist as a separate thing at all.

3.2 · The brands are not where you think they are

There is no Papa Pasta folder. WorkDrive holds the company as business units: seven BUs, two Brand Ops units (ZOR), two INFX units (COR), plus EXCO, MANCO and EVERYONE. The brand material lives inside them — "ZOR TLF" (The Local Farmer) sits under Brand Operations 2, "Divorced Dads" under INFX Labs and EXCO. Any per-brand design has to be built on the BU skeleton, or it will not match how the company files its own work.

3.3 · Zoho is reachable, but not by the platform

Three separate truths, each measured:

DoorStateEvidence
Zoho OAuth token (system user)CRM works; Projects, WorkDrive, Sheets, Cliq, Mail refuseCRM 200; Projects 403; Cliq 401; Mail INVALID_OAUTHSCOPE

So the honest architecture is a harvest: something on our side reads WorkDrive, Sheets, Tables and Projects through the MCP servers and pushes clean text into knowledge bases. Live tool-calling from inside the bots is a later lane, and a riskier one.

3.4 · Identity is missing, so permissions are theatre

Eight groups with zero members. The access model is built and empty. Until staff have accounts, "Finance can see Finance's knowledge" is a diagram, not a control.

3.5 · Nobody owns the pipeline yet

Today the ingestion is me, running scripts. There is no schedule, no source registry, no failure alerts, and no evaluation set — so no way to tell whether retrieval got better or worse after a change. A company brain that only updates when someone remembers to run a script is a demo.

4 · Target architecture

LAYER 1
Company knowledge. Policies, EXCO and MANCO papers, the master sheets, culture and onboarding. Visible to every agent. Small, curated, high authority.
LAYER 2
Unit knowledge — one base per business unit. BU 1 to BU 7, ZOR Brand Ops 1–2, COR INFX 1–2, plus Franchise Services. Each with an owner, a refresh cadence and its own group permission.
LAYER 3
Brand knowledge — one base per brand. Papa Pasta, The Local Farmer, Divorced Dads, each INFX line. Brand books, menus, campaigns, pricing, assets. Attached to brand agents and to the relevant department agents.
LANE A
Harvest (build first). Scheduled read of named WorkDrive folders, Sheets and Tables through Zoho's MCP servers → clean text → ingest → cite. Deterministic, auditable, no write risk.
LANE B
Live action (later). Agents calling Zoho read tools at run time. Powerful, and where governance has to be real — read-only first, writes never.

The wiring rule: a department assistant gets its own BU base + company base. A brand agent gets its brand base + the company base. Nobody gets "everything", because "everything" is how a Finance question ends up answered from a marketing brief.

5 · The order I would build it

Staged by dependency, not by calendar. Each stage ends on a condition that is either true or false.

StageWorkEnds when
S1
Trustworthy single base		 Clean the extractor (HTML entities are still leaking into text), add a 40-question evaluation set with known answers, record retrieval scores before and after every change Every one of the 40 questions answers from the right document, and the score is recorded
S2
Unit bases		 Split into one base per BU; attach each assistant to its own plus the company base; grant by group Finance answers BU 1 only; Marketing answers BU 5 only; neither can retrieve the other's document
S3
Harvest pipeline		 A repeatable job: source registry (folder → base), MCP read, text extraction, ingestion, per-source report; dry-run mode A run with no arguments refreshes every registered source and reports per-source counts without a human touching it
S4
Brand bases		 Create the brand bases from what WorkDrive actually holds — ZOR TLF, Divorced Dads, INFX lines — and build a brand agent per venture One brand agent answers a brand question from brand material and refuses to answer from another brand's
S5
Staff and permissions		 Accounts for the department owners, group membership, invitations through Zoho SMTP, first real users A non-owner staff member signs in, sees only their unit's agents and bases, and cannot open another unit's
S6
Live Zoho lane		 Read-only Zoho tools exposed to the agents that need them — Projects lookups first An agent answers "what is the status of project X" from live Projects data, with the record linked

6 · Decisions only you can make

  1. Unit or brand? I recommend building on the BU skeleton WorkDrive already uses, with brand bases hanging off Brand Ops and the INFX units. The alternative — five venture bases with no BU layer — would fight how your own team files documents.
  2. The two organisations. The console's default landing is your personal workspace, not the company one. Staff should land in infinity-brands. Do you want the personal org removed from the picture, or left as your private scratch space?
  3. Staff accounts, and how many. Eight department owners is the obvious first wave. Names and addresses come from my side; the invitations and the SMTP send need your word.
  4. Zoho scope. The OAuth app behind the system user is missing WorkDrive, Sheets, Projects, Cliq and Mail scopes. The MCP servers cover the gap today; widening the scopes would make the harvest simpler. Your call — it is a re-consent on the Zoho API console.
  5. Who owns content. Each base needs a named human who says what is current. Otherwise the knowledge rots politely.

7 · Known defects to fix, not hide

  • The form ignores autofill. The console's React form never registers a password manager's fill, so Login does nothing. Type or paste. Needs patching before staff arrive.
  • Counts lie. The API reports document_count: 0 and chunk_count: 0 while twelve documents sit done. Do not build dashboards on those fields.
  • Two orgs, one user. A staff member signing in today would land in the personal workspace.
  • Extractor artefact. &amp; and &apos; survive into the text. Harmless to retrieval, ugly in a quotation — fixed in S1.
  • No evaluation set. Until S1 closes, every claim about "quality" is an anecdote.

What I did not do, and will not without your word: create staff accounts, send any invitation or email, widen Zoho scopes, delete the second organisation, or point any agent at a live business system. Everything above is inside the pilot platform, and reversible.
