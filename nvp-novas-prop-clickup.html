Status: PROPOSED — for Ricardo's approval. Prepared 19 November 2026 by Adrianna A. Nothing has been created, changed or moved in the ClickUp space; this document is the plan and the evidence behind it.

At a glance

  • The Novas Prop document is a status register, not a document store. It tracks 68 statutory lines across 6 owned properties plus one tenancy page, but holds 22 documents that are actually in hand and links the rest out to WorkDrive.
  • It is stale: last edited 21 September 2026, and the property pages were last touched between December 2025 and September 2026, while every sale card in ClickUp carries a November or December 2026 deadline.
  • The ClickUp space holds 27 cards and no documents at all — no Doc, no register, no property record, and only two statuses (to do, complete).

7. What to build in ClickUp

7.1 The rule that decides list or document

  • One row per fact → a List. Documents, leases, bills, maintenance entries: these are tables, and ClickUp lists give you filters, formulas, dashboards and bulk edits for free.
  • One reader at a time → a Doc. A property dossier, a handover index, the space guide: long-form writing that a person reads once.
  • A certificate → a file attached to the row. Never a link to WorkDrive, never a sentence saying it exists. The document's worst habit is that 'Attached' usually means 'a URL is in the cell'.

7.2 The six lists

List Rows Fields that matter Why it exists
Property Register 6 Erf, municipality, account no., title deed ref, occupancy, sale status, agent, asking price, power on/off one row per property; every other list points at it
Document Register ~71 Property, document type, status, certificate no., issue date, expiry, days to expiry (formula), file, verified by/on the statutory tables, flattened across all six properties
Leases & Inspections ~26 Property, tenant, start, end, vacated date, rent, escalation, deposit, notice given, outgoing inspection fixes the contradiction in §2.3 — 'lease end' and 'vacated' become two fields

Maintenance Log 73 Property, date, description, cost, supplier, invoice, before/after photos, category the seven maintenance tables
Quotes & Approvals 13 Property, item, quote, approved by, paid, status Bloom's quotes and payments, plus every open quote on the three houses

The Document Register is deliberately one list, not seven. Seven copies of the same 15-line matrix is how the document drifted: each property page was edited on a different day by a different person. One list, filtered by property, means the 'what is missing across the portfolio' question is a saved view instead of an afternoon.

7.5 The closing rule

The card 'Nova Property - stop closing compliance cards without the certificate attached' asks for exactly this, and it is the only thing that stops the document's failure mode coming back:

A compliance card cannot move to complete unless a certificate file is attached and the certificate number is filled in.

ClickUp enforces it with a required custom field on the closing status (Business plan), or an automation that reverts the status. The document's own record shows why: a Riley compliance certificate marked Expired, a Trichardt electrical COC marked 'the attached COC is fake', and a Frances solar COC with 'no signatures' were all sitting in a table that reads as if they were held.
