The one that would win — but is dark
DeepSeek V4.1 Flash NIM endpoint dead

Highest free score in the whole survey — AA 32.5, LiveBench 75.8, Arena 1476 (#35). deepseek-ai/deepseek-v4.1-flash is in NVIDIA's catalogue but returned no bytes in 150 s across three attempts. It is not in OpenRouter's free pool. Ricardo already runs deepseek-v4.1-flash:1017-cloud on the local gateway — that lane is paid, not free.

Two more free providers, if the name matters

These answer on both NVIDIA NIM and OpenRouter free, so they survive one provider going down:

  • Nemotron 3.5 Lightning 30B — AA 5.5, 1M ctx, fastest of the free set
  • Nemotron 3 Nano Omni 30B — text+image+audio+video input, 256k ctx
  • Cohere North Mini Code — OpenRouter only, coding-tuned, unbenchmarked

What each lane actually gives you

LaneCatalogueFree?Verdict
NVIDIA NIM85 models listed · 56 chat-capable free with API key, rate-limited The best free lane by a distance — it hosts all three of the top models. But the catalogue lies: 6 of the models I tested were either not deployed on the account or hung with no response.
OpenRouter17 free-tagged · 15 text-chat free, shared upstream pool Shallow but reliable for the Nemotrons. Free pool is rate-capped: Gemma 4 31B returned 429 from the shared pool on every attempt, and both Inkling models are gated to "agentic harnesses" and refuse a plain API call.
OpenCode Console29 models zero free Cheapest entries are GPT-6 Luna and Claude Haiku 5.5 at $0.10 / $0.50 per million. Nothing on this lane is a free model — it is a paid router that happens to be well priced.
