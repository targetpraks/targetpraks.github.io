The stack rebuilt around what you actually use

Books, Inventory, WorkDrive, TeamInbox, Cliq, People. Not one of those needs a whole-company suite — and the suite is the only thing on the list that charges by headcount.

Leave Zoho One. Keep People Plus for everyone, keep Books and Inventory per brand, and buy WorkDrive, TeamInbox and CRM only for the people who log in.

That build costs about R10,465 a month — R125,584 a year. The same group on Zoho One all-employee is R20,720 a month, R248,640 a year. Same apps, same staff, R123,000 a year difference.

And that is before the three cancellations: Projects, the subscriptions People Plus already includes, and full seats for franchisees.

Three Six moves, in the order I would do them

up to R30,240/yr

1. Cancel Zoho Projects this month

ClickUp is signed and the daily work has moved. Projects at Enterprise is R126 a user; at 20 users that is R30,240 a year for a product you have just replaced. If Projects sits inside a Zoho One org, the saving is not the app — it is the seat you stop paying for.

R0 → check first

2. Find the double-pay

People Plus Enterprise already includes Cliq Standard, Connect Enterprise, Vault Standard, Expense Standard and Recruit Enterprise. If any brand org carries a standalone subscription for one of those, you are paying twice for the same product. This is the cheapest audit in the whole exercise: one pass over the subscription pages.

the biggest line

3. Drop Zoho One, keep the brand orgs

Per-brand instances are the right idea and they are cheap — Books and Inventory are priced per organisation. What is expensive is putting those orgs on a per-head suite. Separate orgs, per-app subscriptions: brand separation without a second payroll.

R588/mo on 20

4. Stop giving franchisees seats

Franchisee interests are support and oversight, not a login to your finance system. WorkDrive client users are R168 a year against R756 a year for a Team seat; Books and Inventory both carry customer and vendor portals at no extra cost; Creator portals handle the application forms. Twenty franchisees: R3,360 a year instead of R15,120.

R50,032/yr

5. Profile the people who never log in

Any staff member who never touches the system — seasonal store staff, people kept on record after leaving, anyone whose file HR maintains — is R14 a month as an employee profile instead of R126 as a user. The one thing you lose is the appraisal, so profile the people you do not appraise in People.

10–17%

6. Annual on everything that stays, monthly on nothing that might go

Books saves 16.7% on annual, WorkDrive up to 16%, Cliq 10%, People Plus 10%, CRM Plus up to 18%. ClickUp was signed monthly — leave it monthly until the team has actually settled into it.

Four What leaving Zoho One costs you

Not money, but capability. Be honest about which of these you use before cancelling anything:

  • One directory and single sign-on — with per-app subscriptions, people log in to each app separately unless you keep a directory product.
  • Creator depth — Standard allows one app. Franchisee portals that need several forms and workflows need Professional or Enterprise at R280–R350 a user.
  • Analytics — bundled in One, a separate purchase on its own. If group reporting reads across the four Books orgs, price Analytics before you cancel.
  • Sign, Mail, Meeting and the rest — anything you genuinely use has to be bought deliberately rather than inherited.
  • One invoice — you will be reconciling four orgs and a dozen line items. That is the real cost of the saving, and it is bookkeeping, not cash.

Five What I need to finish the number

Everything above is list price against an assumed footprint. Two facts turn it into your actual bill:

  • What you are invoiced today, line by line — which orgs are on Zoho One, at which model, and how many seats each.
  • How many people log in daily across WorkDrive, TeamInbox and CRM. That one number moves the build, but it cannot move it anywhere near R20,720.
