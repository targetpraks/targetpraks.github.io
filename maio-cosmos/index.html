The honest read

Astrocartography is a reflective tradition, not evidence. A line describes the texture a place has for a chart; it does not predict an outcome, and it carries no weight at all against schools, hospitals, work, tax, health, or a family's real capacity to move. The family's own standing position — set in writing months ago — is that the map informs the choice and the family makes it.

A high score means the support at this place outweighs the pressure in it — nothing more. It does not mean the place is good, and it does not mean anyone should live there. The Toronto finding is interesting precisely because it is a coincidence of geometry — it is not a recommendation, and this page is not a relocation plan. Note also that no place on Earth has all five members net-positive; the best anywhere is four of five, and the best in Europe is three.

Two of the five charts here belong to children (Donnie, 9, and Gianna, 7), and one belongs to a three-day-old infant. Their maps are context for their parents — developmental weather, never a fixed trajectory, and never an instruction about where a child should grow up. For Sofia specifically: nothing about where an infant lives should be decided from a map of angular lines.

Line quality. The earlier version of this page summed every hit as positive, which measured how strongly a chart is engaged by a place rather than whether the engagement is welcome — a Saturn-MC line credited a city exactly as a Venus-MC line did. Each hit is now split into support (Venus/Jupiter full, Sun/Moon half) and pressure (Saturn/Pluto heaviest, Mars/Uranus/Neptune moderate), and every planet is further scaled by its condition in that person's own natal chart: benefics give more when well-aspected, demanding planets ask less. Mercury is treated as neutral. The score shown is support − pressure.

Verification. The correction is a re-score, not a new engine: the original intensity field still reproduces the previously verified chain to printed precision — Plymouth 28.24 / 9.03, Gijón 21.73, Porto 10.03, Lisbon 10.96, Mississauga 21.66 / 22.61, Rochester 26.00 — and Sofia's MC/IC lines reproduce her own report to the hundredth of a degree. All MC/IC lines were re-solved at a second latitude and showed 0.000° drift, confirming they are true verticals.
