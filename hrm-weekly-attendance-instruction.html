What the automation does

Four jobs, one Wednesday message. Everything below reads from Zoho People and writes only to Zoho Cliq — the attendance system itself is never modified.

JobWhat it checksWhere the answer comes from
1 · Shift allocation Every active employee has a shift (roster) allocated. Anyone with no shift is an exception — they cannot be measured for punctuality at all. Shift grouping by department (getDepartmentShiftGrouping)
2 · Irregular clock-in / early Late arrivals and arrivals off the person's own shift; missing clock-ins. Per-day attendance detail (getAttendanceDetailedReport)
3 · Irregular clock-out / late Early leavers, and the opposite finding — people still on the clock after their shift ends (extra hours). Same day records; the system's own OverTime field
4 · Praise & extra hours Who received the most positive feedback (compliments) in the week, and who is carrying the extra hours. People Feedback module (getFeedback) + extra-hours tally
One guardrail that shapes everything: extra time is measured against each person's own shift, never a generic 09:00–17:00. The org runs several shifts (Week Days, Early Birds, Late Bloomers, Friyays with a short Friday); the same clock-out is two hours of extra time on one shift and one hour on another. Print the shift name on every line.

Data sources — every call verified against the live API

Reach Zoho through the direct MetaMCP product endpoints in ~/.config/maio-stack/secrets.env (ZOHO_MCP_PEOPLE_URL, ZOHO_MCP_CLIQ_URL). Those URLs carry their own auth — send no Authorization header. All People arguments sit under body.arguments.

PurposeToolKey argumentsReturns
Attendance — the spine ZohoPeople_getAttendanceDetailedReport sdate, edate (dd-MM-yyyy), startIndex:1, dateFormat:"dd-MM-yyyy" — omit erecno for all staff 41 employees, each a date-keyed map of day records
Attendance summary (cross-check) ZohoPeople_getAttendanceSummary dateFormat:"yyyy-MM-dd", startDate, endDate, sIndex:"1", limit:200 overTime (seconds), presentDays per person
Shift allocation audit ZohoPeople_getDepartmentShiftGrouping {} all = 40 staff; shiftWiseEmployeesList + shiftInfo (shift names)
Attendance policy ZohoPeople_getAttendancePolicySettings {} hours_per_day 540, half-day & weekend rules, late-night window
Role & reporting line ZohoPeople_getEmployeeBasicDetails searchErecNo:<integer> name, department, designation, reports-to
Public holidays ZohoPeople_listholidays from, to (dd-MM-yyyy) holiday names (locale-prefixed — strip the prefix)
Compliments / praise ZohoPeople_getFeedback fbmode:"myteam", isTypesNeeded:true, fromDate, toDate, dateFormat feedback entries; categories = Positive / Negative / Training
Deliver the brief ZohoCliq_send_message_to_chat path_variables + body (resolve the chat first) posts into the HRM channel
Resolve the channel ZohoCliq_list_channels joined:true, limit:100 find #HRM: BUS (unique_name infhumanresourceteam)
Resolve the channel by name every run, not by a saved id. Look up #HRM: BUS via list_channels and match unique_name == "infhumanresourceteam", then use that entry's chat_id for the send. Zoho ids drift; a stale id silently posts to the wrong room. (Verified 2026-10-09: chat_id CT_2198143728962995184_714625445 · channel_id T2785625000004858027.)

Three parsing traps that will otherwise ship wrong numbers

  • One month maximum per attendance call. A wider date span returns the message "The date period should be within 1 month" inside a status: success envelope — chunk into calendar months.
  • Read OverTime; never recompute it. On a day with no clock-in, TotalHours stays 00:00 while WorkingHours keeps the shift default, so TotalHours − WorkingHours is nonsense.
  • Filter out the non-staff rows. An all-users sweep carries a System System placeholder and the principal's own record. Filter by employee id before counting "N staff".

The Wednesday brief — what HRM and the GM actually read

One Cliq message, fixed order, scannable in under a minute. Lead with the two things that need a decision; put the detail behind a link, never in the message body.

#SectionWhat goes in it
1Headline The week (Mon–Fri, dates) and two numbers: on-time rate and staff missing a shift allocation. Nothing else in the first two lines.
2Shift allocation — the exception callout "All 40 staff have a shift allocated: yes / no." If anyone is unallocated, name them here, first, in its own box — an unallocated person cannot be assessed for punctuality at all.
3Late in / early out — the exception list Only the people who breached the >5-minute tolerance on their own shift. Format: Name — 2 late in (worst +18 min) · 1 early out (Thu). Rank by frequency, cap at the top 6, and say "…and N more" with the link.
4Extra hours Split by kind — after own shift on a working day · weekend days worked · public holidays worked (name the holiday and its date). Top 5 by hours. Never one summed "overtime" number: habitual late evenings are a workload conversation, a worked public holiday is a beyond-scope conversation.
5Praise — most compliments Top 3 staff by positive feedback received this week from the People Feedback module, with the count. Keep it celebratory; no negative-feedback names in this brief.
6Data-quality flags Zero clock-ins across the whole window (an attendance-capture finding, not a zero-overtime finding), and any day over 12h or an overnight clock-out — labelled confirm before relying on this, because a long span and a forgotten clock-out look identical in the record.
7Footer Period covered, the shift names used, and one line: time on the clock only — authorisation, productivity and pay are a separate decision.

Message skeleton — keep it this short

⏱️ HRM Weekly Attendance Brief — week of Mon 6 – Fri 10 Oct
On time: 86% (34/40)  ·  Shift allocated: 40/40 ✅

⚠️ Needs a look
• Thabo M — 2 late in (worst +18 min Tue)
• A. Naidoo — 1 early out (Thu, −42 min)

🕕 Extra hours this week
• L. Harrison — 3h 10m after shift (4 days)
• P. Fourie — Sat worked (6h)  ·  Heritage Day worked (8h 30m)

🌟 Praise — most compliments
• N. Nkambule (5)  ·  J. Smith (3)  ·  M. Dlamini (2)

ℹ️ Data notes  • 1 person with no clock-ins this week — check capture.
Full detail: <link>
Why this is short on purpose. The full ranked table, the holiday cross-check and every confirm-these-days row belong on a linked page. The Cliq message carries only the counts, the exceptions and the names that need a conversation.

Schedule, setup and the two cron jobs

Two jobs: a build job that assembles the data and the linked page, and a send job that posts the short version to #HRM: BUS. Run the build overnight so Wednesday morning only has to send.

Job A — hrm-weekly-attendance-build

Wednesday 05:00 (SAST) · read-only · writes the dataset + HTML to AI-Drive

1.  Resolve the week: Monday–Friday of the week just ended.
2.  Attendance:  getAttendanceDetailedReport  (no erecno, one call per calendar month in range)
3.  Summary:     getAttendanceSummary          (cross-check only)
4.  Shifts:      getDepartmentShiftGrouping    -> allocated vs total headcount
5.  Policy:      getAttendancePolicySettings  (hours_per_day, tolerance baseline)
6.  Holidays:    listholidays                  (for the "holiday worked" bucket)
7.  Praise:      getFeedback  fbmode=myteam     (positive counts per person)
8.  Compute everything in code; write dataset JSON, then render the linked HTML page.
9.  Drop the message body for Job B at:  AI-Drive/Agent-Output/hrm/hrm-brief-<date>.txt

Job B — hrm-weekly-attendance-send

Wednesday 06:30 (SAST) · the only write in the whole flow

1.  Read hrm-brief-<date>.txt
2.  list_channels {joined:true}  ->  find unique_name == "infhumanresourceteam"  ->  chat_id
3.  send_message_to_chat (path_variables + body) -> #HRM: BUS
4.  Record the returned message id as the delivery receipt.
Register the jobs. Create both as scheduled tasks on the Hermes profile that owns the workplace automation, delivering to #HRM: BUS on the Wednesday cadence. Confirm the timezone is Africa/Johannesburg before the first fire — a job set in UTC posts at 08:30 local, two hours after the GM has read his coffee.

Tooling prerequisites

  • The profile must be able to reach the Zoho People and Cliq product endpoints. If its MetaMCP namespace carries no Zoho, hit the direct endpoints in secrets.env — fastest route, no allowlist.
  • The Cliq send tool is a write; keep every People tool read-only. If the namespace is allowlisted, confirm ZohoCliq_send_message_to_chat is active for this profile and only this one.
  • Resolve the exact send_message_to_chat payload shape on the first build (path variables + body) before scheduling the send job — it is the one step that cannot be dry-run safely.

Rules and guardrails

read-only The attendance system is never modified

No create, update, adjust or delete against Zoho People — not a punch, not a shift, not a leave record. This job observes. If a record is wrong, the brief reports it; correcting it is a human action in People.

money & legal Stay Ricardo's call

The brief measures time on the clock. It says nothing about whether that time was authorised, productive or payable, and it never frames anyone as owed an amount or as a disciplinary case. Anything that reads as money, overtime pay or a consequence is flagged for the principal and stops there — the agent prepares, Ricardo signs.

privacy What never leaves the system

Counts, names and durations only. No clock-in GPS coordinates, no employee ID numbers, no leave reasons, no health or medical detail, and no verbatim private messages — in the Cliq message, in the linked page, or in any log.

Numbers must reconcile before the message goes out

  • Per-person extra time on holiday dates exceeds the monthly summary by the size of the holiday shift — this is systematic and both figures are real. Publish one, state which, and say what the other would give.
  • Assert the conservation check: rostered hours = sum of the per-day shift lengths over present days. If it fails, the shift data is wrong and the brief is wrong.
  • A person with zero clock-ins is an attendance-capture finding, said loudly and apart from the ranking — never buried in a list of zeroes.
Never invent a number or fill a blank. If a call fails, report the actual error and ship the brief without that section, labelled missing. A Wednesday brief with a silent gap is worse than a Wednesday brief that admits one.
