The Maio Household Week

Four parts, seven days, one colour per person. Across a row: who owns the job; down a column: whose day. A standing week, no switches — plans and the buy list are on the last sheet. Ricardo pays for all of it.

Standing week · revised 7 November 2026

Who does what

Julie's window

17:00–20:00 is Sofia — feeding and settling. Dinner, baths and bedtime belong to someone else in that hour.

Nights

Julie takes every night, 22:00–06:00, one line. So the morning run is Ricardo's and the kitchen is Julie's.

The rotation

Homework every school day, five sittings, Ricardo → Julie → Lily: 2 · 2 · 1 this week, even next. Boxes are packed the night before, so Friday has no cell.

Who is in the house

Domestic Mon, Tue, Thu, Sat. Gardener Tue and Fri — outside only; the pool is Ricardo's. Lily runs both. Sunday the house is ours.

Stock never runs out

Wednesday Julie checks food and household consumables on one list. Thursday Lily buys, Monday she tops up. Nappies and wipes are Julie's on Sunday.

Ricardo's lane

Drop-off, every pick-up, extra-murals, homework Mon and Thu, bath and bed, Sofia's bath on Sunday, school admin, Sunday walk-round and bins, Friday take-out, the braai when he lights it. He pays for the house.

Fixed points

Drop-off 07:30, doors close 08:00. Grade 1 out 13:30, Donatella 14:00, aftercare 13:30/14:00, field collection 15:00, Friday 13:15 — Reddam House Durbanville calendar.

Who pays

Ricardo pays for everything — bills, fees, groceries, the domestic, the gardener, petrol, anything signed. The board schedules; it never approves or sends.

Worth confirming

Laundry sits inside the domestic's four days; the refuse night is checked against the street — City of Cape Town collections and recycling.

The domestic — action plan

Four days a week, run by Lily. The week is fixed; the month rotates one deep job onto each Saturday.

  • Mon Bedrooms and bathrooms — linen changed, bathrooms scrubbed, floors, mirrors.
  • Tue Kitchen and living — counters, cupboard fronts, oven front, fridge shelves, dust, glass.
  • Thu The girls' rooms and the study — dust, vacuum, blinds; uniforms checked with Lily.
  • Sat The whole house reset — floors washed, kitchen deep, bedding, courtyard, bins washed.

The month, one per Saturday:

  • Week 1 Windows, inside and out, and the tracks.
  • Week 2 Fridge and freezer emptied, shelves washed, mattress turned.
  • Week 3 Curtains down and washed, skirting boards, door tops.
  • Week 4 Rugs shampooed, upholstery, cupboards inside.

The standard: bathrooms dry, floors free of grit, surfaces clear, nothing left on the stairs, and the list she came with ticked off before she goes.

The gardener — plan

Tuesday and Friday, garden and outside only — the pool stays with Ricardo. Lily runs him the same way.

  • Tue Mow and edges — lawn cut, edges trimmed, clippings cleared, beds weeded.
  • Fri Beds and branches — hedges, shrubs, deadheading, leaves swept, compost turned, paths blown.

The month, one job at a time:

  • Week 1 Feed and mulch the beds; lawn fertiliser.
  • Week 2 Irrigation — drippers, timers, leaks, and the borehole pump if it has run dry.
  • Week 3 Trees — dead wood out, low branches up, palms and shrubs shaped.
  • Week 4 Pests and weeds, then tools cleaned, oiled and hung up.

The season:

  • Spring Feed and mulch, seed the bare patches, stake what is tall.
  • Summer Water early, shade the seedlings, watch for pests after rain.
  • Autumn Leaves cleared and composted, lawn scarified.
  • Winter Prune, lift bulbs, feed the soil and plan the beds.

The standard: no bare patches left unsown, no cuttings left in the beds, nothing sprayed on a windy day, and the gate shut behind him. Anything structural — a wall, a pump, a tree down — comes to Ricardo before it is touched.

The list — Julie checks, Lily buys

Julie checks Wednesday against the fridge, the pantry and the cupboard under the sink. Lily buys Thursday. Monday is the top-up. One list, both of them on it.

  • Chicken · mince · fish · frozen peas · stock
  • Box fillers — biscuits, raisins, crackers, cheese sticks, a treat

The house

  • Dishwashing liquid · dishwasher tablets · sponges · cloths
  • Laundry powder · softener · bleach · floor cleaner · bathroom spray
  • Toilet paper · kitchen paper · tissues · bin bags
  • Foil · cling film · baking paper
  • Soap · shampoo · toothpaste · sunblock
  • Nappies and wipes — Julie's, every Sunday
  • Bulbs · batteries · candles · the odd hardware bit

The rule: if it is the last one, it goes on the list that day. Nothing gets bought twice, and nothing runs out on a Sunday night.
