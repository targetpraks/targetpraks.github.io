The Maio Household Week

Four parts, seven days, one colour per person. Across a row: who owns the job. Down a column: whose day. A standing week — same shape until a cell changes, no switches. Ricardo pays for all of it.

Standing week · revised 7 November 2026

Who does what

Julie's window

17:00–20:00 is Sofia — feeding and settling. Dinner, baths and bedtime belong to someone else in that hour.

Nights

Julie takes every night, 22:00–06:00, one line. So the morning run is Ricardo's and the kitchen is Julie's.

The rotation

Homework every school day, five sittings, Ricardo → Julie → Lily: 2 · 2 · 1 this week, even next. Boxes are packed the night before, so Friday has no cell.

Who is in the house

Domestic Mon, Tue, Thu, Sat. Gardener Tue and Fri — outside only; the pool is Ricardo's. Lily runs both. Sunday the house is ours.

Stock never runs out

Wednesday Julie checks food and household consumables on one list. Thursday Lily buys, Monday she tops up. Nappies and wipes are Julie's on Sunday.

Ricardo's lane

Drop-off, every pick-up, extra-murals, homework Mon and Thu, bath and bed, Sofia's bath on Sunday, school admin, Sunday walk-round and bins, Friday take-out. He pays for the house. He does not cook.

Fixed points

Drop-off 07:30, doors close 08:00. Grade 1 out 13:30, Donatella 14:00, aftercare 13:30/14:00, field collection 15:00, Friday 13:15 — Reddam House Durbanville calendar.

Who pays

Ricardo pays for everything — bills, fees, groceries, the domestic, the gardener, petrol, anything signed. The board schedules; it never approves or sends.

Worth confirming

Laundry sits inside the domestic's four days; the refuse night is checked against the street — City of Cape Town collections and recycling.
