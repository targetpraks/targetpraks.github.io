Maio household · weekly planner

The Week

Four parts of the house, seven days, one colour per person. Read across a row to see who owns that job; read down a column to see whose day it is. A standing week, not a dated one — the same shape runs until a cell changes: everyone home, the children here, domestic Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday, gardener twice a week. No switches.

Ricardo never cooks — the cooking row is Julie and Lily only. Standing week · revised 7 November 2026

Who does what

Julie's window

17:00–20:00 is feeding and settling Sofia. Nothing else is booked over it — dinner, baths and bedtime belong to everyone else in that hour.

Nights

Ricardo 22:00–02:00, Julie 02:00–06:00, every night. That split is why the morning run sits with Ricardo and the kitchen sits with Julie.

The rotation

Homework and the next day's lunchboxes travel together and rotate Ricardo → Julie → Lily, one school day each. It carries into next week, so the same weekday never lands on the same person twice running.

Who is in the house

Domestic: Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday — cleaning, laundry, unpacking. Gardener: Tuesday and Friday — garden and outside only; the pool stays with Ricardo. Sunday the house is ours.

Stock never runs out

Lily checks the pantry, the fridge and the household consumables midweek; Julie buys against that list with the Sunday grocery order. Detergents, paper and bulbs ride on the same list as the food.

Ricardo's lane

Drop-off 07:30, every pick-up, extra-murals Monday to Thursday, homework Monday and Thursday, bins out Sunday night. He does not cook — the kitchen is Julie's and Lily's.

Fixed points

Drop-off 07:30, doors close 08:00. Grade 1 ends 13:30, Donatella 14:00, aftercare 13:30/14:00, field collection 15:00, Friday school ends 13:15 — Reddam House Durbanville calendar.

Money and legal

Bills, school fees and anything signed stay with Ricardo. The board schedules and reminds; it never approves or sends.

Worth confirming

Laundry is assumed inside the domestic's four days; the gardener's second day is drawn as Friday; refuse and recycling are checked against the street's night — City of Cape Town collections and recycling services.
