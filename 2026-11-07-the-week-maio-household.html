The Maio Household Week

Four parts, seven days, one colour per person. Across a row: who owns the job; down a column: whose day. A standing week, no switches — the three plan sheets follow. Ricardo pays for all of it.

The week on one sheet

All four parts together, for the fridge. Each part has its own sheet, larger, behind its tab.

Who does what

Ricardo's lane

Drop-off, every pick-up, extra-murals, homework Mon and Thu, bath and bed, Sofia's bath on Sunday, school admin, Sunday walk-round and bins, Friday take-out, the braai when he lights it. He pays for the house.

Who pays

Ricardo pays for everything — bills, fees, groceries, the domestic, the gardener, petrol, anything signed. The board schedules; it never approves or sends.

Who is in the house

Domestic Mon, Tue, Thu, Sat. Gardener Tue and Fri — outside only; the pool is Ricardo's. Lily runs both. Sunday the house is ours.

Worth confirming

Laundry sits inside the domestic's four days; the refuse night is checked against the street — City of Cape Town collections and recycling.

Notes

The domestic — the week

Four days, eight to four, lunch at twelve. Monday is beds, linen and the braai washed; Tuesday kitchen and ironing; Thursday the girls' rooms and the study; Saturday the whole house and the deep job. Lily runs her — the days, the standard, the list ticked off before she goes.

Week 1Windows, inside and out, and the tracks.
Week 2Fridge and freezer emptied, shelves washed, mattress turned.
Week 3Curtains down and washed, skirting boards, door tops.
Week 4Rugs shampooed, upholstery, cupboards inside.

The standard: bathrooms dry, floors free of grit, surfaces clear, nothing left on the stairs.

Notes

The gardener — the week

Tuesday and Friday, eight to four, lunch at twelve. Front garden in the morning, back garden after lunch. The lawn is mown once a week, on the Tuesday, and the solar panel on the geyser is washed once a month. The pool stays with Ricardo.

Week 1Feed and mulch the beds; lawn fertiliser.
Week 2Irrigation — drippers, timers, leaks, the pump.
Week 3Trees — dead wood out, low branches up, shrubs shaped.
Week 4Pests and weeds, then tools cleaned and hung up.
SpringFeed and mulch, seed the bare patches, stake what is tall.
SummerWater early, shade the seedlings, watch for pests after rain.
AutumnLeaves cleared and composted, lawn scarified.
WinterPrune, lift bulbs, feed the soil, plan the beds.

The standard: no bare patches left unsown, no cuttings left in the beds, nothing sprayed on a windy day, the gate shut behind him. Anything structural — a wall, a pump, a tree down — comes to Ricardo before it is touched.

Stock never runs out

Wednesday Julie checks food and household consumables on one list. Thursday Lily buys, Monday she tops up. Nappies and wipes are Julie's on Sunday.

Julie and Lily
