6.2 · Why a folder cannot substitute for a list

In ClickUp a folder is a container and a list is the thing a task is filed into. A task needs a list id; a folder has no task collection of its own. So the KPD lane can hold a brand document's pointer and nothing else until a list exists inside it.

7 · The decision — four ways forward

All four are yours. None involves an agent creating structure on its own initiative, and none has been started.

  1. Build the standard set in `MKT SLA - KPD`
    What it takes
    Your go on creating 7 folders and 19 lists in this one lane.
    What it gives
    KPD gets its own lane with the same shape as every other brand; brand reporting stays clean; the document finally points at a lane that can hold the work.
    What it costs
    Breaks the standing ban on creating folders and lists in MKT, which you set to stop parallel trees. It is your call to lift it for one lane, not an agent's.
  2. Keep filing KPD work in `MKT COR - DID`
    What it takes
    Nothing to build. It is what happens today.
    What it gives
    No new structure; the 7 KPD items already there stay where they are.
    What it costs
    KPD work reports under Divorced Dads. Every KPD task pollutes the DID lane's numbers, and the client and the lane disagree by design.
  3. Intake only in DID, and tag the brand
    What it takes
    Keep DID's `Briefed-In` as the single door and put a KPD tag on each task.
    What it gives
    One intake list, no new structure, and a tag that can filter KPD out of DID's numbers.
    What it costs
    The pillar lists still hold KPD tasks under DID folders; a tag is a report filter, not a home. Someone must keep tagging.
  4. Leave ClickUp out of KPD for now
    What it takes
    Point the document at the empty lane, file nothing new, and keep the Superhuman doc as the record.
    What it gives
    No structure, no mislabelled work, no new debt.
    What it costs
    Work that lives only in a document has no status, owner or due date. The 4 open KPD items already in DID show the team is producing work that needs a home.

What I would pick, if asked: Option A, scoped tight — the seven folders and nineteen lists in MKT SLA - KPD only, mirroring DID exactly, with the KPD document then pointed at its own lane. It is the only option that stops KPD work reporting as Divorced Dads work, which is the defect every other option leaves in place. But it is your ban to lift, and nothing happens until you say so.
