01 The short answer

Two sentences for the wall, then the detail underneath.

Donatello — Grade 4

Reddam, cricket, then hockey

Stay at Reddam on the condoned pass, with the support plan running from day one. One team sport at a time: cricket in Terms 1 and 4, hockey in Terms 2 and 3. Nothing else on a school afternoon.

  • 3 after-school afternoons a season, no early mornings
  • No contact sport — a standing rule, not a preference
  • The open hole: no facilitator appointed, and the school will not run the plan without one

Gianna — Grade 2

Reddam, swim squad, Hip Hop, piano

Everything she already does, plus the squad place she has been given: swim on Wednesday, Hip Hop on Tuesday and Thursday, piano in school hours. Sports Hour is compulsory on Monday and Tuesday.

  • 4 after-school days, no early mornings, Tuesday ends at 15:00
  • Swim letter says either or both sessions — take Wednesday only
  • One real decision left: keep piano at the 2027 rate, roughly double last year's

The one date that decides the year

4 December 2026

The facilitator's name and a recent police clearance go to Mrs Prins. That is my date, not the school's — it leaves three weeks of slack before the school reopens. The Grade 4 place rests on it. Every sport decision below is easy compared to this one.

07 The year, term by term

Four terms, one review point each. The reviews are the point of the plan: the support plan is only as good as the check that it is working.

Term 1 · Mid-January – late March

  • Cricket (Mon match, Tue + Wed practice).
  • Facilitator in the classroom from day one; the 1:1 literacy slot written into his timetable in week one.
  • English and Afrikaans clinics running weekly; speech therapy continuing.
  • Gianna: Sports Hour starts, Hip Hop Level 2 starts, swim squad starts.

Review point. End-February: first reading review. If he is not moving, the school plan gets re-opened — not in November.

Term 2 · April – June

  • Hockey replaces cricket (Tue practice, Thu matches, Fri session).
  • Mid-year reports; the 25% extra time and scribe arrangements are tested in real assessment conditions for the first time.
  • Gianna: winter Sports Hour; optional competitive matches (Wed football/netball, Thu hockey).

Review point. Mid-year report: compare reading age against the Term 1 baseline. This is the review that decides whether he stays the course.

Term 3 · July – September

  • Hockey second half; cross country offered as the cheap alternative if hockey numbers thin.
  • Decide Term 4: cricket again, or swimming as the summer base.
  • Gianna: revisit drama vs dance with a term of Grade 2 behind her.

Review point. Book the 2028 sport sign-ups in the first week of Term 4 — the 2027 forms closed fast.

Term 4 · October – early December

  • Cricket or swimming (his call, made in August, not in October).
  • Year-end: the 2028 plan, including when the rowing route starts.

Review point. Write next year's version of this page with what actually happened.
