What to tell the builder

  1. Firebox opening sized to the unit's drawing, with the unit on site before the brickwork is closed up — 10 mm clearance all round, packed with fire rope.
  2. Firebrick lining to the base and sides, and a solid slab under it — masonry holds heat, mild steel does not like sitting on a cold wet base.
  3. A cowl and flue sized for the opening, not a generic 300 mm, plus a spark arrestor if there is anything combustible above.
  4. Ash drawer that slides out, and a wood store built in beside it while the brick is going up — costs almost nothing then, ugly to add later.
  5. A 15 A plug on the motor side of the braai — the skewer motors are 12 V, but the electric option and a light both want power.
  6. Granite or concrete landing at the front edge, and a 100 mm non-combustible margin from any timber.
