Does it cover an ambulance and a check-up — and which number do you call?

Checked against the offer letter dated 19 October 2026, the official BonCore 2026 benefit brochure, the 2026 Bonitas SOS emergency-services sheet and Bonitas' 2026 Integrated Report.

1Ambulance — covered

Yes, through Bonitas SOS

Emergency medical services are a scheme-wide benefit, not a per-plan line. Bonitas' own 2026 Integrated Report lists “Ambulance and emergency services for all plans” under the cover provided by AZOZA (previously Europ Assistance), the designated service provider for Bonitas SOS.

The 2026 Bonitas SOS sheet says the benefit covers:

  • transfer to the closest appropriate facility, by road or air;
  • emergency response from the scene of the incident;
  • inter-hospital transfers (with authorisation);
  • instructions on managing the emergency while the ambulance is on its way;
  • 24/7 virtual doctor consultations via the Member App.

Call them first. The same sheet is explicit: if a member does not contact Bonitas SOS in an emergency, “they may be held liable for the full costs incurred”, and “ambulances are to be used only for life-threatening medical emergencies. The use of an ambulance for cases that are not deemed emergencies will not be covered.”

2Check-up — yes, but it is a screening, not a full physical

So: the yearly “check-up” in Bonitas language is the pharmacy wellness screening — free and worth using. A comprehensive physical with a full blood panel is not a BonCore benefit; it has to come out of the R1 000 Booster or the member's own pocket.

4What the letter locks in before any of that applies

A three-month general waiting period from 1 November 2026. No claims are paid for the first three months — so nothing routine is funded until 1 February 2027. An emergency that qualifies as a PMB condition is normally still covered during a waiting period under the Medical Schemes Act, but get that in writing from Bonitas before relying on it.

A twelve-month exclusion on bronchitis (to November 2027), including related conditions clinically shown to be caused by it.

The R2 231,25 contribution already carries a 75% late-joiner penalty. The BonCore 2026 rate is R1 275 a month; 75% of that is R956,25, which is exactly the gap to R2 231,25. The penalty is a permanent loading, so if there was any earlier scheme cover, even years ago, it is worth asking Bonitas to recheck the “34 years without cover” figure — every credited year cuts the penalty.

5Two things worth asking on the same call

  • How does a BonCore member complete the online mental health assessment? Unlocking the R1 000 Benefit Booster needs it, but the wellness sheet says the online questionnaire is available on the Member App “excluding BonCap and BonCore” — the plan brochure was printed before that caveat.
  • Which participating pharmacies near him do the wellness screening, and whether the screening is subject to the three-month waiting period.
