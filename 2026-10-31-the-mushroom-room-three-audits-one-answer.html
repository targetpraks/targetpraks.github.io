Your corrections, and what they cost

Seven levels instead of five, colonising outside the tent, two flushes a block: together they take the room from 27.6 kg a month to 77.25, and the cost a kilo from R396.52 to R208.35. That is the difference between a tent that cannot pay for itself and one that pays back in a month if it sells.

Every week the tent does both flushes. A block is cut on flush 1 and again on flush 2 about three weeks later, and 21 blocks turn over every week — so each week carries 21 first-flush harvests and 21 second-flush ones, 3 of each a day, against 3 blocks in and 3 out. The split is half and half: 0.43 kg on the first cut, 0.42 on the second.

One number still rests on something nobody has measured. If flush 2 fails, the room makes 39 kg a month and R4,463 instead of R24,538 — the second flush carries half the crop, so a lost one halves the room. And Joe's 0.9–1.2 kg a block may have been flush 1 alone, in which case a block makes more and everything above improves. Five blocks on a scale settles it.

What the staff do with it

The Plan tab now opens with the routine, not the forecast. One person, every day, five things:

  1. Cook a load in the sterilisation tank — 6 blocks a run
  2. Mix and bag the load that cooled — 6 blocks
  3. Spawn, tag and put them on the rack in the dark — 6 blocks
  4. Move colonised blocks into the tent — 3 blocks
  5. Harvest both flushes, weigh, clear the spent blocks — 3 first flush, 3 second flush

One run a day gives 42 blocks a week against the 21 the tent needs, so the tank is not the limit — twice a week gives 12 and the tent fills to 57%. A skipped day costs three blocks for three weeks running. Today, at 1.1 kg a month sold, the honest instruction is "one block when someone orders"; the routine above is what holds a full tent the day sales reach 19 kg.

What to do now — in this order

  1. Weigh five blocks, flush 1 and flush 2. A R300 scale settles the yield, the flush split and the block weight at once.
  2. Count the colonisation rack. It has to hold 63 blocks to keep the tent full. If it holds 40, the tent runs at two-thirds and the model says so.
  3. Sell it before anything is added. 19.2 kg a month at R526, through the eleven accounts already on the book.

The line that moved The trigger for a second tent is now 12 kg a week over four consecutive invoiced weeks — two-thirds of what one tent can make. The old 6 kg line was set when the room could only make 6.4.
