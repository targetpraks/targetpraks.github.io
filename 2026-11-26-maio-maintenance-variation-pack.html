Section 01 How to read this pack

Every figure here is traceable to a document. Where the evidence stops, this pack says so instead of filling the gap.

Five kinds of material sit behind it: the signed settlement agreement of 28 April 2021 and the final order of 4 June 2021 that made it an order of court; the addendum of 6 December 2023 that recorded the move to Cape Town; the bank ledger rebuilt from the monthly Bank Zero, FNB and Discovery statements; the school and medical records from Reddam House Durbanville, Discovery Health and the treating practitioners; and the attorney correspondence with Merchak Inc. and Alice Attorneys, alongside the client's own file of drafts, letters and schedules.

Amounts are in South African rand. Superscript tags in gold — E1 to E24 — point to the evidence index in Section 13. Anything marked gap is something the attorney will need that this pack does not have.

Four limits stated up front

1. The ledger ends 7 July 2026. Bank transactions from August to November 2026 have not yet been captured, so the current-month picture is built from the recurring instruments (medical aid, school, maintenance) rather than from fresh bank lines.

2. There is no sworn income-and-expenditure affidavit. The income figures here come from bank deposits and 2019–2020 payslips. A maintenance court will require a sworn statement, assessments and, if his means are challenged, the company's financials.

3. There is no valuation of the household contents. The R172 386,80 in Section 02 is a floor built from purchase records — the furniture and homeware bought new between 2016 and 2021 — not an inventory or an insurer's schedule.

4. This is a fact pack, not advice. It sets out the position and the instruction. It does not predict an outcome, and it should not be filed or sent to the other side without an attorney's review.

Why this matters to the variation

By the end of June 2021 she had the R1 400 000 in cash, the car, and the contents of the house — and he had the children's ongoing costs. There is no outstanding capital claim between them; clause 11.1 closed every claim except maintenance. So the only live question is what the children need now, and who pays it. That is a cleaner frame than a fight about the past.

The clause the consultation turns on

Paragraph 2.7 does not require proof of changed circumstances to bring a maintenance application. That matters tactically: the case can be run on the children's actual needs and the parties' actual contributions, with the change in circumstances as reinforcement rather than as the gateway. The attorney should confirm how paragraph 2.7 interacts with section 6 of the Maintenance Act 99 of 1998 in a court that already has the order.

What the addendum settles, and what it does not

It settles the relocation. Ms Maio's decision to move the children to Cape Town — which clause 1.12.6 of the 2021 agreement required his written consent for — was documented, agreed and signed. The children were then enrolled at Reddam, the school the addendum names.

It does not settle maintenance. Clause 1.11.11 does no more than restate the obligation "as per the settlement agreement", and the addendum was never made an order of court. So the maintenance position in 2026 is still governed by paragraphs 2.1 to 2.7 of the 2021 deed — and the addendum's contact terms are contractual, enforceable between the parties, but not an order.

What the 2026 draft variation would have done — and did not

The draft is a contact agreement, not a money agreement. It would have given him a fixed 10-day term-time schedule with a structured objection process, a facilitator with binding powers, and a parenting coordinator. It expressly preserved paragraph 2 of the settlement agreement, so the R5 971,50 cash maintenance and the school and medical obligations would have continued unchanged. E3

It was not signed. The other side's attorneys wrote on 1 June 2026 that he had told their client he could not pay the children's extramural activities that month and had asked her to contribute, and that he was not assisting with school clothes and glasses — and proposed that his contact be limited to alternate weekends until his finances improve. E4 That letter is the clearest statement of the other side's position and the reason a variation application, rather than another round of drafting, is now on the table.

Two of her five short payments are corroborated. May and June were paid at R5 971,50 — exactly R241,99 below the rate she asserts. The bank record cannot contradict her on those two months.

The rate question is real, and it needs the right index. Clause 2.6 requires escalation by CPI on 1 May each year. It was applied in 2022, 2023 and 2024, and not in 2025 or 2026. Her R6 213,49 is a 4% step on R5 971,50. Whether 4% is the correct figure — and which CPI, the annual average or the March release — must be checked against the Stats SA publication before a cent is conceded or refused.

Her silence is evidence too. Between 6 June and 19 October 2026 she did not chase maintenance once. Section 06 What has changed since 2021

Six changes, each with a date. Together they are the substance of the variation: the order was made when he had one household in Gauteng and two children. He now has two households, a wife, a baby, and a dependent father-in-law — and he moved provinces to stay in his children's lives.

1 · The children moved to Cape Town — with his written consent — and he followed

The settlement agreement barred their relocation outside Gauteng without his written consent (clause 1.12.6). He gave it. The addendum signed on 6 December 2023 opens by recording that "Lauren has decided to relocate to Cape Town", and sets out the contact that would follow. The children were enrolled at Reddam House Durbanville — the school the addendum names — from December 2023. He then relocated from Gauteng to the Western Cape to remain a weekly presence rather than a holiday parent. His address on the June 2026 declaration is in Welgemoed, Bellville, and the family home during 2026 was 51 Trichardt Street, Welgemoed. E1 E2 E8 E18
