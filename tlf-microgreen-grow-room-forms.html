The sow log

one line a sowingFORM 02

One line a sowing. Variety, how many trays, and which section they went into — stock order, scheduled growth, or ad hoc. The blackout-out date is the day the stack comes apart; count it forward from the sow date by the variety sheet, not by habit.

Sow dateVarietyTraysSectionGrams seedOut of blackoutInitials
Sections: SO stock order · SG scheduled growth · AH ad hoc. Every tray carries its tag from this line — variety, sow date, tray count — and the tag travels to the punnet.

Harvest and dispatch

cut · weigh · pack · sendFORM 03

Cut, weighed, packed, out. The grams are the number the room lives on — the model says 200–300 g a tray and the research average is 136 g, so the only figure worth arguing about is this column.

DateVarietyTraysTotal gg a trayPacked asBatch codeTo
Packed as: 100 g clamshell · 250 g bag · 500 g bag. Cold: in the cold room inside the hour, 5 °C, covered. Out: under two hours door to door, Tuesday and Friday harvests the same day.
Clear and clean, the same afternoon: spent tray out — medium and roots to compost, mat to the bin, never beside the rack · tray washed, sanitised, dried upside down · level wiped · floor swept · anything mouldy gone from the room.

The weekly clean

once a week · top downFORM 04
