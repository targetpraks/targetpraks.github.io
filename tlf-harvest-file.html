The Concept

The Harvest File is a premium, tabbed recipe book given to The Local Farmer's most valuable customers. It works like a classic flip file — open, flip to a segment, pull a recipe — but it is built to be updated forever.

  • Flip-file format. A premium binder with tabbed segments: Welcome, Cocktails, Salads, Foods, Pantry & Powders, Seasonal Updates. Each recipe lives on its own dated card, so any card can be swapped without reprinting the book.
  • Always current. Every recipe card carries a version stamp. New harvests, new varieties, new dishes — the file is refreshed on a fixed cadence, and customers always hold the latest edition.
  • Digital twin. A QR code on the cover opens the live digital edition — the same recipes, updated monthly, on any phone. The physical file is the keepsake; the digital edition is the living document.
  • Built on the farm. Every recipe features what we grow: 32 microgreen varieties and 6 mixes, oyster, Lion's Mane and king oyster mushrooms, plus our mushroom and microgreen powders and chips.
  • A sales engine, not a brochure. Each recipe lists its featured products with a reorder QR — inspiration converts directly into orders.

One-line pitch: "The farm's best ideas, in your kitchen, always fresh." The Harvest File turns a supplier relationship into a partnership — and keeps our products on their menu.

2

Why Now — The Opportunity

Restaurant and wholesale clients don't buy more because they don't know what to do with the product. The Harvest File removes that barrier.

  • Menu inspiration drives orders. Microgreens and mushrooms are high-margin menu items, but chefs need ideas. A recipe book that arrives with the delivery turns product into dishes — and dishes into repeat orders.
  • VIP retention is the cheapest growth. Acquiring a new client costs 5–7× more than keeping one. A tangible, premium gift that updates monthly keeps TLF top-of-mind every single week.
  • The farm is a content engine. Microgreens cycle in 10–14 days; mushrooms in 28–35 days. New varieties and seasonal harvests give us a natural, endless supply of new recipes — the book never runs out of material.
  • Differentiation. Competitors sell boxes of greens. We sell a partnership: recipes, training, and a VIP program. That is a harder relationship to replace.
  • Value-added products get a stage. Mushroom powder, microgreen powder and chips (12+ month shelf life) are our highest-margin products. The Pantry tab teaches customers how to use them daily.

The Flip File — Anatomy

A5 landscape premium binder, ~40 sleeves, tabbed dividers. Every element is designed to be swapped, updated and re-issued.

TabContentsUpdate
WelcomeVIP welcome letter, how the file works, QR code to the digital edition, farm story & season calendarQuarterly
CocktailsMicrogreen syrups & garnishes, mushroom-infused spirits, zero-proof optionsMonthly
SaladsMicrogreen-forward salads, dressings, crispy mushroom toppingsMonthly
FoodsSmall plates & mains featuring oyster, Lion's Mane and king oyster mushroomsMonthly
Pantry & PowdersMushroom powder lattes, seasoning blends, microgreen powder boosters, chips snacksQuarterly
Seasonal UpdatesNew-recipe insert packs, harvest highlights, chef spotlightsQuarterly
  • Recipe card format. One recipe per card, printed on 300gsm stock with a version stamp (e.g. v2.1 · Aug 2026), season tag, prep time, yield, featured products and a reorder QR.
  • Swap, don't reprint. When a recipe updates, the customer receives a single replacement card — not a new book. The file stays current at minimal cost.
  • Digital twin. The QR on the cover opens the live edition: same tabs, same recipes, searchable, with video links and push updates. Physical file = keepsake; digital = living document.

Segments & Sample Recipes

Four working recipes to prove the concept — one per core segment, all built on products we already grow and sell.

Lion's Mane Old Fashioned

Cocktails
Prep: 5 min · Yield: 1 serve · Featured: Lion's Mane powder, microgreen garnish · v1.0 · Aug 2026
  • 60 ml bourbon or rye whiskey
  • 15 ml Lion's Mane maple syrup (1 tsp Lion's Mane powder warmed into 100 ml maple syrup)
  • 2 dashes aromatic bitters
  • Orange peel + a small bunch of basil or pea-shoot microgreens to garnish
Why it works: Lion's Mane's gentle umami rounds out the whiskey's oak; the microgreen garnish adds fresh aroma with every sip. A signature cocktail that puts two TLF products on one glass.

The Garden Harvest Salad

Salads
Prep: 10 min · Yield: 2 serves · Featured: TLF 6-mix microgreens, crispy oyster mushrooms · v1.0 · Aug 2026
  • 100 g TLF microgreen mix (pea shoots, sunflower, radish, broccoli, basil, amaranth)
  • 50 g oyster mushrooms, pan-fried until golden and crisp
  • Citrus vinaigrette: 2 tbsp orange juice, 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp honey, ½ tsp Dijon, salt
  • 2 tbsp toasted sunflower seeds
Why it works: Microgreens deliver concentrated flavour and nutrition in a light, chef-friendly base; crispy oyster mushrooms add the umami crunch that turns a salad into a dish.

King Oyster "Scallops"

Foods

  • Microgreen salad to serve

The Update Engine

The book is only valuable if it stays fresh. The update engine is simple, scheduled and tied to the farm's own rhythm.

1
Harvest → Recipe
New variety or seasonal glut becomes a tested recipe. Microgreens cycle every 10–14 days; mushrooms every 28–35 days — a constant pipeline of material.
2
Digital refresh
Digital edition updates monthly. VIPs get a short "What's new" note with the two new recipes — no printing, no postage.
3
Quarterly insert pack
Physical file holders receive a printed pack: new cards, one seasonal feature, a harvest note. Swap in, done.
4
Retire & archive
Outdated cards are retired with a version note. The digital archive keeps every edition — a growing library that shows the farm's evolution.

  • Reorder QR on every card. Scan → the recipe's featured products pre-loaded in the order form. Inspiration converts to revenue in one tap.
  • Chef credit. Signature dishes carry the chef's name — partners become co-authors and promote the book themselves.
  • Upgrade path. Gold members see what Platinum includes. The physical file is the visible status symbol that drives upgrades.

Brand & Design Direction

Premium farm-to-table: the file should feel like a gift from the farm, not a supplier catalogue.

  • Palette. Deep forest green, leaf green, cream and kraft — earthy, premium, unmistakably "grown here".
  • Typography. Serif display (Playfair-style) for recipe names; clean sans for instructions. Editorial, not corporate.
  • Photography. Macro shots of microgreens (dew, texture, colour), moody mushroom plates, natural light. Every recipe photographed before it ships.
  • Details. Botanical line-art dividers, kraft cover with debossed logo, numbered edition on the inside cover ("No. 014 of 100").
  • Digital twin styling. The same palette and type in the live edition — the QR opens something that looks like the book, not a website.

Recommendation

One recommendation, not a menu: build the digital edition first, pilot the physical file with the top 10 restaurant clients, then scale.

  • Start digital. It costs almost nothing, updates monthly by default, and proves the recipe engine before we print a single binder.
  • Pilot physical with 10. The top 10 restaurant clients get the full experience — file, launch cards, first insert pack. Their feedback shapes the final design before we spend on 50+ units.
  • Then scale. Open the Gold digital tier to retail VIPs, roll Platinum to 50 clients, and lock the quarterly insert calendar.
  • Own it in-house. Design, photography and the digital edition are all buildable inside the group (INFX) — the only external cost is print and postage.

Decision needed: approve the concept and the 30-day build sprint. First deliverable: the digital edition with the 12 launch recipes, ready to show the pilot clients.
