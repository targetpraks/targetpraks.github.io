01 What this document now is

Version one of this PRD mapped the document's 70 tables onto the lane. That was the placement answer. This version is the operating answer: it carries what the brand actually is and what it sells, and then turns the document's filled-in tables into a running system inside ClickUp — including the recurring work that keeps the numbers honest.

Three things changed in the reading:

  1. The Client Profile is not a form — it is filled: 11 client segments with priority scores, 8 services, 17 partners and 25 supplier categories. That is the brand's commercial model, and it belongs in Marketing Plan as the reference the campaigns are aimed at.
  2. The campaign chain exists but is broken in one place: the master content table's Campaign column does not hold a campaign — it holds the content format (static 19, story 15, reel 6, blog 6, Email 4). So none of the 50 published pieces can be attributed to any of the 8 campaigns. §9 shows the fix inside the existing lists.
  3. The SEO section is a template that was never re-pointed at Infinity Brands: its keywords are interior-design terms (Architecture, Interior Design, 3D Rendering, Green Point), its landing page table is empty, and there is no negative-keyword table anywhere. §11 states what that means for the audit Ricardo wants.
The one rule this PRD keeps Everything lands in a list that exists. No folder, no list, no custom field is created by this document; where something has no home (a link index, a contact sheet, a 400-row keyword grid), it becomes the content of the list it serves — a description, a task body, a reference record.
