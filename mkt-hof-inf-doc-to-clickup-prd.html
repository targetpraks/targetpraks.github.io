01 What this PRD decides

The brief: carry one brand document into ClickUp without creating anything. That makes this a placement document, not a build document. It answers three questions in order — what is actually in the document, which existing list owns each part of it, and what can be loaded into those lists for quick reference with nothing new invented.

The document is a template shell that has been filled for Infinity Brands: five sections of instructions, twenty-five field dictionaries that explain how to fill the forms, and 70 tables holding 564 real rows of brand, campaign, content and keyword data. The instructions and dictionaries are the document's own teaching material — they are not ClickUp records, and pushing them into lists is the single most common way a migration turns into noise. They stay in the document.

The rule this PRD follows File by the work a row produces, not by the page it sits on. A page can feed two lists; a table can be one list's records and another list's tasks. Where a section is pure reference — a link index, a contact sheet, a colour palette — it becomes the content of the list it serves, never a new list.
