17 Second review — what it changed

A second Codex pass reviewed this PRD against the live lane and the source. Its verdict was “needs fixes before the monthly cycle starts”, with three of Ricardo's ten asks fully covered and seven thin. Every finding is answered here; the ones that change what gets loaded are marked in the load.

17.1 Campaign attribution is not inferred in the load

§9's chain and §10's join said “the load supplies the Campaign”. It does not, and it should not: the source never records which campaign a piece serves, and title-matching would be invention. The Campaign field is created empty on every content task. A crosswalk must exist first — strategy objective → campaign → topic → output — with Ricardo's approval; until then the honest answer to “which campaign produced this post?” is “unknown”.

17.2 The keyword set is not HOF's in the load

All 25 keywords are interior-design terms from an unadapted template. They load as reference with the provenance stated on every task, and one Ena-owned validation task asks for a franchise-keyword rebuild before any of them is used as a positive target. Nothing is labelled Positive or Negative by assumption.

17.3 One destination per row — the manifest

v3 reconciled totals that the phase list did not add up to. The rule now: each source row has one destination and one treatment (task / record / document-only), and the ledger is the manifest. Where v3 said “39 open audit items to Creative Projects” and “40 audit records to Asset Library”, the split is explicit: all 40 stay as Asset Library records, and the 39 open ones generate a chase task — one task, not 39 duplicates, in Creative Projects.

17.4 The recurring cycle has a contract

Each of the 11 definitions gets one owning list, a first due date set in a second pass, the reporting cutoff, the source account, the required outputs and a reviewer. The loop is audit → prioritised repairs → assigned follow-ups → retest evidence, not “produce a number”. At the stated cadences that is roughly 233 instances a year; the load creates the definitions only, with recurrence set in ClickUp.

17.5 One publication field, and a duplicate to retire

The lane now carries Published URL on all 19 lists, and this work added Live URL on the four content lists. Published URL is canonical; the loader writes there. Live URL is a duplicate to retire on Ricardo's word — nothing is deleted without it.

17.6 Audience, roster and suppliers are three different things

v3 said the top three segments are “the audience every campaign is written for”. The source disproves it: seven of the eight brand campaigns address franchisees. Campaign audiences are now stated per campaign. The brand list is still the space's lanes, and no named client or vendor roster exists anywhere — suppliers are categories (Franchise Legal, Hardware Supplier), not vendors.
