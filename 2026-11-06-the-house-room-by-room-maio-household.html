How this is built

The rules behind the rooms

  1. One owner per job per day. The backup only steps in when the owner says so.
  2. Rooms, not categories. A job lives where it happens — the pool test is outside, the bills are in the study, homework is at the kitchen table. If a job does not belong to a room, it does not belong on the list.
  3. The split is by day. Baths are 4/3 to Ricardo, bedtime mirrors it, Lily cooks Tuesday and Thursday, Ricardo does Sunday's batch.
  4. Feeding is not a chore. The nursery sits outside the fairness maths — 17:00–20:00 and the night feeds are why the rest is distributed the way it is.
  5. Two configurations, not a rota. "Ricardo away" exists so you can price a trip before booking it. It puts 39 hours on Julie; it is not a week to alternate casually.

Assumptions to confirm

  • PoolThere is a pool and it is not on a service contract.
  • LilyHer share is printed in her column — confirm what she actually wants to carry before this goes on the fridge.
  • GardenNo gardener assumed; the Saturday lawn slot is Ricardo's.
  • HelperNo domestic helper assumed. The toggle shows the week with laundry, linen, kitchen-closing and the deep clean covered.
  • CustodyThe board assumes the children are with you. "Kids at Lauren's" empties the school run, the homework table and their bedrooms.
  • BinsRefuse and recycling now share one collection day in Welgemoed — confirm the street's day before trusting the Tuesday slot.
