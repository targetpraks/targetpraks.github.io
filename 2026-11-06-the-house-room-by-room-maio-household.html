How this is built

The rules behind the rooms

  1. One owner per job per day. The backup only steps in when the owner says so.
  2. Rooms, not categories. A job lives where it happens — the pool test is outside, the bills are in the study, homework is at the kitchen table. If a job does not belong to a room, it does not belong on the list.
  3. The split follows the days people are in. Domestic Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday for cleaning, laundry and the deep clean; Lily covers Wednesday, Friday and Sunday. The gardener takes the garden twice a week and nothing else — the pool stays with Ricardo. Lily runs both of them: their days, their standard and the list of what is still outstanding.
  4. The kitchen belongs to Julie and Lily — with one exception: the braai is Ricardo's when he lights it. He pays for all of it, drives the road, runs bath and bed, and keeps the school admin.
  5. Feeding is not a chore. The nursery sits outside the fairness maths — the 17:00–20:00 window and the nights are why the rest is distributed the way it is. Nights are Julie's, one line, 22:00–06:00.
  6. No configurations. No "Ricardo away", no custody switch, no helper toggle. Everyone is home, the children are always here, and the board runs until a cell changes.

Fixed points

  • DomesticMon, Tue, Thu, Sat — cleaning, laundry, uniforms.
  • GardenerTwice a week, drawn Tuesday and Friday. Garden and outside only.
  • StaffLily runs both — days, standards, the list. Managed, not counted.
  • NightsJulie takes every night, 22:00–06:00, one line. Bath and the baby's washing every second day.
  • HomeworkEvery school day, both girls, rotating Ricardo → Julie → Lily.
  • BoxesPacked the night before — Friday has no cell. Lunch is the meal at home; the boxes are a separate job.
  • MoneyRicardo pays. The board schedules and reminds; it never approves or sends.
  • BinsSunday night, with the house walk-round.
