What to know before you pay

Six carts, not one — and that is the whole finding

  • Prices are the listed selling prices read from each supplier's own product page on 1 November 2026, before delivery. They move; re-check the row you are about to pay for.
  • Six carts, not one. No South African shop holds all 29 — three of them (Exclusive Books, Wordsworth, Readers Warehouse) carry real shelf stock, Takealot has three titles in a warehouse, and the rest are ordered in by whoever will take the order.
  • Two titles have exactly one local route each: Ruby René Had So Much to Say and Millie Fleur's Poison Garden, both Takealot, both a month out.
  • Takealot's own search would not render on the machine that built this, so 'not at Takealot' means 'not confirmed at Takealot' for the titles not listed above. Searching by hand may turn up cheaper copies.
  • Bargain Books sits behind a bot wall and could not be checked at all. They are worth a phone call for the picture books.
  • Editions differ across the list: the Tibetan Book of the Dead is the Penguin Classics Deluxe from the reel, Dream Yoga is the Sounds True paperback, and Secret Teachings and the Grand Symbol are the cheaper reprint editions. Hardbacks run two to four times the price.
  • Amazon.co.za is never the answer here — on the nine children's titles it does carry, it prices at roughly double the local route.

What could not be verified

  • Amazon.co.za as a cheaper route — R7,435.98 for nine children's titles against R3,950 locally, and two of the eleven missing entirely.
  • Bargain Books — Bot wall — the catalogue never served. Unchecked, not confirmed absent.
  • Takealot search for the other titles — Their search endpoint returns 'page not found' from this machine. The four Takealot prices here come from live product pages.
