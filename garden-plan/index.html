🌤️ Our Climate

Cape Town has one of the world’s five Mediterranean climates — the same as Southern California, Provence, and parts of Chile. Welgemoed sits on the Tygerberg Hills slopes, giving us slightly more rainfall and cooler summer nights than the city average.

⚠️ The Cape Doctor — Strong SE wind in summer. We need a windbreak hedge or shade cloth on the southern boundary to protect young plants.

🗺️ Garden Architecture

The garden is laid out with fruit trees on the north (so they don’t shade the vegetables), four rotating annual beds in the centre, perennial herbs between them, soft fruit along the south edge, and a windbreak on the southern boundary.

🫘 Bed 4 — Legumes & Cucurbits The Three Sisters

Sweetcorn, climbing beans, cucumber, squash, baby marrow, butternut, melon, and watermelon. The classic Three Sisters method: corn provides support, beans fix nitrogen, squash shades the soil.

⚠️ Critical: Corn must be planted in blocks (not single rows) for wind pollination. Minimum 12 plants in a grid pattern.

💧 Watering Guide

SeasonFrequencyMethod
☀️ Summer (Dec–Mar)2–3×/week deepDrip irrigation, early morning
🍂 Autumn (Mar–May)1–2×/weekDrip or hand
❄️ Winter (Jun–Aug)Rarely neededRain mostly sufficient
🌸 Spring (Sep–Nov)1–2×/weekDrip, increasing

💡 Water the soil, not the leaves. Deep, infrequent watering encourages deep roots. Mulch reduces water loss by 50–70%. Drip irrigation is 90% efficient vs 60% for sprinklers.
