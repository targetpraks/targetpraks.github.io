01

Who we are

Built for the new generation

Papa Pasta is a South African quick-service pasta concept built for lives that move between gaming, sport, work and food.

Pasta is extruded and dried in store, then cooked from dried to order. Nine signature dishes and nine core sauces carry a focused menu, supported by salads and a drinks offer. It is not an Italian restaurant and does not present itself as one.

The store is a crisp white room grounded in matte black, lit with 6,000 K ACDC functional linear lighting. Each franchise chooses its own signature colour — applied cleanly across the glossy metro tiles, concrete stucco counters, stools, crest poster and programmable RGB LED accents (shown here in Royal Blue & White; preview live in 3D below).

    05

    Fit-out

    Fit-out scope, to be agreed

    Papa Pasta’s fit-out covers the store interior: the all-electric kitchen (induction preferred), the glass-fronted pasta theatre, counters and approved signage.

    Equipment is checked against measured site dimensions and confirmed services before it is ordered. The fit-out programme is set with the landlord once commercial terms are agreed.

    No build cost is assumed in this pack, and no opening date is committed.

    The landlord’s side

    The landlord keeps control of the building’s services and centre rules: supply capacity, connection points, duct and drainage routes, fire requirements, signage rules and loading arrangements.

    The split between landlord’s works and tenant’s works is to be confirmed, as is the condition in which the unit is returned at lease expiry. Both are recorded in the lease.

    Inside tenant scope, subject to agreement

    Programme

    The fit-out programme is agreed with the landlord once terms are signed. No opening date is committed in this pack, and none should be read from it.

    Where this pack stands

    This is an internal review pack, prepared for discussion. The tenant entity, lease terms and site-specific services schedule are not yet confirmed. All figures are planning references, not commitments.
