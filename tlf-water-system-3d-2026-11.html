What the model shows, and what it deliberately leaves out

Everything you can see is built to the drawing set: the room is 6,000 × 5,050 mm, every machine is 1,300 × 600 × 1,960 mm, and each sits where the client's layout puts it. The tanks are 870 mm across. The ring main runs at 1.0 m, the manifold at 0.35 m, and the clean tank stands on a 600 mm plinth so its water line sits about 0.8 m above the ring — that height is the pump.

Water only in the shared pipeEvery machine keeps its own floats and its own pump.

Not modelled, because they are decisions for the installer rather than the concept: the ceiling, the electrical enclosure, the drainage falls, and the inside of the filter housings. The humidifier box and tent sit outside the room, as they do on site.
