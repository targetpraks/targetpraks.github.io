The Week

Four parts of the house, seven days, one colour per person. Teal Ricardo, rose Julie, amber Lily, slate the domestic, sage the gardener, grey the children's own jobs.

Who does what

The same week, read by person.

How the week holds together

  • Ricardo doesn't cook. The kitchen belongs to Julie and Lily — he does the school, the sport and the garden instead.
  • 17:00–20:00 is Julie's. Cluster feeding. Dinner, baths and bedtime belong to everyone else in that window.
  • Nights are split every night: Ricardo 22:00–02:00, Julie 02:00–06:00.
  • Paid help is part of the week, not a fallback: the domestic is in the house four days, the gardener twice.
