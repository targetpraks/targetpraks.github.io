Quick takeaways

  • Option 1 (SYSTEM 3E) gives you the exact “dry‑stack” story but requires import logistics and local approvals before you can sell a square metre in SA.
  • Option 2 (AAC) is the safest local route – AAC is already common in SA, so you can hit the market faster with less regulatory friction.
  • Option 3 (SIPs) is premium; the cost advantage only materialises if you eliminate freight via local fabrication.
  • Option 4 (ICFs) offers ICF performance with a broader market footprint; a few SA distributors already exist.
  • Option 5 (perlite‑enriched blocks) lets you stay local with minimal change to existing block manufacture – a potential niche if you source SA perlite.

Next steps

  1. Contact suppliers – ask for landed‑cost quotes (duty, freight, VAT) per m² for Options 1, 3‑5.
  2. Local testing – get a small batch tested against SANS 10400 and South African building standards.
  3. Cost‑benefit model – build a spreadsheet that includes labour savings, material cost and freight to decide which option offers the best margin.
