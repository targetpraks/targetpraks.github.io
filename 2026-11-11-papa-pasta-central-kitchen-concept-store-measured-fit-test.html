The retail plan is 1.96 m deeper than the room

I measured all three drawings off their vector geometry rather than the printed scale bar, then set the Papa Pasta retail plan and the Ryan Hockley lighting layout into the concept store at true size. The width fits. The depth does not — and the 1.96 m that does not fit is all back-of-house.

04 Two ways to close the gap

Recommended · panel 2

Trim 1.96 m off the north service band

  • Removes the 1200 SS shelving run, the two-bowl sink and drainer, the head of the pasta drying racks and the staff door.
  • All of it is back-of-house, and all of it already exists next door in the kitchen's wash-up area, dry store and shelving.
  • The counter shortens 358 mm, from 5 355 to 4 997 mm.
  • Two fittings move with the band. Bar seating, till, bowl dispensers, shopfront and the other fittings hold position.
  • No kitchen equipment moves. No new structure. No new joinery beyond a closing wall.
Alternative · panel 3

Keep the plan, enlarge the store north

  • The retail plan and all 20 fittings land exactly as drawn.
  • The 800 mm cooking line — 6-pan GN1/1 gas oven and combi steamer, tilting pans, induction, 1 650 tables — is displaced.
  • There is nowhere to put it: the aisle to the packaging line is only 1.30 m, so the packaging line moves as well.
  • That is a full kitchen re-plan, a new canopy and a new services drop.

06 What happens next

  1. Confirm the concept store's role. Is this room the Papa Pasta retail unit, or a demonstration counter inside the central kitchen? The 38 m² tag and the order–collect counter say demonstration; the retail plan says shop. The answer decides whether the 1.96 m matters.
  2. If it is the shop, issue panel 2 as the set-out. One new wall closing the store 1 958 mm north of the existing one, the north service band relocated to the kitchen's wash-up and dry store, and the retail plan issued with its north 1 958 mm deleted.
  3. Re-issue drawing 03 at 5 000 × 6 100. The retail plan's north band comes off, the counter shortens 358 mm, and the drawing is reissued so it can be filed against the kitchen drawing without a fit test on every revision.
  4. Ask Ryan Hockley to confirm the two relocated fittings. Two of the sixteen linear fittings fall inside the removed band. The illuminance figures above stand for the rest of the layout; the BOH zone needs re-checking once the band moves.
  5. Only then order. Nothing on this sheet has been ordered, sent or committed. The 38 m² tag and the two drawings disagree by 2.6 m² and that should be settled with the architect before anything is fabricated.
