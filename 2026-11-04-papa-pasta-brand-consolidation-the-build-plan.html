00 — READ FIRST

Three collisions that cost money before a single task moves

These are not brand-tidiness problems. Each one is currently printing, publishing or pricing something that contradicts itself — and every downstream deliverable in this plan inherits whichever answer is chosen. They are decisions, not work.

Collision 1 · The fee schedule is withdrawn but still published

The zone fee was restructured on 4 September 2026 from R1.5 m to R80,000 per zone, withdrawing five tiers from R800,000 to R4,500,000. The Prospectus and the draft FDD still print the withdrawn schedule — a price 10 to 56 times what will actually be charged.

Two further fee models sit alongside it as current: per-unit at R350,000 plus 6%+2%, and R250,000 for five years at 4%+3%. The operating models run 5.0% royalty and 1.0% levy swept monthly. Nothing is averaged here and nothing is changed — this is disclosure and legal territory, and it is Ricardo's to rule.

Collision 2 · The portion standard disagrees by 37%

The recipe manual yields 70 servings from a 10 kg batch — 190 g each. The menu and costing model was rebuilt at a 140 g standard on the CFO's 3 September call, about 94 servings from the same batch. The opening stock model was built on 120 g and is short 105.6 kg against 140 g.

That is a 37% swing in the pasta cost of every plate, and it means the menu cannot be priced until one number is ruled.

Collision 3 · A retired tagline still sits inside the logo

Brand Manual v2.0 demotes Fresh. Seasonal. Quality. to a quality seal and makes LEVEL UP YOUR HUNGER. primary. Every logo master, the franchise wiki front page and the Brand GM knowledge base still carry the retired line as fixed. Any fascia, bag or menu printed this month is wrong on one side or the other.

04 — WHAT I ADDED

Beyond the brief, because the brief has holes

Four things were not in the goal as stated, and the plan cannot finish honestly without them.

1 · A canonical ruling before the manuals

  • Three palettes and three type systems are all marked current. Building fifteen manuals on three different foundations produces fifteen wrong manuals. One ruling, then produce.

2 · Searchable text for the manual

  • Every agent, franchisee and designer is meant to read this document. It cannot be copied, searched or quoted today — so in practice nobody reads it.

3 · A withdrawal notice on the prospectus

  • Until the fee architecture is ruled, the published prospectus carries a schedule withdrawn in September. Legal, not brand — flagged, not touched.

4 · Real photography, scheduled

  • The manual bans untreated stock and there is not one photograph of a real plate anywhere in the brand. Campaigns built on renders will read as renders.
