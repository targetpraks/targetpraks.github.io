The rule this payload proposes

  1. An Owner is a member of the space the work lives in. The workspace read is the source; a typed name never is.

  2. Ownership is taken from the handle, which states unit, tier and role. The team is a second opinion: when it disagrees it is reported, never applied over the handle.

  3. A person in no team, or in a team that contradicts their handle, is listed. The row they would own stays Draft.

  4. No reporting line is invented. ClickUp holds none, so none is drawn until the teams carry one.
