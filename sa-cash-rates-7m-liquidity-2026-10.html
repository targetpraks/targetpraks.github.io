Yes — and the best of them lets you keep a slice liquid

Investec SmartRate, 120-day notice: 7.95% nominal (8.25% effective) on R7m — R556 500 a year before tax. Minimum R100 000. It is the highest rate in the country that does not lock your money up.

The useful detail: SmartRate has a dial. Set 10% available on demand and the rate drops to 7.88% (8.17%), with R700 000 instantly withdrawable and the other R6.3m on four months' notice. Set 20% on demand and it pays 7.82%.

If four months is too long, 32-day notice pays 7.75% — still clear of your bar, and 8.03% effective.

5  Four things that matter more than the last half-point

Tax is the bigger lever

R556 500 of interest becomes R306 075 in your hand at a 45% marginal rate, or R406 245 if a company earns it at 27%. The entity question is worth R100 000 a year; the gap between the best notice account and the best fund is worth R39 000. Worth an hour with your accountant.

Deposit insurance stops at R100k

CODI covers R100 000 per depositor per bank — 1.4% of R7m. Insurance is not the reason to split. Counterparty quality is: stay with the systemic banks, and let a second bank compete on rate.

Ladder the tranches

Four notes of R1.75m at 32, 60, 90 and 120 days gives you a maturity every month without ever starting a withdrawal. It also means a rate move hits one tranche at a time instead of all of it.

Use the on-demand dial

SmartRate's 10% setting is the only published way in this market to hold R700k instantly available while the balance earns 7.88%. For a business that needs a buffer, that is the whole product.
