Why four blocks, and not one

A single artwork block forces every store to redraw the mark for its own field. That is how a network ends up with forty versions of one crest. Four blocks end the argument before it starts: the drawing is fixed, and the four things allowed to vary — field, ink, spot and lower arc — are named in advance.

01

White

The mark reversed to white on the black field. The default for anything lit, dark or printed on board.

02

Black

The mark in black on paper or any light field. The default for everything a customer takes home.

03

Colour

One spot — Volt, Violet or Coral — on black. Campaign, stickers, neon, the three seconds a promoted post gets.

04

Franchisee

The Living Crest of §3.10: the store’s registered colour as the field, wordmark still white, the lower arc carrying the store’s name and zone.
