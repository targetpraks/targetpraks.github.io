One claim I overruled with evidence

Two assessors read visibility: private and concluded the group shares were nominal and staff would arrive to nothing. That is wrong: the share table carries a use grant to the matching group on all twenty projects. Private simply means "the owner and whoever it is shared with" — which is exactly what a department space should be. What is true is weaker and real: the groups have no members yet, because the platform still has one account. The access is pre-staged, not live.
