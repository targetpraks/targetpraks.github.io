00 How to read it

  • Every row is a real provider, with a link to the page the price came from. Where the provider publishes no fee, the row says "on enquiry" — it does not guess.
  • Prices are per year, per term, per month or per session exactly as the provider states them. They are not converted here, so nothing looks cheaper than it is.
  • Where and when is information, not a filter. If a child wants something on this list, the week gets rearranged around it. That is a different conversation from the one where a spreadsheet tells them no.
  • What it develops is the plain version, not marketing copy — why a parent might want this for a child who reads below his grade, or for one who needs to be seen doing something well.
  • 2026 prices. Assume 5–10% on most of these for 2027. Term fees move quietly between seasons.
For the discussion: mark anything either child's eye lands on — even if the time looks impossible, even if it looks expensive. Bring the marked page back and I will price the chosen ones properly, check the actual clash, and tell you what it does to the year. The school plan is here if you want the fixed week beside it.

07 Five questions to ask any of them

Same five, every provider. The answers sort them faster than any brochure.

  • What does the term cost in total, including the kit, the uniform and the match fees?
  • How many sessions a week, and what happens if we miss one?
  • Is there a trial or a first free session?
  • What is the group size, and who coaches them?
  • Do you take a child who is small for his age and reads below his grade, and what does that look like on your field?
