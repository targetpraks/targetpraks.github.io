The first three weeks

Twenty groups is a menu, not a to-do list. Three a week, one real-world outing, and by the end of the month there are familiar faces. Nothing here costs money to join.

Week one

Join three, post nothing

Durbanville / Northern Suburbs Mammas, #ADS in Welgemoed and Durbanville. Read the pinned rules in each. Then walk Durbanville parkrun on Saturday — 5 km at Meerendal, walkers welcome, no membership, no pace pressure.

Week two

Answer three people

Girls of Cape Town, Edgemead Runners and Home Improvement Cape Town. Reply to three posts with something useful. Ask Edgemead when the walkers meet. Do not advertise yet.

Week three

Show one room

Mamahood, Tygerberg Hiking Club and Renovating Moms. Post one before-and-after in #ADS in Welgemoed, and book one sound bath or women's circle from Cape Town Conscious Community. By now the names are familiar.

Group etiquette

six rules that keep her welcome

Small suburb groups have long memories. These six habits are the difference between being the designer everyone recommends and being the person whose posts keep disappearing.

01

Answer the join questions properly

Most of these groups are private and auto-decline blank requests. One honest line about living in Welgemoed and what you do is enough.

02

Read the pinned post before the first post

Several local groups only allow advertising on certain days, or ban links in a first post. Breaking that rule gets a post deleted and, in some groups, a member removed.

03

Five helpful comments for every one promotion

The women who get recommended in suburb groups are the ones who answer questions. The ad is a by-product of being useful, not the other way round.

04

Personal profile first, business page second

Local groups respond to a person. Put King Protea Designs in the profile, post from the personal profile where the rules allow it, and let the work speak.

05

Never message a stranger cold

Answer in the thread. If she asks for a quote, move to DM. Cold DMs are the fastest way to be reported in a small suburb group.

06

Advertise only what can be proved

Her own before-and-after photographs, her own scope, her own contact details. No invented awards, no stock rooms presented as her work.
