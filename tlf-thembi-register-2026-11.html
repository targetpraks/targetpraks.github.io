6The three questions you asked, answered

Partly Is Hermes connected to the applications?
To Zoho: yes, and it is reading right now. As this was written, Hermes listed the live TLF project from Zoho — “Zoho Books Migration — The Local Farmer, 8 open, 1 closed”. That lane is read-only by design.

To the TLF app itself: no. There is no connector, no API and no webhook between Hermes and the app. Hermes cannot read a tray count or fix a screen, and nothing in this register can be done by asking Hermes to do it — it goes through Geoff and Thembi. What Hermes can do is hold the list, chase the list, and check the evidence against it.
No Does the app plug into the TLF CRM today?
Nothing pushes from the app into Zoho CRM now — no purchase order, no invoice, no stock movement. The design for it is written (Zoho Flow carrying app events into CRM and Books), the build is not done. That build is W5.3, and it is the item that decides whether the CRM is a record or a rumour.

What exists lives inside the app with no screen that shows it — which is exactly why W4.1 exists. Until that button ships there is nothing for you or Cindy to look at.
