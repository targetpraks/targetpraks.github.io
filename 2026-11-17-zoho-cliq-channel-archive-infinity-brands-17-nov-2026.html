What happened

Every internal Infinity Brands channel — the EXCO and MANCO rooms, the BUS, MANCO and SUPPORT lists, and the private working channels — was archived through the Cliq API. Archiving stops new messages and leaves the history readable; it never deletes anything, and any channel can be restored with unarchive.

Held back on purpose: 13 client-facing channels (#ITC: CIN7 Support, #SLA FIN: EXP RUSTENBURG, #CLI MKT: KPD, #SLA FIN EXCO: EXP RUSTENBURG, #CLI OPS: Legal, #CLI ZOHO: KPD, #Global Partner Network-Announcements, #SLA FIN: BIZZACC, #TLF-External-Channel, #Nova Property, #EXCO MKT, #SLA HRM: EXP RUSTENBURG, #EXP-External-Channel) and our own #Gotham City line. Cutting a client or franchisee channel off mid-week is a bigger decision than a ClickUp migration, so it waits for your word.

Could not archive: #ZOR EXP: SUPPORT (Nadine Vorster), #Polotech Court Case Updates (Jaques Mulder), #Provident Fund (Jaques Mulder). Your Cliq role on those three is member, not channel admin, so the API refused; the channel creators can archive them.
