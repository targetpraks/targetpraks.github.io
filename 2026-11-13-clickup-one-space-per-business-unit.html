What changed today

Departments left the shared roof

HRM, ADM, ITC, MKT and SAL lived in one corporate Space because five was the limit. Each has its own Space now, with its own lists straight in the Space — no folder layer needed once a department has a door of its own.

The services desks got doors too

FXS - INFX Solutions, FXL - INFX Labs and FLF - FluxFlow Solutions are three separate Spaces, three separate intakes. A Zoho ticket and a FluxFlow build no longer share a room.

One scaffold retired, with nothing lost

The old corporate Space and a probe Space were removed. Every list in them was empty — zero tasks, checked list by list before deletion — and every structure in them was recreated in the new Spaces first. Governance did not go with them: GOV Decision Register, GOV Programme Register and GOV Meeting Actions now sit in REP - Reporting & Notes under GOV - EXCO & MANCO.

Filters work again — so the views earn their keep

Every Space carries a Blocked & waiting view that filters those two statuses. That view did not work this morning; now it does, and it is the first thing a GM should open.
