What each layer does

Team — the people list you can @mention and assign to. Filled for 12 BUs.

Space — the working area. Everyone in a BU has Full edit on their own space; the six executives also sit in EXCO and MANCO.

Folder — nothing to share: every folder in the workspace is open and inherits its space, so folder work follows the space automatically. Only worth separating them if you want tiers walled off.

Chat — the BU channel is bound to its space, so it mirrors the space list on its own.

How they were opened

A private channel can only be joined one invitation at a time — there is no bulk route. An open channel carries the whole workspace, so each SUP: channel was switched to open; every member is inside it now, and anyone hired later joins automatically.

Reversible: say the word and any channel goes back to private with only its own desk in it.
