3. Three things I did not do, and why

The "0.71 GB of duplicates" does not exist in BND 2

I hashed all 494 files in the folder. Result: zero byte-identical files. The 124 groups in the audit were same-name same-size matches, and most of them straddle the marketing tree — same document kept in two trees, not two copies in one. Nothing was deleted, because nothing in BND 2 was a duplicate.

The 80 empty folders turned out to be a plan, not junk

Checked folder by folder through the API. 143 of them are the MANCO monthly reporting skeleton — a folder per month per BU, four files filled in — and 25 are the quarterly recordings skeleton. Deleting those deletes the structure the reporting is built on. They stay. Only the three genuinely orphaned folders went to the trash.

The satellites are registered, not swallowed

Finance, Sales, IT, MANCO, INFX, HR and Marketing each keep the TLF folder they own, and every one is listed with a link in the register inside 00_Index & Register. The reason is permissions, not tidiness: BND 2 is shared with Brand Ops, Manco and Exco — not with the Finance or Sales teams. Dragging Finance's debtors book into BND 2 would have cut the finance team off from its own documents overnight. IT's folder was the one real mess — twelve payment PDFs loose at the root — and those are now filed in Payment Packs.
