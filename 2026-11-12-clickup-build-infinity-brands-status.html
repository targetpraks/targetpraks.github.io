Two honest footnotes. ClickUp will not delete a status on this plan, so on 03, 04 and 05 its own to do stays alongside Open (and complete alongside Closed on 04) — harmless, and gone the moment you rename or retire them by hand. Every status the specification asks for is present.

What the read-back proves

  • Structure: 5 Spaces, 17 Folders, 23 Lists, 161 custom-field placements — all created through ClickUp's API, re-runnable, logged object by object.
  • Views: 37 live. 33 are the specification's own; the other 4 are the default Gantt, Table, Team Docs and Overview that ClickUp attaches to every new List. I did not delete them — that is yours to call, not mine.
  • Statuses: all five models match the specification exactly. verified
  • Nothing invented: every object name comes from the specification's CSV configuration pack, not from me.
