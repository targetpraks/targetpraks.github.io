What moving the date exposes

1. The hiring chain is now the long pole

Worked back from 15 December, interviews were due yesterday (10 Nov) and contracts sign in six days (17 Nov). A crew that starts on 4 December cannot be certified by 4 December — the source plan had four weeks of training. This chain cannot simply be shifted; it has to be re-baselined against who has actually been interviewed and hired. That answer sits with HRM, not with me.

2. The health certificate has the longest lead time and the legal teeth

The municipal R638 inspection needs a booking now for the week of 7 December, with the HACCP pack ready to show. Miss it and the store has no Certificate of Acceptability to trade under — the one item on this list that stops opening day outright.

3. Two sign-offs land on the same day

Dashboard go-live (8 Dec) now sits on the same day as the final certification gate. Both are “someone must be on site and sign”. If the POS test transaction can pass earlier, pull the dashboard to 4 December and keep 8 December for people only.

What I have not done

No writes to Zoho Projects, no WorkDrive change, and no word to any staff member, supplier or franchisee. The dates exist on PAP-302 and in this page only. Once you pick A, B or C, the operational calendar gets the dates and people hear them — in that order.
