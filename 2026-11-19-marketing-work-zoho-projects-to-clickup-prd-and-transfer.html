6 How it was executed

  1. Target first. Function field created; Daily and Meeting Action lists added to every live lane; intake lists completed inside Team Operations.
  2. Pilot, then scale. Three items created and read back field by field before the bulk run — which is how the date automation, the multi-assignee block and the users-field quirk were found instead of assumed.
  3. Bulk run, resumable. One ledger row per item as it lands; parents before subtasks; a re-run skips what exists.
  4. Second pass for dates. 308 items re-dated after the automation had overwritten them.
  5. Read-back verification. All 183 live lists read, every task matched to its Zoho id, 0 missing; a random 40-item audit returned 40/40 with Brand, Engagement Tier, Function, Work Format, status, assignee and source footer present.
Verified state. 524 of 679 items exist in ClickUp, each carrying its Zoho id in the description footer. Ledger: ledger.jsonl (524 rows, Zoho id → ClickUp id). Audit: field_audit.json, state-check.json.
Concurrency, stated plainly. A second thread was working the same MKT space at the same time: it moved the intake lists into Team Operations, deleted the lane-root copies, and at 11:56 deleted the seven new brand lanes on your instruction. That is why the run shows a mid-flight interruption, and why 155 items were skipped rather than filed. The final numbers above were re-read after all of that settled.
