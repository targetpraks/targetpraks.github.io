Pack sizes and monthly volumes

Every supplier was asked what pack they sell. That is the wrong way round: we are the buyer, so we decide the pack. This is that decision, line by line, with the monthly volume behind each one — so a supplier can put a price next to a pack instead of asking us what we want.

Decided 23 November 2026 · 137 lines · 98 worked out from the bill of materials, 39 estimated · prices are the cheapest quote we hold today

The volume model

27,144 bowls

a month: 174 bowls a store a day × 26 trading days × 6 stores, before the 5% waste allowance.

Exploded through

98 of 137

lines carry a real volume, taken from the dish BOMs into the dough and sauce BOMs. The other 39 have no dish on the menu yet and carry an estimate, flagged in the table.

Now in the drafts

61 letters

rebuilt so every line reads item — pack — monthly volume. The six questions ask for a price on that pack, not for a guess at ours.

The method Read this before arguing with a number.

The chain. Menu BOM (“10 Core Menu BOM” / “11 Seasonal Menu BOM”) gives grams of each component per dish. The dish mix gives bowls per dish. Each dish points at a dough recipe (“06 Pasta Dough BOM”) or a sauce recipe (“07 Core Sauce BOM CK” / “08 Seasonal Sauce BOM CK”), and the recipe turns that into ingredient grams. Sum across the menu and the month, and you have what we actually buy.

Two assumptions, both labelled. Bowls are split equally between the dishes of a family, and seasonal drops take 25% of a store’s bowls. Neither is measured sales data — there is none yet. They are stated so you can move them.

Sanity check. Every sauce recipe’s ingredient lines sum to 98–101% of its batch mass, and the four dough recipes land on 301 kg of dried pasta a month. If the model were wrong, those two numbers would not close.

What the supplier now reads The line format, and the five questions.

PP-WHEAT-SA — SA stone-milled whole wheat flour — 25 kg bag — 139 kg a month at one store, 836 kg across six

Then: price per that pack excluding VAT · minimum order in packs · lead time · how long the price holds · delivery cost to Bedfordview and who carries it. Plus halaal and food-safety certification, and a line that lets them quote a different pack if they must, so nothing bounces back unanswered.
