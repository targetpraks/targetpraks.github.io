The prices that move the model

  • Cooking oil — R669 per 20 L drum (Cuisine Foods). That is R33.45/L, zero-rated, free Friday delivery to Bedfordview, two-drum minimum, order by 12:00 on Thursday. First real oil price the model has had.
  • Mozzarella — R40.00/kg (Foodservice Company) against R111.00/kg (La Marina). Same day, same city, a 2.5 kg loaf at R99.99 in the festive catalogue versus R277.50 at La Marina. See the decision below.
  • Cheddar — R40.00/kg (FSC loaf) against R93.00/kg (Jozi bulk). Same pattern, same caveat.
  • Frozen vegetables land between R22.50 and R44.50/kg from N1, which undercuts the fresh Ferreira Fresh lines on spinach, broccoli, peas and butternut. Frozen also removes the trim loss that the yield column was carrying on the fresh lines — check 04 SKU Master column E before switching, because a frozen price against a fresh yield percentage understates the saving.
  • The 2 kg sauce pouch now has five real options: R0.714 (Peak Pak 200×300), R1.036 (DH 165×260), R1.041 (Peak Pak 250×350), R1.449 (DH 200×300), R2.245 (DH 250×350 custom).
  • Batch labels: R2.185 each at 1,000 flexo, or R3.31 each digital with no plate and no minimum.

Three things worth your word

  1. The Foodservice Company price ends tomorrow. The festive catalogue runs 2–28 November. Mozzarella and cheddar at R40/kg will make every cheese dish look cheap until Thursday, and wrong from Friday. If those grades are not what the menu specs, they should come out of the slots — say the word and I'll pull them, or leave them and I'll flag them as promo-only in the notes.
  2. Pouch barrier, not just price. Peak Pak's R0.714 bag is 70 micron co-extruded PA/PE; DH's R1.449 is 62 micron metallised PET/LLDPE. For a 2 kg frozen sauce that difference decides shelf life, so the cheapest pouch is not automatically the right one. I have both in the slots and neither is committed.
  3. Label minimums. 163 labels a month across the network means the 1,000-label flexo minimum buys eight years of stock. The digital route (200–500 labels, R3.31 each, no plates) is the one that fits the actual volume.
