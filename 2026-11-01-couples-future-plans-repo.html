# Our Future Plans — the couple's repo A private repo (GitHub/GitLab, or a shared Obsidian vault) that you and Julie treat like a shared backlog for the life you're building together. Not a spreadsheet of chores — a place for the dreams, trips, home projects, money goals, health goals, and the regular reviews that turn "someday" into dates on the calendar. ## Why a repo - **One source of truth.** Both of you can see it, add to it, comment on it. No more "I thought you were handling that." - **It works async.** Drop an idea when it occurs to you; discuss it at the next review instead of letting it evaporate. - **History you can look back on.** A year from now you'll see what you planned, what happened, and what changed. ## Recommended structure | Folder | What goes in there | |--------|--------------------| | `docs/` | Your shared vision ("north star"), values, and the ground rules for how you use the repo | | `dreams/` | Big dreams without dates yet — sabbatical, second home, business, kids' education, etc. | | `trips/` | Travel bucket list and planned trips | | `home/` | House projects, moves, renovations, big purchases | | `money/` | Savings goals, investment targets, debt payoff | | `health/` | Health and fitness goals you're chasing together | | `reviews/` | Quarterly and annual review notes | | `registers/` | Templates: bucket list, ideas backlog, wheel of life (couple's version) | ## The cadence that makes it live 1. **Monthly check-in (30–45 min)** — what's done, what's next, add anything new to `dreams/` or `ideas-backlog`. 2. **Quarterly review (90 min)** — score your Wheel of Life as a couple, move one dream into a plan with a date, review money and health goals. 3. **Annual planning (half day)** — set the year's themes and big bets; archive what no longer fits. ## How to start 1. Create a private repo (GitHub/GitLab) or a shared vault, both of you with access. 2. Copy this scaffold in. Fill `docs/shared-vision-template.md` together — that's your "why." 3. Each of you adds 3–5 things to `registers/ideas-backlog-template.md` before the first monthly check-in. 4. At the check-in, pick one thing to own each, with a due date. Use issues/PRs or tasks for the work. ## What already exists in your stack Your Life Planner vault already holds Ricardo's side of this: - `registers/travel-bucket-list.md` — 46 destinations - `registers/ideas-backlog.md` — 6 ideas - `registers/house-lists.md` — reading/movie/recipe lists - `registers/wheel-of-life-scores.md` — Wheel of Life scores over time - `Wheel-of-Life-Framework.md` — the framework behind the scores Those are your personal registers; this repo is the shared layer you build with Julie. --- **How to use this scaffold:** copy the folder into your private repo, edit the templates together, then start logging. It's yours — reshape it as you go. *— Splinter, Ricardo's master*
