Two aborts, nine minutes apart: the first hit the wedge signature (Interrupted system call on a plain touch), the second found the path gone entirely. The related night-check report for 2026-11-16 is also missing from AI-Drive/Agent-Output/courage/ — the only gap in that series — which fits one and the same volume outage. This is the known Hiksemi wedge mode: the mount stays listed and reads hang or return EINTR instead of erroring, so only a hard probe catches it. The probe did its job; the consequence is a lost night that nobody was told about until now.
