The trap in the new settings page

Settings → Time Tracking → Availability → "Enable Time Tracking in Spaces" renders a 15-space picker. Ticking a space there does nothing: the row is a mirror of the per-space ClickApp, and the clicks do not persist. Proof: I ticked PPP and NVP there → the row read "15 of 15 Spaces", then after a page reload it reverted to "13 of 15", and the API still showed both spaces disabled. The working control is the per-Space Time Tracking ClickApp (Spaces manager → Settings → ClickApps, or App Center → All ClickApps → Time Tracking).
