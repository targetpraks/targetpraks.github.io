Why two routes. The gateway is the fleet's control plane — one place to see and revoke what the house exposes. The per-profile entry is what an agent actually calls inside a session, with the 77 HA tools and nothing else, so nobody's context is flooded with the rest of their namespace.

04What "full access" means here

  • Control — ha_call_service operates any entity: lights, climate, covers, media, locks, scripts. ha_bulk_control for groups.
  • Build — ha_config_set_automation, ..._script, ..._dashboard, ..._helper, ..._scene, blueprints, zones, calendars, labels.
  • See — ha_search, ha_get_state, ha_get_history, ha_get_logs, ha_get_automation_traces, camera snapshots, system health.
  • Administer — ha_restart, ha_manage_updates, ha_manage_backup, HACS, apps.

That is deliberate: you asked for full access, so nothing is read-only. The brake is human, not technical — an agent can turn the aircon off, and the log of who did what is in the HA logbook.
