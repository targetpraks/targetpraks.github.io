Two files, every time

A document leaves here in one shape: a markdown record in the vault and an html edition a person reads. This page is the read look — hero photo, a diagram, a matrix, and links that go somewhere.

The rule

  • A diagram, always. In a read edition a diagram is not decoration, it is proof the idea was simplified before it was written.
  • Tables with contrast. Header bar, hairline rules, zebra rows — so a number can be followed across a row.
  • A hero image when the subject has a face: a property, a place, a product.
  • Links to what is being talked about. A claim about a tool names the tool and links it.
  • One link per document. It carries both editions.

Which look for whom

you, mid-task staff, clients

Deep brief read, with a matrix share — editorial cover

What builds it

The publisher renders the markdown with pandoc and wraps it in the tier's template. The vault is Obsidian; the page lives on the public site, which is why one link works from a phone anywhere.
