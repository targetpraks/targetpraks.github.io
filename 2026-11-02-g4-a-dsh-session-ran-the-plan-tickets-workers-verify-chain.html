Why the reasons matter more than the files

The two dispatch reasons are the part the article actually claims for a Router: it decides who gets what, and says why — it "cannot do the work". Here the reasons were structural, not decorative: T1 was isolated so the implementation could not be co-designed with the assertion, and T2 was authored by a different worker so the test derives from the ticket contract, not from the producer's code.

What G4 does and does not prove

  • Proven: DSH rc.5 has the routing primitive — a main session that spawns isolated subagents with recorded reasons, then judges their output. Model selection and discovery are present (list_subagent_models, subagent with modelSelection/modelDiscovery).
  • Not proven: the published dsh-superagent-prototype bundle itself (Router preset, Executor preset, GitHub trigger, work items, panel). It is cloned at ~/AI Hub/dsh-superagent-prototype and builds, but it is not installed: setup requires a target repository, a dedicated GitHub account the agent acts as, and the operator's answer on who may ask for work.
