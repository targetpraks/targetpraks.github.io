03 Four rules that decide the plan

One activity a day, the highest one.

A gym session and a 10,000-step walk on the same day pay 100, not 200. Plan the day, not the week's total. A tracked 30-minute workout at 70%+ heart rate is worth 300; a gym visit is worth 100 — so the watch beats the turnstile.

Per-adult ceilings.

Know Your Health 25,000 · exercise 25,000 · Cardio Fitness 10,000 on top · Eat Well 12,000. Diamond for two adults means roughly 50,000 each — one engaged partner and one passive one stalls the household at Gold.

Points reset 1 January; status carries.

The level you hold today was earned in 2025. Everything you earn from now to 31 December sets the level you hold through 2027. Nothing earned in 2026 survives into 2027 as points.

Some points are calendar-gated.

Flu jab points only exist for jabs given March–September — the window is closed, opens again in March. Colon screening needs age 45+, mammogram 40+, vision test 60+. Dental, HIV and the online assessments have no window.
