Vitality: the last 56 days, then 2027

You have the mechanic right. Points restart from zero on 1 January — but the status you end this year on is the base you carry into next year. So this year's job is to land the level, and next year's job is to bank it early. Everything below is split on that line, with one building to do it in and the letters to send for pricing.

31 December 2026 Points year closes. Everything earned to here sets the level you hold for all of 2027.
1 January 2027 Points go to zero. You keep the status, and start earning the next level from scratch.
21 March 2027 Julie's double-points window closes — six months after Sofia. Her points halve on that day.

A re-test only pays the difference if it is taken at least 90 days after the first, and the best result of the year counts — so a check this month plus a clean re-test in February pays the 22,500 into 2027, not into this year.
