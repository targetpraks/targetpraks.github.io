Do these five things first

  1. Open a shrinkage account and point adjustments at it Right now 100% of adjustments land in Cost of Goods Sold, so stock loss is invisible. One new account, one default setting, and from the next posting you can see the loss.
  2. Run the stock count that already exists, and only post it with your sign-off Count number 1 is built with 61 lines. Count it in the mobile app, reconcile variances, sign off, then approve. That single cycle turns a dead module into a monthly control.
  3. Settle the 24 inactive items holding stock 244 units and R62,436.26 sit on items Zoho will not even let into a count. Reactivate or write them off — the valuation is overstated until you do.
  4. Put a real approval control around adjustments Zoho cannot approve an adjustment, and 14 active users are unrestricted Admins. Limit the role, and make the weekly register review a standing meeting item.
  5. Set reorder points and fix the 46 over-committed lines No item has a reorder level while 46 show negative availability. Pick the top ten stock holdings and set levels this week.
