The six gates

  • Independent — not owned by a PE consolidator or a firm that is itself rolling up. This gate alone removed three names.
  • Recurring — the majority of revenue is retainer or contract, not one-off project work.
  • In band — EBITDA €150k–1.5M for an add-on, or €1.5M+ for a platform.
  • Succession pressure — founder age, no declared successor, or a partner recently departed.
  • Not distressed — revenue flat or growing, no insolvency filing.
  • Evidence — one link that shows the name and the size, re-tested on the day of publication.

Read the size columns carefully. Where a figure comes from filed accounts, a transparency report or a published ranking, it is stated as reported. Where it is an estimate, it is labelled as one. EBITDA is not public for almost any firm this size — so no purchase price can be set from this page alone. That is what their accounts are for.

What this would cost, and what it would sell for

Every candidate above sits at the bottom of the ladder the reel describes. The bands that apply:

  • Spain — under €300k EBITDA: 3.0–4.5×. €300–700k: 4.0–5.5×. €700k–1.5M: 5.0–6.5×. Above €1.5M: 6.0–8.5× (Capittal sector data).
  • Portugal — professional B2B services 4.0–7.0× (0.6–1.2× revenue); clinics 5.0–8.0×; micro-firms under €800k turnover 2.5–3.5× (2026 sector matrix).
  • Greece — no reliable published ladder. Anchor on the Iberian bands, then discount for financing cost and exit liquidity.

The honest arithmetic: a €4.68M-revenue audit firm at a 12–18% EBITDA margin is €560–840k of EBITDA, which prices at roughly €2.2–4.6M at 4.0–5.5×. Merge five of those into a platform above €1.5M EBITDA and the same earnings re-rate to 6.0–8.5×. That gap — not growth, not synergies — is where the return comes from. The margins are an assumption; the revenue is reported.

Next step

Nothing here has been contacted, and nothing will be without your word. The sequence that makes sense:

  • Pick a platform. One per country at most — Grupo Castilla or RibéSalat in Spain, Cingel in Portugal, Iatropolis or Mesogeios in Greece.
  • Data request. Three years of accounts, client concentration, partner ages, and the fee-base split between recurring and one-off. That last line kills more deals than price does.
  • Then NDA and approach — and not before, because a firm that knows it is on a list prices itself accordingly.
