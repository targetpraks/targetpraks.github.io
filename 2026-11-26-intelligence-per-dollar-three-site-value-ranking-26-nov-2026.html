What breaks this list. Vellum prices whatever host is cheapest — DeepSeek V4.1 Flash is quoted at DeepInfra's $0.098/$0.294 there versus $0.30/$1.20 official on LLM Stats, which is why its Vellum ratio is double its LLM Stats one. Pure ratios also reward cheap and shallow: GPT-OSS 120B reaches sixth on $0.04/$0.17 while Artificial Analysis scores it 3.8 — it wins on arithmetic, not on usefulness. And the frontier buys badly by this measure: Claude Fable 5.2 is 3.7 points per dollar and Opus 5.5 is 7.4, the two worst ratios in Vellum's set. Finally, the per-dollar axis is academic for the Ollama Cloud lane, which bills flat against the company subscription.
