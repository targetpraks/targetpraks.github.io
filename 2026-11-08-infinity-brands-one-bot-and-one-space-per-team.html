How a person gets in

  • Each person needs an invitation link for their own work address — it is issued per person, not shared.
  • The invitation puts them into the Infinity Brands organisation and the team group their space belongs to.
  • Inside the space, the team bot is already the default assistant: they open the space and type.
Invitations are generated from the server and printed as links — no email is sent from here, so nothing reaches a staff inbox until you send it yourself.
  • No live company data yet. This is Stage B of the PRD: the bots answer from their instructions and any documents filed into the space, not from Zoho.
  • Nothing leaves the building. The console is only reachable on the office network, and vendor telemetry is switched off.
  • Everything a bot did is a record. Each answer keeps its model, tokens and cost against the run.

What is not switched on yet

  • Company knowledge. Filing policy documents into the spaces turns the answers from general to specific — that is the next piece of work.
  • Live Zoho. Reading real finance, HR and sales records is Stage D, and it waits on the five open PRD decisions.
  • Per-person access. The one remaining step is the list of people and their work addresses.
