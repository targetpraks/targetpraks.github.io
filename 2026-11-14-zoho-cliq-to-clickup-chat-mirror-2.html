What the Cliq structure actually is

Cliq is organised as a BUS / MANCO / SUPPORT triad per business unit, plus organisation and external tiers:

Tier Count Examples

private 5 #Provident Fund, #Gotham, #LOXBOX
external 23 #CIN7_April_Go_Live, #SLA FIN: EXP RUSTENBURG, #ZOR EXT EXP: Support

The BU triad is the reusable pattern: each unit gets a business channel, a management-committee channel and a service-desk channel. ClickUp had a single channel per unit; it now carries the full Cliq triad.

Notes / open items

  • Cliq levels private and external were inventoried but NOT mirrored - ad-hoc client and case chats.
  • The sidebar channel list is a per-user follow list; the authoritative full list is Chat > All Channels.
  • ClickUp 4.0 collapses the Home/Chat sidebar below roughly 2,000 px of viewport width, which is why chat looked empty in a 1,700 px window.
  • Reverse direction (ClickUp-only channels such as #CORP, #WINS, #RANDOM into Cliq) was NOT done: the token carries ZohoCliq.Channels.CREATE, but duplicating a live company chat needs Ricardo's word.
