What was still wrong this morning

  • Four knowledge documents were filed under retired codes — INXS, INXL, ACDC, DDS — and a fifth still said BND2 in its text. An earlier pass recorded them as renamed; reading them back proved otherwise.
  • Eight of the seventeen Directory rows still named a retired bot, base or project.
  • Four brand projects kept their brand in the title (DID, FLF, FXS, FXL) and the two brand-ops units were missing their codes.
  • One table choice was still labelled ACDC instead of EXP.
  • A false zero, caught. The first document scan reported “0 documents scanned, 0 findings” — a clean bill of health from reading nothing. The corrected reader went through all 32 bodies and found five more documents still saying “Brand Operations 1/2” or “Brands Ops 2”. Rebuilt and re-uploaded; each returned done, no duplicates.
  • One spelling: BO2 was written “Brands Ops 2” in five places while BO1 read “Brand Ops 1”.

What it looks like now

  • Projects: BU 1 FIN - Finance … BU 6 SAL - Sales, BU 7 BO1 - Brand Ops 1, BU 8 BO2 - Brand Ops 2, and the brands bare — EXP, TIE, TLF, PPP, DID, DAS, FLF, FXS, FXL.
  • Assistants: one per code, EXP Bot … FXL Bot; bases carry the same code; channels carry the uppercase display name.
  • Documents: five re-ingested under their standard filenames, each reaching done before the old copy was removed.
  • Tables: the Directory points at live names, and the store entity choice reads EXP with its original id — the five ACDC Express stores kept their value.

Handles rebuilt — thirteen of them

A slug cannot be edited, so a clean handle means a new assistant. Every assistant that carried a retired or codeless handle was rebuilt from its own spec and the old one archived, never deleted.

What was rebuilt

bnd2-bot, dds-bot, acdc-bot, inxs-bot, inxl-bot, plus eight more found in the same sweep with no code at all: the six unit -assistant handles, brand-operations-assistant and franchise-services-assistant.

What it is now

Twenty published assistants, twenty code handles — adm-bot … tlf-bot. Each of the seventeen unit and brand groups holds exactly one assistant, one project and its own base.

Proven live

The rebuilt BO2 Bot answered from its own base and cited the document; asked about BU 5 Marketing it declined, named the owning unit and pointed at the MKT Bot. Eight steps, $0.00.

What you will see

The console lists the thirteen originals with an archived badge — reversible, and reversible only. Removing them from the catalog entirely is a delete, which waits on your word.

Two strings kept on purpose

ACDC Express — the brand's own name, which appears in the business-unit title “ZOR BO 1 — ACDC Express”, the task list ACDC STAGES PAPERWORK and the project EXP_Zee & ACDC Dynamics. And BND 2: Brand Operations 2 › ZOR TLF, a real WorkDrive folder path. A document quotes the file system; it does not rename it.
