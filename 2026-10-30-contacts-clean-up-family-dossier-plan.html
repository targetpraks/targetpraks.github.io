4 · A security problem worth fixing today

Plain-text passwords sit in three of these cards. The notes on Donatello and Gianna — and the note you sent me as the example — carry the children’s Gmail and iCloud passwords in clear text. Those notes sync to iCloud, get read by every app with contact access, and sit unencrypted on every backup.

The right home is Apple Passwords — end-to-end encrypted, synced, and available to the kids’ devices. I have not touched those lines. Say the word and I strip them from the cards after you confirm they are stored in Passwords.

ID numbers are different: they are identity facts, not credentials, and the vault already holds them in the Family Identity Documents Register with check-digit validation. The dossier block points there rather than duplicating them onto a synced card.
