2. Screen sharing — the dial works, one correction

The office mini is behind the Infinity bastion, so it is never dialled directly. The Dock app Work Mac mini builds two key-only SSH hops: Air → personal Mac mini (holds the office keys) → bastion → macserver → INFs-Mac-mini, then speaks VNC over the loopback port. Both forwards bind 127.0.0.1 only; nothing is exposed on either LAN or the tailnet.

Why it failed at first: the app was pre-filling the infmac account. The password Tuan sent at 07:50 is attached to the ricardo account, so the sign-in was always going to be rejected. The app now dials ricardo. Verified end to end up to the sign-in sheet: the tunnel carries the VNC greeting (RFB 003.889) and Screen Sharing asks for ricardo.
