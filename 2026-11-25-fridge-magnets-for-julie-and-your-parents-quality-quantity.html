Quality What the evidence actually says

RapidStudio — 4.8 from 9,816 reviews on its own site; the magnet product itself 4.6 from just 20. On HelloPeter it sits at 3.7, and the recurring complaint is dark prints and slow weeks. Read the small print as a warning about colour, not about the magnet: send bright, well-exposed photos, and if a set comes back muddy, that is the thing to raise — their own returns line is the safety net.
PrintWild is the safest print in the list — a real photo print face-mounted under 3 mm acrylic, the same process as their wall prints — at R46.44 and two to three weeks. MagSnap is the strongest hold: a 0.8 mm full-back magnet on waterproof vinyl, which matters on a busy fridge door, but at 6.35 cm it is a small face. Canvas & More at 0.6 mm is a fridge tile, not a keepsake.
