Donatello and the school — everything we have looked at, joined up

Two months of work sits behind this decision: a school's letter, four routes, twelve schools scored, a repeat-versus-promote argument tested against the research, a support stack priced line by line, and two readings. This page puts it in one place, in the order it happened, with a link to each document it came from. Nothing here is new — it is the file, assembled.

Where it stands, 14 November 2026

The place is confirmed. The method is not. On 10 November Mrs Márjó Prins, Head of Early Learning and Junior Preparatory at Reddam House Durbanville, confirmed in writing that Donatello has a Grade 4 place for 2027 — "with the understanding that the support plan discussed will be in place from the very first day of the new academic year". She asked for two things with it: the facilitator's name once appointed, and a recent police clearance for that person.

The fork that is still open. Curro Uitzicht — the school every scoring exercise on this file ranks first — will take him only if he repeats Grade 3. Its own screening puts his Maths as very strong and his English well below a Grade 3 level, and its support lead, Veronica Handley, wrote that sending him to Grade 4 "would do him a huge injustice". The support professionals at his current school put his English at the same level. So the Grade 4 place now confirmed is a decision taken against both schools' reading of him, and the plan attached to it is what has to answer that.

What is being built around him, whatever the school. An accommodation-format psycho-educational assessment; a structured-literacy programme (Barton) run by a classroom facilitator; a reading pen for access in class and in tests; and weekly play therapy for the anxiety underneath it. All four were chosen on the local decision gate, all four are still to be booked or bought.

The one thing no document on this file can do for you: put the three options to him and let him choose, and take him to stand in the school before anything is signed. Both readings say to, and neither has happened yet. Section 10.

Why this list decides the school question, in one sentence. Every section below is a version of that question.

02 The decision, in the order it happened

This is not a tidy process — it moved as documents arrived. Laid out on one line, it reads like this.

  • 17 Sep 2026Reddam's letter lands. He did not meet the pass criteria in two of three terms and fell below the minimum in English and Afrikaans. Hettitia Dircksen recommends considering a repeat of Grade 3, and writes the sentence the whole file turns on: "Should he go to Grade 4, he will require substantial ongoing support … Grade 4 would also necessitate a reader, scribe and ideally a facilitator."
  • 15 Oct 2026The priorities are set: trajectory first, confidence and belonging second, the mainstream path third, cost last. Every score on this page is weighted against that order, not against mine.
  • 26 Oct 2026Four routes are priced and profiled — repeat at Reddam, Grade 4 with a facilitator, a specialist day school, boarding with support — alongside ten schools profiled by education system. The weighted matrix puts the specialist day school first at 3.95 against 3.65 for Grade 4 with a facilitator.
  • 31 Oct 2026The argument is tested. The retention research is read properly (a bare repeat is worth about zero; the gain tracks the intervention), Curro Uitzicht's offer arrives verbatim in writing, and the two schools are put head-to-head: Uitzicht 0.89, on staffed capacity over goodwill.
  • 5 Nov 2026The meeting at Reddam with Mrs Márjó Prins and Mrs Hettitia Dircksen. The request goes in writing as Grade 4 in 2027 with support from day one — three lines and one ask: confirm the place with the support plan in writing.
  • 10 Nov 2026Prins confirms the Grade 4 place, conditional on the support plan being in place from the first day. She asks for the facilitator's name and a police clearance. That is the day the decision stopped being "which school" and became "which plan".
  • 10–11 Nov 2026The support stack is chosen and priced: Barton as the literacy programme (gate 0.986), Little Sunshines as the facilitator route (0.995 pairwise), the C-Pen Exam Reader 2 as the in-class and exam reader (0.913), the assessment practices named, and a daily home programme written for both houses.
  • 13 Nov 2026Every school within reach is scored on one rubric (Curro Uitzicht 4.60, Tafelberg 4.40, Dunatos 4.20), seven models are polled anonymously (Tafelberg first, 5 votes to 2), and the two smallest-class options are compared directly.
  • 14 Nov 2026The therapist is chosen: Marleen Germishuys, 1.8 km away, clinical psychologist, play therapy — 0.94 in both directions against the specialist 600 m from the house.
