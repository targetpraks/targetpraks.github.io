The 8 still blank are the converted items no supplier has quoted: tamper sticker, insulation sleeve, vacuum sauce pouch, batch label, distribution carton, retail 500 g pack, and the 600 ml cup and its dome (Greenhome's cold cups stop at 500 ml). Those need a packaging converter, not a price list.

2. The VAT basis is now explicit, and three suppliers are restated to ex-VAT.

The model's pack cost is meant to be what we actually pay — net of VAT, because VAT is reclaimed. Three suppliers quote gross:

  • Urban Foods — the supplier's own report heads the column “Incl Price”. Thirty-three lines, and Urban wins most herbs and vegetables. Restated ÷1.15.
  • Eurosaf — “prices includes VAT”. Olive oil R166.75 → R145.00 a bottle; olives R45.43 → R39.50. Restated.
  • Akhalwaya — “Vat Included” on both flour lines. Not in a priced slot, so the log carries the basis only.
  • Bandini, Atlas, Daisy, Zandam, Cheese Pretoria, Greenhome and Detpak all quote excluding VAT — no change.

The quote log now has a VAT basis column that records what each supplier actually said, while the SKU master holds the ex-VAT cost. Provenance and working cost, kept apart so this cannot drift again.
