3What this actually means

Instant access costs about 0.85%

The best instant-access account on this list pays 7.05% (Investec PrimeSaver). Against 7.90% at 90 days that is R8 500 a year on R1m — the price of keeping the money one click away.

Notice is placed when you ask, not when you open

A notice account is an indefinite term: the balance keeps earning and only the amount you release is committed for the notice period. Confirm that partial notice is allowed before relying on it.

Deposit insurance stops at R100 000

CODI covers R100 000 per depositor per bank. Above that, splitting across banks buys insurance — and usually a better tiered rate with it.

Tax can outrank the rate

Interest above the annual exemption (R23 800 under 65, R34 500 from 65) is taxable. R79 000 of interest on R1m becomes R43 450 at a 45% marginal rate.
