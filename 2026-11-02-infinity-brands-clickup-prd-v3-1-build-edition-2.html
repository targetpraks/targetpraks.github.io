The five views, identical everywhere. Predictability beats cleverness: same names, same order, every Space. 00 Requests, 01 This week, Recurring — Board, Once off — List, Everything.

The sixteen fields. Four do the structural work: Brand, Work Type, Function, Quarter. Four run the service clocks: Service Tier, Response Due, Resolution Due, First Response At. The rest are plumbing. Detail in fields.csv, including which are required and where.

A task has one home. The Space records who is accountable. Brand, work type and quarter are fields, because a field cannot steal a task from its function the way a folder can.

4. Goals and OKRs

Space 10 holds the cascade. A Goal Folder per year, a Goal Folder per quarter inside it, and the company goal in the year folder. ClickUp Goals are separate objects from tasks, with four Target types: Number, True/False, Currency, Task.

Level Object Owner Check-in
Company, annual Goal in Folder 2027 Ricardo Monthly
Team, quarterly Goal in Folder 2027 Q1, named <Team> — Q1 Function lead Weekly
Individual Task Target on that person's own task The person As the work moves

Naming rule, because the platform has no quarter field: 2027 Q1 — Finance — Close by day 5, three months running. The quarter lives in the folder and the name, never in a free-text field somebody forgets.

5. Meetings

Space 11, private. A Folder per team, one Doc per year inside it, one page per week: W01 · 2027-01-04 · Monday. AI Notetaker joins the calendar meeting and writes the notes Doc, which is what makes the archive self-filing rather than a filing chore. The human fills decisions and actions; automation A09 turns action lines into tasks in the owning Space.

The fifteen-minute order, the same every week: last week's actions, this week's numbers against target, blockers, decisions needed, the Goal check.

6. Chat, clients, and the order they arrive

Chat is included on Core and above, unlimited messages and history, with task creation from a message. It also has no Cliq importer, and retention controls only on Enterprise. So Chat is wave two: two groups, one operating month, measured on tasks created from messages rather than messages sent. Cliq goes read-only only after an export for legal and HR is signed off.

Clients live in Space 08 as a Folder per client, shared with a guest while the Space stays closed. View-only guests are free; permission-controlled guests cost a member seat beyond the allowance; same-domain accounts cannot be guests at all. That last rule matters: an employee with a company address is always a member, never a guest.

7. Migration and coexistence

This is the part v3.0 did not have, and it is the part that decides whether any of the rest happens. The capability is verified: ClickUp's spreadsheet importer takes Excel, CSV, JSON, TSV or TXT and imports into an existing List, Folder or Space, carrying tasks, subtasks and nested subtasks with status, priority and assignee mapped to existing values, then prints an import report. There is no Zoho Projects importer, so the route is export, then import.

Source What moves What does not Owner
Zoho Projects Projects, tasks, subtasks, status, priority, assignee by email Comments and attachments Project lead
Cliq Nothing directly All history; export for legal and HR Bruce Wayne
Coda Documents and tables, rebuilt Rollups and nested formulas, replaced per rollup Doc owner

Three rules for the window. Nothing is deleted anywhere. Both systems run for one full cycle, so a missed recurrence shows up in the old tool before the new one is trusted. And the import runs into existing Lists, never into a fresh List that then has to be reorganised, because reorganising after import is how fields get lost.

8. The pilot

Thirty days, two cohorts, and a bar that can fail.

Cohort Who Job Why them
Proof Finance daily control, Technology service desk Show that daily regime work can leave Zoho They use Zoho hardest, so their verdict is real
Visibility Leadership Goals, minutes, one dashboard Visible to you in week one, no guests, no migration

Measured, not felt: tasks created in ClickUp as a share of new work in the cohort's function, Monday minutes captured without a human filing, requests arriving through a form rather than a message, and time to answer "what is outstanding for this person".

The kill criterion, stated before the pilot starts: if the proof cohort has not moved the majority of its new work into ClickUp by day thirty, stop. Keep Zoho, keep the goals and minutes in ClickUp if they earned it, and do not fund a second year of two systems.

Step-up triggers. Business becomes Business Plus only if one of these fires: sub-teams need permission sharing; more than ten Teams are wanted; scheduled report sends pass forty; or Docs need page protection. Otherwise the step-up is a cost with no use.
