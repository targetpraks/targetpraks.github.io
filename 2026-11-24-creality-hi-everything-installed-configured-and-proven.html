04

How a print goes out now

three routes, pick one
  1. One command. plate.py &lt;stl&gt; --upload puts the G-code on the printer; add --start and it begins. Then watch it on the screen or in Fluidd.
  2. Fluidd in the browser. http://192.168.0.148:4408 — drag the G-code in, press print. No app, no account, works from any device on the house Wi-Fi.
  3. The slicer. Export the G-code from OrcaSlicer and drop it into Fluidd. The one-click print button is the piece that is not wired (next section).

Every one of those ends at the same place: a file the printer can start. None of them has been used yet — starting a job is yours to call, so the plates are sitting staged and unstarted.
