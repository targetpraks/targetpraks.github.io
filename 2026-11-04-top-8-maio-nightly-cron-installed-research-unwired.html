The defect: the research queue is 99.9% noise

When research is due, the cycle writes a work queue to data/ops/sweep/<category>.json for a research worker to pick up. That queue is built by walking the entire shared candidate registry (174 products) for every category, rather than only the products that belong to that category.

Concretely, the espresso-machines queue opens with adidas-adizero-boston-13 against criterion heat_up with a 180-day TTL — a running shoe being asked to brew. The registry has no category field at all (data/candidates.yml carries only id, name, brand, price, currency, links, suppliers, price_checked), so the sweep has no honest way to scope itself today.

The six shoes categories show 0 relevant only because their products carry no evidence under a separate list directory — the pool is real but the join misses it. Either way the queue is unusable as written.

Honest by designNone of this silently corrupts a ranking. Research logs skipped, the cadence clock does not advance, and the sweep file names the queued work rather than dropping it. The failure is that a worker wired to this queue would burn real money researching 4,114 wrong cells.
