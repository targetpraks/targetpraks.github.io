Other ways to spend less

  • Cheapest possible basket: R3,714 — but split over four orders (Loot 5, Exclusive Books 3, Takealot 2, Amazon 1). Saves R236 against the two-order plan before delivery fees, and buys four sets of admin.
  • Loot-only: R3,201 for nine titles — one order, but everything arrives in 5–22 working days.
  • Amazon-only: R7,435.98 for nine — roughly double, and two titles missing.
  • Trade-off worth knowing: The Word Collector is R185 at Takealot against R247 at Exclusive Books, and Milo's Monster R169 against R260. Both are already in Cape Town stock, so if the two orders bother you, take those two from Takealot and the rest from Exclusive Books — three orders, R3,797 including the Loot four.

Notes and what could not be verified

Read this before ordering

  • No supplier in South Africa holds all eleven. These are US, UK and Australian picture books; the local trade keeps a shallow range of them, and the rest are ordered in.
  • Takealot's own search would not render from this machine — the results page loads empty and their search endpoint returns "page not found". The two Takealot prices above come from the live product pages, so they are real; every other title is unverified there, not confirmed absent. Search Takealot directly in a browser and you may find more, quite possibly cheaper.
  • Bargain Books was blocked by its bot protection. Worth a call to a branch if you want a fourth price.
  • Prices are list prices, ex delivery, read on 1 November 2026. Loot charges delivery at checkout; Amazon.co.za is free over R450; Exclusive Books is free to store, and free to the door over R450.
  • Editions differ. Where a book exists in both paperback and hardback, the row shows the cheapest edition found; the hardback is listed at Loot and Exclusive Books where it exists, at a higher price.
  • One content caution from the reel itself: The Dark personifies the dark, which may unsettle a sensitive child — the original poster flags it.
