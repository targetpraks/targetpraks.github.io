To confirm before anything is ordered

  • Machine make and exact footprint — drawn 800 x 600 x 1950; eight fit only if each is <= 802 wide
  • Ceiling height — assumed 2700; aircon underside 2200, top 2500
  • Door position, size and swing — REV B shows a 1206 break in the south wall at x 376-1582; drawn as a 900 leaf opening in
  • Electrical capacity — 8 machines ~1760 W + aircon ~1.2 kW input; one socket per machine
  • Water and drain points — machine irrigation feed and tray drain; aircon condensate outfall
  • Is the east wall external? — sets the outdoor-unit position and a gravity condensate run
  • Wall build-up — brief says 200; REV B draws a 114 mm double line
