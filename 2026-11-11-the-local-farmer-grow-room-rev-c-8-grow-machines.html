REV C drew eight machines 802 mm wide. That was not the machine — it was the widest unit that happened to fit the herb-shelf run. The real machine is 6 tiers × 6 trays = 36 spaces, and over a metre wide.

Building to 802 mm would have produced a drawing for a machine you cannot buy.

REV D rebuilds the room around the real unit and proves the fit: at any width from 1 100 to 1 400 mm, eight machines still fit — as 2 + 2 + 3 + 1, with 965 mm working aisles and a 1 200 mm west passage.
