Eight grow machines, four to a row

Revision C of the space plan P033-200.00 — herb shelving replaced by two rows of four grow machines, plus the aircon the plan was missing.

It fits — exactly, with no slack anywhere. Four machines per row have to be 860 mm wide or less: 4 × 860 + 3 × 80 = 3 680, and 3 680 is the entire distance from the work-bench line to the south wall. Order 850 and you keep 40 mm of tolerance at the wall. A 900 mm machine still works if the gaps drop to 60 mm and both rows slide 100 mm north. A 1 000 mm machine does not — you would get six, not eight.

One compromise, stated plainly: the easternmost 210 mm of the work-bench frontage sits behind Row B's end panel — you work that end of the bench from the bay side. Everything else is clear.

The one hard constraint: machine width. The 3 680 mm row length is set by the work-bench line and the south wall, and nothing else in the room can move to give you more. Order machines at 860 mm wide or narrower and the eight fit as drawn.

Aircon

Where it goes

  • High on the south wall, centred over the aisle — x 3485 to 4435 mm, underside 2 400 mm above the floor.
  • 450 mm clear above the machine tops, so nothing blocks the throw.
  • Air runs north down the aisle, parallel to the tray faces — not blowing across the crops, which is what dries a microgreen out unevenly.
  • Condensate must be trapped and piped to a drain; it will run constantly.
  • Outdoor unit on the far side of that wall — confirm it is an external face.

Sensible total3 050 W = 10 400 BTU/h

Specify an 18 000 BTU/h (5.3 kW) inverter split. That is 10 400 plus 20 % headroom and the extra capacity a humid room needs; 12 000 BTU/h is the floor if the room is fully internal and well sealed. The installer's heat-load calculation governs.

Note the two crops in one room: mushrooms want 15–18 °C, microgreens 18–24 °C. One split serving both means the set point is a compromise — worth a separate zone if the mushrooms stay.

What else changed

Out

  • The three herb shelving runs of REV B — gone.
  • Nothing else in the room moves. Sink, west shelving, fridge, bin, work bench, north shelving, humidifier and the two JoJo tanks all stay where ACDC drew them.

In

  • Eight grow machines, 860 × 600 × 1 950, in two rows of four.
  • One 980 mm aisle between the rows, serving every machine.
  • A 2 270 mm clear harvest / pack / trolley bay on the west side — it takes a further row of four later if the west shelving ever moves.
  • Split aircon, high wall, over the aisle.

Where the numbers come from

  • Room, fittings and dimensions: P033-200.00 REV B, ACDC Dynamics, 2022-04-01, sheet 1 of 1 at 1:25. The base drawing's own dimension strings do not reconcile with its labelled 5 050 × 6 000 overall: the right-hand run reads 1 500 + 1 161 + 540 + 540 + 540 + 1 000 + 600 = 5 881, the left-hand run 1 350 + 1 850 + 650 + 600 + 480 = 4 930, and none of the groupings I could form reach 6 000. REV C therefore takes the labelled 5 050 × 6 000 as the truth and places each fitting at its labelled size, so the new work is dimensionally checkable even where the base is not.
  • Grow-machine footprint: a commercial 4–5 tier microgreen rack module. Rack configurations and tray counts — Bootstrap Farmer flood-and-drain rack, HerbSpeak microgreen rack guide.
  • Aircon sizing: sensible load × 3.412 = BTU/h, plus headroom — Hydrobuilder grow-room BTU calculator, Subcooled grow-room HVAC calculator.
