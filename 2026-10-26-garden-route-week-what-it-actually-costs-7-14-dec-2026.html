Seven nights in Knysna, 7–14 December 2026, costs R8,664 to R22,038 for the whole week. Fourteen live seven-night totals for two adults and two children, every one of them a link you can open. The median is R16,050. The three-bedroom homes — the ones with a second room for the cot — start at R17,610. Add fuel, food, the animals and a contingency and the week lands at about R33,000 all-in.
The two catches, before you fall in love with a house. 1. It is a five-hour drive with a 10-week-old. Cape Town to Knysna is about 490 km and 5h15 door to door with two stops. That is exactly why the first plan said no overnight — this is your override, and the week below is built so the drive is the only hard day. 2. Six weeks out, the peak week is already thinning. The same search moved a week later comes back thinner and dearer, and Plett for these dates prices higher again. Book this week.

4. The week, planned for a 10-week-old and two children

One activity a day at most, two do-nothing days, no drive longer than 45 minutes except the two travel days. Every place is a link.
The one non-negotiable on the drive: leave at 06:30, not 09:00. The N2 between Caledon and George in December is a slow crawl after 10:00, and a 10-week-old who has been in a car seat for three hours in the heat is a hard afternoon for everyone. Two stops, 20 minutes each, feed and change at both.
