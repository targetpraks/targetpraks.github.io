Off means the domestic is not in. Wednesday, Friday and Sunday Lily holds the house — the checks, the list and anything that broke since Saturday.

Monday

Linen day

  • Beds stripped and changed first — all four.
  • Bathrooms: dry, no marks, towels out.
  • Braai washed and covered after lunch.
Tuesday

Deep day

  • Kitchen deep clean, inside and under.
  • Living & dining: surfaces, glass, floor.
  • The week’s ironing, then uniforms in.
Thursday

Rooms day

  • The girls’ rooms, top to bottom.
  • Bathrooms again — mid-week reset.
  • Study and passages, then floors.
Saturday

Reset day

  • Whole-house reset before the weekend.
  • Deep jobs 1–4 off the standing list.
  • Courtyard and bins, uniforms washed.

The standard — what done looks like at 16:00

Bathrooms — dry, no marks, mirrors clear, towels changed.
Kitchen — counters clear, dishwasher on, nothing left in the sink.
Bedrooms — beds made, linen changed Monday, floor walkable.
Living — table cleared, floor free of grit, surfaces clear.
Laundry — basket empty, one load in, school kit dry by Sunday.
Outside — braai clean, bins out Sunday night, nothing loose.

One rule over all of it: nothing of Ricardo’s is moved to another room without Lily knowing where it went.

Lily’s line — Friday

  1. Walk the house against the standard above.
  2. Write the list: what did not get done, what ran out.
  3. Hand it over before the domestic leaves Saturday.
  4. Nothing gets bought twice, nothing runs out on a Sunday night.

Wednesday, Friday and Sunday are Lily’s days in the house — the domestic is not in on those days.
