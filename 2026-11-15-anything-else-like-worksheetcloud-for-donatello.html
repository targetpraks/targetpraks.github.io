01 · The baseline

What WorksheetCloud is, so “like this” has a meaning.

From its own pricing page and FAQ: Grades 1–12, CAPS and IEB, 13 subjects, worksheets, practice exams with memos, study notes and video lessons (videos English only, mainly Grades 1–9). R195 a month per learner on the 12-month plan (R2 340 a year, 12-month commitment) or R299 a month month-to-month. No free trial — a 7-day money-back guarantee instead. Your membership rolls into the next grade each January at no charge.

Where it is strong

A single subscription that covers every Grade 4 subject, matched to what the school actually sets — including Afrikaans to Grade 9. Printable, so it can be done on paper with a memo.

Where it does not fit him

No read-aloud of the questions. Its audio is listening-comprehension content, not accessibility — the questions themselves stay text on a screen. And it is grade-locked: no adaptive level, so a Grade 4 learner reading at Grade 2 gets Grade 4 words at Grade 4 length.

06 · The buy

Two subscriptions, R3 462 for the year

R1 712 + R1 750

  • Nessy Reading & Spelling — the daily 20-minute slot. Start here, and if he will not sit with it, swap to Saving Grace's Reading Booster (R2 490, first two lessons free, no card).
  • StudyChamp Grade 4 full-subjects bundle — the papers for school tests, with memos, done on paper exactly the way he will be examined.
  • Free on top: the Mac reading his PDFs aloud, and Nal'ibali or Book Dash stories for volume.

Skip for now: Edublox (R18 274 — a school-team question), IXL (audio stops helping after Grade 2), Khan Academy Kids (age ceiling), Siyavula and Olico (grades 7–12). WorksheetCloud stays the fallback if the school's own material turns out to need CAPS-shaped video lessons, which StudyChamp does not have.
