The Staff Door

How Infinity Brands staff should reach the agent platform — and why the tailscale link we have today cannot be it. Prepared for Ricardo Maio, 13 November 2026.

The problem, exactly

The chat link we are handing out points at Ricardo's personal Mac mini, on Ricardo's personal tailnet. The platform does not even live there — it lives in the office, and the mini merely relays it.

Staff browser must be signed into the tailnet → ricardos-mac-mini:10030 · tailscale serve → ssh tunnel 127.0.0.1:18080
pid 92382, up 18h → macmini-inf (office) :8080
docker: agenticos-app / db / redis

Identity on that path is rickymaio@gmail.com — one man's personal account, not a company door. Every person we invite would join his tailnet or receive a share of his machine.

That is fine for one engineer testing. For eight department owners, and later for all staff, it is three failures at once: an access model with no company identity, a per-person manual share for every joiner and leaver, and a company service riding on one person's hardware.

What I recommend

Put a company door on the company domain, with identity checked before anything reaches the platform: a Cloudflare Tunnel running from macmini-inf, published as agenticos.infinitybrands.co.za, with Cloudflare Access allowing only @infinitybrands.co.za addresses — and the platform keeping its own invite-only login behind that.

Two doors, both per-person: the edge checks who you are, the app checks whether you were invited. Nothing else on the machine is reachable. Staff install nothing — they tap a normal https link on a phone, the way they already use Zoho.

What I rejected, and why

OptionWhy it loses
Share the mini with each person
what was asked of us this week		A machine share is whole-machine access — screen control (3283), the model gateway (11434), the Maio cockpit, Plex, the agent ports. One gmail address per person, granted by hand, revoked by hand.
A company tailnet for all staffClean network identity, but every staff member installs a VPN client to use a chat tool, and paid seats per user. Right answer for admin access, wrong answer for staff. Keep the tailnet for us.
Funnel on the mini (quickest)Publishes a company service on Ricardo's personal tailnet domain, keeps the office relay hop, and leaves identity at the app login only.
A public hostname with app login onlyOne password between a stranger and a platform that holds shared Zoho credentials. No edge identity, no per-person audit, no rate limiting.

The order of work

By dependency, not by date. Each stage ends on a yes/no fact, never on a week.

StageWhat happensDone when
1 Settle the door and answer this week's request. Nobody gets a whole-machine share. Tuan has reached the console once through access limited to the console port only — or he holds a seat on the new door instead.
2 Stand the door up: tunnel from macmini-inf → agenticos.infinitybrands.co.za; Access policy limited to company addresses; app stays invite-only. Two named pilot staff sign in from a phone on mobile data, no VPN installed — and an address outside the company is refused at the edge.
3 Identity behind the door: SSO (Google route already live, or Zoho Directory through generic SAML) and an entitlement list mapping each member to a Zoho user. A suspended member's session dies and their next tool call is refused; the list answers "who is this person in Zoho".
4 Retire the relay: no staff route through the mini, no ssh hop. The mini keeps admin access only, on a company-owned tailnet with tagged devices and group rules. tailscale serve status shows no staff-facing service, and staff use only the company hostname.
5 Harden for real staff use: rate limits, session expiry, audit retention that names the human, backups, patch cadence. An audit record answers "which person received this salary row" — not just which connection asked.

Risks I would not ship past

  • Shared Zoho identity. The platform calls Zoho as one shared connection while each person has their own app account. Before staff use it on real records, those two must be linked — otherwise a suspended person keeps read access through the shared connection.
  • The office box is the whole service. One host, 76% full disk (18 GB free), no confirmed backup of the platform's database. A staff tool needs a restore someone has actually run.
  • Internet exposure changes the job. TLS, session expiry, upload limits, lockout and a patching rhythm stop being optional once the link works from anywhere.
