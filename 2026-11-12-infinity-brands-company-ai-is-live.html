Three ways to test it, easiest first

1. From your phone — the real test

  1. Open the link above, sign in.
  2. Pick Finance Assistant.
  3. Ask: “When does the blue gate open?”
  4. It should answer “only on Tuesdays” and cite the source document. That answer exists only in the knowledge base — no model knows it — so a cited answer means the whole chain works: document → local embedding → retrieval → model → answer.

Then ask something real: “Which Zoho Projects portals and Cliq channels do we use?” The assistant should give the portal id, the three projects with their open/closed counts, and the four channels, cited from the Zoho map document.

2. One command, six checks

python3 "Work/Infinity Brands/AgenticOS/tools/test-pilot.py" --verbose

It checks the address, sign-in, model access, all eight assistants, knowledge retrieval, and that an answer is actually grounded — then prints a pass or fail for each. Exit code 0 means all six passed. Add --json to keep a record.

3. Interrogate one assistant

python3 "…/tools/test-pilot.py" --bot Marketing --ask "what belongs in a store opening brief?"

Three things still open — one of them needs you

  1. Staff accounts. Right now there is exactly one login: yours. Inviting the eight department owners is your call — and the mail is ready to send those invitations.
  2. Knowledge content. Two documents are loaded: a pilot proof page and the Zoho map. The real corpus — SOPs, brand manuals, policies — sits in WorkDrive, which still refuses our reads on a missing permission. That is the next piece of work, and it is the difference between a working platform and a useful one.
  3. One command in your own Terminal. The link you tap is carried by a tunnel that a background service should keep alive. The service is written but macOS blocks an agent from loading it, so run this once:
    ~/AI\ Hub/bin/agenticos-tunnel.sh install
    Without it the link works now, and stops working after a reboot.
