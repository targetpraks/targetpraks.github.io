“Higgsfield vs ComfyUI” —
what's real, and what fits this Mac

kintu, 11:36, published 3 Nov 2026. The feud is real. The eight “open-source replacements” are real repos — but only one of them fits the machine you just put ComfyUI on.

Checked 4 Nov 2026 · repository stars, licences and last push read live from the GitHub API · prize and cease-and-desist claims confirmed on comfy.org and ComfyUI's own post

What's true

ComfyUI really did launch a $25,000 “Open Source Higgsfield” challenge, Higgsfield's lawyers really sent a cease and desist on 28 October, and Comfy published the letter. Winners are due 6 November.

What's stretched

“120 apps”, “entire platform recreated” overstates it. Most of these are early repos: a handful of stars, video-first pipelines, some still calling paid inference behind the scenes.

What it means here

Nearly everything in the video is video, and video is the one thing a 24 GB Mac with 39 GB free disk cannot absorb today. The image-side project fits immediately; the rest is a deliberate later decision.

The pattern behind the table: the video's headline is a fight about openness, but its substance is video. Six of the eight need a video model (MiniMax H3, LTX 2.5, Wan-class) plus many frames of latent state. On an M4 Pro with 24 GB unified memory that is minutes per clip, and the weights alone are 10–20 GB — against 39 GB free on the system disk.

What this changes for the stack you just built

  • Nothing is broken. ComfyUI 0.44.0 on :8188 with 10 MCP tools across Hermes, dsh and omp is already the “open side” of this argument.
  • ComfyArt is the one immediate candidate. MIT, image-based, rides the SDXL lane we already run — a canvas over generations, no new weights.
  • openfield is the interesting one later. It exposes MCP to agents, which is exactly how our three harnesses already work — but it is video-first and its licence is non-standard.
  • openjutsu is the flagship (★1,539) but it is a character-swap pipeline for video: MiniMax H3 + segmentation. Real project, wrong machine — for now.
  • Skip the fragmented ones. Freefield exists as a scattering of tiny repos; Katana and re-angle had no repository of substance behind them when I looked.
  • A video lane is a budget decision, not a link: ~20 GB of weights on the cold store, slower iteration, and your word before anything is downloaded.

If you want to act

Option A — image side, today: trial ComfyArt against our SDXL lane, one new service, revert by stopping it. No new weights, no disk risk.

Option B — video side, deliberate: pick one target (openjutsu for character swap, backlot for camera moves), put the weights on the Hiksemi cold store, and accept minutes per clip on MPS. Needs your go because of the disk budget.

Option C — do nothing: the argument in the video is already won on this Mac. The stack answers image requests in ten seconds; that is the part of the promise it can keep.
