3 · Proof, not wiring

Two tests were run against every bot, inside the platform.

Each bot answers its own question — 17 of 17

FIN Bot answered from the BU 1 role sheet. PPP Bot located the Papa Pasta menu costing model in 03_Finance & Costing. TLF Bot named the TLF documents that live with other BUs. TIE Bot named its five store folders — Witbank, Brooklyn, Bellville, Sandton, Parklands — and flagged that the listing may hold more.

Each bot refuses a question that belongs to another unit — 17 of 17

“I can't answer that from here — the Papa Pasta menu costing model isn't in my knowledge base, and Papa Pasta isn't part of my unit.” — BND2 Bot, asked a Papa Pasta question
“I can't tell you — it isn't in my knowledge base. I searched the Titan Electrical records for ‘Papa Pasta’, ‘menu costing model’ and menu/food-cost terms. Nothing matched.” — TIE Bot

The scoping test is tools/test_all_units.py — re-runnable, and idempotent.

4 · What the company's own documents revealed

  • Two brands sit in Brand Operations 1, not one. The BU 7 folders spell them EXP (ACDC Express) and TIE (Titan Electrical), each mirrored across the ZOR and ZEE engagement networks — four folders, two brands, two networks. Titan Electrical was missing from the plan until the Local Farmer's register named it.
  • Brand Ops meetings run the three brands together. The TLF register states it plainly: “Brand Ops meetings cover TLF, ACDC Express and Titan Electrical together.” The meeting record therefore sits at the BND 2 root, on purpose.
  • Two registers are gold. TLF and Papa Pasta each maintain a document register — the map of every drawer, the rules, and the satellites that live in other BUs. Both are now knowledge, verbatim.
  • The numbering disagrees. The 2026 role reference lists nine BUs (BU 4 = Information Technology, BU 7 = ACDC Express, BU 8 = The Local Farmer, BU 9 = FXS). WorkDrive files Data Services as “BU 4” and IT as “BU 3”. Every assistant is addressed by code, not number, so the conflict cannot corrupt an answer — but the BU should settle which scheme is canonical.
  • INFX is two units, not one. INFX Solutions (client services, platform development and delivery) and INFX Labs (implementations, brand incubation) are separate COR units the role reference does not list. Both now have an assistant; neither has role definitions, and their bots say so.
  • Divorced Dads has no material. The brand folder in INFX Labs holds nothing. No brand knowledge was invented for it — its assistant states the gap.
  • The KPI scorecard is referenced but absent. The role reference defers all weightings to “a separate KPI scorecard document”. It is not in WorkDrive under that name, so no bot can answer a weighting question yet.

Re-running this

Four idempotent tools live in AgenticOS/tools/:

  • build_unit_seeds.py — slices the role workbook per unit and writes the factual inventories.
  • provision_units.py — base, documents, bot, project, channel, for every roster entry.
  • test_all_units.py — own-question and cross-unit-refusal proof for the whole roster.
  • harvest_workdrive.py — a WorkDrive workbook in, one document per sheet into a base.
