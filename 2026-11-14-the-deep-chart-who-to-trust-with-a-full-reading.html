02 What "full chart" actually means

Most people selling "a reading" are selling an hour of personality. These are the four things worth insisting on — every name below clears at least three.

1 · The whole chart

Sun, Moon, Ascendant, every house, every aspect, the ruler of the chart — not a sun-sign scaffold with transits bolted on.

2 · Timing, not just character

Transits, progressions, solar return — or dashas and divisional charts in the Jyotish lane. "Who am I" without "when" is half a reading.

3 · Something you keep

A recording or a written summary. Anya records; Kerby sends a written summary; Sharma's fee includes the prepared chart.

4 · A second session

An ongoing relationship with a return price, not a one-night event. Anya, Diana Rose and Garven all price the repeat visit.

07 What to send, and what to ask

Send the birth data exactly as you have it

19 August 1984 · 20:20 SAST · Johannesburg — with the recorded time source. Your chart is already computed to the arcsecond (Swiss Ephemeris, Sun Leo 26.92°, Moon Taurus 26.28°, Asc Aries 1.19°), so no rectification is needed and you should not pay for one.

Ask each one the same three questions before paying

How long is a full first reading; do I get a recording or written summary; and what does a follow-up cost? Sharma publishes all three. Anya publishes all three. Garven, Simpson and Kerby publish none — those are the three conversations to have before any money moves.

Say what you actually want read

Not "tell me about myself" — name the live questions: the business, the move (Rhodes, Portugal, the town shortlist), the family charts, the 2026 turn. Every practitioner on this page does better work against a real question, and the good ones will tell you when a question is outside the chart.

Refuse the upsell

Gemstones, ritual packages, "removal" ceremonies, or a fee that climbs once you are on the call. Sharma's page explicitly rules this out; that is the standard to hold everyone else to. If a reading ends with a product, the reading was the sales pitch.
