The parent appoints the facilitator to support his son, Donatello Maio, in Grade 4 at Reddam House Durbanville from the first day of the 2027 academic year, on the following terms.

1 The learner and the school

  1. 1.1 The learner is Donatello Maio. In 2027 he is in Grade 4 at Reddam House Durbanville.
  2. 1.2 The school has confirmed his place for 2027 on condition that his support plan is in place from the first day of the year. This agreement is how that condition is met.
  3. 1.3 Reddam House Durbanville is not a party to this agreement and carries no obligation under it. On the school's premises the school's rules, timetable and safeguarding procedures apply.

2 The appointment

  1. 2.1 The facilitator is appointed as Donatello's in-class facilitator — a learning-support role. She supports the class teacher's programme; she does not replace the teacher and does not take a class of her own.
  2. 2.2 The appointment is personal. The facilitator renders the services herself and may not send anyone else in her place.
  3. 2.3 The facilitator is responsible for her own income tax. Nothing in this agreement makes her an employee of Reddam House Durbanville.

3 Term — Term 1 guaranteed, the rest earned

  1. 3.1 Term 1 is guaranteed. The appointment is secure from Wednesday 13 January 2027 to Friday 19 March 2027 — the whole of the first term of the 2027 school year.
  2. 3.2 The rest of the year depends on Term 1. From Tuesday 6 April 2027 to the last school day of 2027, the appointment continues only if the Term 1 review in clause 3.3 is passed.
  3. 3.3 The Term 1 review. By Friday 26 March 2027 the parent will review the term against Schedule A and give the facilitator his decision in writing. If it is passed, the appointment continues for the rest of the year on the terms of this agreement. If it is not, the appointment ends on 19 March 2027 and neither party owes the other anything further.
  4. 3.4 The 2027 term dates, for reference: Term 1 13 January – 19 March; Term 2 6 April – 25 June; Term 3 20 July – 22 September; Term 4 5 October – 8 December. The school's own published calendar governs if it differs.
In plain words. Term 1 is hers whatever happens. If the term goes well — the boy settles, the reading programme runs, the reports arrive — she has the year. The review date is written down so that neither side is guessing in March.

4 Place of work and hours

  1. 4.1 The facilitator works at Reddam House Durbanville, on site, every school day of every term.
  2. 4.2 Hours: 07:30 to 15:00, Monday to Friday. That is the school day in full, plus one hour after dismissal (14:00 to 15:00) during which she stays at school and takes Donatello through his homework.
  3. 4.3 Those hours are provisional until the school publishes its 2027 timetable. A 14:00 dismissal is the assumption; the hour then follows the real dismissal time — 15:00 if the day ends at 14:00, later if it does not. She is on site and ready before the first bell, and she remains until the homework hour is finished.
  4. 4.4 No work is required on public holidays or in school holidays. Extra sessions in the holidays may be agreed in writing and are paid at R600 an hour.
  5. 4.5 If she cannot attend, she tells the parent and the school as early as possible — before 06:30 where she can — so cover can be arranged.

5 What she does

  1. 5.1 Reads and scribes in class as Donatello needs it, so that the reading and the writing do not stop the learning.
  2. 5.2 Delivers a structured, scripted one-to-one literacy and spelling programme — 45 minutes a day, four days a week, inside the school day — following the programme as written, and keeps the session records. The programme and its levels are agreed in writing before the first day of Term 1.
  3. 5.3 Scaffolds him inside the Grade 4 classroom — breaking tasks down, keeping him with his class rather than beside it.
  4. 5.4 Runs the homework hour at school each day, from homework set to homework done and packed.
  5. 5.5 Liaises with the class teacher, the remedial staff and the school's support team, and attends meetings about Donatello when asked and given reasonable notice.
  6. 5.6 Writes a short monthly report to the parent by the last school day of each month: what was covered, what moved, what did not, and what she will try next. A full written review is due by Friday 19 March 2027, in time for the Term 1 review.
  7. 5.7 Uses his reading pens with him where reading would otherwise stop him — the RocketPen Reader in class, the RocketPen Exam pen in tests — as the school permits. The pens are access; the daily reading sessions are the cure. She keeps the two separate.
  8. 5.8 Follows the school's rules and safeguarding procedures, and keeps everything she learns about any learner at the school confidential.

6 What the parent provides

  1. 6.1 The programme materials for the agreed levels, and the tutor training that comes with them. The parent buys these and hands them to her; she buys nothing herself.
  2. 6.2 Donatello's two reading pens — the RocketPen Reader and the RocketPen Exam — bought from Inclusive Solutions (order QU27610) and delivered on 19 November 2026 — with their earphones.
  3. 6.3 The information she needs to do the job: his reports, his support plan and his teacher's guidance, shared with her in confidence.

  5. 8.5 Whatever the school requires of an adult working on its premises — induction, sign-in, identification. She completes it before the first day.

9 Keeping in touch

  1. 9.1 A WhatsApp group is opened before the first day of term, with the facilitator, her mother and the parent, for the ordinary business of the day: whether she is in, what homework came home, a message from the teacher, a change to the plan.
  2. 9.2 Anything about Donatello's progress goes in writing: the monthly report by email, and copies of school correspondence by email.
  3. 9.3 The group is for logistics and for her own support. It is not the place for other learners, and not the place for a difficult conversation — those are a call or a meeting.

10 Confidentiality and safeguarding

  1. 10.1 Everything she learns about Donatello — his reports, his diagnosis, his therapy, his family — stays confidential. It is used to teach him and for nothing else.
  2. 10.2 Nothing about him is posted, shared or discussed outside the people working with him, without the parent's written agreement.
  3. 10.3 She never takes a photograph or a recording of him, or of any other learner, on her own device.
  4. 10.4 If she has a concern about his safety or wellbeing, she tells the parent the same day.

11 Ending it

  1. 11.1 During Term 1 either party may end this agreement on two weeks' written notice.
  2. 11.2 From Term 2, either party may end it on one full school term's written notice, so that Donatello is not left without support mid-term.
  3. 11.3 Either party may end it immediately, in writing, for dishonesty, for a safeguarding concern, for being under the influence at school, or for repeated unexplained absence.
  4. 11.4 On ending, she returns anything belonging to the family or the school, and hands over her session records and her last report.

12 The whole agreement

  1. 12.1 This document, with Schedule A, is the whole agreement. Changes are made in writing and signed by both parties.
  2. 12.2 If any part of it cannot be enforced, the rest stands.
  3. 12.3 It is governed by the law of the Republic of South Africa.
