02

What the free plan actually withholds

the three things a CEO page needs
  • No custom domain. A custom domain starts on Creator (R1 741 a year). Until then the address in every bio is `beacons.ai/ricardomaio` — the platform's name first, yours second.
  • Their logo stays on your page. Removing Beacons branding is a Creator Plus feature — R5 223 a year, which is R5 114 a year more than owning your own domain.
  • No personal front door. There is no LinkedIn, no X or Instagram link, no email or WhatsApp route. A stranger who wants to reach you has to guess, or pick a company and hope.

None of that is a fault in Beacons. It is a creator-commerce product doing exactly what it advertises — storefront, courses, email, media kit, a 9% cut of sales. You are not selling anything through it, so you are carrying its branding and its URL for nothing.

The pick: your own page on your own domain

The decision gate ran the seven options against three-year cost, control of the address, fit for a multi-business personal page, upkeep, and platform risk, and returned option E — a single-page site hosted free on GitHub Pages at `ricardomaio.co.za` — at 0.87. Next best, Carrd Pro Standard, scored 0.09. Staying on the free Beacons plan scored 0.01.

That matches what you already have: the fleet publishes HTML to GitHub Pages from this Mac today, so the page is a data file plus one command, not a new tool to learn. It also fixes the thing the free plan cannot: the address in your bios becomes your name, the logo disappears because there is no platform, and nothing can reprice you.

08

Why not the others

one line each
  • Stay on Beacons Free — R0 and it works, but every bio link announces a creator tool and the logo stays. Fine for a side project, not for the front door of a group of companies.
  • Beacons Creator — R1 741 a year buys the domain and leaves the branding. You would still be renting the look.
  • Beacons Creator Plus — R5 223 a year to remove a logo from a page that sells nothing. That is R5 114 a year more than the domain you would own instead.
  • Linktree Pro — R3 134 a year. Brand recognition for teenagers, not a business card.
  • Carrd Pro Standard — the only serious off-the-shelf runner-up at R440 a year all in. Worth it if you want to edit the page yourself in a browser; you would not, and it locks the design to Carrd's blocks.
  • Lnk.Bio Unique — R957 in year one, R522 a year after, for customisation depth you do not need on a six-link page.

What I could not verify

The public page hides the real hrefs and the click counts behind a login — a headless browser was met with Cloudflare's challenge, so the link inventory above comes from the page text, not from the Beacons dashboard. The plan is inferred as Free from two visible facts (no custom domain, Beacons footer present), not read from your account.

Two naming facts, both checked from this Mac: ricardomaio.com is taken — registered in 2004 and live as the portfolio of a different Ricardo Maio, a photographer — and ricardomaio.co.za is available, as are .me, .net and .io. That is why the recommendation is the local domain and not the obvious one.

If you would rather not move yet

the two-hour version
  • Delete the Codeblaze and Titan Electrical buttons, and replace the empty Hobbies block with a contact block (email plus WhatsApp) — that alone makes the current page useful.
  • Add your personal socials above the company buttons: the page should answer *who is this* before it answers *what does he run*.
  • Set a review date. A free page with someone else's name on it is a placeholder, not a decision — if Beacons is still the home in six months, it should be because you chose it.
