Before you press print

  • Machine: Creality Hi, 260 × 260 × 300 mm bed, 0.4 mm nozzle, sliced with Creality's own profile — 0.20 mm layers, 150 mm/s outer wall, 300 mm/s inner, 6 000 mm/s² acceleration, spiral mode where the shape wants it.
  • Already on the printer: every G-code file was uploaded over the network and sits in the printer's file list, named after the plate.
  • Filament: PLA at 220 °C, bed 60 °C. Every plate is single-tool (T0), so one PLA spool in the rack is enough for any job; two spools cover all seven plates at 1 133 g, with 867 g to spare.
  • Bed fill is the share of the 248 mm usable square covered by plastic. The vase plates are low by design: a 240 mm vase needs height, not floor.
  • Files: each plate is one merged STL with the arrangement baked in; individual parts are in stl/<person>/; the generator scripts are in tools/.
