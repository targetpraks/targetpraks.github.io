The deep jobs 1–4 — Saturday

Choose one deep job each Saturday. Confirm the rotation with Lily on Friday.

  • 1 · Windows and glass — inside, and the outside of the ground-floor panes.

  • Breakfast kitchen reset — dishes, counters, table. Lunchboxes and bottles into bags.

  • Wednesday — checks and list · cooking · laundry.

The Friday walk — four steps, then hand it over

  1. Walk the house against the standard at 16:00 — every room, not a glance.
  2. Write the list: what did not get done, what ran out.
  3. Hand it to the domestic before she leaves on Saturday.
  4. Nothing gets bought twice; nothing runs out on a Sunday night.

Thursday — the check-and-plan list

The week's plan, written down before the weekend: what is short, what is finished, what the domestic and the gardener owe. Julie does hers on Wednesday.

Open: her real load

  • The nights. 22:00–06:00, the feeds and the resettles. see the split below

  • Gianna pickup on the days Lily cannot; tennis run Tuesday 15:00.

The night split is not settled in the record

10 Open questions

Compare the options below. Use the notes sheet to record what the household agrees and when the change starts.

Decisions to make together

  1. The nights. Split 22:00–02:00 / 02:00–06:00, or Julie takes the whole 22:00–06:00?
  2. The gardener's days. Tuesday and Friday, or Tuesday and Saturday?
  3. The bins. Tuesday evening, or Sunday night with the house walk-round?
  4. Homework. Donnie Mon/Wed and Gianna Tue/Thu, or every school day rotating Ricardo → Julie → Lily?
  5. Cooking. Does Lily also cook on Saturday? The week board gives Ricardo Monday, Wednesday, Friday and Sunday, while Julie’s sheet assigns those evenings to her.
  6. Baths and bedtime. Who covers the evenings assigned to Julie during her protected 17:00–20:00 feeding window?

Assumptions still standing

  • Lily's 12.8 hours a week — the number most likely to be wrong.
  • The pool has no service contract — if it does, three tasks and about 1.5 hours disappear.
  • Bed linen — nobody has said whether it is the domestic's or Lily's.
  • The 07:30 drop-off — not on the children's switch, so it stays even in a week without them.
  • No away week has ever been run. The surge configuration exists on paper (Julie 38.7 hours) but has never been used.
  • Green waste — leaves with the household refuse, or its own day?
  • The four deep jobs — the rotation order is proposed, not agreed.
