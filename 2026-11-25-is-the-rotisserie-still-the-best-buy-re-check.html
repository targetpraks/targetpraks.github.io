Outdoor Warehouse — checked, and the answer is no

What they actually stock

  • Braai grids: 26 items — griddles, tripods, U-braais, one rotisserie: LK's Large Flat Basket & Spit, R1 599.
  • Braais: 23 items — Technipunch, LK's, Kamado Jan, Z Grills, BraaiMaxx, Dreem Fire. No rotisserie braai and no skewer frame.
  • Braai accessories and firepits: no rotisserie, no skewer carousel, no souvla machine anywhere in the tree.

What that means

  • OW cannot supply a multi-skewer frame at all. Its one rotisserie is a single shaft with a flat basket — a chicken cage, not an espetada rig.
  • It is, however, the cheapest way to own a rotisserie: R1 599 versus R14 455. Worth knowing before you spend.
  • LK's is a solid SA brand and OW has stores, so it is the low-risk cheap experiment if you want to test a spit before committing.

Honest caveats on SpitAlles. No customer reviews on any of its nine products yet — it launched this season. The carousel end plates are thin stamped steel and the skewers are slim, so it is built for sosatie and souvlaki-sized pieces, not a loaded 5 kg roast. The shaft is 600 mm, so it cooks over part of a 1200 mm firebox rather than spanning it. None of that is disqualifying at R3 007 — but it is why the gate still puts the proven R14 455 frame first.
