4. What was deliberately not changed

  • The platform binding stays approval: "writes". For platform tools the platform holds none to writes by design ("these tools act with the authority of whoever is asking"), and those are exactly the staff-access and configuration actions (invite a member, publish an agent, create a routine). ALWAYS_ASKS tools — memory sharing, correcting someone else's claim, publishing, org/deployment changes, deletions of routines and watches — cannot be waived at all.
  • organizations.chat_may_waive_approvals stays false for infinity-brands. Flipping it is org-wide: it enables "Approve everything" in every chat for every seat and loosens routine action approvals — it would change the unit bots, not just Iron Man.
  • Iron Man's two routines are untouched. "Platform night watch" (21:10 Mon–Fri) and "Monday platform brief" are pinned to version b8925f51-… and keep running that frozen spec with their standing action consents. Re-pinning them would invalidate those consents and could park them unattended, so it is a separate decision.

5. What is still queued, and what now holds the line

Nothing on the six org connections. Every tool Iron Man can reach runs on his word: zoho_write and zoho_mcp_call (the proxy that can reach Books and Invoice), mail_send, cliq_messages, the ClickUp deletes, the GitHub merge/delete/repo tools, and mac_exec / mac_write. The 24 h baseline was 77 cards (clickup_clickup_create_task 46, zoho_mcp_call 25, six others); that queue is now 0.

What still stops a call, and cannot be turned off:

  • The platform binding — agenticos_* stays on writes, so publishing an agent, creating a routine, changing the org or inviting a member still asks. The platform holds none to writes for its own tools by design.
  • ALWAYS_ASKS — sharing memory, correcting someone else's claim, publishing an agent, org/deployment changes, deleting a routine or watch, inviting a member. No policy waives these.
  • The instructions — the only brake on money, legal, deletions, staff access and anything leaving the company is now the seat's own rule: prepare it, show Ricardo the exact change, wait for his word. That is a rule, not a control. It is written in the published spec, so it travels with every run, but it can be argued with by a model where a card cannot.
  • The audit trail — every call is in agent_runs / the run transcript, so a wrong write is visible after the fact even when nothing asked first.
