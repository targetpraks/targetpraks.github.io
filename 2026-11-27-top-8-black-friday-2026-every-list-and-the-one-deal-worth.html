How this was measured

Method. The 24 list files were read from the Top 8 engine (apps/top8/content/curated/sections/, last touched 25 Nov 2026). Prices were then pulled live from each retailer's own catalogue feed in a real browser session on 27 Nov 2026 — the JSON the shop itself serves to its product pages — and every discount computed in code as (compare-at − price) / compare-at. No price here was read off a marketing banner, a Google snippet or a newsletter. Where a store's compare-at price is a standing markdown rather than a Black Friday price, the row is labelled Sale, not BF rather than being counted as a special.
