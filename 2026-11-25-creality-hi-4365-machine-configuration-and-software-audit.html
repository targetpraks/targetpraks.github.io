Creality Hi-4365 — machine, configuration and software

Audited 25 November 2026 · 117 printer objects, 135 config sections, a 16-check live test

The printer is sound, the filament path is half-repaired, and the configuration has one bug that would quietly ruin the job.

The tube fix moved the CFS from 0 of 4 slots delivering to 2 of 4. Everything mechanical passes. What stands between this machine and Donatello's plate is two stubborn slots and a fan macro that halves every cooling command.

2 · Configuration faults, ranked by damage

Every line below is quoted from the files on the machine, and each was re-checked against the file myself.

FaultWhat it does
1filament_sensor arrives disabled Runout protection is pause_on_runout: true in the config, but the live object reads enabled: false. With max_extrude_only_distance: 1000 the machine will grind a dry nozzle for hours without noticing — the exact failure that killed the previous unit.
2[gcode_macro M106] halves the fan Whenever Rmode.flag == 0 — which is every print, because START_PRINT calls Rmode_exit — any fan command above S=102 is halved and everything below is floored at 20 %. Your slicer's cooling curve never reaches the nozzle.
3min_temp: 0 on both heaters, max_temp: 320 A failing thermistor reads as a low positive temperature, so the fault never fires. The nozzle is also allowed to 320 °C against a 300 °C rating.
4idle_timeout: 99999999 No idle shutdown. A cancelled or paused job leaves the hotend at temperature indefinitely.
5Corner velocity has three values and the worst wins The file says 8.0, the finish and pause macros say 5, and line 781 of START_PRINT sets 10 — so every print runs at 10 with acceleration 12000. That is where corner ghosting comes from.
6gcode_arcs resolution: 1.0 Arcs are flattened into 1 mm chords. On Dummy 13's small round features that is visible faceting.
7START_PRINT never re-levels the gantry Z_TILT_ADJUST is commented out at line 803. The saved mesh spans 0.74 mm with a 0.4 mm high left edge, so the mesh spends its headroom compensating for a tilt that could be removed.
8Probe at 45 °C, print at 215 °C The mesh and the Z datum are taken cold, bridged by one hand-typed constant with no recorded basis for the value.

No slicer setting produces true full cooling on this machine. Overhangs, bridges and the purge tower are exactly what a two-colour job leans on, so this is patched before anything prints.

4 · What is not a problem, though it looks like one

  • Bed mesh fade 5/50 — leave it. On a 51-layer, 8.38 mm part, layers 1 to 31 get full correction and even the top layer keeps 93 %. It never prints uncorrected where it matters.
  • The extruder is configured correctly. Rotation distance 6.9, 16 microsteps, pressure advance 0.04, spreadcycle rather than stealthchop. It advances exactly 100.0 mm when asked for 100 mm.
  • Z offset is not missing. This machine probes with the nozzle itself, so z_offset: 0 is the design, not a gap. What needs verifying is the temperature-compensation constant, not an offset.

6 · Order of work

  1. CFS delivery — blocks everything. Free the two dead slots, or move both job colours into the two proven ones. Done when each colour completes a load, a cut and a retract on its own.
  2. Runout guard on. Done when pulling filament at the toolhead pauses a running job within a second.
  3. Patch the fan macro. Done when S=255 gives 255 at the pin and S=0 gives 0.
  4. Z datum. Gantry levelled, probe accuracy at three points under 0.01 mm, a one-layer square measuring 0.14–0.18 mm at full temperature.
  5. Motion. Shapers measured on this frame, arcs at 0.2, corner velocity 5 everywhere including line 781.
  6. Extruder truth. Mark 120 mm, ask for 100, measure what actually entered the hotend.
  7. Slicer contract. Slice plate 1 and read three things: the header, one toolchange block, the tail.
  8. Safety config. Minimum temperatures above zero, 300 °C ceiling, explicit heater watchdog, a finite idle timeout.
  9. Supervised smoke job — two colours, three forced changes, an hour. Then Donatello's plate, first hour watched.

7 · How this audit was made, and its limits

Codex was asked first and refused: the workspace is out of credits. Rather than drop the second opinion, the same evidence bundle went to an independent reviewer, and I re-checked its load-bearing claims against the files myself — the corner-velocity override at line 781, the commented-out gantry levelling at line 803, the probe-at-45-°C sequence, min_temp: 0 on both heaters, and the duplicated virtual_sdcard section. All five held.

Three things remain genuinely unknown and are named rather than guessed: whether Orca's numeric T1 matches the box's own T1A…T1D naming, what the temperature-compensation constant's units are, and why the runout sensor arrives disabled. The first sliced plate answers the first; a job log answers the others.
