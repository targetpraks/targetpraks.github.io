4. The Titan question, answered

Titan Electrical is closed — the space you asked for is an archive

ClickUp has sixteen spaces and not one of them is Titan. WorkDrive holds 210 GB and 13,878 files under the Titan name. That is not a contradiction, it is a state: the brand was wound down and its records still exist. The automation sweep of 2026-11-23 recorded Titan as closed and switched its workflows off; the platform's Titan project was deleted from the console; and the knowledge bases carry no BU 13.

So I am proposing the Titan space be named ARC TIE - Titan Electrical (Closed) — a visible archive prefix outside the numbered live series, exactly as you asked for a space of its own, without teaching anyone that there is a thirteenth business unit. Data Services gets the same treatment: ARC DAS - Data Services (Retired) — 17.2 GB, no ClickUp space, and its lists survive only in Projects.

If you would rather Titan sat inside the numbered series as BU 13 TIE - Titan Electrical, say so — it is one word in the name and I will build the matching ClickUp space at the same time so the two systems never disagree.

The brands are already the slicing dimension everywhere else

ClickUp's function spaces do not run one bucket per department — they run one bucket per brand inside each department. Finance carries FIN ZOR - EXP, FIN ZOR - TLF, FIN ZOR - PPP and the matching FIN ZEE - … set, alongside four store folders. That is the proof that splitting the Brand Ops folder by brand is not a new idea imposed on the company: it is the same cut the rest of the structure already makes, finally applied at the level where the brands live.

It also sets one rule for the brand spaces: a brand space holds the brand's own material, never a copy of its finance or HR files. Where an EXP document also belongs to Finance, it stays in BU 01 FIN - Finance / FIN ZOR - EXP and the brand space points at it. Two homes for one document is how the 13 TB got to be 13 TB.

If I move a folder into a new space without sharing that space first, the people who could open it this morning cannot open it this afternoon — and they will not report a permissions change, they will report lost documents. So every new space is shared to the same groups before the first folder lands in it, and the member list is read back as the proof.

There is a second, quieter half of this. 24 of the 125 WorkDrive groups still carry the BND name — including every brand team group: BND 1: Business Unit, BND 2 MKT: NAT, BND 1 COM: FSC, BND 1 PER: ICU, BND 2 SOL: CSR, Brand 1 Team, Brand 2 Team, BND Compliance, BND Performance, BND Marketing.

Renaming folders alone does not finish this job

Every sharing dialog, every "who has access" panel and every notification will keep saying BND 1 until the groups are renamed or split. That is the difference between "we renamed the folders" and "we stopped doing BND1 and BND2". It is also the one step I will not do blind: a group carries its members, and splitting BND 2 MKT: NAT into TLF and PPP halves needs a human to say who goes where. Phase 4 prepares the exact rename list; the brand-specific EXP and TLF groups already exist and can take the space shares today, but there is no PPP group and no TIE group — those need creating.

8. Plan — stages, each with a boolean exit

No dates. Each stage closes when its exit condition is true, and the next stage starts only then. Inside a stage the work runs in parallel.

Stage 0 — Prove the lane, before touching a live folder

Create one throwaway private space, share it to a test group, rename it, read it back, trash it. Ten minutes of work that proves create, share, rename and read-back on this token with this org's admin settings.

Exit: the probe space exists, carries its share, renames, and lands in the trash — all four verified by read-back. If any leg fails, the whole plan stops here and I bring you the error, not a workaround.

Stage 1 — Rename the nine spaces that stay in place

FIN, HRM, ADM, ITC, MKT, SAL, FXS, FXL, NVP, SNR EXCO. Nothing moves; every id, share and link survives because a rename keeps the folder id.

Exit: the 19 team folders read back with exactly the target strings (byte-for-byte compare, not by eye), and each renamed space's member list matches its pre-rename member list row for row.

Stage 2 — Split the brand ops folders

Create ARC TIE and BU 09 PPP. Share both to the groups that can open BND1 / BND2 today. Rename BND1 → BU 07 EXP - ACDC Express and BND2 → BU 08 TLF - The Local Farmer. Then move, by id: ZOR TIE and ZEE TIE into ARC TIE; Papa Pasta into PPP. Rename the six desk folders (ZOR EXP→EXP ZOR, and so on).

Exit: no space named BND1 or BND2 exists; each brand space's file count and byte total equal the sum of the parts that entered it (counted through the API, before and after); every moved folder's parent_id reads back as its new home; every new space shows the expected groups as members.

Stage 3 — Signposts, so nobody has to guess

A 00 - READ ME (moved) note in every vacated slot and every renamed space: old name → new name, the full number map, and the date. Relabel the 12 legacy drawers inside EVERYONE (BU 3: ADM, BU 4: INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY, BU 5: DATA SERVICES … and the three ZOR BU n brand drawers) so the drive stops teaching two numberings at the top and five underneath.

Exit: every changed space has a signpost naming its old name; the number map is filed once in EVERYONE and once per renamed space; EVERYONE's drawers carry the new labels with their contents untouched.

Stage 4 — The groups (needs your call, see decision 3)

Rename the BND-branded groups to their brand names and split the two "Business Unit" groups by brand. Create a PPP group and a TIE archive group. Re-share each brand space to its own group and remove the old BND shares.

Exit: no team folder is shared through a group whose name still begins with BND; every brand space's access list is brand-pure; a named person from each brand confirms they can open their space.

Stage 5 — Optional tidy-ups, each authorised on its own

  • Move the 50 brand-named recordings into their brand spaces; leave the 68 untagged ones labelled.
  • Rename the eight Zoho Projects projects whose names encode the old slots (a write to a live business system — yours to authorise).
  • Create BU 10 DID - Divorced Dads and REP - Reporting & Notes the day the first document for either exists.
Exit: each item is either done and verified, or explicitly declined and recorded as declined.

9. Scope — what this job does not touch

  • The 1,116 old-numbered names below the top level. Recordings, store folders and archive drawers keep their historical names. Renaming a 2024 recording to say "BU 03" fixes nothing and destroys provenance.
  • Zoho Projects (27,168 files, 578 folders). Those are attachments of live project records. Moving them detaches them.
  • The inside of MKT (11.3 TB, 613k files) and ITC (209 GB, 126k files). Their internal trees do not mirror ClickUp at all — that is a separate reorganisation with its own census, and I will not smuggle it into this one.
  • Anything in Shared with Me or My Folders. Those are not team folders and are not ours to file.

10. Verification — how you will know it worked

  1. Every claim in the receipt comes from a read-back, not from the write call's 200.
  2. Each space is counted through the API before and after: files, folders, bytes. A move that loses a file is a failed move, whatever the response said.
  3. The 19 names are compared as strings against the ClickUp API response, not against this document.
  4. Each new space's member list is read back and compared to the source space's, group by group.
  5. A per-item ledger (moves.jsonl) records old id, new parent id, read-back parent id, and verdict.
