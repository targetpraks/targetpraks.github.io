Your direct-cable plan — I'd change it

recommendation: both machines into the router, not into each other The Air has no Ethernet port; a cable means a USB-C dongle, which is 1 GbE at best. If you plug that cable straight into the mini, you consume the mini's only Ethernet port for a private link and leave its house uplink on Wi-Fi — strictly worse than today. If both go into the router (or a switch on it) you get the same 1 GbE path between them, plus a wired house uplink for the mini, and no second subnet to confuse the services that bind to *.

A cable only beats the router if you mean a Thunderbolt cable — TB4/TB5 creates a bridge0 that already exists on both machines and runs at 40 Gb/s. That is worth doing only for bulk file or model moves; it does nothing for agent traffic, which is small and latency-bound.

3

The Air's firewall is off and it sleeps after a minute on AC — which will drop your Orca and T3 sessions mid-thought. Two lines in Terminal:

sudo pmset -c sleep 0
sudo pmset -c displaysleep 15
sudo /usr/libexec/ApplicationFirewall/socketfilterfw --setglobalstate on
4

Decide the mini's recovery story your call

This was point 1 and it is still open, because it is a security trade, not a setting. FileVault is on and there is no auto-login, so after a power cut the mini sits at the lock screen and the whole fleet stays down until someone touches the keyboard.

My recommendation: buy a UPS. It fixes the actual failure (power) and keeps FileVault, which is what protects the vault, client data and credentials if the machine is stolen. Roughly the price of a good dinner.
The alternative is FileVault off + auto-login in System Settings → Users & Groups — zero-touch, but that SSD becomes readable to anyone who walks off with the mini.

5

One-time file mount on the Air you, once

This was point 2. The rail is healthy and resolving correctly; it just has no keychain entry for the mini's address, so it refuses to mount rather than pop a dialog:

~/.config/maio-stack/air-mount-devmini.sh --connect

Tick “remember in keychain”. After that it mounts by itself forever. You do not need it for Orca — Orca reads the mini over the wire.
