Two corrections to what I told you earlier

correction 1 · the MAC is not randomised — I was reading a mask ifconfig on macOS 27 reports 02:00:00:00:00:00 for both interfaces, which looks like a rotating private address. It is not. Asking the peer's ARP table returns the truth: Ethernet is d0:11:e5:10:18:46 — stable, and safe to reserve against.

The Wi-Fi address is private though: c2:f3:a5:ec:69:7e carries the locally-administered bit. Never base a reservation on the Wi-Fi address — always reserve the Ethernet MAC.
correction 2 · Orca cannot be bound to the tailnet I offered to move Orca off *:6768 onto the tailnet only. I checked before doing it: orca serve has no host or bind flag. --pairing-address only changes what the client is told to dial, not what the server listens on. That fix does not exist at the app level. The honest options are a pf rule (root — I can write it) or segregating guests and IoT onto the guest SSID so "trusted LAN" means something again. The second is the better fix, and it is router work.
