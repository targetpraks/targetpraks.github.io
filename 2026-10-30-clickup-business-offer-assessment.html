The two that would actually hurt, and only if we change how we work:

Section 4Your seat question: what happens when we add licences

Short answer: ClickUp bills added seats pro-rata for the remaining period, but nothing on their help centre or in Laura's email says the added seat keeps your negotiated rate. That is the gap to close before signing.

Three clauses to demand on the order form

  1. “The contracted rate of $11.90 per user per month applies to all additional seats added during the term, billed pro-rata for the remaining period.”
  2. “If the workspace falls below 39 paid seats, the per-seat rate is unchanged for the current term” — or a stated ratchet, so it cannot be re-priced mid-term.
  3. “At renewal the discount does not fall below 30% off the then-current Business list price” — or a named cap on the increase.

Section 5Six things to put to Laura before we sign

  1. Is $5,569.20 per year or per month, and over what term? The arithmetic says a year, but her email never writes it.
  2. What is the same 39 seats at monthly billing? If the monthly premium is small, keeping the freedom to drop a seat is worth more than the discount.
  3. Put the added-seat rate in writing on the order form — the clause in section 4, word for word.
  4. Is 39 a floor for the discount? Ask directly: if we sit at 35 seats at renewal, does 30% still apply?
  5. Quote Enterprise at the same 39 seats and the same 30% — one number, so the comparison is real rather than a choice made in the dark.
  6. Get the landed rand price if Auervo resells it — inclusive of VAT and their margin — instead of converting her dollar figure ourselves.
