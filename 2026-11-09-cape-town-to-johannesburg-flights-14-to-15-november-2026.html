One thing you need to know first

Nothing lands in Johannesburg between 10:00 and 10:45. I checked every nonstop on the route for that morning — FlySafair, LIFT, Airlink, SAA and CemAir. The schedule jumps straight from 09:05 to 10:55. So you pick your side of the window: the 08:50 flight lands 10 minutes late, or the 07:00 flight lands 55 minutes early.

The 08:50 was the pick because 10 minutes late is a much smaller miss than an hour early — and an hour early is exactly the thing you said you did not want on the Sunday. If Saturday has a firm 10:00 start that you cannot be late for, take the 07:00 instead (details below, R202 cheaper).

If you want it cheaper

R3,809 total — FlySafair 07:00 → 09:05 on Saturday (R1,854) and the same 18:10 return (R1,955).

Saves R202 and buys you a calmer arrival window, but you are at O.R. Tambo at 09:05 with a venue check-in that probably is not ready yet.

Cheapest overall is the 20:00 Sunday return at R1,854, but that puts you home at 22:15 — not a late afternoon flight.

Before you book

  • FlySafair's fare includes one free carry-on; the first checked bag is a paid extra. For one night, carry-on only is the cheap path.
  • Bag fees are higher at the airport than online.
  • Book both legs on flysafair.co.za — they sell per leg, so two one-ways is exactly the R4,011, with no hidden bundling.
  • Prices were checked 9 November; they move daily and this is a two-week-out booking.
