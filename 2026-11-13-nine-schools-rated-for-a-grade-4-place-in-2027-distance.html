Grade 4 in 2027 · rated shortlist · 13 November 2026

Nine schools, rated for him — by distance, price and what they actually staff

An exercise, as asked: every school within reach of Welgemoed that could host a boy entering Grade 4 in 2027 who reads and writes below grade level and needs a reader, a scribe and an in-class facilitator. Scored out of 5 on the same six criteria, weighted the way the priorities were given — support first, then distance, then price.

The answer in one line

Curro Uitzicht wins on the weighted score (4.60 of 5) and the decision gate agrees — 0.83 against Tafelberg in a head-to-head. Nothing else in reach combines a 12-per-class cap, therapists inside the school day, a confirmed 2027 fee of R101 520 and 13 minutes from the front door. Tafelberg is the wildcard: better on price by a mile and 15 minutes away, but the family cannot give itself the place — the district does.

The full ranking, the weighting and the two gate runs are below. Prices are the schools' own published figures for the year shown; where a school publishes none, that is said instead of guessed.

01 What he needs, in the schools' own terms

Reading and writing below grade level in English; support in place at school since August 2024 and continuing outside it; the support professionals at both the current school and Curro Uitzicht have put him at a Grade 3 level for English. What that translates into, school by school, is a package: a smaller class, a learning-support teacher with hours in the timetable, and a reader, scribe and extra time granted for assessments. A school that cannot staff the first two cannot deliver the third properly.

So the scoring below rewards, in order: a model actually built for this (30%), staffed support on site (20%), class size (10%), distance from Welgemoed (15%), published 2027 price (15%), and what he leaves with at 18 (10%).

03 The five that matter, in detail

#1

Curro Uitzicht

Assisted learning, Uitzicht + Sonstraal Heights · 13 min · 9.3 km · R101 520 / yr (2027)
4.60 of 5
  • Needs fit Built for exactly this: CAPS with academic intervention, learner not removed from the matric track.
  • Support staffed Occupational therapist and learning-support teachers on site at the primary school; a curriculum coordinator and counsellor at the high school; their fee letter bills a scribe or reader per sitting.
  • Class size Maximum 12 per class (15 in Grade 8).
  • Distance 9.3 km to the Sonstraal Heights campus; the primary campus in Uitzicht is further east, about 20 minutes as the family's own schedule has it.
  • Price R8 460 per month confirmed for 2027 — middle of the field, and it includes the therapy that others bill separately.
  • Pathway to matric NSC to matric, but a narrow basket: 6 subjects at NSC level in 2025.
#2

Tafelberg School

Government remedial, Bothasig · 15 min · 12.5 km · Government fee — on enquiry
4.40 of 5
  • Needs fit A remedial school as its core business, Grades 1–12, CAPS-based — reading and writing difficulty is the reason it exists.
  • Support staffed Therapy is a standing department, not an outsourced arrangement.
  • Class size Small remedial classes; exact caps not published.
  • Distance 12.5 km, 15 minutes off-peak.
  • Price A public school fee, a fraction of any private option — the value outlier of the whole list.
  • Pathway to matric NSC is written, but at remedial pace; confirm the subject range and whether the route stays open to a degree.

Held back by: Admission is not the family's to give: the district places, intake runs early in the year, and there is a waiting list.

The comparison only holds if the therapy is priced in. Curro Uitzicht's fee carries the occupational therapist, the learning-support teachers, the counsellor and the concession admin. 06 What the decision gate said

The shortlist went to the local decision model twice: once with all six serious options, then a head-to-head when the first answer was not decisive. Both runs are shown with the question that produced them, because the same model gives different-looking answers to different questions.

Run 1 · six-way choice

Tafelberg School 31.7%

Confidence 0.14 — a two-way split, not a pick. So the question was narrowed.

Run 2 · head-to-head

“Is Curro Uitzicht better than Tafelberg, given that one has a place and the other needs a district placement?”

Curro Uitzicht 83.0%
Tafelberg School 17.0%

Confidence 0.44. The moment place-availability enters the question, the answer stops being close.

The two runs do not contradict each other: the first asks which school is best on paper, the second asks which is actually available. Tafelberg's 31.7% is a real finding — it is the best value in the set — and it stays on the list as the option to take if a placement materialises before January.

07 What only a phone call settles

Everything above is what the schools publish. Five facts are not published and they decide the rest:

  1. Dunatos' fee for 2027 — 10 minutes from home, the tightest staff ratio on the list, and no price anywhere. One call.
  2. Tafelberg's placement route and its timing. Whether a Grade 4 place is possible for January, what the district needs (the reports already exist), and what the fee actually is.
  3. Hillview's existence as described. Its site did not load; confirm grades, class size, fees and whether the dyslexia tier is staffed or aspirational.
  4. La Vigne's 2027 schedule and its Grade 10–12 plan — a per-grade fee sheet, and the year the first full matric cohort writes.
  5. A place at Curro Uitzicht for January 2027, and whether the Grade 4 admission is still conditional on repeating Grade 3. That condition is the one fact that can reorder this entire table.
