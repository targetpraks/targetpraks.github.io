4. My own number was wrong — corrected

The mirror does not count what Zoho counts

In the first PRD I reported ITC as 126,650 files and MKT as 613,024 files, taken from the TrueSync mount. The API says 494,507 and 613,091. The difference is native Zoho documents: the mount shows them as .zwriterlnk / .zsheetlnk stubs and a recursive walk misses the ones that never materialise locally. So a mirror census is fine for proportion and wrong for counting — a factor of four in Information Technology, where the records are almost all native documents.

From here, every figure comes from storage_info on the team folder, and no bulk operation reads the mount at all. That is also why the mount's current state does not block this work: the restructuring was done through the API, and the API is the authority.

The mirror is also wedged right now

At 06:37 the local TrueSync mount listed the new root correctly but answered Resource deadlock avoided inside BU 07 and BU 08, and showed a stale child list for ZOR 01 ("EXP - ACDC Express", which the API says moved out). The file-provider process was in a frozen state. I restarted TrueSync; the root and most folders recovered, two still return the deadlock.

Consequence for the work: anyone — human or agent — reading the drive through the Finder mount this morning will be told something false. Read through the web app or the API until the mirror settles. If a folder still deadlocks after a full sign-out and re-sync, that is a Zoho support ticket, not a Friday.

5. What is genuinely still wrong

5.1 Titan has 228 GB and no address

A closed brand is the one thing that most needs a clear, boring label. Instead it lives at ZOR 01 - Brand Ops (TIE) / TIE - Mr Titan Electrical — a folder named after a structure we abolished, holding a brand we wound up. "Mr Titan Electrical" is also not the brand's name; it is one of its trading names.

5.2 Two spaces exist for a file type, not a unit

ZOR 02 - Meeting Recordings is a space whose only purpose is that its contents happen to be videos. Every team folder in the list above is a business unit or a house body; this one is a container. And the recordings are split across both leftovers — ZOR 01/Shared - Meetings & Recordings holds the 91 BND1 recordings plus the video assets, ZOR 02 holds the 27 BND2 recordings plus nine meeting folders. Nobody looking for "the meeting about the Rustenburg store" will know which of two spaces to open.

5.3 The names inside the brand spaces still run backwards

ClickUp says EXP ZOR, EXP ZEE, TLF ZOR. The drive says ZOR EXP, ZEE EXP, ZOR TLF. Same three words, opposite order — which is exactly the sort of difference that looks like nothing until two people are searching for the same folder.

5.4 Papa Pasta answers a different question

ClickUp's PPP space holds four desk folders: HOF, ZOR, ZEE, Store Development. The drive holds eleven functional drawers: 00_Index & Register through 10_Archive. Both are good. They are not the same taxonomy, and pretending otherwise would mean dismantling the best-organised library in the company to satisfy a naming rule. Recommendation: leave the PPP drawers, and put the desk view on top later only if Papa Pasta's GM asks for it.

5.5 A brand space with nothing in it

BU 10 DID - Divorced Dads was created empty. It matches ClickUp, and an empty folder is harmless — but it now advertises a brand library that does not exist. Keep it, name it as a placeholder in its description, and let the first document fill it.

6. The rule set, sharpened

  1. A team folder is a unit, never a file type. Finance, ACDC Express, SNR EXCO, and a closed brand each pass that test. "Meeting Recordings" does not.
  2. Closed and retired things take a prefix, not a number. ARC for archive. They stay visible and searchable, and they never consume a slot in the live series that a future brand might need.
  3. Level two matches ClickUp only where ClickUp has a folder for it. The desk grammar is <BRAND> <LEVEL>. Where the drive holds something ClickUp does not describe (Papa Pasta's drawers), the drive's own scheme wins — the rule is about names, not about demolishing good libraries.
  4. A brand space holds the brand's own material, never a copy of finance or HR. Those stay in the function towers, in FIN ZOR - EXP and its siblings, which is where ClickUp already slices by brand.

7. The five moves that finish it

Move 1 — Give Titan its own archive space

Create ARC TIE - Titan Electrical (Closed), share it to the same six groups that can open ZOR 01 today, then move TIE - Mr Titan Electrical into it and rename it TIE - Brand Library. Read the member list back before the move, not after.

Done when: the Titan library returns 13,878 files and 210 GB inside its own space, and the six groups still appear on its share list.

Move 2 — One archive for the recordings, not two

Rename ZOR 02 - Meeting Recordings to ARC BND - Meeting Recordings 2023-2026 and move ZOR 01/Shared - Meetings & Recordings into it. The result is one place, honestly named after the structure that produced it, holding all 118 recordings and the video assets.

Done when: a single space holds both archives plus Video Assets, and the file count equals 61 + 91 + 1 video asset, read back through the API.

Move 3 — Retire the two leftovers

ZOR 01 is then empty apart from its READ ME; ZOR 02 no longer exists under that name. Move ZOR 01 to the trash — not deleted, recoverable, and reversible by the same route. Nothing inside it is still ours to keep.

Done when: no team folder is named ZOR anything, and the trash holds the empty container.

Move 4 — Turn the three desk folders the right way round

ZOR EXP → EXP ZOR, ZEE EXP → EXP ZEE, ZOR TLF → TLF ZOR. Three renames, no content touched, each now matching the ClickUp folder of the same name. Papa Pasta's drawers stay as they are, per §5.4.

Done when: the six desk names inside EXP and TLF match the ClickUp folder list, string for string, and the file count in each is unchanged from the pre-rename read.

Move 5 — The groups, when you can name the people

24 of the 125 groups still carry a BND name, and every sharing dialog keeps teaching the old structure until they are renamed. This is the only step that needs a human: splitting BND 2 MKT: NAT between The Local Farmer and Papa Pasta is a judgement about people, not a rename.

Done when: no brand space is shared through a group whose name begins with BND.

8. What I did not do, and why

I have not moved, renamed, shared or trashed anything. Moves 1–4 are bulk changes to a live business system and they wait for your word — the same rule that applied yesterday still applies, and it is the reason the PRD exists at all. Move 5 additionally needs you to say who belongs to whom.
