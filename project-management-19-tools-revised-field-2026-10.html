Why it wins on your five tests

Nifty prices flat above 31 people — $399 a month billed annually, unlimited members, unlimited projects, unlimited client guests. At 50 staff that is R90,644 a year including VAT, and it stops being a per-seat conversation for good.

What I have not proven

Three things to test before you sign

  • PDF proofing — checked since this page was first written, and it is not there. A search of Nifty's entire help centre for "PDF" returns the upload file-type list and report exports and nothing else: no PDF proofing article exists, and there is no approve/reject sign-off state either. Their own answer to versioned approval is to embed Filestage. Nifty replaces Filestage for video and image rounds only.
  • Depth at 50 non-technical users. Twenty thousand teams use it; that is not the same as your GMs using it. One live brand, one manager, one week.
  • Vendor size. Smaller than Asana or ClickUp. Flat pricing is a gift at 50 and a question at 150 — ask what happens if you double.

The reason this is a correction, not a wobble

On Sunday the brief was manager clarity, and Asana won. Yesterday it became review-plus-relations, and ClickUp won. Today you named a flat-priced tool with proofing, portfolios and check-ins that I had not put in the field — so the answer moved again. I would rather hand you the corrected number than defend a stale ranking.

Where the open-source route still stands

OpenProject Community stays a legitimate third path for a different reason: it is the only option where you own the data outright and pay nothing for 50 users. It still fails two of your tests — no proofing, no per-person staff picture — and it still needs someone to run it. It is a good answer to a different question: sovereignty, not management.

The call, plainly

The file type splits these two, so the rule is simple. Mostly video and image rounds → Nifty Unlimited, R90,644 flat, flat forever, and it answers four of five tests at a price nothing else matches. PDFs with sign-off → ClickUp Business, R136,305, because it proofs PDF and video and carries an approvals feature. Asana Advanced only if a GM's calm is worth R193,000 a year more. Trello, Basecamp, monday, Wrike, Smartsheet, Jira, Notion and ProofHub are all priced and all out — the reasons are on page one, not in my head.

Still open from yesterday

By "reviews", do you mean reviewing the work or reviewing the person? All three tools on this page do the first. None of them does appraisals — if that is in scope, it is a second product and I would build it into the plan now rather than bolt it on later.
