What I changed today

  • Turned off “Automatically add recipients to Contacts” in Settings → Compose. It was on, so every address you ever typed — every typo included — became a permanent suggestion. Zoho confirmed the save, and it is one click to reverse.
  • Left “Suggest contacts based on your usual email-sending pattern” on. It is a convenience, not the cause — the cause is the bloated records behind it.
