Open source — asked and answered properly

Yes, there is. It will replace Zoho Projects and it will not replace Filestage.

Open source genuinely solves the thing you hate about Zoho Projects — tasks that cannot point at other tasks, and a screen that fights you. What it does not solve is review of creative work. That split is the whole answer, so I have priced it as two decisions instead of one.

The honest headline

Every one of these six fails the same two tests: no proofing of images or video, and no proper per-person view of what a manager's staff actually did. They fix your structure complaint. They do not fix your review complaint.

The proofing holeWhy Filestage has no open-source twin

I looked for one. There isn't a maintained one.

Creative proofing is a small, hard market: frame-accurate comments on video, version stacking, reviewer groups, approvals, and a link a franchisee can open without an account. The commercial field is Filestage, Ziflow, PageProof, GoVisually and Frame.io — all closed. On the open-source side the nearest neighbours are document management systems, and they are a different product.

What exists in open sourceWhat it actually doesWhy it is not Filestage
Paperless-ngx, Mayan EDMS, Teedy, OpenDocManScan, store, index, version and route documents through approval stepsBuilt for records, not creative. No frame-level video comments, no annotation canvas, no reviewer groups
Nextcloud (+ Deck)Files, sharing, comments, a kanban boardComments attach to a file, not to a timecode. No approval state on a version
OpenProject / Plane attachmentsAttach the file to the task and discuss itA comment thread beside a file is not proofing

The tell: Nifty embeds Filestage

The commercial all-in-one closest to doing everything — Nifty — ships a Filestage embed so customers can bring real proofing into it. When the vendors who sell the one-platform dream integrate the specialist instead of rebuilding it, that is the market telling you the specialist is not replaceable by a free download.

So the open-source route is two stacks, not one

Stack A — replace Zoho Projects only

  • OpenProject Community for delivery and structure
  • Filestage stays for review of the work
  • Licence cost: R0
  • Annual cash: Filestage only, R37.7k–R62.3k

Stack B — buy one platform

  • Nifty Unlimited R90,644 flat, or
  • ClickUp Business R136,305 if PDFs and sign-off matter
  • Filestage cancelled for image and video rounds
  • Annual cash: R90.6k or R136.3k, all in

On cash alone Stack A wins by roughly R28,000–R53,000 a year against Nifty. That is the entire prize — and it is smaller than one month of an ops person's attention at the wrong moment.

The callWhat free actually costs

Three numbers nobody puts on the open-source slide

5–8 days
Install, configure, single sign-on, import live projects, train managers — before anyone does work in it
½ day / month
Backups, upgrades, security patches, someone's password reset. Forever, not once
R15,107
What one day of that upkeep costs you, implied — because Nifty's whole annual fee only buys six such days

That last figure is the argument in one line. Self-hosting OpenProject only makes financial sense if the person maintaining it costs you less than R15,000 a day — and it assumes somebody wants the job. At 50 staff in a group with no sysadmin, that person is either you or a contractor.

Where I land

The recommendation, unchanged by the free option

Open source answers your structure complaint and none of your review complaint, so it cannot consolidate Filestage — which was the requirement that started this. If you want the open-source route for the principle of owning it: OpenProject Community, keep Filestage, and name the person who owns the server before you install it. If you want one platform and one bill: Nifty Unlimited for image and video rounds, or ClickUp Business where PDFs and sign-off are the real flow.

What I would do next, in order
