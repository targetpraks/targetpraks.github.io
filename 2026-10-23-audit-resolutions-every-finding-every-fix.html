launchctl getenv OLLAMA_HOST → 127.0.0.1:11434 ← my value, ignored

The app overrides it. There is no preference domain to flip: defaults read com.electron.ollama holds only a file-picker size, and ai.ollama.app does not exist.

  • Replace it: quit the app, drop its login item, run ollama serve under a LaunchAgent with OLLAMA_HOST=127.0.0.1:11434. You lose the app window; you gain a closed port.

I did not choose for you, because one of these removes a UI you use.
