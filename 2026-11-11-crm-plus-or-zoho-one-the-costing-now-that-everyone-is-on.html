CRM Plus or Zoho One — now that everyone is on People Plus

Zoho's own list prices, read today in rand, applied to 40 people on payroll. The short version: People Plus did not make Zoho One cheaper — it made it optional.

Buy CRM Plus for the customer-facing seats, not Zoho One for the building. People Plus Enterprise already carries the layer everybody needs — HR, chat and the intranet — at R126 a head.

CRM Plus then only has to serve the people who sell, support or market: R280 a user for a light seat, R798 for a full one. At 40 heads that lands near R9,000–R15,000 a month.

Zoho One all-employee costs R20,720 a month for the same 40 people. It only wins once about 20 seats genuinely need the full customer stack — or the day you decide to run the whole group on one suite.

One What each licence actually buys

These are three different shapes of product, not three prices for the same thing. That is the whole reason the comparison keeps getting muddled.

LicenceWhat it carriesHow it is pricedPer unit, per month
People Plus Enterprise HR core, attendance, shifts, performance, LMS, HR help desk, Recruit, Expense, Vault — plus Cliq and Connect, which is what makes it usable for the whole company. Per user. No obligation to cover everyone. R126
CRM Plus A customer-facing stack: Zoho CRM, live chat (SalesIQ), help desk, email campaigns, social, surveys, projects, analytics. No Books, no WorkDrive, no Sign, no inventory, no POS, no HR. Per named user, tiered. R280 / R798
Zoho One 50+ apps: everything in People Plus at full depth, plus Books, Inventory, POS, Projects, WorkDrive, Sign, Creator, Flow, Analytics, Desk, CRM, Mail, Directory, device management. Per employee on payroll, or per named user. R518 / R1,260

Two facts settle most of the argument. Zoho One's own plan details list the People module at full depth — learning management, HR help desk, OKR, compensation — so One duplicates what People Plus already does. And CRM Plus carries no finance, file or project-approval apps, so it can never replace the suite for the people who run the business.

Two other crossover points worth carrying in your head. If the customer-facing seats sit mostly on Starter at R280, the suite does not win until 56 users — at 40 heads, never. And if you would otherwise buy flexible One seats, all-employee beats it from the 13th seat.

Four The rule to buy by

CRM Plus — when the customer-facing team is a minority

Sales, marketing, service and support, licensed one by one, mostly on Starter with Enterprise for the few who run campaigns, dashboards and blueprints. The rest of the company does not need it, and People Plus already gives them the place they work.

Zoho One all-employee — when most of the company needs the suite

The trigger is roughly half the payroll needing the full stack, or a deliberate decision to collapse the brand orgs into one suite with one invoice. Then R518 a head for everything is better value than any pile of separate licences — but it is a consolidation decision, not a saving.

Plain Zoho CRM — when the seat only runs a pipeline

A pure sales rep never uses surveys, live chat or the help desk. CRM Enterprise at R560, or Professional at R322, keeps R238 to R476 a seat a month that CRM Plus would otherwise charge for tools nobody opens.

Five The trap in the all-employee model

Zoho's own fine print is the whole risk: all-employee pricing requires a licence for every employee on payroll. Our 40 is a payroll figure for the group — but the moment store staff, franchisee staff or seasonal teams land on a group payroll, R518 multiplies by that headcount. Two hundred people on payroll would be R103,600 a month before anyone sells anything.

That is why per-seat CRM Plus is the safer structure for a franchise group: it scales with the people who touch customers, not with the people who carry a payslip. If the group's payroll is going to grow through stores, the flexible and per-seat models are the only ones that stay sane.

Six What still needs confirming before any switch

  • The paid seat count on the current Zoho One org. The directory holds 257 identities with 49 active; the invoice decides how much is being paid for seats nobody uses. That number is worth more than the plan choice itself.
  • How many people actually work in CRM. The answer moves the structure A price, not its direction — but at 20 it flips to the suite, so it needs to be counted, not estimated.
  • Whether Payroll stays outside. Zoho One bundles People at full depth but South African payroll is a separate consideration; if payroll sits elsewhere today, the "One replaces People Plus" line in structure B needs checking before it is treated as a saving.
