2 · Why the dropdowns won’t move

Three independent checks, all made in the live signed-in app and API today:

  1. The product disables the control. In the Share dialog, eight of the nine rows carry the app’s own disabled state — class cu-member-item__permission permission-change-disallowed, computed cursor: not-allowed. Only the owner’s own row is different, and an owner cannot be demoted by design. The bulk “Edit all” control is marked …dropdown__readonly and opens no menu.
  2. The API refuses the level. Writing anything below full edit on a Space answers:
PATCH /v3-user/access/4627083/space/901211092557/acls {"permission_level": 3} 403 {"err":"Unable to edit space permissions.","status":403, "ECODE":"ACL_025","timestamp":"2026-11-18T09:47:23.759Z"}
  1. ClickUp says it is a plan feature. “On our Enterprise Plan, you can also set permissions on Spaces.” Folders, lists, tasks and docs take per-person levels on Business — Spaces do not. See plans and the Enterprise page.

A list grant does override the Space: the five read comment on the lists while the Space row still reads full edit. That is why the rule holds in practice and the dialog stays unchanged. The same override logic is documented under sharing locations and permissions.

3 · The one gap that remains

  • A new list inherits the Space, not the old lists. Anyone who creates a list inside FIN starts it at full edit for the five, because that is the Space’s level. Today all 38 lists are covered; the 39th is not automatic.
  • Nothing else in the Space is exposed. Doc count in FIN is 0, so there is no document surface left at full edit, and the Space holds no dashboards or goals.
  • The desk room is unaffected. Channel membership follows the Space; the five stay in the FIN room.
