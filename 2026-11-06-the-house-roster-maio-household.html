How the split works

The five rules behind it

  1. One owner per task per day. A task with two owners is a task nobody does. The backup only acts when the owner says so.
  2. The split is by day, not just by person. Baths are 4/3 to Ricardo, bedtime is the mirror image, Lily cooks Tuesday and Thursday, Ricardo does Sunday's batch. Nobody owns "bath time" in general — they own Monday's and Wednesday's.
  3. Feeding is not a chore. Julie's 17:00–20:00 window and the night feeds sit outside the fairness maths — they are the reason the rest is distributed this way. Nothing gets scheduled over them.
  4. Two configurations, not a rota. "Ricardo home" is the fair week. "Ricardo away" is the surge week: it exists so you can see the cost of a trip before booking it. Alternating them casually would put 38 hours on a woman seven weeks postpartum.
  5. Kids hold real jobs. Table, pets, recycling, their own rooms. Small, owned, not "helping Mom".

Assumptions to confirm

  • Pool There is a pool and it is not on a service contract. If a pool service exists, delete three tasks and the chart drops by ~1.5 h.
  • Lily Lily is counted at ~10 h/week. That is the number most likely to be wrong — confirm what she actually wants to carry before this goes on the fridge.
  • Garden No gardener assumed. If there is one, the 45-minute Saturday lawn slot goes.
  • Helper No domestic helper assumed. Toggle "with helper" above to see the week with two cleaning/laundry days covered.
  • Custody The board assumes the children are with you this week. Switch to "Kids at Lauren's" and the school-run, homework and lunchbox load disappears.
  • Bins Refuse and recycling are collected on the same day in Welgemoed since 1 Feb 2026 — confirm your street's day on the City's calendar before trusting the Tuesday slot.
