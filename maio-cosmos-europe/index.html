What changed, and why it matters

The old scoring treated every planetary line as a plus. A place sitting under a Venus line and a place sitting under a Saturn line could come out with the same number — even though they do very different things to a life. Two families of line were being added together as if they were the same kind of thing.

Support lines

  • Venus · Jupiter — full weight
  • Sun · Moon — half weight
  • Mercury — neutral (it amplifies whatever it touches)
  • Scaled up when that planet is well-aspected in the birth chart

Pressure lines

  • Saturn · Pluto — heaviest demand
  • Mars · Uranus · Neptune — moderate demand
  • Scaled down where the planet is well-aspected — a well-integrated Saturn asks less of you
  • Pressure is not doom: it is drive, structure and change. It just isn't the same as ease.

Every place now carries two numbers: what it gives (support) and what it asks (pressure). The gap between them is the net. A city can be intensely activated and still be a bad fit — that was the blind spot.

The clearest example. Marbella and Cascais look similar on the old map. Marbella carries Julie's Pluto-MC at 1.5° and Mars-MC at 1.7° — pure demand, zero support, net −6.2. Cascais carries her Sun-ASC at 1.1° with nothing pulling against it — net +7.4. The old model could not tell those apart.

The honest headline. There is no place in Europe where all five charts come out net-positive at once. The best the continent manages is three of five — and where that happens (Oslo, Groningen, Leeuwarden, Nice) two members are carrying real pressure while three gain. That is not a failure of the search; it is the shape of the family's collective chart in this part of the world.

Meanwhile Greater Toronto — outside this frame — is the only place on Earth where all five charts fire strongly at once. Europe is where the family's history, language and family ties are; it is not where the maps converge.

What stands out. Lisbon and Porto come out genuinely positive for both parents — Portugal is where Ricardo's citizenship already sits, so this is a place where the map and the paperwork agree. The Greek side of the family lands well too: Rhodes and Thessaloniki both carry strong support for Julie. And London is Julie's strongest place in this frame by a wide margin — Sun-ASC and Venus-ASC at around two degrees, with Donnie the only one carrying pressure there.

What falls away. Gijón, Bilbao, Marbella, Madrid, San Sebastián and Edinburgh all turn negative once line quality is counted — every one of them was carried by Mars, Pluto or Saturn pressure. The Asturias coast in particular was the headline of the earlier relocation work; on the corrected model it is a place of demand, not ease.

The honest read

Astrocartography is a reflective tradition, not evidence. It describes the texture a place takes on in a chart — it does not decide where anyone should live, and it never outranks schools, hospitals, tax, work, or family.

What this corrected model is good for is honesty about direction: telling the difference between a place that will give something and a place that will ask something. The earlier version could not do that, which is why Gijón and Marbella and Plymouth looked like winners. They were not winners. They were heavily activated.

For Donnie, Gianna and Sofia this is parental context and nothing else. A child's map is weather, not a trajectory — and no decision about where a child grows up should be taken from a line on a map.

The family's real decision remains what it has always been: schools, work, health, and the capacity to move. This map is one input into that, and a modest one.
