01The two clubs you named

Both are in Milnerton / Mouille Point. One has a proper membership scheme; the other does not publish one at all.

Hellenic Community of Cape Town — the Hellenic Club

24 Bay Road, Mouille Point, Cape Town 8005 · map · 021 433 2374 · info@hcct.co.za · hellenic@hcct.co.za
Family R2,500 / year Individual R1,875 Pensioner R750 Student R250 No proposer required Expires 31 Dec 2026

Founded in 1900, one of the oldest Greek communities in South Africa. The clubhouse on Bay Road was built in 1979 and opened in 1983. Membership funds the church, the clergy and the community centre — this is a community subscription, not a restaurant card.

What the family gets

  • Greek school: nursery, primary, high school and adult classes — fees page
  • Choir and Greek dancing classes — community centre
  • Hellenic soccer club — hellenicsoccerpark.spawtz.com
  • Greek Kitchen and venue hire at member rates
  • Voting rights in the community and invitations to the calendar: New Year's Eve, Easter, the dance events

How to join

  • Form: hcct.co.za/register/family-membership-plan
  • It asks for you, Julie, and up to four children, then a password and a card
  • I have filled every field into a prefill sheet — only Julie's mobile, the password and the payment are left
  • Alternatively EFT on request: ask the office

The one trap: the plan page says “R2,500 for access until December 31, 2026”. Join today and you buy two months for a full year's fee. Either ask for pro-rata or submit on 2 January — the Christmas and New Year events are the best two months of the Greek calendar, so paying now is not wasted, it is just not a year. My draft email asks the office directly.

The Portuguese Club — Milnerton

10 Donegal Street, Milnerton 7405 · map · Rae, 078 422 3570 · Facebook · Instagram
No published membership form No published fee Phone Rae

This is a club in the old sense: a ground, a bar and a kitchen, with the community's events on top. Tuesday and Friday bookings, the Sorriso karaoke and live-music nights, five-a-side football leagues, and the big match screenings. There is no website, no membership page and no email address on the open internet — the Facebook page and Instagram are the whole public front, and both give the same mobile number.

So this one cannot be done by form. It is a phone call, and I have written the script for it: what to ask, what membership should include, and the offer to help on the events committee — which is how you actually get inside a club like this.

The Portuguese layer most people miss: the real Portuguese network in South Africa runs through the Portuguese Welfare Society (founded 1975, NPO 001-170, runs Lar Rainha Santa Isabel) and the Academia do Bacalhau, the Madeiran dining society with a Cape Town chapter. Your mother is from Portugal — those two are the doors to the older families, and they are relationship doors, not form doors.
