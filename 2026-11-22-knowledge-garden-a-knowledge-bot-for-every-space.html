Knowledge garden — a knowledge bot for every space

Author: Adrianna A · Date: 2026-11-22 · Space: AI-Space Question from Ricardo: "Can we make sure that all our systems have a knowledge bot and that it has automation that is improving, updating and maintaining all knowledge within its correct environment and space?"

Answer: yes — and it is running now. Seven spaces, seven named bots, one engine, nine scheduled runs, 26 decisions waiting on you. Every number in this report comes from a run made while writing it.

1. What "knowledge bot" means here

The post that prompted this (Nicholas Charriere, 2026-11-21) draws the line I used: a butler is attached to a person; a gardener is attached to a space. The gardener's edits change things for everyone in the space, so it needs an owner who signs off.

So each bot here is:

  • attached to a space, not to a conversation or a person;
  • owns its corpus — one root, one index, one digest, one owner;
  • proposes, never silently rewrites — except in the two spaces this lane actually owns;
  • signed off by a human for anything that changes what people read.

The company lane already had this model on paper: PRD-SYSTEMS-MAINTENANCE-AND-KNOWLEDGE.md v3.1 (2026-11-20) gives Infinity Brands unit bots their KBs, DOC Bot, SOP Bot and Superman. This build is the other half — Ricardo's own estate — which had no gardener at all.

4. What the first pass actually found

These are real, and they are the work the bots will now keep doing:

  • The vault rots at the seam between notes and time. 288 broken links, almost all of them pointing at a note that was archived or renamed after the link was written — for example Personal/4.Research/log.md still links ../5.PARA/4.Archive/2026-11-13-nine-schools-rated-for-a-grade-4-place-in-2027-distance.md, and the Reddam archive notes point at ~/AI-Drive/Agent-Output/splinter/... attachment paths that have moved. 1,553 notes have no inbound link at all; 1,044 of those have been untouched for 30+ days and are the real candidates for linking or archiving. 90 notes are missing frontmatter or required fields.
  • The Work drive is carrying duplicate weight. 206 groups of identical documents and 375 repeated basenames, plus 422 empty files (test fixtures and truncated exports). The Zoho audit artefacts and the HR export folder account for most of it.
  • Agent output never forgets. 229 files are past their 90-day retention, and the same PRD or report often exists three or four times under near-identical names. That is what "the vault holds the finalized copy" should have prevented.
  • AI Hub has a staging backlog. ~/AI Hub/staging/vault-handoff/ holds documents that were staged for the vault and never filed — some four copies deep (...-5.md).
  • DevMini is clean at the document level: 24 findings in 9,624 documents, all broken links in docs that point at vault root files by relative path.

8. Honest limits

  • The vault is still hand-maintained. The gardener can prove what is broken; only Beast can repair it. That is deliberate.
  • A bot is only as good as its checks. Duplicate detection on a code mirror is meaningless (git worktrees are copies), so it is switched off for DevMini; retention is meaningless for a reference archive, so it runs only on agent output. Each space's rule is visible in ~/AI Hub/kgarden/spaces.json, not buried in code.
  • Search is lexical, not semantic. FTS5 finds phrases and stems; the vault's hybrid vector search (qmd, refreshed 03:50 by nexus-vault-index) remains the semantic lane. The gardener's index adds the per-space cut, zone metadata and the zero-hit log.
  • The internal disk is at 96% (19 GB free). The gardener's state is 520 MB. If that becomes uncomfortable, the first fix is to store only excerpts in the index rather than full bodies.
