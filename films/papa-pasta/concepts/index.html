Papa Pasta · Franchise films · Concept phase

Eighteen concepts.
Three films, three hands, two directions each.

Low-resolution design tests, not finished films. Nothing here is graded or upscaled until a direction is chosen: pick the two or three you want to see at full quality and the masters are built from those. Every file is 640×360, silent, and between 10 and 16 seconds.

One rule across all three: the fee line comes from the executed agreement, not from a direction — 3.0% royalty · 3.0% marketing levy · 6.0% paid to Papa Pasta · R2,500 per store per month · 1.0% minimum local marketing. An earlier round of briefs carried 5%; that came from an investor review and was withdrawn.

One question still open: does a zone partner operate every store inside the zone, or does each store's operator run their own? The Batman and Claude zone films sell the first model; the Codex zone film sells the second. Both cannot ship.
