04 · Why It Balances

Three structural fixes, not three lucky numbers
FixWhat it isHours released
Pool the SLA outbound An SLA is sold to franchisees already inside the network — so one outbound campaign serves all four SLA brands at once, instead of four separate ones. 82.7 h → 20.7 h. 62.0
Centralise the hunting Calls, LinkedIn and Apollo-built research run once for the group, not repeated on every book. Five books' worth of hunt collapses into one. 86.7 h → 17.3 h. 69.2
SLA = account management The SLA brands come off the Deal Maker and sit on the SDR side as accounts: check-ins, renewals, upsell. 42.7 h → 18.5 h per brand. 96.8
Released — while doing exactly the same amount of real sales work228.0
Why an SLA is not a hunting book

There is nobody to cold-call. The franchisee is already a customer. A pipeline book carries 160 calls, 80 emails, 40 LinkedIn touches, 20 booked meetings and 20 discovery calls; an SLA carries six meetings, a report and hygiene.

Costing an SLA like a hunting book overstates it by 2.3× on the SDR side and 1.7× on the Deal Maker side. That error is what made this look like a hiring problem.

The three structural fixes do the work — 228 hours a month released, while the same amount of real selling happens.

Marketing is fully captured and needs no attention. SDR carries 28 unverified rows — the work is likely real, the proof is missing. Deal Maker has almost nothing verified to count.

The uncomfortable truth

If the CRM pipelines, email templates and push automation are not loaded, the Deal Maker cannot hit the number — and that is a management accountability, not a salesperson one. The manager owns the tooling. Blaming the closer for a missing automation is blaming the driver for a missing road.

07 · What To Do First

Six actions, in order
  1. Pool the SLA outbound. One campaign for four brands. Releases 62 hours a month, costs nothing.
  2. Centralise the hunting. Run calls, LinkedIn and research once, not per book. Releases 69 hours.
  3. Move the SLA brands to account management on the SDR side. Releases 97 hours of Deal Maker time.
  4. Adopt the matrix at §01. Olwethu 183%→86%, Nkuli 206%→70%, Andile 32%→87%, Alex 37%→70%. No new head.
  5. Attach the proof, then start signing off. Twenty-eight unverified SDR rows and zero sign-offs — the cheapest two fixes on this list.
  6. Hold the cap. Two to three pipeline books per person, SLA accounts carried separately. The rule that stops it recurring.
A matrix, not a hiring round.

Every hour works. Nobody breaks. No new hire.
