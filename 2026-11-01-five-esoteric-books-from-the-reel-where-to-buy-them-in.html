What to do with this

  • Today, one cart: A New Earth + Secret Teachings on Takealot — R 748, both in a warehouse now.
  • Then the three imports: Grand Symbol (R 304), Dream Yoga (R 483), Tibetan Book of the Dead (R 402).
  • Editions differ. Nothing here is a single uniform set: the Tibetan one is the Penguin Classics Deluxe you saw on the reel; Dream Yoga is the Sounds True paperback; Secret Teachings and Grand Symbol are the cheap reprint editions. Hardback or PRS originals cost two to four times more and none of the local shops stock them.
  • There is a R 225 Secret Teachings in stock too — a print-on-demand reprint of the 1928 text. The gate still chose the R 579 Tarcher edition (0.99): this book is its plates and diagrams, and the cheap facsimile reproduces them badly. Buy the cheap one only if you want the words, not the plates.
  • Cheaper but slower: every import gets a little cheaper if you are willing to wait — but never by more than about R 80, and one of them (Exclusive Books, Dream Yoga) has no stated ETA at all.
