05 Monday morning — the call

One call to Marleen Germishuys on 064 684 3633, or the free fifteen-minute consultation she advertises. Seven questions, in this order.

What is your fee for a 50-minute child session, and do you bill Discovery Health directly?

Her fee is the one number nobody publishes. Ask which funds she is contracted in with, what the scheme code is for play therapy, and whether you claim back or she submits.

Do you have a playroom, and can you see a nine-year-old after school?

A dedicated playroom matters for a boy who will not sit and talk. Ask about slots after 15:15 on weekdays — Reddam House Durbanville releases him at 15:15 — and whether Saturday exists.

How soon can we start, and do you keep a cancellation list?

The answer that decides it. If the first opening is weeks away, the fallback is René Ohlhoff at 65 Chavonne Street, bookable online at R650, while the queue at Marleen runs.

Will you work with the school?

Name them: Reddam House Durbanville, Mrs Monika Davies in learning support, and the Grade 4 support plan for 2027 with a classroom facilitator. Ask whether she would join a case conference or write a short paragraph the school can act on.

Will you work with the doctors already involved?

Donatello has a neurological ADD diagnosis, a 2026 psycho-educational assessment by Inez Saunders, a speech therapist and an occupational therapist. Ask whether she reads those reports first and whether she will coordinate rather than start from zero.

What does the work look like, and how will we know it is working?

Play therapy for anxiety and low confidence usually runs weekly for months. Ask what she would review at six weeks, whether parents get a feedback session, and whether she sees the child alone or with you in the room for the first session.

Would you see Gianna too, in a separate session?

She is seven and in the same house. Ask whether the practice takes siblings separately and whether the same therapist for both is better or worse.

Not medical advice. This is a shortlist of registered practitioners with their published facts, and a set of questions. The clinical decision — whether play therapy is the right modality, how often, and for how long — belongs to the treating psychologist and to Donatello's doctor. 06 Honest gaps

What this page does not know.

  • Fees. Four of the seven publish no fee. Marleen Germishuys, Charlene Wessels, Dr Symington and Alé Radmore are blank on price — the call above is how that closes.
  • Availability is as published, not as confirmed. No one was phoned today. "No waitlist shown" is the absence of a waitlist notice, not a promise of a slot.
  • No independent quality ratings exist for therapists. Google review counts measure bedside manner and parking, not outcomes. The ranking rests on registration, qualification, modality fit, availability and distance — which is what there is.
  • Registration numbers are self-published. Marleen (PS 0131431), Charlene (PS 0137545 / PR 0719730) and Tanja (HPCSA Clin. Psych., verified by Psychology Today) are checkable on the HPCSA iRegister at hpcsa.co.za — worth one lookup before the first appointment.
