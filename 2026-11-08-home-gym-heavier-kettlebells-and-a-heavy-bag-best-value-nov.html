Correction to v1 of this brief. I wrote that the TITLE 1.2 m bag ships empty and that you'd stuff 35 kg of rags into it. That was wrong. TITLE SA's own FAQ on the range says plainly: "Is this punching bag filled? Bags in this range are supplied ready to train." So the TITLE 1.2 m bag at R1 599 is a filled ~35 kg bag, locally made — which makes it a genuine equal of the Damascus 40 kg, not a DIY project. If you go the wall route, it's a straight coin-flip between the two on price; TITLE has 217 reviews at 4.83 behind that range, Damascus has the local factory.

Bracket sizing, from TITLE's own spec sheet. TITLE's standard bracket suits bags from small to large (0.5–0.9 m); their large 120 cm bracket is the one specified for XXL and up. So a 1.2 m or 40 kg bag needs a bracket rated for it — the TITLE large bracket at R1 359 or the R789 300 kg-rated mount. The R669 standard bracket is not it.

5 · Do this

  1. Order the kettlebells first — one page, two sizes, R3 378. They're in stock and need nothing from you but a delivery address.
  2. Order the TITLE Boxing Home Station, R6 999 directly from TITLE SA — free delivery over R1 999, all three bags in the box.
  3. Before it arrives, confirm your floor. Two rawlbolt holes into concrete or tile. If the gym floor is a floating deck or over a membrane, stop and tell me — the wall route in section 3 is the fallback.
  4. If you'd rather have a heavier bag, skip the station: large bracket R1 359 + chain R215 + TITLE 1.2 m filled bag R1 599, with the speed-bag platform R1 399 beside it. R5 241 all in, free delivery, and one order from one supplier.
  5. Set the station up with the bag at hip height and the speed-bag platform at forehead height — the platform's height is what most home setups get wrong, and it's the difference between the bag keeping time and fighting you.
Still worth adding, and not in either basket: bag gloves or hand wraps. Bare knuckles on PVC or leather is how hands get wrecked in week two. Say the word and I'll price a pair against the same rubric.
