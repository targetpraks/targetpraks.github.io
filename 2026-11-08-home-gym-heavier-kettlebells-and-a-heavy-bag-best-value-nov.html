2 · The boxing bag

Damascus 40 kg, filled — R1 200 plus freight

Damascus Sport has been making boxing equipment in South Africa since 1967 and runs a service centre at Unit 40 Gunners Park, Goodwood — fifteen minutes from you. Their 40 kg reinforced-PVC bag arrives filled, hangs from reinforced chain straps with a bottom D-ring to stop it swinging, and is the only option here where a real manufacturer will look at the bag if it splits.

One number to close before you order. Damascus quotes its web prices on collection in Johannesburg. A 40 kg bag to Cape Town adds courier — budget R250–R400, so R1 450–R1 600 landed. Call the Goodwood branch on 021 531 2114 and ask two things: do they hold a 40 kg bag in Cape Town, and what is freight if they don't. If the answer is vague, the fallback is the same bag on Takealot at R1 650 all-in, delivered.

3 · Hanging it

300 kg-rated adjustable wall bracket — R789

A 40 kg bag hit hard puts several times its own weight through the mount, so the bracket rating matters more than the bag price. The adjustable wall mount states a 300 kg load on 2 mm steel, gives five reach settings (43–63 cm) and four heights, has a swivel so the bag turns with your combinations, folds flat when not in use, and comes with the expansion anchors. 5.0★ but only 2 reviews — the rating is what I'm buying.

The bracket I nearly recommended and shouldn't have. The TITLE standard bracket at R669 has 51 reviews at 4.5★ — but its own spec says it holds TITLE bags "from small to X-Large", and X-Large is about 31 kg. A 40 kg bag is outside its stated range. R120 more buys the right rating.

5 · Do this

  1. Order the 24 kg and 32 kg Ligum kettlebells on the same Takealot order — one product page, two sizes. R3 378 delivered, 3–5 working days.
  2. Add the 300 kg bracket (R789) to the same order and the 600 mm chain (R215) separately — TITLE SA ships from Cape Town, usually next day.
  3. Call Damascus Goodwood on 021 531 2114 about the 40 kg bag: stock in Cape Town, and freight if not. Collect if they have one — that saves the R250–R400.
  4. If the call doesn't settle it in a day, order the same bag on Takealot at R1 650 delivered and stop thinking about it.
  5. Mount it into brick or concrete, never plasterboard or a timber truss — the bracket is specified for concrete walls. Two M10 anchors minimum, and hang the bag so the bottom sits at hip height.
One thing you'll need and didn't ask for: bag gloves or wraps, if you don't already own a pair — bare knuckles on PVC is how hands get wrecked in week two. Say the word and I'll price a pair against the same rubric.
