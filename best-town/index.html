Seven views of the same question. Every town scored from the same two birth charts with Swiss Ephemeris — then drawn on its own map with the astrocartography lines that actually cross it. Where a region has no line activation, it says so.

Method: relocated-angle astrocartography — natal planets conjunct a relocated angle within 3° are scored, weighted by planet and angle. Same engine and weights as the verified 2026-08 Gijón study, which reproduced to ≤0.1 on 27 of 28 published scores. Coastlines: Natural Earth (50m within regions, 110m for the wide view).

Scores measure how strongly a place activates the two charts — his Moon-IC (home), her Sun-ASC (identity), the shared Mars-MC (action). Astrocartography is reflective, not causal: it never overrides schools, hospitals, tax, or a newborn's needs. A reflective planning aid, not advice.
