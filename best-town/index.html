How to read this

Four things worth knowing before you trust a dot.

The lines describe texture, not destiny

The score measures how strongly a place activates your two charts — his Moon-IC (home and roots), her Sun-ASC (identity and visibility), and the shared Mars-MC (action and career). It is reflective, not causal, and it never overrules schools, hospitals, tax reality, or a newborn's needs.

Asturias wins the lines; Portugal wins the airport

Gijón and Oviedo top the chart scoring, but Asturias has no direct flight to Greece — Rhodes becomes two legs, or a 3h drive to Bilbao. Porto, Lisbon and Málaga all fly Athens direct. That is the real trade on the table, not a dealbreaker either way.

Why not Bilbao, when its airport is the best in the north?

It was scored — it just came in at 16.3, around #50, and the reason is line position rather than anything about the city. Julie's two strongest lines run far to the west: at this latitude her Sun-ASC line crosses at −6.8°E and her Pluto-MC at −6.4°E, while Bilbao sits at −2.9°E — roughly 300 km east of where those lines actually pass. So Bilbao catches the shared Mars-MC action band (hers 0.26°, his 1.39°) and nothing else; every one of her other activations falls wide of the 3° scoring band. Santander is the revealing middle case: only 100 km further west, it pulls her Pluto-MC to 2.6° and her Sun-ASC to 2.9° and scores 27.3. Bilbao is the better city with the better airport; it is simply in the wrong place for these two charts. If connectivity ever outranks the lines, then yes — Bilbao belongs on the list for exactly the reason you are asking.

The floor is 100k — the villages were set aside

Ribadesella (6k), Llanes (4k) and San Vicente de la Barquera (4.4k) scored beautifully and were excluded: too small to give a family of five fun week in, week out. The answer was already a real city — the filter just removed the noise around it.

Where you live now scores zero — and that is fine

Welgemoed and the whole Cape Town metro sit at 0.0 in this model: no line activation for either chart. That is a fact about the model, not a verdict on a life you built. It simply means the activated lines are elsewhere.
