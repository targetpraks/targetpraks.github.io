02 The children

Equazen is the buy. Two paediatric trials used this exact 9:3:1 fish-oil and evening-primrose blend, one of them 90 children over twelve months. The meta-analysis behind it puts omega-3 at g = 0.24 for attention in children, rising to 0.52 in children whose blood omega-3 was genuinely low and falling to 0.03 in those who were not — which is the argument for testing before you spend R1,770 on a three-month starting course.

MycoBrain Kids is the mushroom you asked for, and it is a single species, not a complex. 100 mg of a 4:1 lion's mane fruiting-body extract per naartjie chewable, 1–3 a day, age 5 and up. At two tablets a day that is 200 mg of extract — around a fifth of the adult gram — and there are still no randomised trials of lion's mane in children at any dose. It is a low-risk, low-expectation addition, not a treatment.

The six-species complex for a child has no basis. MycoMatrix is 2 g of extract a day from 120 capsules — an adult dose of six species at once — and Foodgrown's 6-strain is the same shape at R341.99. Neither prints a child dose, because none exists. The decision lane scored it 0.009. If the paediatrician wants a multi-species product later, that is his call to make, not a checkout decision tonight.

03 Yours

Nothing on these three shelves adds a mechanism your stack does not already have. The honest list for you is four restocks at prices that are genuinely good:

  • Creatine 600 g, R189 — 120 servings at 5 g, R1.58 a serving. The best value on any of the three sites.
  • Lion's Mane 60 caps, R174 — 1 g a day for a month at R5.80. Cheaper per day than Doc Marty's 90-day bottle even after its 25% discount.
  • Magnesium glycinate, R122 — 66 mg elemental a day at R4.07. The threonate in the old cart cost three times as much per milligram for a funded, borderline trial.
  • NAC, R194 — only if the tub is low. 500 mg capsules, one or two a day.

Two things I am deliberately leaving out. MycoBrain 180 is the better-specified mushroom — named fruiting body, dual extraction, hericenones assayed at 0.423% — but at R667.50 plus R70–100 delivery it is R8.19–8.53 a day against R5.80, and you would be committing 90 days to win a specification, not an effect. MycoSleep 180 is reishi, GABA, magnesium glycinate, theanine and lemon balm for R7.42 a day; you already take two of those five, and no capsule fixes a sleep problem that a sleep study would explain.
