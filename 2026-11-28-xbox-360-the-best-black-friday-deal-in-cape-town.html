Why there is no "Black Friday discount" to find

Two hard facts frame the whole search:

  • The console is discontinued and its digital store is gone. Microsoft retired the Xbox 360 Store and Marketplace on 29 July 2024; discs and games you already own still play, and per-game online services vary.
  • No national Black Friday promotion in South Africa covers it. I opened Cash Crusaders' live Black Friday 2026 promotion: 24 items, every one a new Dixon house-brand appliance or accessory (a fridge, a soundbar, a fan, a power bank). Not one console, and none of their second-hand shelf. Their second-hand prices are set store by store, so the R1 499.90 is the price today and the price next week.

The one dated Black Friday clock in the market is Bob Shop's, which runs 2–29 November 2026 — so tomorrow, 29 November, is the last day any online festive pricing stands.

Before you drive to Paarl

The store's own line is 021 872 7062 (WhatsApp available); the listing has a "Request actual photos" button — use it, because the site says it shows a stock image and "pricing of products differ from store to store due to condition".

  1. Confirm what is in the box — console, power brick, AV or HDMI cable, and at least one controller. A wired 360 controller from R231 on Takealot if it is console-only.
  2. Confirm the model. Model 1439 is the Slim (S) — the 45nm board that fixed the red-ring failure. If they hand you a 2005–2007 phat unit, walk away; that is the generation that dies.
  3. Ask them to boot it in front of you, disc in, and to show the dashboard and the storage screen reading 320GB.
  4. Read the warranty terms before you pay. The six months covers a pre-existing defect confirmed by a service centre — and the exclusions list power surge and load-shedding damage explicitly (warranty policy). Plug it into a surge protector.
  5. Know what you are buying into. No store to buy games from any more: discs only, and second-hand discs run R150–R300 each on Gumtree in Cape Town. Budget a handful of discs into the decision.
