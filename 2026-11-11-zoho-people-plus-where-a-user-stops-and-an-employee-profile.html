Where a user stops and an employee profile begins

A user logs in. An employee profile is a record with no door. Zoho has drawn that line explicitly for performance appraisals — which is exactly the one thing your staff use People for.

No, not everyone has to be a user — but everyone you appraise does.

Zoho support states it plainly: employee profiles cannot be included in an appraisal cycle, and calls this "one of the key differences between a User and an Employee profile license". So if staff must rate themselves and their manager, they need user licences. At 40 people that is R60,480 a year.

The alternative — 3 HR users plus 37 employee profiles — is R10,752 a year, and the ratings move to a form instead of the HR record.

One The functional difference, side by side

An employee profile is still a full record — it appears in every form, report and org chart. What it does not have is a door. No login means no self-service, and self-service is where appraisals live.

Two things worth knowing. A paid People Plus account needs a minimum of five users, and users can be assigned to different apps in the Admin Console — so one licence can be spent on People alone, or across People, Recruit, Expense, Cliq and Connect. Employee profiles cannot be assigned to anything: they live only in People.

Three The three honest structures at 40 staff

A. Everyone a user — what you run today

R60,480/yr

40 × R126. Everyone self-rates and rates their manager inside People, and everyone keeps Cliq Standard and Connect. This is the only structure where the appraisal record sits in the HR system for all 40 people.

B. HR and managers as users, staff as profiles

R18,816/yr

3 HR + 6 managers on user licences, 31 employee profiles. The appraisal cycle covers the nine users only — your staff cannot self-rate, because they cannot be in the cycle. Saves R41,664 and quietly kills the purpose.

C. HR as users, ratings moved to a form

R10,752/yr + forms

3 HR users, 37 employee profiles, and the self-rating and manager-rating collected in Zoho Forms or Survey — both have free plans, a Basic form plan is R140 a month. Cheap and workable, but the ratings live beside the HR record instead of inside it, and staff lose the intranet.

Four What I would do

Your staff do two things in People: rate themselves and rate their manager. Both are self-service actions, and Zoho has closed the door on doing either as an employee profile. So the question is not "can we avoid users" — it is "what else are those user licences buying, and is that worth R50,000 a year".

  • If the intranet and chat matter — and last week's decision says they do — the user licences stay. Connect is licensed per user, and dropping to profiles removes people from Cliq and Connect entirely. The appraisal is then a bonus, not the justification.
  • If People is really only two ratings a year — structure C is the honest answer, and it is a five-figure annual saving. Move the ratings to a form, keep People as the HR record for the 3 people who work in it, and stop paying R126 a head for a survey.
  • Either way, profile the people who genuinely never log in — seasonal store staff, employees kept on record after leaving, anyone whose record HR maintains but who never touches the system. At R14 against R126, that is the saving with no downside at all.
The one number to check first. Who in the 40 actually logs in? If the answer is 12, then 12 users and 28 profiles is R23,328 a year instead of R60,480 — and nothing is lost. Zoho's own admin logins report gives that list in a minute.
