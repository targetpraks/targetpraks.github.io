The recommendation

Finally. You. — Return to Yourself

Plettenberg Bay · 12–21 February 2027 · women only · nine nights · own en-suite bedroom

R36,925 from €2,000 per person

  • Nine nights, everyone in her own en-suite room — the only option here where privacy and company are both guaranteed.
  • It scores 5.0 from nine reviews on BookRetreats, and the host collects guests from George airport, so she never drives a strange road alone.
  • Daily yoga plus the deeper work — breathwork, kinesiology, EFT, hypnotherapy — which is what a sixtieth usually calls for: not more fitness, but permission to stop carrying everyone.
  • The listed R4,103 nightly rate is about 48% of the next women-only option’s R8,616 nightly rate. Meals are unconfirmed and may cost extra.

Four things to pin down before paying

  1. Which meals the €2,000 covers — the listing is filed as bed & breakfast and does not itemise them.
  2. The single-room guarantee, in writing, with no supplement.
  3. The cancellation ladder, because a ten-day trip is a big commitment.
  4. How much of the programme is talking-therapy versus movement, so it matches what she actually enjoys.

Emoyeni — New Year retreat

Emoyeni Retreat Centre, Mooinooi, North West · 29 Dec 2026 – 3 Jan 2027 · mixed · 5 nights

R7,100 R1,420 / night · en-suite single

What a day looks like

The centre publishes the rhythm, and a guest describes it precisely: a soft bell, stretching and meditation, breakfast of fruit, yogurt and cereals, an hour of work in the kitchen or garden as a contribution, then teaching and meditation; lunch is the main meal; a nap, a walk, more practice; supper is soup, salad and fresh bread, then a closing discussion. This retreat is mostly in silence, with guided meditation, gentle movement and walks in the bushveld.

Who is there

Up to 18 guests and four residents; the meditation room seats about 25. Three lacto-vegetarian meals a day, linen supplied. No wifi, no pool, no television — deliberately.

What guests say

07 — Giving it well Six steps from shortlist to a card in her hand

Pick the shape, not the price

Silence and teaching (Emoyeni, Choden), cleanse and doctor (Bodhi), or women and therapy (Finally. You., Rewild). One conversation answers it: “Would you rather sit in silence for five days, or spend nine days with a group of women?”

Book the room, in her name, on a date

A deposit against a named date beats a voucher she has to redeem. For Finally. You. that means an email to the host today — February is the only 2027 date published so far.

Get four things in writing

Single room with no supplement · exact group size and age range · what the price includes meal by meal · the cancellation ladder. Every one of these hosts answers email promptly; none of them publish all four.

Clear the detox with her doctor

Bodhi's cleanse and Santé's programme restrict food. If she takes chronic medication, a five-minute call to her GP before booking prevents a bad week.

Book the flights the same week

George for Plettenberg Bay (transfer included), Cape Town for Struisbaai (three-hour transfer, R500 each way), Durban for Ixopo (two-hour drive), Johannesburg for Emoyeni (90 minutes).

Put one sentence in the card

“For the year you put yourself first” lands better than “happy birthday, here is a retreat”. Ask the host to write a welcome note — every one of them will.

08 — Method and limits What I could verify, and what I could not

Verified today

  • Dates, room types and prices read directly from each organiser's page: Bodhi Khaya (retreat pages carry per-room prices), Emoyeni, BRC, Breathe & Rewild, Finally. You.
  • Cancellation terms: Bodhi Khaya and Emoyeni publish theirs; the others do not.
  • Google ratings and the full star distribution for each venue, read on the listings today.
  • Hotel classification, guest quotes and the day-by-day shape of the Rewild week.

Limits, stated plainly

  • TripAdvisor blocks automated reading, so its scores here come from search results — BRC and Bodhi both show 4.2 from 12 reviews, a sample too small to weigh against Google's.
  • BookRetreats blocks direct reading too; the 5.0-from-nine figure for Finally. You. is from the listing's search result and should be confirmed on the page.
  • The retreat itself has no Google listing — it runs in a private villa, so only the platform reviews exist.
  • Flights are deliberately not priced: a fare quoted today for February is not a fare she will pay.
  • Breathe & Rewild has no independent reviews I could find. That is a fact about the evidence, not a judgement on the retreat.

All of this assumes she is in South Africa and willing to travel within it. If she is abroad, or flying is off the table, the list re-runs against that constraint.
