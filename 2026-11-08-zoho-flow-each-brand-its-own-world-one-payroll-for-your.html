Each brand its own world — and Infinity's One is everything else

The brand's Zoho One belongs to the franchisees: they register themselves inside it, each keeps its own Books, and they all share that brand's Cliq and Connect. Your own handful sit in there only to run the brand. Everything else happens in Infinity's One.

What Zoho allows. Zoho Books runs several organisations under one login, each with its own books — managing multiple organisations. Cliq allows one organisation per Zoho account — Zoho Cliq help. Zoho One pricing counts every employee on the All-Employee plan — Zoho One pricing FAQ. Ours. The two-world split, franchisees self-registering inside the brand's instance, independent franchise books and a brand-only intranet are the group's own model — not a licence audit of any one org. One thing to settle before you scale: franchisees inside a brand's Zoho One are licensed users of that instance, so check whether that brand sits on per-user pricing rather than all-employee, or every franchise headcount becomes a seat.
