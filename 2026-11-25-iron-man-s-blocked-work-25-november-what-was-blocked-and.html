The one root cause

Iron Man lost his lane onto the host. Not his model, not his instructions, not his permissions — his workspace. The lane (/workspace/bin/host, the key beside it, ssh) had been seeded by hand on 24 November into his user workspace only. Every workbench workspace is a separate bind mount under ~/agenticos-sandboxes, so the moment a conversation runs inside a project (Gotham City), the seat gets the project's workspace — which had no bin, no .ssh, no ssh client config. Seven runs, seven versions of the same sentence: "no route to the platform".

Measured, not inferred:

Check Project workspace (before) User workspace
/workspace/bin/host absent present
/workspace/.ssh/config absent present
container sandboxd-xp-…80b51e38 (Gotham City) sandboxd-xu-…0cf5dacf (Iron Man)

Fix, now live: ~/agenticos/host-lane/host-lane.sh on macserver, on cron every five minutes. It gives every Iron Man workspace (user and project) its own ed25519 key, its own bin/host, and authorises the public half on the host with the same from="172.17.0.0/16" bridge-only guard the hand-seeded key carries. Each workspace keeps its own key, so revoking one workspace never touches another; a workspace that is deleted has its key pruned on the next run.
