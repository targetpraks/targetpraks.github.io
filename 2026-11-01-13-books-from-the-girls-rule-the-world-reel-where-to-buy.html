The honest caveat. Nine of these thirteen are US picture books that no South African bookshop keeps on a shelf, so "local" means "a South African shop that will import it for you". Only Wordsworth and Readers Warehouse show genuine in-stock stock (their pages say in stock, dispatches in 1–3 working days, and Wordsworth offers in-store collection in about an hour). Two titles — Ruby René Had So Much to Say and Millie Fleur's Poison Garden — have exactly one local route each, both on Takealot, so expect a month on those. Prices above are the listed selling prices before delivery; Exclusive Books delivers free over R450, which the eight-title order clears easily.
