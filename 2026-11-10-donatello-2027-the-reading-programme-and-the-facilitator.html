The pick
Barton Reading & Spelling System

Gate 0.9856 · confidence 0.9407 · beat four alternatives · logged 2026-11-10T21:32

A scripted, Orton-Gillingham-based programme in ten levels, taught one-to-one. The publisher states it plainly: "one of seven well-known Orton-Gillingham-based systems … much stronger on teaching spelling", and it also teaches morphology, grammar and prosody. For a boy whose decoding sits at Grade 1 efficiency while his comprehension is intact, and whose spelling collapses without a choice list, that emphasis on spelling and word structure is the whole point.

Why it wins on his numbers, not on marketing

What he needsWhat Barton does about it
Decoding. Reads ~90 wpm, about a Grade 1 efficiency. A phonemic-awareness gap, not a vocabulary gap. Every student starts at Level 1: Phonemic Awareness — all seven phonemic-awareness skills, no letters at first. It does not assume he can hear the sounds.
Spelling and orthographic mapping. Spelling 50% with choices, 90% with the word in front of him — he recognises words he cannot retrieve. Spelling is taught as its own skill in every lesson, with the tiles and the finger-spelling routine, plus prefixes, suffixes and syllable types. This is the specific mechanism that builds orthographic mapping.
Delivered by a hired facilitator, not a specialist teacher. "Anyone who can pass our 5-minute Tutor Screening. No degree or previous experience required." A new tutor watches the level's training videos on a Sunday and teaches on Monday. The script carries the demand, which also keeps the pressure off you at the kitchen table.
Intensity. Weekly 45-minute sessions have moved him from 58 to 90 wpm in two years — real, but slow. "At least two hours of one-on-one tutoring each week, year-round, with sessions no longer than one hour." Four 45-minute sessions in the school day is three hours a week — comfortably above the floor, and it costs no extra time in his afternoon.
Cost per rand of gain. Materials run "less than $100 a month" amortised. Levels 1–3 for the first year come to about R16,800 once. There is no per-session market rate on top, because the facilitator is already paid to be in the room.

How to start it — four small steps

  1. The facilitator takes the 5-minute Tutor Screening before you hire them. It tests whether they can hear the sounds accurately — that is the one hard requirement. Ask for the result as part of the interview.
  2. Donatello takes the 10-minute Student Screening. Barton requires it before Level 1. If he does not pass, the programme says to wait and build the prerequisite first — better to know that in November than in February.
  3. Buy Level 1 and Level 2 now (digital, about R10,700) so the facilitator can train in the December holidays.
  4. Fix the slot with the school: which 45 minutes of the day, and whether it is in-class or a pull-out. Get it in writing with the name of the facilitator.

What the year actually costs

Their published bands are specific about who sits in each one: junior is 18–21 and still studying (R8,000–10,000), mid is 21–24 with tutoring or facilitator experience and still studying (R11,000–14,000), and senior is 25–45 with teaching experience and a teaching, psychology or remedial degree (R15,000–18,000). The placement fee is 10% of the candidate's 12-month income, once off.

Which band to hire. A junior cannot hold a scripted literacy programme on top of classroom support. The floor is mid. The one I would actually hire is senior: a qualified teacher who can run Barton and still read the classroom well enough to know when to step back and let him struggle productively. That is the difference between a facilitator who builds independence and one who quietly does the work for him.

The police clearance — the only date that can break the plan

SAPS charges R190,00 per application and quotes about 15 working days from receipt in Pretoria — and its own page carries the warning that Criminal Record and Crime Scene Management "is currently experiencing a backlog with the issuing of Police Clearance Certificates which has extended the waiting period for finalization". A 2026 guide puts the realistic turnaround at five to eight weeks. SAPS clearance page

Consequence: if you appoint by 1 December, the certificate should be back before the school opens in mid-January. If you appoint in January, it will not be. So either appoint in November/early December, or tell the agency you will only consider candidates who already hold a current certificate — and ask the school in writing whether a certificate dated within the last 12 months is acceptable, which widens the candidate pool a lot.
