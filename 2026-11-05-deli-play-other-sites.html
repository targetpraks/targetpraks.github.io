The sites you have not looked at

You knew two: Bon Amis at Bloemendal, and the Matrix bay at Willowbridge. This is what else is on the market right now — a restaurant with the kitchen already built, a farm with an approved restaurant consent, a dark eatery on a wine estate that already had a playground, eleven retail units priced per square metre, and eight land plays. Every figure was read off a live page on 5 November 2026.

Why IPIC matters more than it looks. A commercial kitchen is the single most expensive thing you would install. That is Bon Amis's concept minus the estate, minus the sale, minus the weather risk, at a rent you can model today.

Why these, and what I threw away

I searched the live commercial portals — Brightwave, Anvil, Rennie Knight Frank, Galetti, Pretkor, OfficePlace, Private Property and Property24 — for ground-floor retail or restaurant space of 200 to 600 m² across Durbanville, Bellville, Brackenfell, Kraaifontein, Tyger Valley, Parow, Milnerton, Paarl and the Stellenbosch edge, plus every farm and smallholding within about 25 km of Durbanville.

I dropped sealed mall corridors with no outside space, dead industrial boxes above 600 m², offices, and any listing where the rent or the contact was not printed and I could not confirm it another way. Where a figure is not published I have said so rather than estimating — a guessed rent is worse than no rent when you are deciding what to offer.

The known site moved while we were working. Brightwave's Tyger Valley index, updated 2 November 2026, now prices the two Willowbridge Village units at R78,000/month for 300 m² (R260/m²) and R121,000/month for 242 m² — R500/m². Against R260/m² for the larger unit in the same centre, that is either a listing error or a re-specified unit. Until Salome or Brightwave explains it in writing, do not plan on the old R63,000 figure.

The rent reality check

Of the eleven units, nine publish both size and rent, so they can be compared honestly. The band runs from R86.67 to R300 per m², median R165. Willowbridge at R260/m² sits in the top quartile — that is what a centre with Woolworths and Dischem charges. IPIC at a blended R164.73/m² is almost exactly the median, with a kitchen already built.

What I could not confirm

  • Whether iL Tesoro's building is actually empty. Closed and delisted, yes. Available, unknown.
