Bottom line

Buzz — remove It is a chat client you have effectively stopped using: the last human message is 7 Nov 12:59 UTC, and volume collapsed from 905 events on 4 Nov to 6, then 2, then 2 per day. It costs 5 Docker containers, 7 agent bridge processes and ~1.9 GB.

Orca — keep the app, drop the broken job This is not idle software. 408 Codex sessions since 30 Oct (129 on 30 Oct; 43 / 50 / 105 / 43 on 4–7 Nov), two worktrees (maio-os alone is 7.7 GB), and a Hermes TUI has been running under its terminal host for 27 hours. What genuinely does not serve you is the launchd job com.maio.orca-serve, which exits 1 every time it starts.

If the goal is to stop Orca being part of the stack, the correct move is not to delete the app: it is to (a) retire the failing orca-serve launchd job, (b) move any long-lived Hermes TUI out of an Orca pane into a launchd-owned process, and (c) prune its caches. Deleting the app would take 508 session records and a live TUI with it.

Do not touch — evidence says live

  • Firefly III holds 9,606 transaction journals and 28 accounts. That is real bookkeeping, and its MariaDB data (225 MB) sits inside the colima VM disk, not in the host bind directory I can see. Export before any teardown; it is money data and out of bounds without your sign-off.
  • The seven local app containers (chromacommand, constellation, infx-fnd, divorced-dads, signet, the-good-stuff, papa-pasta) are live dev deploys: every repo committed between 5 and 7 Nov, papa-pasta-bench has 28 uncommitted files. They can be stopped when you are not working on them, but they are not abandoned.
  • hindsight (4.9 GB volume) is the memory bank; metamcp and home-assistant are in stack-up.sh; d3-flash-mlx is the decision gate; nomic-embed-text is the embedding model.
  • ~/.hermes/installs (3.7 GB) — both trees were written on 7 Nov, so the toolchain may still resolve through them. Prove it before pruning.

Proposed order of work

  1. Delete the 5.9 GB of unused apps, plus Buzz's app and relay stack after a Postgres dump (~1.9 GB).
  2. Run the cache/model reclaim batch (~48 GB) with df measured before and after, and a gate check on the destructive step.
  3. Retire the failing com.maio.orca-serve job; leave Orca itself alone.
  4. Separately: decide whether Firefly should be archived and whether the four un-exposed demo apps should be stopped between sessions.
