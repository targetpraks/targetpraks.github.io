Read this before you click buy

The Kindle warranty gap is the one real catch. Every Kindle on Takealot is a parallel import sold by a marketplace seller. Amazon.co.za does not sell Kindle devices at all — searching its store returns Kobo and off-brand readers only. So a Kindle bought here has no local Amazon warranty and returns go through the seller, while a Kobo bought from Amazon.co.za carries a 1-year limited warranty and 30-day returns handled locally.
  • Delivery is free on all of these. Takealot's free-delivery threshold is R650 and every item here clears it; Amazon.co.za quotes free delivery on both Kobo listings to Cape Town 8001.
  • All prices include VAT. Amazon's Kobo pages state it explicitly; Takealot prices are shelf prices.
  • The BF500 code needs a signed-in Amazon account and is described on the page as "applied at checkout". Treat R4 174.15 for the Libra as a maybe until the basket shows it.
  • What I could not verify: Amazon.co.za shows no price history, so the Libra and Clara BW drops there are measured against the list price and Takealot's history, not their own. The Boox Go Color 7 has no history either. And a sale price can change between this reading and your checkout.
  • Cheapest is not always right: the R2 699 Kindle is the best price in the country and the worst support story. The R2 734 Kobo costs R35 more and gives you a local warranty, local returns and no lock-screen ads.
