01 How it was audited

The auditor was given a git workbench with the PRD, a snapshot of the evidence and two read-only wrappers — one to the Superhuman/Coda MCP client, one to the ClickUp API — plus a conduct file that forbade writes to either system, secrets in the repo, and any claim without the command that produced it. It collected live data itself rather than trusting the snapshot, then wrote reviews/AUDIT.md incrementally and committed after each step. No write was made to ClickUp or to the document.

Working files: papa-pasta-audit-2026-11-21/reviews/AUDIT.md (the full review, 220 KB), reviews/live/ (its own live reads), reviews/commands.jsonl (every command), reviews/AUDIT claim ledgers. The PRD was not editable by the auditor and was not edited by it.

02 What it found that I had wrong

The brand identity was not blank. I reported the four brand-persona answers as empty. They are written, in rich text, and describe a premium fast-casual positioning — which contradicts the Company Snapshot's “affordable, everyday” positioning. My extractor had not read rich-text cells; the audit read them recursively. The real finding is stronger than the one I published, and revision 3 carries it.

And the Recurring list id was wrong. Phase 7 pointed at 1501000000001622, which is Meeting Tracker. The Recurring list is 1501000000002318. A load run from that edition would have filed thirteen reports into the wrong list.

The other corrections carried into revision 3

  • Phase 2 said “≤ 10 tasks” for a payload that is 14: 1 research, 6 optimisation actions, 1 link building, 6 goals.
  • Five blueprint families were listed; there are six — Customers was missing, and it is the richest.
  • “25 distinct keywords” is 25 filled cells and 24 distinct strings.
  • “Twelve dated anchors” overstated: only the holiday, Valentine's, Spring Day and menu-switchover rows carry exact dates; finals is tentative.
  • “No working move endpoint” was stale: ClickUp documents a v3 move endpoint. The plan now says it is unused and untested here.
  • “One UI toggle” undersold recurrence: due date, schedule, trigger, new-instance choice and history retention, then save.
  • Channel has no TikTok and no Pinterest option — the plan now says both travel in the description rather than promising a filterable value.
  • Company Snapshot is 12 of 13 fields: Legal Name still reads “Company Name”, Established is blank, the vision sentence is truncated.
  • Franchise language does exist in mission and vision, so the COR argument is about the audience the marketing serves, not about the absence of franchise words.

04 Where the auditor and I disagree on filing

Its sharpest structural objection is that the space's own rule — “Work Format is a field, never a list” — forbids using Recurring or Briefed-In as destinations, and that Function should decide the pillar list. My reading is that this space has both lists by design, and the conventions name them. Eighteen page assignments differ between the two readings.
