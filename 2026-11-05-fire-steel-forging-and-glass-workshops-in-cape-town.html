Fire & steel: where you can actually forge in Cape Town

Three places within an hour of the city will put a hammer in your hand and a piece you made out of hot steel in your bag. Glassblowing is the harder ask — the studio everyone recommends closed.

Prices and dates below are from each venue's own booking page, checked today. Drive times from the CBD.

The short answer: book the blacksmith day at Babylonstoren's Soetmelksvlei — R2,200 each, a full day in the Paarl winelands, four places only, and lunch is included. That is the one built for a couple. If what you actually want is a knife you forged yourself, the answer is Eyrie Forge in Elsies River: two days, one-on-one, Friday and Saturday.

01Forging — three real options

All three are working forges with an instructor at the anvil next to you, not a demo. Ranked by how well each fits "a day out with Julie" first, then by how close you get to a finished blade.

Best with Julie

Babylonstoren · Soetmelksvlei

Paarl winelands · resident blacksmith Dan Devonshire · 16+

  • PriceR2,200 per person
  • LengthFull day, from the Babylonstoren ticket office
  • You makeA hand-forged carving fork, or a soup ladle
  • IncludedShuttle to the farm, entry, refreshments, farm lunch at the Old Stables
  • Group sizeOnly 4 places per session
  • Drive45 min · 54 km from the CBD
  • DatesCarving fork: 27 Mar 2027 · Ladle: 6 Mar, 5 Jun, 26 Jun 2027

Why this one: it is the only option that is genuinely an occasion — shuttle up the Simonsberg, a historic farmyard, fire and anvil, then a long lunch. You leave with a fork or ladle you forged, and neither of you needs any experience.

02Glass — the honest picture

You asked about blowing glass. In Cape Town, that specific thing is currently not on offer to the public, and it is worth knowing why before you spend an afternoon driving to Paarl.

Red Hot Glass — the Venetian-style studio at Spice Route outside Paarl, the one every guide recommends — is listed as permanently closed on its Google Maps listing. Its own website is still up and still says "phone to confirm opening times", which is exactly how closed businesses look online for a year or two. Do not plan a day around it without calling first.

03What I would book

  1. This week: email Eyrie Forge for the registration form and ask two questions — the price, and whether you and Julie can take the same Friday–Saturday as a pair. That is the only option that ends with a knife, and it is 26 minutes from home.
  2. For the occasion: book two of the four places on a Babylonstoren blacksmith day — 27 March 2027 for the carving fork, or 5 June 2027 for the ladle. It is the best day out of the three and the only one where lunch, the drive and the view are part of the gift.
  3. If you want to test the water cheaply: Solms Forge's R1,000 Playtime session on a Saturday, then decide whether the two-day knife course is worth the money.
  4. Glass: hold it. Unless you are happy with a kiln/stained-glass class, the honest move is a phone call to Smelt in Johannesburg before booking anything in the Cape.
