03 — The qualification, and the university confirms it

A Bachelor of Education in Foundation Phase from Rhodes University, 2022, with distinction. That is the four-year, phase-specific degree for Grades R–3 — the band in which a Grade 4 learner who is still learning to read actually needs a teacher. It is not a general BA with a one-year teaching diploma added.

It is also not only her own document. Rhodes' own 2022 notice names her among the six Education students the Dean nominated for the university's Top 100 Academic Achievement Award.

04 — The classroom this job describes, she has already taught

At Hillview Academy, from March 2023 to September 2025, she was the support teacher inside a Grade 4 assisted class of eight learners carrying ADD, ADHD, dyslexia and autism spectrum disorder between them. Her week was small-group literacy and maths interventions plus one-to-one scaffolding across the academic day. She substituted for absent teachers at short notice, and co-ran the aftercare for 25 learners from 07:30 to 18:00.

Hillview closed at a week's notice in September 2025 when its owners became insolvent, which is why the school itself can no longer be phoned and the reference below is the thing to verify against.
