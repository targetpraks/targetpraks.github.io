3. The rulings this plan stands on

  1. One number, one home. Sheets hold numbers; ClickUp holds the conversation — gaps, sign-offs, escalations. ClickUp gets no Actual, no Progress %, no Evidence field, and no mirror of the key results. The 485-task / 5,208-subtask structure is struck.
  2. Blank is not a defect; zero is not blank; and a zero with evidence is a measurement, not a gap. Four states with a fifth for "not due yet", computed from a schedule, never inferred from a blank.
  3. The activation gate, not a purge, and no quota. All 375 key results keep their hygiene; one becomes an FY2027 commitment only when target, owner, cadence, source and weight are approved in writing. I will not promise "30–60 active" — Codex is right that a number becomes a quota. Whatever passes, passes.
  4. Nothing is invented, and nothing is carried forward silently. An FY2026 target is not an FY2027 target. It is kept as history with its own dates, and a FY2027 target is approved separately.
  5. Canonical: OKR = B; A frozen read-only, its weights not lifted — Codex is right that its hierarchy is different, so A's weights are evidence, not decisions. KPI = the existing tracker, repaired, never replaced.

4. What "filled out" means

A cell holds a number only when the number names its source. A cell with a source and no number stays blank and becomes a task. No carry-forward, no estimate, no default owner.

4.1 OKR workbook B — rebuild in this order, because the ID forbids any other

Order matters: freeze the key, then change the people, then migrate the tabs. Doing it the obvious way breaks every unit tab.

  1. Freeze an immutable key. A new KR ID column of literal values (INF-FIN20) written before anything else changes, copied from the current formula output. The CONCAT formula stays where it is and is never edited again.
  2. Repopulate against the real population: 375 key results, not 434. The 59 separators stay as layout only and never enter the commitment model — no owner, no cadence, no task, no score.
  3. Effective-date the owner. Owner (name) is added beside the unit code, with Owner from date. The unit code column K is not edited until every unit tab's VLOOKUP has been re-pointed at the frozen KR ID; then it is edited once, in one pass, and the tabs are re-tested.
  4. Targets are versioned, not relabelled. Existing FY2026 targets keep their year and their value; a new Target FY2027 column is approved row by row. 2026.0 is never overwritten with FY2027 — that is exactly the silent carry-forward the honesty rule forbids.
  5. Cadence is a property of the metric, not a policy: Monthly, Quarterly, Annual, Milestone or Event. Management checkpoints are monthly for every active key result; measurement follows the metric.
  6. Source is a definition, not a label. For each active key result: the query or report, the filters, the unit, the period, the owner of that source, the freshness rule, and one worked example. Anything less stays TARGET TO BE SET BY <owner>.
  7. Actual is pulled, never typed, with source and timestamp, once the source definition exists. Where no source exists it stays blank and becomes a task.
  8. Master declared the single source; unit tabs are generated from it, after a field-level reconciliation of all 19 tabs — not a wipe.
  9. SCORE tab: weight per objective, achieved score per unit per month, MANCO roll-up — weights approved here, for this hierarchy. A's weights are not imported.

11. The audit and the panel, and what each changed

Codex (read-only, on this machine, against the real files): verdict DO NOT SHIP on v0.2. Its five demanded fixes are S2 (rehearsal and rollback), the record model in §4.1, the calculation repair in §4.2, named accountability, and proven ClickUp provisioning.
