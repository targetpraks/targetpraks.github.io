What this means

The trail is thin, and it has stopped

45.48 units were adjusted in six months across a warehouse holding 85 stocked SKUs worth roughly R610,000 at cost. That is not a disciplined count rhythm; it is a handful of corrections. The last full stock take was done on 18 May 2026 as 28 separate manual adjustments — the dedicated Stock Count module, which is enabled in this org, has been used zero times ever.

Nothing has been adjusted since 21 October 2026. In the 34 days after that, 32 invoices and 72 bills were booked on the same org, so the business did not stop moving stock — only the corrections stopped. That makes the silence a process gap, not a quiet period.

  • Volume is far below what a two-warehouse operation should show. Adjustments touch only 65 of 795 items all-time, and only 85 items hold any stock at all.
  • Stock takes dominate the reason list, not damage or theft. All-time, 54 of 86 posted documents are "Stocktaking results"; damage is a single document, write-off a single document, and theft none.
  • One document distorts the whole ledger. Two "New Stock Ordered" entries in July 2025 account for R98,234 — 82% of all R119,059 of value ever adjusted. That is opening stock booked as an adjustment, not a correction.
  • One person, one location. Every line in the window is Anne Fourie, and every line is at Infinity Brands (Warehouse). The second warehouse ("INF Head Office Procurement Purposes") has never carried a single adjustment or a single transfer order.
  • Near-perfect netting is a warning sign. August's ten documents net to +15 units but only +R2,956 of value, because most of them are same-cost pairs moving quantity between similar SKUs. That is relabelling, and it inflates the activity count without changing the stock position.

What this report does not cover

These numbers are quantity adjustments only. Total stock movement is much larger and lives elsewhere:

  • Invoices — sales out (200 recent invoices on the first page, 32 of them since the last adjustment).
  • Bills — purchases in (200 recent bills on the first page, 72 since the last adjustment).
  • Transfer orders — zero exist. No inter-warehouse movement has ever been recorded.
  • Packages — 167 shipments, which reduce or move stock depending on configuration.
  • Assembly / composite items — 103 item groups with 554 child items; assembly builds are not adjustments.
  • Credit notes and purchase returns — 25 credit notes, plus any vendor credits, are not in this dataset.

Do not read "net +11.48 units" as the stock change for the half-year. It is the correction layer only.

How the numbers were produced

Endpoints, row counts and the one trap in the data
  • GET /inventory/v1/inventoryadjustments — 90 rows on one page (per_page=200), dates 2025-05-01 → 2026-10-21, 86 adjusted / 4 draft. This feed is line-level.
  • GET /inventory/v1/inventoryadjustments/{id} — all 90 documents pulled individually. 89 have one line, one has two.
  • The trap: the list feed's total column is 0.00 on every outward (negative) line, and value_adjusted is 0 on all 90 rows. Reading value from the feed alone reports R0 for everything that left the building. The true signed value is on the document header, and it equals Σ(quantity × line price) on all 90 documents — verified row by row with zero mismatches.
  • Currency confirmed ZAR from GET /inventory/v1/organizations (org 716443079, "Infinity Brands", currency_code: ZAR).
  • Window filter applied locally on date between 2026-05-24 and 2026-11-24 inclusive.
