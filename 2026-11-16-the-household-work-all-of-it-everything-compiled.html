The chain, in order

1 · The House Roster — the instrument

6 November · Splinter · the base document

The full operations roster. 61 task lines across five weekly sections (morning run, school & activities, food, home & admin, evening & night) plus eight monthly items, and 76 fixed points laid on a week grid. Every task has one owner, a backup, the days it runs and its minutes.

The arithmetic is computed from those minutes, not estimated: 4,910 minutes of household work in the base week — 3,265 chores, 1,435 care, 210 family dinner. Per person, the fair week is Ricardo 30.8 h, Julie 30.1 h (13.2 chores + 16.9 care), Lily 12.8 h, shared 1.9 h, kids 2.7 h — 78.3 h in total over 203 items.

Four switches, each written out as its own section so the record carries all of them: Ricardo home / away (the surge week puts Julie at 38.7 h), kids with us / at Lauren's (60.3 h, 142 items), no help / 2×-per-week helper (Lily 12.8 → 7.4 h), and print/clear ticks. Five rules underneath, and an assumptions list that is still the honest part of the document.

2 · The Week — the boards the family reads

6–7 November · Batman and Adrianna · the visual cut

The same week re-cut as a colour grid: four sections — household maintenance, food & cooking, big kids, small kid — seven days across, one colour per person (Ricardo, Julie, Lily, domestic, gardener, kids). Read across to see who owns a job; read down to see whose day it is.

Batman built it for the week of 2–8 November and added a per-person breakdown — Ricardo 15 cells, Julie 12, Lily 7, domestic 4, gardener 1, kids 1 — with a plain "worth confirming" list. Adrianna built the week of 9–15 November, carrying the standing guardrails: Julie's protected 17:00–20:00 cluster feed, the night split (Ricardo 22:00–02:00, Julie 02:00–06:00), and money and legal staying with you. A third cut, the week by section, prints one section per page.

3 · The cleaning week — what the domestic actually does

11 November · Batman · the newest cut, and the one to pin up

Cut out of the roster to answer the narrow question: what happens on each of the domestic's four days. Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday, 08:00–16:00, every hour with a job and a room — linen day, deep day, rooms day, reset day — so nothing gets decided at the door in the morning.

It also fixes the standard: what done looks like at 16:00 for bathrooms, kitchen, bedrooms, living, laundry and outside. And it names the line of authority: Lily runs the domestic — the days, the standard and the list, walked on Friday and handed over before the domestic leaves Saturday. Ricardo pays for all of it.

Still open inside the work

Seven things the documents flag but nobody has answered

  1. Lily's real load. Counted at 12.8 h/week — the roster itself calls this "the number most likely to be wrong". Everything downstream of her column moves if it is.
  2. The pool. Three tasks and ~1.5 h of chores assume no service contract. If there is a service, delete them.
  3. The gardener's days. Tuesday and Friday are assumed in every board. The real pair moves two cells per board.
  4. Bed linen. Nobody has settled whether linen is the domestic's or Lily's.
  5. Bins. Refuse and recycling are assumed on Tuesday for Welgemoed — Cape Town collects both on the same day in your area since February 2026, and your street's day is unconfirmed.
  6. The 07:30 drop-off. It is a school-day job but it is not on the children's switch, so it stays in the Lauren's week. It should come off with the children.
  7. The away week. The surge configuration exists on paper (Julie 38.7 h) but no actual trip has been run through it.
One defect found. The vault note for the cleaning week says "No styled reading edition was published for this run" — but that page is live (I fetched it: HTTP 200). The note is stale against its own source. Worth having Beast correct, since that note is the one someone will read in five years.
