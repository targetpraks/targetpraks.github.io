05 Notes for the receiving broker

  • The area is measured off the drawings, not certified. Read to internal wall faces on the architect's 1:75 sheet; the method returns 10.2 m² on an office the plan prints as 10 m², so treat 101 m² as accurate to about 2%. A SAPOA or BOMA figure would come from a surveyor on site.
  • The space plans are marked "issued for comment", not as-built. Every sheet carries the draughtsman's standing note: do not scale, figured dimensions govern, check all dimensions on site.
  • The 2006 set is the council submission, not the current layout. Use it for structure, levels and elevations; use the 2021 sheets for how the floor is actually partitioned.
  • Nothing newer is on file. The last drawing in the building file is dated 3 November 2021.
