The runnable form is build_spine.py: dry-run by default, and it refuses to write without the exact string I-APPROVE-S3. Every object it creates, it records the id of, so the undo is a list of ids.

Two things in the PRD contradict each other, and one of them must lose before the first write

The Business Unit field. §1 calls the current field a defect — 13 options, mixing brands and functions. §10.5 says keep all 13, because the field mirrors the twelve numbered spaces. Both cannot be true, and it changes the field set.

My recommendation — two fields, each honest:

  • Business Unit stays the workspace register (13): it matches the space tree one-for-one, so 0 of 375 rows are stranded.
  • Function is added as its own 7-option field: FIN · HRM · ADM · ITC · MKT · SAL · FXS. That is the discipline axis the spine needs, and it stops one field carrying two meanings.
  • The Brand field carries the 8 brands: INF · EXP · TLF · PPP · DID · FXS · FXL · ALL.

Say "two fields" and the build runs as planned. Say "one field, seven" and I drop the 6 brand options from Business Unit — which strands nothing either, because the 48 retired-code rows already resolve to a function or to their brand lead.

How it is undone

ClickUp archives, never deletes. Folders and lists created by the plan are archived by their recorded ids; tasks are archived as their list goes. The 75 existing tasks and the five template lists are untouched — archive of those is a separate decision per §10.2, and purge needs your explicit approval after a close.
