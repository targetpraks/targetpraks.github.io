The structure answers itself

  • As an individual, you win every one of the fifteen. A company pays €3,000 of social security and €2,000 of accounting a year before it earns a euro, then 22% corporate tax, then 5% on the dividend. On €13,000–€28,000 of revenue that overhead alone costs more than the tax difference.
  • The crossover is revenue, not price. A company starts to beat you at roughly €26,000–€41,000 of annual revenue, depending on the property's value. That is two flats, or one large beachfront villa — not one €64,000 apartment.
  • 1 July 2027 changes the maths, but only at size. If the 15.45% non-EU transfer tax lands, a company saves 12.36% of the price on everything except a new build. On the four largest resale, auction and fixer-upper deals, that flips the ten-year return in the company's favour (A5, F1, F2, F3).

1Individual or company — the arithmetic

Same building, same guests, same rent. What changes is what the Greek state takes, and when.

Both structures use the light-touch operating model: 24% of revenue in platform, co-host, utilities, supplies and turnover costs, plus €1,920 of fixed annual costs.

A company is not a tax rate. It is a €5,000-a-year subscription — and on a €13,000 revenue flat, the subscription costs more than the tax it saves.

Where the company does win

Two thresholds, and they pull in opposite directions. On income, the company overtakes once revenue is large enough that your personal marginal rate passes 25.9% and the €5,000 overhead stops mattering:

  • €150,000 property — company wins above €36,400 of annual revenue (profits retained) or €40,800 (distributed)
  • €250,000 property — above €32,200 or €37,900
  • €400,000 property — above €25,900 or €30,500, because depreciation shielding grows with value

On capital, the company wins only if the non-EU transfer tax is enacted. It saves 12.36% of the price up front. On the €389,000 Archangelos house that is €48,080 — enough to outweigh a decade of company overhead.

The order of operations that follows: complete at 3.09% as an individual before 1 July 2027 if you can. If you cannot, and the ticket is above roughly €150,000, take the company. If you are buying one €64,000 flat either way, stay an individual.

6What the two constraints cost you

You asked for one beachfront or near-beach property in each section, and one in the most popular place. Both are available. Both are paid for in yield.

Net yield, individual ownership, after everything.

The beachfront premium

F3, one of four unfinished villas on a 5,000 m² waterfront plot at Tholaros, returns 3.8% net. The best listing on the page returns 7.1%. You pay about 3.3 points of yield for the water.

That is not automatically wrong — beachfront land cannot be replicated, and the exit multiple is a different bet. It is wrong only if you told yourself you were buying income.

The popularity premium

Lindos (F1) returns 3.6%. Rhodes Town's new builds (N1, N2) return 2.5%–2.6%. The Old Town listed townhouse (F2) returns 1.6% before you have restored it.

The most popular place on the island is the most expensive place to earn a living from. Rhodes pays you to be twenty minutes away from where everyone wants to be photographed.

7What can go wrong, in order of cost

The downside column assumes rates fall a quarter and occupancy falls a fifth at the same time.

Auction mechanics

30% of the hammer price is due immediately, the balance within a short window, and you generally cannot look inside first. Lots can be withdrawn or adjourned. Four of the five auction lots here bid on 25–27 November 2026 — that is four weeks, not four months, to raise a deposit, run a title search and decide.

The unfinished flats

A2 is 65% built under a 2006 permit. Permits lapse, and an unfinished property cannot get the AMA short-let registration until it has power, water and a completion certificate. Budget the re-licensing, not just the plasterboard.

Short-letting in a block

An Athens court held in July 2026 that a short-let in a residential building requires the co-owners' consent, and a bill is being drafted to codify a 51% approval threshold. Buy a maisonette, a detached house, or a building whose rules already permit short-lets.

Two dated risks

1 July 2027 — non-EU transfer tax rises from 3.09% to 15.45%. On the €389,000 house that is €48,080. 31 December 2026 — the new-build VAT suspension expires. If it lapses, 24% lands on the price: €93,600 on the €390,000 Rhodes Town flat.

Downside test

Rates down 25%, occupancy down 20%, works up 30%. The renovated auction flat (A4) still returns 3.3% net. The Rhodes Town new builds fall to 1.4%. The beachfront villa lands at 2.0% — it survives, but it stops being an income asset and becomes a land holding.

Fully managed instead of light-touch — 20% to a manager plus a 15.5% platform fee — takes A4 from 7.1% to 3.4%. The manager is not a cost line; it is the deal.

8Method, and what I could not verify

Purchase costs. 3.09% transfer tax, 1.2% notary, 1.5% legal, 0.5% registry, 0.5% incidental — 6.79% of price. New builds carry no transfer tax while the VAT suspension runs, and 3.7% of fees.

Works. €100/m² for a cosmetic refresh, €350–450/m² for a dated flat, €700–1,100/m² for a full restoration or a shell, each plus a 10% contingency already inside the figure. Furnishing €12,000, or nothing where the developer delivers equipped.

Revenue. Area-based nightly rates and annual occupancy: Rhodes Town and Old Town €125 at 45%, Lindos €140 at 42%, Ixia–Ialysos €95 at 45%, Archangelos €78–82 at 42–44%, the south coast €110 at 40%, Tholaros beachfront €180 at 42%, Embonas €70 at 35%. These are conservative readings of a market where two data providers disagreed badly — AirROI and Airbtics put materially different revenue on the same island — so the model leans on the rate and occupancy range I could corroborate rather than either provider's headline.

Honest gaps. The Ialyssos auction lot (A1) publishes no bedroom count — 82 m² on a ground floor, treated as two bedrooms. Two lots I found were withdrawn mid-research and are excluded. The AMA and co-ownership position of each specific building is not verified for any listing here; that is a lawyer's job before deposit, not a spreadsheet's.
