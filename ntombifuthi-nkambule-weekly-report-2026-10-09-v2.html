The allocation and logged fields are not what they look like

Allocated is the owner record’s work_values — on this portal a recurring-task template default stamped on generation, not a human estimate. Logged is log_hours.total_hours — a cumulative per-task total, not a per-day timesheet; the portal’s own time-log report returns zero rows. So neither may be divided by worked hours to make a “utilisation rate”; the honest rates above are output counts against present hours.

Definition of every metric on these pages

  • Supposed hours — sum of ShiftEndTime − ShiftStartTime over her present days. It is per-day, so a half Friday counts as half.
  • Recorded hours — TotalHours from Zoho People, which nets out paid breaks.
  • Present hours — raw LastOut − FirstIn; the widest figure.
  • Late in / early out — more than 5 minutes after shift start / before shift end.
  • Past shift — clocked out after ShiftEndTime; the “extra hours after work” column, corroborated by the system’s own OverTime.
  • Output counts — tasks closed/opened/touched from Projects timestamps inside the window; documents from WorkDrive ZUID match.
  • Not used as a rate: work_values allocation (template default) and log_hours (cumulative per task) — both shown, both labelled.

The template this report establishes — run it for anyone, any week

  1. Resolve the person into their keys per product (erecNo, ZUID, ZPUID) — passing one where another is expected returns silent emptiness.
  2. Route by department (BU 6: ADM Head Office: Administration here) to the systems that actually hold their work.
  3. Sweep hours first (People), output second (Projects), evidence third (WorkDrive), narrative last (Cliq).
  4. Print rostered / recorded / present side by side and never collapse them into one number.
  5. Pull attendance after close of business, not during the day — Friday’s record moved between two reads.
  6. Report the whole board beside the individual, so no single week is read in isolation.
