Creality Hi · Klipper, macros and slicer

Set for quality. The speed was never in the printer.

Three layers decide how a part looks: the printer's own firmware config, the start macro it runs, and the profile in the slicer. All three were pointing the same way — faster. All three are now changed, read back off the machine, and then handed to three independent reviewers to find what I had missed. They found four things.

The start macro

The printer runs a Creality start sequence before every job, and that sequence was quietly resetting the machine to its old fast defaults — 12,000 mm/s² acceleration and a 10 mm/s corner speed. Fixing the config alone would have been overwritten on the next print. Both files are patched.

The slicer profile

This is where most of the damage was. The file named "quality" was running 160 mm/s inner walls, 180 mm/s infill, two perimeters and 6000 mm/s² acceleration. It is now a genuine quality profile — process-hi-quality-v2.json — riding on a machine profile that makes the fast numbers impossible: machine-hi-quality.json caps acceleration at 3000 and corner speed at 5, whatever the job asks for.

What the council caught

Three reviewers, run separately, given the files and the live printer and told to be adversarial: one on Klipper semantics, one on the slicer profiles against the OrcaSlicer source, one on real-world failure modes. These are the findings that changed something.

"The machine is capped at 3000" — wrong, and it was my claim

Klipper lets a job raise the limits at run time, and Orca's emit_machine_limits_to_gcode switch is ignored for Klipper machines entirely. What is true: the profile cannot emit more than 3000, and the machine now rests at 3000 / 1500. A hand-edited G-code file can still go faster — that is Klipper, not a setting.

Gap fill was still printing at 250 mm/s

A real one: 3,342 gap-fill moves in the test part at the old fast speed, exactly where surfaces meet. Now 50 mm/s.

First layer was only half slowed down

First-layer walls were at 25 mm/s, but first-layer infill stayed at 60 and first-layer travel stayed at full speed. Now 30 mm/s and 50 %.

The touch-screen rapid mode doubled the feed rate

Rmode applied M220 S125 and raised velocity to 500 mm/s. Now 100 % and 300 mm/s.

The arc count I published was off by one

My regex counted G21 as an arc move. The real figure in the first slice was 62,816, not 62,817.

What the council confirmed as right

Corner speed 5 as the quality direction — Klipper's own resonance guide says not to go above 5 with input shaping on. The 0.16 mm layer with 0.42/0.45 lines. Six top layers giving 0.96 mm. The 2.62× time increase: credible, and driven mostly by structure (more layers, twice the walls), not by every feature slowing down.

Still open — none of it guessed

Pressure advance is still 0.04

Not calibrated for this filament. A 15 × 15 × 80 mm tower is built and staged, marked every 10 mm, sweeping 0.020 → 0.100 with Klipper's own TUNING_TOWER. It needs eyes on the finished surface.

Klipper reference →

Input shaping is being re-measured

Factory MZV at 44.6 Hz X and 24.8 Hz Y. The Y figure sits in the 25 Hz region where Klipper warns even MZV can over-smooth, so it is being measured rather than trusted.

Klipper reference →

Both heaters get a fresh PID

The bed's factory numbers are coarse (20 / 0.1 / 0.06). Extruder and bed are both re-tuned and saved as soon as the bed is free.

The printer takes twice as long as the slicer says

The slicer says 1 h 28 m for this cradle; the machine tracked to roughly 2 h 50 m. That is a real gap, not rounding. Planned test: the same circle sent as arcs and as pre-cut steps, timed on the machine, to see what the 0.1 mm arc setting actually costs.

The filament box invents "used up" and pauses

It paused a job at 0 % today and had to be cleared with a host reboot. On a multi-hour plate a pause can leave a seam in silk, so it is a real risk until it is caught in the act with its state recorded.

Read back from the printer after the changes: gcode_arcs resolution 0.1, square_corner_velocity 5.0, bed_mesh fade 2 → 10, max_accel 3000, max_accel_to_decel 1500, pressure_advance 0.04, shaper mzv 44.6 / 24.8 Hz. Slices made on the laptop, real slicer output, not estimates — the estimates in this house have been wrong by 9–13 % before.
