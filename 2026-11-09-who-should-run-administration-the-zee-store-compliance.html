5 · What I would do

  • Name one owner of the seat, and make it Anne. Publish the four pillars as her remit so the split is visible. Stage one ends when every recurring task in the Administration project has an owner who is not "whoever is around". If you are choosing a long-run successor rather than relieving the person already in the seat, §7 ranks Elmain Ruiters first — treat that as the appointment question, separate from today's relief question.
  • Give compliance its own name. Insurance, COCs, inspections and vehicle trackers go to Ntombifuthi as a written monthly responsibility with a filing calendar. It is the half that fits her profile, and it is the half where a quiet miss costs the most. Stage two ends when every compliance item has a due date in the system, not in someone's head.
  • Take the approval gate off the CAO's desk. Jaques signs and sets the standard; he should not be closing building quotes. Stage three ends when his own open queue is under ten items.
  • Put a checklist, not a warning, behind Anne's stretch verdict. Her risk is documented by the assessment and visible in the overdue count. The fix is a two-minute pass on compliance and money-adjacent tasks before they close — not fewer tasks.
One honest caveat. The assessment measures self-reported preference, and the task record measures what got recorded. A person who does quiet work without a ticket looks smaller in this data than they are — Ntombifuthi is the likeliest to be under-credited on that count, and Anne the likeliest to be over-credited. The recommendation holds under both readings, but the gap between their fit scores is smaller than the gap in their ticket counts.

Method and evidence

  • Seat fit scores: the BU 6 · Administration · Administrator and ZOR BU 2 · Administrator blueprints in Constellation → Positions, scored against the most recent fit run (2 November 2026) for 45 staff on file, 10,581 assessment responses.
  • Staff list, reporting lines and joining dates: Constellation → Staff and Zoho People employee records.
  • Task volumes, overdue counts and turnaround: Zoho Projects, BU 3 ADM — Administration (1,755 tasks, 1 January – 9 November 2026), BU 8 Brand Ops 2 (1,254 of 1,488), and for the outsiders BU 1 FIN — Finance (2,687), BU 2 HRM — Human Resources (752) and BU 7 Brand Ops 1 (580).
  • Topic counts are keyword classifications of task names — Zee Store, compliance, building, paperwork — and are indicative of mix, not an audit of effort.
  • Verdict bands: strong, fit, stretch, not-fit, assigned by weighted rubric against each seat's blueprint, including hard must-haves.
