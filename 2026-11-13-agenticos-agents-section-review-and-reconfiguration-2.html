5 · Cost — the one thing I did not decide

Need your word: monthly budget per assistant

Seventeen assistants have no monthly spend cap. Nothing in the platform stops a long loop; the only ceiling is the prepaid balance on the company key.

The three platform assistants already carry one: AgenticOS Bot $100, Architect Bot $20, DOC Bot $100. The estate assistants — the ones staff will actually use — carry none, which is backwards.

My proposal: $10 a month each on the sixteen unit and brand assistants, $25 on the two document and architecture desks. At deepseek-v4.1-flash rates that is room for thousands of turns, and it turns a silent runaway into a message.

Say “budgets at 10” and I will set them. No cap is applied until you do.

Everything else on cost is now bounded: the model is deepseek-v4.1-flash on every estate assistant (DOC Bot stays on inkling-2-small, which is the vision model, deliberately), and step count is capped at 24 where it was the platform default.
