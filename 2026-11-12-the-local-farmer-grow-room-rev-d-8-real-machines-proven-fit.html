A real machine.
A measured fit.

At W = 1 300 mm, 8 machines fit as 2 + 2 + 3 + 1 = 8, giving 288 tray spaces, with 965 mm working aisles and a 1 200 mm west passage. The original wall-and-island arrangement fails; this fit uses back-to-back central rows and removes the north surface. Door access and the actual tray-pull envelope remain unconfirmed.

The fit, in arithmetic

Each central row uses 2 x 1 300 + 1 x 50 = 2 650 mm within 2 950 mm. The south row uses 3 x 1 300 + 2 x 50 = 4 000 mm within 4 450 mm. The east spur uses 1 300 mm within 1 685 mm.

The two central rows share a 50 mm rear slot. Below the bench, the depth budget is 3 780 mm. Subtract the three 600 mm machine depths and the rear slot: (3 780 - 1800 - 50) / 2 = 965 mm. Equal aisles attain the largest possible minimum working aisle for this zoning arrangement.

The proposed 3 + 3 + 2 wall-and-island scheme fails: the middle row needs 2 650 mm, but two 900 mm side aisles leave only 2 050 mm; its east and south rows also overlap by 100 mm.

This is the maximum of the declared fixed-fitting zoning family: two back-to-back central rows, a south row and a north-east spur, with at least 900 mm clear at every working front and along the west connector. The model does not prove a global optimum over every rotated or relocated arrangement. Moving retained fittings, changing the aisle criterion or discovering a door could change the capacity. The drawing is a dimensioned feasibility study, not construction approval.

Confirm before ordering or building

  1. Machine: confirm W, 600 mm depth and 1 960 mm height, including feet, plumbing, cables and any required wall clearance. The comparable shelf supports the 1 300 mm width assumption; it does not establish this client’s tray geometry.
  2. Trays: the client production model establishes 6 tiers x 6 trays = 36 spaces. Tray symbols show positions only. Obtain actual tray dimensions, loading direction and withdrawal distance; standard 1020 trays have not been assumed.
  3. Operation: 900 mm is an assumed one-person service and walking criterion. Confirm the combined operator and tray-pull envelope fits the 965 mm aisles. Opposing machines must be serviced one at a time; trolley passing is not proven.
  4. Access: REV B has no door. Measure its position, clear opening and swing; reserve its approach before releasing this layout. No door or swing has been invented.
  5. Installation: approve removal of the herb shelves and north surface. The bench and west work surface remain. Confirm back access in the 50 mm slot, flush wall installation at the east spur and south row, ceiling clearance, power, water, drainage and ventilation.

Sources: the supplied REV B plan and BRIEF2 define room geometry and the client’s production capacity. The Urban Organics comparable shelf supports the assumed overall width and height only.
