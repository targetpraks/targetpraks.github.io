Why the Helios wins as a gift. It is R4 149 cheaper than the Nural, it is the model 1 035 owners rate 4.7/5 (92% would recommend), it throws air at 120 km/h from a brushless motor, and it comes with a free R440 heat-protect spray this weekend. As a present it is the safe, genuinely excellent object; the Nural is the one that impresses someone who already cares about hair tools.

The trap on the Dyson side. On Takealot the base Supersonic and the Nural are both R6 999 today — the Nural is normally R1 500 dearer. Buying the base model at that price is paying full Nural money for last year's feature set. If you go Dyson, go Nural.

02 Independent testing, not the marketing

TechRadar ran the ghd Speed and the Dyson Supersonic Nural side by side. Their findings, in their words: the ghd Speed is “clearly more powerful” and hotter at maximum, with four heat and four speed settings against Dyson's three; the Nural is the better-featured machine — the scalp-protect sensor genuinely works and you can get right up to the head, and it is 455 g against the ghd's 620 g. Design and comfort was called a draw.

What that means here: raw drying speed and simple controls → ghd. Comfort, weight and intelligent heat → Dyson. The ghd Helios sits below the Speed on airflow but is the model with the deepest owner record, and reviewers of the Speed still describe it as fast and loud rather than quiet.
