R46 460 a year outside school

At school, free: Sports Hour (hockey and netball matches), swim squad.

07 Two things to know before you commit

  • No rugby at Reddam. It is not in either 2027 extra-curricular booklet — the school runs cricket, tennis, softball, swimming, chess, golf, MTB, dance, football, netball, hockey, debating and drama. Rugby is a club decision outside school, and it collides with the mentor's no-contact rule. That is a judgement for you, not a timetable problem.
  • Coding is not at school either. Neither booklet offers coding, robotics or LEGO. Everything in that group is bought: R11 400–13 440 a year per child, and it is the single biggest outside line for both of them.
  • Season shape. Cricket is Terms 1 and 4, football and hockey are Terms 2 and 3, chess runs all year, and swimming and Hip Hop run all year. Their lists mostly fit the seasons already — the only genuine summer clash is cricket against tennis on a Monday.
  • Prices are 2026 unless marked. The RDMS, RDDA and Raconteurs lines are the published 2027 rates; the outside providers are 2026 and will move 5–10%.
