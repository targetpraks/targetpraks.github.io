5. Two smaller improvements

Disk guard

The host hit 100 % once with nothing watching. A read-only check now runs every 10 minutes as a systemd user timer, writes ~/agenticos/readiness/disk-guard.json, and fails the unit above 90 % so systemctl --user --failed shows it. It never deletes anything. First run: 72.9 % used, status ok, timer active.

A correction on the unit-count bridge

My earlier note said the ClickUp unit-count bridge needed a systemd unit with Restart=always. Reading the helper shows that is wrong: it speaks a lockstep frame protocol over SSH stdin/stdout and is started per challenge by the caller, with a bounded window. Keeping it "always running" would break the pipe it needs. What it actually needs is its caller (the Air at 192.168.88.70) staying honest — the local helper checksum verifies against SHA256SUMS.json, and it did tonight.
