Method notes

  • Logged hours are the per-session log_hour entries from Zoho Projects timesheets, read per task with module:{% raw %}{"type":"task","id":...}{% endraw %} and summed in code. Each carries a start time, an end time and a creation timestamp; on every one of the 88 entries the creation timestamp matches the session end, confirming they are live entries rather than backfills.
  • Present hours come from Zoho People daily attendance (biometric clock), not from the timesheet.
  • Overdue is computed directly (open, not closed-type, end date before 09 Oct 15:00 SAST), not taken from the ${overdue} macro, which returns closed tasks too.
  • Allocation is the work_values entry on her owner record for each task, not total_work.
  • Times are SAST (UTC+2). Zoho Cliq returns America/Los_Angeles timestamps, converted here.
  • Zoho Sign's MCP endpoint is disabled (error 31006), so the signature queue could not be read independently. Signing status rests on the signed PDFs filed in WorkDrive and her own confirmations.
