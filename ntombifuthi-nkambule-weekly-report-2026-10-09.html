The allocation figure is template noise, not an estimate

Of the 39 tasks she touched or closed this week, 33 carry exactly 15.92 h of allocated work. That is the ADM recurring-task template's default applied on generation — it appears on Recurring Tasks, Ad-hoc and Briefed-In alike, on a five-minute renewal tracker and on a franchise agreement signing, identically. Dividing the total by her working hours gives 19.8×. That is not a utilisation rate; it is 39 × the template. No allocation-based utilisation figure should be quoted for this team until the template default is replaced with real effort estimates.

Time logging is not in use

Zoho Projects' get_time_logs returns zero rows for her for 03–09 Oct, and zero rows for the whole portal for October, September and July. The log_hours totals that do exist are cumulative per task, rolled up months or years after the fact — they cannot be split by day and so cannot be matched against a working week. Hours logged versus hours worked is not answerable in this portal, and any percentage quoted from it will be an artefact of when the rollup last ran.

Method notes

  • Allocation is the work_values entry for her single owner record on each task (owners_and_work.owners[]), not total_work. Only 5 of 243 tasks have more than one owner.
  • Logged is log_hours.total_hours — a cumulative per-task figure. It is not a timesheet and it is not per-day.
  • Overdue is computed directly (open, not closed-type, end date before 09 Oct 15:00 SAST), not taken from the ${overdue} macro, which returns closed tasks too.
  • Zoho Sign's MCP endpoint is disabled (error 31006), so the signature queue could not be read independently. Signing status above rests on the signed PDFs filed in WorkDrive and her own confirmations.
  • Times are SAST (UTC+2). Zoho Cliq returns America/Los_Angeles timestamps, converted here.

The three decisions this report leaves open

  1. Fix the allocation template. Until the 15.92 h default is replaced with real effort estimates, no utilisation or capacity number from Zoho Projects is trustworthy for this team.
  2. Decide whether daily time logging is required. If it is, it must be turned on and enforced; if it is not, stop reporting against it. Today it is neither, and the numbers it produces are misleading.
  3. Put the EOD back on a fixed trigger. Her EOD was the most reliable signal of what happened in ADM — thirteen entries in September, one this week. It is the cheapest fix on this page.
