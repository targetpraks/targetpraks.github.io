Monthly or annual — Asana, ClickUp and Hive at 40 seats

Every vendor charges more when you pay monthly. The question is which one charges least for the flexibility, and which one lets you give seats back. Two of the three do. One does not.

Prices read from each vendor's own pricing page and price calculator on 19 October 2026 · FX 16.461903 ZAR/USD · VAT 15% included in every ZAR figure
The short answer

Hive Teams + Proofing, paid monthly, at R17,417 a month — because it is the only one whose monthly price cancels Filestage. ClickUp Business is the cheaper monthly seat (R14,388) and the more honest monthly contract, but it leaves Filestage on the bill, and together they land at R19,578 a month — R26k a year more than Hive.

Asana Advanced is out on monthly: R23,088 a month, no video proofing, and a timer that only records what you type in afterwards.

05The path I would take from here

  1. Start with ten seats, monthly, not forty. Hive Teams + Proofing at ten people is R4,354 a month. Put the people who actually review work on it: the brand managers and the designers.
  2. Run the one-day kill test inside that pilot: a 20-page PDF, a 60-second video, an image set, and one external approver reached by email with no account. The test passes only if it ends with an Approved stamp carrying a name and a date, and the card moves. If it fails, Hive is out and you go to ClickUp + Filestage at the same seat price.
  3. Spend one full day in the Hive bell and Home page before you judge the inbox. Pin Approvals to Home, filter the bell to Approvals + @mentions + Assignments, push set to "Away or offline". If that still feels like work, the inbox is worth its price and ClickUp is the answer — but now you know what you are paying R26k a year for.
  4. Convert to annual before the third month ends. That single step saves R54,522 a year on Hive — more than the pilot costs — and it is the only reason to touch monthly at all.
  5. Do not add the External users add-on until a real client asks to log in. Hive's email approver needs no account, so most client review can happen without paying R45,434 a year for seats.
