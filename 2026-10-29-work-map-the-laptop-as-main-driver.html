Three things to settle before this is solid

1 · the dependency you are creating The mini has FileVault on and no auto-login. After a power cut or reboot it stops at the lock screen and the whole fleet — Orca runtime, Paperclip, gateway, crons — stays down until someone logs in at the keyboard. Making the laptop your driver means the mini becomes a server you depend on. Either enable auto-login (that needs FileVault off — a real trade-off, your call) or accept a rare walk to the mini after an outage.
2 · the Air's file access is not wired /Volumes/Hiksemi 1TB is not mounted on the Air and there is no keychain entry for it — the mount rail logs “NO KEYCHAIN ENTRY … not mounting” every 10 minutes. You do not need it for Orca. If you want Finder access to the SSD, run air-mount-devmini.sh --connect once on the Air and type the password yourself — I will not type it.
3 · two cockpits, one seat The Air's T3 Code is running its own local server with 2 projects — it is not connected to the mini. T3 stays the cockpit when you are sitting at the mini. Keep it that way for now: one cockpit per machine is simpler than two half-connected ones. If Orca becomes your daily driver everywhere, then we move the seat to Orca deliberately instead of drifting into it.
