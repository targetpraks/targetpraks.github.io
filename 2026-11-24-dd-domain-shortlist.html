GoDaddy, behind Domains By Proxy, parked on GoDaddy name servers and redirecting to divorcemag.com — which itself now answers “Site Not Configured”. A twenty-six-year-old publisher asset: expect a five-figure ask, not a registration. Not worth chasing while the product is still at R99 a month.

Live, and it is one man’s Google Site about cooking his way through a divorce. No product, no store, no app — but the exact South African name is his until at least February 2027.

05 Where to buy, and the two traps

  1. .co.za and .org.za at xneelo — R110 a year, registration and renewal the same number. Afrihost is R197 to start and R109 to renew, so xneelo wins the first year and ties after that.
  2. gTLDs at Porkbun, billed in dollars — .app is R153 there against R369 at xneelo. A South African card carries a small conversion fee and you are done.
  3. Expect the premium check at checkout. A registry can flag a name as premium and only the cart shows the real number. If any of these three comes back above the price on this page, stop and tell me — I will re-price it.
  4. Renewals are the real cost. The three recommended names renew at R260, R110 and R218 — predictable. The names in section 03 were left out because their second year costs two to ten times their first.

What I did not do: I registered nothing, contacted neither owner and spent nothing. Both the .com and the .co.za belong to other people; whether to make an approach is your call, not mine.
