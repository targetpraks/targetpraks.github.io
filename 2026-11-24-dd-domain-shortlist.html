05 Where to buy, and the two traps

  1. .co.za and .org.za at xneelo — R110 a year, registration and renewal the same number. Afrihost is R197 to start and R109 to renew, so xneelo wins the first year and ties after that.
  2. gTLDs at Porkbun, billed in dollars — .app is R153 there against R369 at xneelo. A South African card carries a small conversion fee and you are done.
  3. Expect the premium check at checkout. A registry can flag a name premium and only the cart shows the real number. If any of the three comes back above the price on this page, stop and tell me — I will re-price it.
  4. Renewals are the real cost. The recommended three renew at R260, R110 and R218 — predictable. The names in section 03 were left out because their second year costs two to ten times their first.

the verdict on that name

Side brand, never the letterhead

The app produces court-ready documents. Whatever address sits on that paperwork gets read by a judge, a mediator and the other side’s attorney, and a profane domain becomes their first exhibit. App review and paid ads treat it as a risk flag too, so you would fight the platforms before you fight the case. Used as a venting channel or a merch line, linked from the app but never printed on a court document, the name has real energy and costs R110 to hold.

What I did not do: I registered nothing, contacted neither owner and spent nothing. Both the .com and the .co.za belong to other people; whether to make an approach is your call, not mine.
