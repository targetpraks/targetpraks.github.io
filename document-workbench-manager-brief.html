What is expected of every manager

  1. Open your view, not the whole table. Brand leads work the brand view; department heads work their department view; anyone taking their own queue uses My assigned work.
  2. Take ten rows this week. Read Next action and do the one thing it names — find the document, link it, get it approved, tick it complete.
  3. Decide the rows that are not yours. Not applicable, shared group reference, folder or system: each is a decision with a reason, not a blank left for the next person.
  4. Leave evidence behind. A link that opens, an attachment, or a note naming who approved it and when. Completion without evidence is not completion.
Vengeance: a row left undecided is a row that fails an audit. Ten rows a week per manager closes the register inside a quarter — and the person who inherits your work will thank you for a link that opens.
