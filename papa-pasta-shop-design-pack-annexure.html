The proportion is fixed by the pack. This annexure chooses the accent only — it does not re-weight the triad, and no store may invert it. The measured figures above are computed from the colour value itself, so a future edit to the hex re-derives them: they are not typed in.

Why the plate leads with the hex and not a trade name

Paint names travel badly. A hex value, an RGB triple and a physical sample do not. The nearest RAL is given as a starting point for a paint match, never as the specification.

The closure cost, in plain terms

LIT surfaces: recoloured in an afternoon, store open, no dust. PAINTED surfaces: recoloured over days, store closed, dust and smell. Five of the ten surfaces above are painted — including the tile wall and the sign — so a colour change here is a refit event, not a refresh. A franchisee should know that before choosing, not after.

The white field and the black grounding are not on this list. They are not the store's colour to choose; they are the store's structure. The split is counted from the ten rows above — 5 LIT, 5 PAINTED — so it cannot drift from the table.

The calibration rule

Each zone is read with a spectrometer against the plate, and adjusted until the measured value is ΔE ≤ 3 from #1B4FD8. The RGB triple above is a starting point that gets you close on this product family; it is not a guarantee across a different batch of modules. Calibrate, then record the values that actually matched.

Three things this card deliberately does not contain

No prices — a specification is not a quotation, and a pack that prints a number becomes a contract term. No supplier names — the specification is brand-neutral so a franchisee can buy locally. No "or similar" — where an equivalent is permitted, the main pack defines what equivalent means (§06.1).

Order in this sequence

1 → signage (longest external approval) · 2 → counter and facing panels · 3 → tile · 4 → LED, drivers and controller together (one batch, or the zones drift) · 5 → paint last, on site, after the dust. Paint ordered early is paint mixed in one batch and applied in another month's batch — and the store has two blues.

The five things this annexure may not do

  • It may not change the triad, the proportion, or the white field and black grounding.
  • It may not change the lighting base temperature, the levels, or the luminaire schedule.
  • It may not introduce a colour that is not on the network menu.
  • It may not alter the crest, the EST line or the brand line.
  • It may not carry a cost, a quotation, or a supplier name.
