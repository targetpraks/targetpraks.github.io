Where the claims come from

  • A pg_dump of a database with no rows is still a valid archive — it is just tiny. That is why the 100 KB floor, not gzip -t, is the check that caught this: PostgreSQL pg_dump · SQL dump backups
  • Streaming a tar out of a container (docker exec … tar czf - … > file) keeps the host tar binary out of the write path: Docker volumes — back up, restore, migrate
  • docker cp preserves source metadata, which is why the staged copies carry the container's own timestamps: docker container cp
  • The deny(1) file-write-create line is macOS App Sandbox policy, not a disk fault: Apple — App Sandbox
