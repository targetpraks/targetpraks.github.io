The ruling

Don't buy People Plus

Every app in it — People, Recruit, Expense, Cliq, Connect, Vault — is already carried by the Zoho One seat we hold. It would be a second bill for the same software, and it drops the suite the brands actually run on.

The one trigger that changes it

If the group ever leaves the suite, HR goes to People Plus Enterprise, not to One. Five HR seats cost R7,560 a year there against R75,600 for five flexible One seats — the same 10× gap.

Where the money really is

208 of the 257 directory identities were inactive in July. Each one still holding a full seat costs R6,216 a year. Cleaning that up is worth more than any choice between these two plans.

For client quotes (we resell One and CRM at 15% monthly / 40% annual): People Plus Enterprise is the cheap HR wedge when a client only wants HR, and One is the whole-company sale. Same maths, opposite order.
