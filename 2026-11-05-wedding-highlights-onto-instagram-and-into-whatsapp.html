The catch nobody mentions: Instagram says reels over 3 minutes won't be recommended to people who don't follow you. Your 5:15 is fine for everyone who follows you — it just won't be pushed to strangers. For a wedding film that is exactly the right trade.

Do not upload the original 16:9 file to Instagram. It would be shrunk into a small strip with big empty bars above and below, and on a phone that reads as broken. What I changed inside the files

  • Reel: original 1920×1080 re-framed onto a 1080×1920 canvas (full frame at 1080 wide, blurred background fill), H.264 High, CRF 19, AAC 192 kbps, frame rate kept at 25 fps — converting to 30 fps would only duplicate frames and add judder.
