Company decision lane — deployed

Status: live on the company Hermes host, 2026-11-13. Written by: Bruce Wayne (Chief of Staff) for Ricardo and IT. Scope: Infinity Brands only. Separate installation, separate log, separate key.

1. What this is

A decision model the company Hermes seats can call for bounded decisions — triage, routing, completeness checks, risk pre-checks, scores — instead of spending a full chat turn on a closed question. It answers in about a second and returns typed answers, not prose.

  • Model: Decision 3.0 clef:27b on Ollama Cloud.
  • Endpoint: POST https://ollama.com/v1/systemone — the model refuses ordinary chat calls.
  • Credential: the company OLLAMA_API_KEY already in ~/.hermes/.env on the host; read at call time, never written anywhere else. No new key was created for this.
  • Why cloud-only: that host has no local decision model and no Ollama daemon.

3. Does it know when to call it

Yes — the block in each SOUL.md gives the five gates, their thresholds, the otherwise paths, and the boundary (bounded decisions only; never text, code or multi-step reasoning). Without that block the tools existed and nothing would have called them; that was the gap found on 2026-11-13.

5. Cost and limits

  • Every call bills the company Ollama account. One call is roughly a 300-token decision, about a second of wall time.
  • The platform still carries no price row for ollama.com models, so per-run cost reads zero until pricing is entered — watch the account itself.
  • The model is answer-only: it cannot write text, and it rejects chat-style calls by design.
  • A routing shortlist of seven options rarely clears the 0.70 bar; keep shortlists to three or four.

6. Rollback

Delete the company-gate block from each config.yaml and the <!-- company-decision-lane v1 --> block from each SOUL.md, or restore them from ~/.hermes/company-gate/backups/<timestamp>/. The lane itself is inert when nothing calls it. Nothing else on the host was changed.
