Positive and negative keywords are one audit, not two. 7.4 How the cadence is governed

  • Monthly — statistics by the 5th working day, audits by the 10th, the optimisation sprint closes by month end. One owner signs the month's report row.
  • Quarterly — campaign review, brand and asset audit, competitor and partner scan. These feed the next quarter's campaigns, not the current one.
  • Annually — next year's always-on calendar loaded before December, OKRs reset with the year's actuals.
  • No new fields, no new lists. Work Format = Recurring is a field value, not a list. The lane has one Recurring list and that is where all of this sits.
