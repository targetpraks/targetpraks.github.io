7.2 The order of a load, if you say go

What follows is the order work would move in if and when you say go. Every step writes only to lists that already exist. There is no step that creates a folder, a list, a field, a status, a view or a document, and no step that moves or copies an existing task.

  1. Nothing loads today

    The document holds no Divorced Dads work item yet — its campaign and content tables are empty. The only real bodies are 400 keywords, 40 collateral items and 39 audit metrics, and none of them is urgent. So phase 1 is deliberately: leave it.

  2. When you say go: SEO first, because it is the only finished body of work

    The 400 rows of Logic Master Keyword go to SEO Research & Management › KW Research, 50 rows per run, deduped on Keyword + Location against what is already in the list before any create. No new custom fields: the columns map onto the fields in §7.1 and the rest travel in the description.

  3. Then the registers, as records not tasks

    40 collateral rows → Creative & Design › Asset Library; 39 audit metrics → Creative & Design › Creative Projects as one project with 39 checklist items; 3+5 email segments → Content Management › Email. These are rows to keep, not work to schedule, so they carry no dates.

  4. Then the brief's outreach, as three campaigns

    Get Involved's three tracks — private beta (500 fathers, US/UK/AU/ZA), partnership, investment — become three tasks in Campaigns & Promotions › Campaigns, not a new list. The roadmap's dated milestones become subtasks of one programme in Team Operations › MKT COR - DID Projects.

  5. Intake stays empty until a human briefs something

    MKT COR - DID Briefed-In is the one intake list. It fills when a person submits a request through the help desk — not from a document import. The plan does not seed it.

  6. Assign one person, never several

    The space refuses multiple assignees (multiple_assignees: false). Every created item gets Ena Dizon (105744008), the marketing owner; any second name goes in the description.

  7. Dates go in twice, on purpose

    A space automation rewrites due_date to the following Monday within about five seconds of create. So: create with no date, then set the real date in a second call — and leave genuinely undated items undated. Deadline law for anything briefed stays four working days.

  8. Prove it afterwards, read back three times

    List reads in this space return false zeros. After any write pass, read the target list three times, three seconds apart, and require all three to agree before reporting a count.

  9. Nothing else. Ever, in this space.

    No Daily lists (retired), no second intake list, no second meeting list, no pillar folder that does not already exist, and no "Divorced Dads" folder beside the lane. The structure is the point.
