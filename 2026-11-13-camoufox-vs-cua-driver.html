Camoufox vs CUA Driver

They are not rivals. Camoufox is a browser that hides you — a patched Firefox whose fingerprint is spoofed in C++ so a site cannot tell a bot is there. CUA Driver is a hand that uses your Mac — a native daemon that reads windows, clicks and types in real apps without stealing your cursor. One evades detection on the web. The other operates your actual desktop. Pick by target, not by preference.

Camoufox

Anti-detect browser · Playwright launcher

A debloated Firefox build, driven by Playwright through the Juggler protocol. Every launch draws a fresh, internally consistent device identity from BrowserForge and spoofs navigator, WebGL, fonts, screen, voices, timezone and WebRTC — before the page's first script runs.

  • Scope Web pages only, in a browser it launches
  • Control Python / TS code — ordinary Playwright objects, sync + async
  • Identity Spoofed in C++, not JS-injected, no navigator.webdriver
  • Footprint ~200 MB per instance, headless-first, no desktop presence
  • Status Open source since v146.0.1-beta.25 · PyPI 0.5.9 · self-declared "under development"

CUA Driver

Background computer-use driver · 62 tools

A daemon on the real machine: accessibility trees, per-window captures and background input, plus a CDP lane that binds one Chromium tab to its native window. The user keeps working in the foreground while the agent acts behind them, with its own visible cursor.

  • Scope Any app or window on macOS / Windows / Linux
  • Control 62 MCP + CLI tools — observe, act, verify; agent-driven
  • Identity None. Your real browser, real profile, real IP, trusted input
  • Footprint Runs invisibly over your session; no Space switching
  • Status v0.47.10 installed here, healthy; commercial backing (trycua)

Which one for the job in front of you

The pattern that uses both

Get in once, as me

CUA Driver binds the logged-in Chrome tab or the native portal window, does the one-off thing — a supplier portal, a bank read-out, a form only a human session can submit — then leaves the window as it found it.

→

Do it 1 000×, as nobody

Anything that repeats against a hostile site (prices, SERPs, listings, geo-specific data) goes to Camoufox as Playwright code: fresh identity per context, proxy-matched timezone and locale, no desktop in the loop.

Two things to hold in mind. First, Camoufox's purpose is defeating bot detection — that breaks the terms of most sites it is aimed at, and multi-account use is the sharper edge of it. It is a scraping tool, applied to targets where scraping is defensible. Second, CUA is not a stealth tool: it acts as you, with your cookies and your IP, so anything it does on a live business system carries your authority. Both need the same discipline — decide the target and the permission before the first click, not after.

Bottom line

Choose Camoufox when the enemy is a website and you need volume, fresh identities, or geo-consistent scraping — and you are happy to own it as a Playwright pipeline.
Choose CUA Driver when the target is your own desktop: logged-in portals, apps with no API, OS dialogs, visual QA — or when being a real human's browser is the advantage.
Running today CUA is installed, healthy and wired into the agent tool surface. Camoufox is a 10-minute install on any Homebrew Python ≥ 3.11, but it adds a new lane, not a new app on your screen.
