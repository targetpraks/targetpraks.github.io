Method. Claude: full catalogue mirror, capture 19 Oct 2026 — 986 directory connectors (942 web-directory + 13 Anthropic-built + community entries) out of 6,368 total records; matching ran against the curated tier. ChatGPT: OpenAI's own plugins repository (62 packages, openai/plugins) plus the public ChatGPT plugin-directory mirrors and the legacy connector list — 185 verified entries; the in-product directory is larger and has no public index. Mine: manage_connections status queried per connector slug — 54 enabled, 5 authenticated (supabase, discordbot, canva_mcp, cloudflare_mcp, todoist_mcp). Zoho verified live today: CRM, Projects, Sheet, WorkDrive and Mail each answered HTTP 200. GitHub verified via gh auth status (targetpraks). Google Workspace, Notion, Box, Todoist CLI and Himalaya were checked on PATH in this profile and are absent — they are not counted as live.

Caveat that matters. My roster rule keeps the stack lean: no extra MCP servers beyond my entry. Tier 1 is activation, not expansion — but Tier 2 needs your explicit go, because it adds vendor accounts to my surface.
