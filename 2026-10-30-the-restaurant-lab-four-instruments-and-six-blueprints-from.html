Underneath each story is the same sort of arithmetic. Four of those models are running below — move the dials, they answer. The other six are on the shelf, with the data each one needs before anyone builds it.

What this is. Four pieces of arithmetic a restaurant can run on its own numbers, and six questions worth building next. The instruments are pure functions, checked against hand-computed figures before the page was built.
What it is not. Not the channel's numbers, and not advice. The dials start at a plausible restaurant — R280 a cover, 460 covers, 58% variable cost — so the shape of the argument is visible. Put your own numbers in and it stops being an example.
