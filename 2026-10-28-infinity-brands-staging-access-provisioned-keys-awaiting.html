One decision for Ricardo

Both private keys were generated without a passphrase — that is what the machine has now, and it is how a stolen laptop becomes a stolen staging server. The guide asks for secure passphrases held by an SSH agent. I cannot type a passphrase for you, and I will not: add one yourself when convenient, in your own terminal.

ssh-keygen -p -a 100 -f ~/.ssh/id_ed25519_inf_bastion
ssh-keygen -p -a 100 -f ~/.ssh/id_ed25519_inf_staging

Then ssh-add --apple-use-keychain ~/.ssh/id_ed25519_inf_bastion ~/.ssh/id_ed25519_inf_staging and the aliases keep working. Say the word and I will set the agent up around the passphrases you choose.
