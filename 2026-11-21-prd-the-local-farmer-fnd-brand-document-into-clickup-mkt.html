07 The client list: what the document has, and what it does not

You asked for the client list. The document does not contain one. It contains six buying segments, 41 segment rows, 41 persona rows and four brand-profile answers — and zero named clients or franchisees. A scan of every table for company suffixes (Pty, Ltd, CC) returns nothing.

Gap, stated plainly. There is no roster of current franchisees or accounts, no contract status, no territory assignment, no renewal date. The personas describe the kind of client; they cannot stand in for the list of clients. The document's own tech-stack field says Zoho, which is where that roster most likely lives (Zoho CRM), not in this document.

What this PRD proposes instead of inventing one: one register item in Marketing Plan 1501000000001548 titled “TLF client & franchisee roster — source of truth: Zoho CRM”, refreshed on the quarterly audit cycle (§12), carrying name, territory, status, start date and account owner. The row count is one; the CRM remains the system of record. Until the roster is supplied, no campaign can be attributed to a named account and no report can say which franchisee is underperforming — which is exactly why it belongs in the plan.

The negative-keyword gap. The document has a 400-row keyword master built on the wrong brand, a 12-row keyword logic, and a 6-row keyword-to-landing-page register. It has no negative keyword table and no search-terms review step. In a paid-search account that is the difference between spend that converts and spend that does not. How the audits force work rather than describe it. Each audit item is dated, assigned to the account lead, and cannot be closed without a linked output: a log entry in Optimization, a corrected profile in Social, a goal update in SEO Goals, or an asset record in Asset Library. The quarterly cycle is four items, so a missed audit is visible in the same month it was due.
