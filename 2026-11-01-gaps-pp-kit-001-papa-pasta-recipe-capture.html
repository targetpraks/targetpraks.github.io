Settled question 1 — Farmhouse: is the batch 10.00 kg or 10.20 kg?

Answer: 10.20 kg is the batch. Ricardo's ruling D2 (2026-10-30): "the rows are the recipe; the total is the typo." The ingredient rows (60/18/8/6/5/3/2 % = 102 %) sum to 10.20 kg at the 10 kg basis and 40.80 kg at 40 kg. The 10.00 kg / 40.00 kg figures are the printed total row (a typo) and appear only there.

Settled question 2 — Are there more than four recipes in the full system?

Answer: No. Exactly four recipes exist in PP-KIT-001, and every source enumerates the same four:

  1. 1 · Bokgat Base — The Everyday Standard (Basic)
  2. 2 · Protein+ Pro — The Signature Flagship (Advanced)
  3. 3 · Farmhouse — Recommended Addition #1 (Intermediate)
  4. 4 · Chickpea Power Pro — Recommended Addition #2 (Intermediate)

Evidence of the closed set:

  • top4_kitchen_manual_fresh_v2.html: cover chips 1-4; "Four recipes, four distinct jobs."
  • recipes_source.json: exactly four recipe objects (slugs bokgat-base, protein-pro, farmhouse, chickpea-power-pro).
  • data/registry.json: exactly four locked @v1 versions.
  • data/bench/*.json: exactly four bench payloads.
  • public spec page (…/2026-10-28-the-bench-papa-pasta-internal-kitchen-system-pp-kit-001.html): "Four formulas are already verified, costed and written as a method" — table lists the same four, no others.

Searched but found no fifth recipe: seasonal, trial, retired or otherwise. Papa_Pasta_Golden_Balance_Formula_V2.pdf describes eight historical build options (flagship, SA-local, high-protein, max-prebiotic, super-green, chickpea, farmhouse, value) — research-stage concepts from which the four Fresh-Daily recipes were consolidated; none of them ships as a fifth locked PP-KIT-001 recipe, and none has a complete recipe card in any source.
