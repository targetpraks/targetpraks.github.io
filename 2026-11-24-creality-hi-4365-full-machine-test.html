Repeated with both runout switches armed, and with the box fresh off a restart: every load runs, every switch stays open. Then the extruder was walked forward in 10 mm steps for 200 mm with the white spool selected and loaded — the switch never closed once.

A 106-second load on a slot that reads a real white spool is the hub pushing hard against something. On the machine that was replaced, the same load took 22 seconds.

4 · The test that settles it

Everything can now be excluded except the box's own drive, so this is a two-minute check at the machine and it needs no software:

  1. Unclip the PTFE tube from the output port of the CFS — the end nearest the box, not the toolhead.
  2. Press Load for slot 2 on the touchscreen, and watch the open port.
  3. Filament comes out → the hub is gripping and the fault is along the tube or at the toolhead. Nothing comes out → the hub's drive wheel is not gripping the spool, and the box is the fault.

Tell me which one you see and I will take it from there — either way the printer underneath is good.
