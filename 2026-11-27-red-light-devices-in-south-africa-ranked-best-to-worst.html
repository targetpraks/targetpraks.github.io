How the order was decided

  1. Regulatory and clinical backing — medical-device registration (TGA, FDA, SAHPRA licence) or a published clinical trial base. This comes first because it is the only part a seller cannot invent on a spec sheet.
  2. Published irradiance, at a stated distance. A figure quoted at 7.5 cm is not the same claim as one at 15 cm, and a figure with no distance at all is not a claim.
  3. Wavelength coverage and dose control — 660 and 850 nm are the evidence-backed pair; 1060 nm and 940 nm add reach; flicker-free, pulsing and timers make a dose repeatable.
  4. Coverage and engineering — panel area, LED count and binning, EMF, beam angle, build.
  5. Warranty and local support — three years and a South African importer beats a seven-day return window.

What the ranking means in practice

Rows 1–7 are the same argument: panels that publish a real irradiance figure at a real distance and carry three-year local backing. The only reason the top two are not the obvious buy is stock — the X3000 PRO and X3000 are sold out, which leaves the X5000 at R44 225 as the best whole-body unit you can actually order today, and the X900 at R11 390 as the same engineering at a third of the size.

Row 8 is the value outlier. The Soleira 350 W publishes the strongest figure in the entire table at the most conservative distance (220 mW/cm² at 10 cm) for R5 595, or R5 169 inside the Train and return set. It sits below the big panels only because it covers one area at a time.

Row 9 is the only clinically validated face device here. Omnilux is the mask line the trial literature actually names, and at −51% it is the cheapest genuinely good thing on this page.

Row 10 is the trap. The NeuroActive 1200 W looks like the cheap full-body panel until you add the R4 549 stand it ships without (R13 974), and notice there is no warranty, no certification and no stated measurement distance.

The one caveat that applies to every row: these irradiance numbers are each manufacturer's own claim, measured on their own bench at a distance of their choosing, and no independent South African test data exists for any of these units. Compare within a brand, not across brands. The ranking is a reading of published specs and paperwork — not of measured photons.
