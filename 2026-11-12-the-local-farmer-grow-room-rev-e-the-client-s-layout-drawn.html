What was proved

Exact rectangle and facing checks; containment in the room; no machine overlap with another machine or any retained fitting; full working fronts; clear, connected aisle regions; exact aisle widths and width-chain closure; shelving free of machines. This draws the fixed arrangement without a packing search.

Confirm before installation

  1. Confirm the supplied machine envelope, feet, plumbing and cables against the scheduled rectangles, including flush installation at the east and south walls.
  2. Confirm actual tray dimensions, loading direction and withdrawal distance. The elevation shows tray positions diagrammatically.
  3. Measure door location, clear opening and swing; none was supplied or invented.
  4. Confirm back access at the 50 mm rear slot, ceiling clearance, power, water, drainage and ventilation.
