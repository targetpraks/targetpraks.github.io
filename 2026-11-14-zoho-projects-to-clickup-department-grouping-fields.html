Zoho Projects → ClickUp:
the fields that group work by department

What the Zoho project tree already records, what the ClickUp workspace already has, and the smallest set of custom fields that makes “this work belongs to that department” true everywhere.
The answer

You do not need new fields. Zoho already holds the whole taxonomy and ClickUp already has the fields built — the gap is coverage: three fields (Business Unit, Brand, Work Format) sit on a fraction of the lists, and Zoho’s four department fields are defined but never filled. Put those three on every list and make the department questions mandatory at intake; nothing else is needed.

03 The three things that are wrong

  1. Brand and Business Unit stop at the intake list. A task is created tagged, then moves to a project, recurring or register list where the brand is no longer recorded. The tag that matters most for grouping is the one that gets left behind.
  2. Department mixes two levels in one list. 79 options contain both FINANCE and FIN Franchisee: Service and FIN Head Office: Support — the business unit and the sub-team in the same picker. Nobody chooses reliably from 79 options, so nobody chooses at all.
  3. Zoho never fills the organisation fields. Business Unit 0.3%, Department 0.1%, Division 0.2% across 13,157 tasks. Whatever ClickUp is built to receive, the source is not sending it.

04 The plan — three fields, nothing new

One field per question, on every list that can hold work. This is a placement run, not a schema change: all three fields exist already.

Do not add

FieldWhy not
Division, Job Role, Manager no These describe the person asking, not the work. They belong on the request record, where Zoho already holds them, not on 473 lists.
Province no 14% filled in Zoho and only relevant to brand-ops site work; it belongs on the site visit, not the list.
Service Tier no 2% filled. Real value, but the SLA boards already carry it — four lists, not 473.
Frequency no Already answered by Work Format plus the list name; a second rhythm field would disagree with the first.

05 Fix the source, or the fields stay empty

Placement alone will not fill these fields — someone has to set them. Three small Zoho changes do that, and each is a one-time edit rather than a habit.

  1. Make Business Unit and Department mandatory on the request form. They are picklists on the portal already; the form simply does not ask for them. 0.3% filled today.
  2. Put the Work Classification block on the 13 matter projects. Store openings, migrations and EXCO packs are exactly the cross-department work that needs a department tag, and they are the 1,662 untagged tasks.
  3. Map the importer once. The FXS Import batch (1,059 tasks) can be given Brand = INFX Solutions and Business Unit = FXS in a single backfill, which alone removes 64% of the untagged pile.

06 What I did and did not touch

Read-only throughout: Zoho Projects v3 (portals, projects, task lists, task layouts, 13,157 tasks) and ClickUp v2 (spaces, folders, lists, list fields). No field, list or task was created, changed or deleted. A field placement run belonging to the build kit was finishing while I measured, so the ClickUp figures are a snapshot at 11:05 SAST, after it completed.

One slip found in passing: the SLA field F18 Store / Client Code reads back from the API as f_d on the four SLA lists — worth a rename in the UI.
