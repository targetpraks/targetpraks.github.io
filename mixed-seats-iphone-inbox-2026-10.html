Three questions, checked one tool at a time

Mixed seats: the model you want is not sold. What is sold is a second-class seat, free.

You asked for basic seats for most and higher seats for a few. I checked every tool on the list. No vendor lets two paid tiers sit in one workspace — ClickUp says it in writing. What they do sell is a free viewer/guest seat beside the paid ones, and that is where the saving actually lives.

“It is not possible for users to be on different plans in one Workspace.” — ClickUp's own help centre, on pricing per user role and plan.

The per-seat tools — can you mix tiers, and who rides free

So the split you want is a viewer split, not a tier split

You can put 12 managers on paid seats and 38 staff on free seats — but only if those 38 never own a task, never log time and never change a status. monday's viewer cannot edit anything at all. The day a staff member has to complete work, they need a paid seat, and the saving evaporates.

The honest read

monday Pro with unlimited free viewers is the only tool where your split idea works at full size — R64,745 for 15 managers plus as many read-only staff and franchisees as you like. Asana can do it too, at R85,156, and its guests can at least be assigned and comment. Everything else either caps the free seats or has none. But a viewer cannot do work, so this is a saving for people who watch, not people who deliver.

What this changes

Mobile is not a differentiator — it is table stakes, and almost everybody passes. The exceptions cut both ways: OpenProject's app is beta, so your free option has a shaky phone story, and Filestage has none, so the reviews you rate highest are the ones you cannot do from a car. If a manager signs off work on a phone, that is a mark against keeping Filestage as the review layer.

Question threeThe inbox

There are two different things called an inbox, and only one of them is worth having.

A notification tray tells you something happened. An inbox lets you sit down, read the comment, reply, assign it and clear it — the whole loop without hunting through projects. Only four tools on the list give you the second kind.

What the three answers add up to

ClickUp is the only tool that wins all three outright — a real tabbed inbox that handles comments, a full iPhone app, and a guest model that scales with paid seats. monday wins the seat question with unlimited free viewers and pays for it with a weak inbox and a viewer who can never touch the work. Asana is second on all three at nearly double the price. Nifty makes the seat question disappear at R90,644 flat but has no triage inbox. And Filestage, the tool you rate highest, has no iPhone app — which is the first real argument I have found against keeping it as your review layer.

What I would confirm before you commit

  • The ratio, not the tool. Tell me how many of your 50 actually create, assign and complete work. That number — not the headcount — sets the bill in every option.
  • One week, two tools, same work. ClickUp with a tabbed inbox against monday with free viewers, run on one brand's live projects. That test settles the inbox and the seat model at the same time.
  • Nothing has been bought, installed or signed up for. All of this is read from the vendors' own pages on 19 October 2026.
