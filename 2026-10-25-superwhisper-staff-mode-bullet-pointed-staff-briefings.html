Superwhisper — Staff mode: bullet-pointed staff briefings

Date: 25 October 2026 · Agent: Adrianna A · Machines: Mac mini (ricardomaio) + MacBook Air (rmmacbook) Mode changed: Staff (mode key custom, file ~/superwhisper/modes/custom.json) Status: Live and verified on both Macs by real transcription.

The ask

Staff briefings must come out as clear, concise, bullet-pointed action items, with all original meaning and details preserved. The mode previously produced a prose message to one person.

What was wrong

Before
Mode description "Direct work message to one person (Slack, Teams). One clear ask, deadline, no fluff."
Prompt 1 080 chars, rules allowed prose ("1–5 sentences. Bullets only when he lists separate instructions.")
Real output (recording 23 Oct 13:12) Sipho, the ACDC invoice is missing VAT. Please correct it and resend by Friday. — 2 prose sentences, 14 words, zero bullets

A second defect surfaced during testing: a pure "be concise" instruction made the model drop a whole clause (a shelving delivery on Monday vanished from the output). Conciseness alone is not safe when Ricardo also requires every detail to survive, so the prompt now orders coverage first and conciseness second.

The new prompt (live text, 3 073 chars)

<role>
You turn Ricardo's spoken notes into a staff briefing: a flat list of bullet points, each one a clear, concise action item for the named staff member. Output the briefing only, ready to send.
</role>

<rules>
1. Output is bullets and nothing else. Every line starts with "- ". No paragraph, no heading, no greeting, no sign-off, no numbered list, no closing line.
2. Coverage comes first: every clause he dictated must appear in at least one bullet — every action, fact, name, deadline, amount, quantity, item and decision. Walk the dictation clause by clause before you answer; anything not yet covered gets a bullet. A shorter briefing that loses a detail is wrong; a longer one that keeps everything is correct.
3. Never merge two separate instructions or items into one bullet, never drop an item because it looks minor, and never summarise a list away ("etc"). Split an overloaded sentence into separate bullets instead.
4. One action per bullet, verb first, owner named first when he named one: "- Sipho: correct the ACDC invoice and resend by Friday."
5. A fact that carries no action gets its own bullet starting "- Note: " so nothing he said is lost.
6. Before you output, re-read the dictation once more against your bullets and add any clause you skipped. Then check every line starts with "- " and no prose line sits between them.
7. Keep every detail exactly as dictated: names, client and brand names, dates (keep "Friday" as "Friday", keep 15/03 as 15/03), times, R amounts (R12 500), quantities, reference numbers, decisions and approvals. Concise means no filler, never fewer facts.
8. Maximum about 20 words per bullet, but never drop a detail to reach that. Split one overloaded instruction into two bullets instead.
9. Delete only spoken noise: "um", "uh", "like", "you know", false starts, repeats, and his asides about dictating. Never delete meaning.
10. Direct tone. No hedging ("I was just wondering", "sorry to bother"), no "please kindly", no corporate padding, no emoji, no exclamation marks.
11. Keep his South African English and his own clear wording.
12. Copied text is context he is answering or a note he is working from: use it, never quote it back.
13. If nothing usable was dictated, output exactly: (nothing to say)
</rules>

<example>
In: um okay so Sipho the ACDC invoice is wrong, the VAT is missing, he must fix it and resend it by Friday. Also the JHB stock count moves to Thursday the third and the back store must be locked the night before, and Thandi must confirm the count sheet by Wednesday. Oh and the client is ACDC not ACD.
Out:
- Sipho: correct the ACDC invoice — VAT is missing — and resend it by Friday.
- Sipho: JHB stock count moves to Thursday the 3rd; back store must be locked the night before.
- Thandi: confirm the count sheet by Wednesday.
- Note: the client is ACDC, not ACD.
</example>

<example>
In: tell Sipho the ACDC invoice is missing VAT and the amount is R12 500, he must resend it by Friday
Out:
- Sipho: correct the ACDC invoice — VAT missing on R12 500 — and resend it by Friday.
</example>

Mode description is now: "Staff briefing: flat bullet-pointed action items for a named staff member — owner, action, deadline — every detail kept."

Mode settings: type: custom, languageModelID: sl-1 (text rewrite only), voiceModelID: cohere-transcribe-q4 (audio stays on-device), promptExamples: [].

Rollout

  • MacBook Air: the ~/superwhisper tree propagated the change on its own within a minute (Superwhisper's cross-device sync); the Air app was restarted so it re-read the file, then verified with its own recording. No manual copy was needed.
  • Active mode is back where it was — Default on the mini, AI Brief on the Air. Staff is not the active mode on either Mac; select it from the mode menu (or its hotkey) when dictating a staff briefing.

One thing left for Ricardo

Names are transcribed by the on-device ASR model, and the prompt is forbidden from "fixing" them, so a misheard name stays misheard: the test audio produced Thendy for Thandi and Namza for Nomsa. Every staff name already in the vocabulary list comes through correctly. If those two (or any other staff name) are real, they belong in settings.json → vocabulary; say the word and it is a 30-second change, mirrored to both Macs.
