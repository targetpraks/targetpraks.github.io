  • Body: 850 mm across × 600 mm deep — 375 mm spare each side, and the motor and gearbox live in that gap.
  • Depth is 600 into 600: flush, no slack. That is the one thing to test before you burn it.

Ordering

  1. Open the product page and pick Build in 600 in the size dropdown.
  2. Add to cart, checkout with your Cape Town address — shipping shows as R549.
  3. Nothing else is needed: do not buy a cowl, flue pipe, extractor or fan. Your flue through the thatch already does that work, and the unit is open-topped like the fire you have now.

When it lands — before you light it

  1. Dry-fit it first. Lift it into the opening with no fire. It should slide in and sit level on the hearth; the front face flush with the brick.
  2. If the brick is out of plumb and it will not seat, stop and use the 14-day return — swap to the Build in 500 (R11 999), which gives 100 mm of tolerance behind the body and still sits flush at the front.
  3. Check the skewers clear the lintel through a full turn by hand before the motor goes on.
  4. Leave the motor on whichever side has more room; keep its cable away from the fire.
  5. First fire: burn down to grey coals before any meat goes on. Espetada high and fast, souvla low and slow — you control the height with the coal bed, since this unit's rotation is fixed.
