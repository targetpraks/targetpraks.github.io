The two things still wrong — and they are big

1. Packaging is almost entirely unpriced. The packaging master has 21 items. Exactly one of them — the takeaway bag — carries a price. Tubs, lids, bake trays, salad bowls, cutlery, napkins, slushee cups, dome lids, straws, sauce pots, pouches: all blank.

That means every takeaway and every slushee in the menu is costed as if the container were free. A 750 ml tub with a lid is realistically R6–R9. On a R90 pasta that is 7–10 points of margin that the model cannot see.

On file and unused: the Unibrands 21 Packaging Price List (May 2026) covers most of these items — but it prices them FOB in US dollars, before freight and duty. I have not loaded those numbers, because landing freight and duty as if they were the cost would repeat exactly the kind of error this audit is fixing.

2. The model has no VAT column, and three suppliers quote VAT-inclusive.

  • Urban Foods — the supplier's own report heads the column “Incl Price”. That is 32 lines, and Urban is the winning supplier on most herbs and vegetables.
  • Eurosaf — “prices includes VAT” on the olive oil and olive lines.
  • Akhalwaya — “Vat Included” on both flour lines.
  • Bandini, Atlas, Daisy, Zandam and Cheese Pretoria all quote excluding VAT, which is what the model assumes.

If those three are being read as ex-VAT, roughly 35 ingredient costs are overstated by 15% — and it flows straight into food cost. I have recorded the basis in the quote log but have not restated any price: that is a money decision and yours to make.
