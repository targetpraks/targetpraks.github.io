01 / Supplier response

Quote the complete machine.
Declare every exception.

Use the accompanying CSV response sheet. Keep our reference numbers and add one row per alternative, accessory or excluded service. A blank technical answer means “not confirmed”.

Commercial return

  1. Identity: manufacturer and exporter legal names, Chinese registration/business licence identifier, factory address, manufacturer/trader status and proposed contracting company. Provide beneficiary identity for normal due diligence.
  2. Comparable price: USD FCA named factory or consolidation location, Incoterms 2020. State exact location, quantity, unit price and line total. FOB named port may be added as an alternative; separate freight and insurance if CIF Durban is offered.
  3. Order structure: quote one store and repeat-store discounts. Show base subtotal separately from A3, C4, options, spares, packing, inspection, freight and installation. Do not add both extruders.
  4. Terms: minimum 30-day validity requested; state MOQ, production lead time after drawings/deposit, payment milestones and warranty. No fixed deposit is offered in this RFQ.
  5. Technical file per powered model: actual model photograph and rating plate, datasheet, full dimensions/service envelope, kW/current/phase/frequency, wiring diagram, safety report and certificate details.
  6. Deviations: identify every unmet requirement. No model, motor, material, refrigerant, control or electrical substitution after approval without written consent.

Shipping and support

  • Itemised export packing: net/gross weight, case dimensions, CBM, lifting points, restraints and moisture/corrosion protection. Label cases with document and item references; provide packing photographs.
  • Solid-wood packing/dunnage to applicable ISPM 15 requirements. Declare refrigerant type/charge for forwarder assessment. Supply commercial invoice, packing list, origin details and proposed HS code per item.
  • English HMI and operating/service manuals; critical spares with part numbers, prices and lead times; request five-year spares availability. State remote-support hours and commissioning scope.
  • Define warranty start, parts/labour coverage, exclusions and who pays international freight. Identify any actual South African service partner; do not imply one exists without evidence.
Pre-shipment release: agreed drawings, model-specific regulatory assessment, factory acceptance tests and independent inspection must be complete before buyer authorises shipment. Final payment terms will be agreed with the successful supplier.
02 / Site and import interfaces

State the load.
Prove the fit.

Current store size, board rating and counter heights are planning inputs from the earlier pack. They are not a surveyed installation drawing.

Electrical capacity: unresolved

A 60 A, 400 V three-phase service provides approximately 41.6 kVA. The previous pack’s 46.9 kW estimate with three 8 kW woks would already require about 67.7 A at unity power factor if balanced and simultaneous. A blanket 0.6 diversity factor does not establish that it fits.

  • Provide footprint plus open-door, tip, wash and service envelopes. Final hot-line length, canopy capture, make-up air, fire provisions, drainage and safe public separation require local fit-out approval. A 1.5 m hood is not pre-approved.

South African approvals

Supply model-specific safety reports, rating plate, wiring diagrams and conformity declarations. The importer will establish applicable compulsory specifications with NRCS and obtain required approval(s), or written non-scope confirmation, before shipment. The accredited laboratory must identify applicable standards and editions for each machine. CE marking and ISO 9001 do not replace applicable South African approval.

Food-contact evidence must identify alloys, plastics, seals/coatings and cleaning compatibility. The local food-safety and environmental-health team must approve the installed workflow and premises. References are listed at the end of this pack.
